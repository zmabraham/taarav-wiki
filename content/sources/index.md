## Series: מסביב לחסידות (Mesaviv L'Chassidus / Around Chassidism)

**Publisher:** Otzar HaChassidim, 770 Eastern Parkway, Brooklyn, NY  
**Editor (all volumes):** Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin  
**Series note:** A multi-volume documentary history of Chabad. Volumes combine primary sources (letters, government documents, court records) with scholarly analysis.
