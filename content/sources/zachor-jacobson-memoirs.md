# Zachor — Memoirs of Rabbi Israel Jacobson

**Source type:** Hebrew memoir book (40 scanned pages), published posthumously ~1995  
**Author:** Rabbi Israel Jacobson (ר' ישראל דזייקאבסאן), born 3 Kislev 5656 (1895) in Zuravichy  
**Compiled/Edited by:** Shalom Dovber Levin, 15 Shevat 5756 (Brooklyn, NY)  
**Original writing:** 5718–5719 (1957–1959), per instruction of the Lubavitcher Rebbe (7th)  
**Died:** 17 Sivan 5735 (1975)

## Overview

These memoirs constitute a primary-source account of Chabad history across six decades (1907–1940s), written by a student who was present at seminal events in Lubavitch, Rostov, Leningrad, and New York. The Rebbe (7th Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson) gave explicit approval and encouragement for the publication, noting that readers "will certainly learn much from them, especially regarding genuine and specifically Chabad activism."

Portions were previously published in:
- "Toldos Chabad b'Russia HaSovietit" (History of Chabad in Soviet Russia)
- "Toldos Chabad b'Artzos HaBrit" (History of Chabad in the USA)
- "Kerem Chabad" journal, issue 3, pp. 241ff
- "Di Idishe Heim" quarterly, 5724–5735, in Yiddish or English translation

## Family Background

- **Birthplace:** Zuravichy (זוראוויץ), Belarus
- **Father:** R' Yaakov Leib Jacobson — shochet (ritual slaughterer), communal leader, mohel
- **Mother:** Frieda Malka, daughter of R' Avraham Nachum of Alkav
- **Paternal grandfather:** R' Avraham Shlomo — shochet in Paritsh and Zuravichy, student of the Tzemach Tzedek, then Maharash; died 25 Elul 5661
- **Great-grandfather:** R' Yaakov Leib — shochet in Zuravichy in time of Tzemach Tzedek
- **Great-great-grandfather:** R' Yeshaya — first of four generations of shochatim in Zuravichy; lineage traced to the Shelah HaKadosh
- **Paternal great-uncle:** R' Tuvya — shochet in Bobruisk, follower of Admor R' Shneur Zalman of Kapust (author of Magen Avos)
- **Maternal grandfather:** R' Avraham Nachum of Alkav — died 29 Tammuz 5665, prosperous landowner/businessman
- **Maternal grandmother:** Marat Rachel Dvasha — died 19 Kislev 5673
- **Siblings who emigrated to USA:** Brothers R' Yisrael Yeshaya and R' Yosef Hillel (uncles, emigrated after Shavuot 5667); brother Chaim Shimon emigrated young; siblings Shlomo Tzvi, Nissan, Avraham Yosef, and sister Rivka Leah also later emigrated (before Purim 5683)
- **Sister:** Sasha Hinda — married R' Alter Beylin (shochet)
- **Wife and daughter:** Chaya Sarah mentioned in family photograph caption

## Key Persons Encountered

### At Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch (1908–)

- **Rebbe Rashab** (R' Shalom Dovber Schneerson) — Author witnessed him saying Chassidic discourses (maamarim) in the "kleiner zal" (small hall), then the large hall after fire; first encounter Shabbat parshas Noach, 2 Marcheshvan 5668
- **Rayatz** (R' Yosef Yitzchak Schneerson) — At Lubavitch, managed yeshiva admissions; wore shtreimel; author was entrusted to him by family connection
- **Rebbetzin Rivka** (מרת רבקה) — Widow of Maharash; grandmother of Rebbe Rashab; every day fed young students at her table; interceded for students to gain yeshiva admission; passed away during author's time in Lubavitch
- **Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah** — Wife of Rebbe Rashab; provided daily soup (the "Rebbetzin's krupnik") for younger yeshiva students
- **R' Yechezkel Himelshtein** — Mashgiach (supervisor) at Tomchei Temimim; later became rabbi in Rodnya
- **R' Yechiel Komisar** — Rebbe Melamed (teacher) at TT; originally from Schedrин; follower of Koydanov; later moved to Rostov; sons Shmaryahu Zalman and Aharon
- **R' Leib Babin** — Melamed, called "der Vieytker melamed"; teacher of Bava Kamma and Gittin; Rebbe Rashab said "only the teacher remained in him"
- **R' Meir Zeitzig from Piotrkow** — Later rebbe melamed at TT
- **R' Shlomo Chaim Kutan** — Shochet in Lubavitch; taught shechita to students; author stayed in his relative's home
- **R' Shmuel Gronem** (Estherman) — Mashpia (spiritual mentor) in Lubavitch; famous for his discourses and warmth; called Rashag
- **R' Shlomo Risin from Rogatchev** — Mashgiach on Chassidus in the large hall; later rabbi in Avaravichy near Homel
- **R' Elchanan Dov from Rozov** — Appointed mid-winter as mashgiach on Chassidus in the large hall; served as gabai to both Rebbe Rashab and Rayatz
- **R' Yishaaya Beylin** — Gave author room and board in Lubavitch; relative of Shlomo Chaim Kutan
- **R' Gershon Chen** — Older student who was asked to watch over the young author
- **R' Yaakov Zecharya Moskalik** — Student called "di Mame" (the Mother) for nurturing younger students; later rabbi in author's hometown Zuravichy
- **R' Shlomo Risin** — mashgiach on Chassidus in large hall

## Key Anecdotes and Teachings

1. **The shochet and Neshamos d'Atzilus:** A butcher told the Rayatz (when he was a young shochet student) — after hearing the Rashab's discourse on "neshamos d'atzilus" — "if souls need a warm shawl, what do we need?"; hence he urged the shochet not to rush but sharpen his knife perfectly.

2. **Rebbetzin Rivka's intercessions:** Widows, orphans, and poor families would beg Rebbetzin Rivka to intercede with her grandson (Rebbe Rashab) to accept their sons into the yeshiva; she would cry and plead on their behalf.

3. **Yechidus invitation to write memoirs:** On 11 Marcheshvan 5718 (1957), exactly 50 years after his first yechidus with Rebbe Rashab, the author entered yechidus with the 7th Rebbe who instructed him to write his memoirs.

4. **The Rashab's first discourse in large hall:** After the fire destroyed the small hall (winter 5668), the Rashab began delivering his discourses in the large hall — a significant shift in the physical setup of the yeshiva.

5. **R' Shmuel Gronem and crying student:** At a farbrengen, R' Shmuel Gronem told a student: "You cried? Your soul cried!" — explaining that one who merits to study at TT has divine providence leading him there.
