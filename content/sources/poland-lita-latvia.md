# Toldos Chabad bePolin, Lita vLatvia

A comprehensive Hebrew-language historical work documenting the history of Chabad Hasidism in Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia. The book covers the period from the founding of Chabad communities in these regions through World War II and the Holocaust, including the activities of the Sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe (Rayatz), the rescue efforts during WWII, the fate of individual rabbis, yeshiva students, and community members, and the history of the Lubavitch library.

## Major Sections

### Early Community History (Pages 1–30)
Founding of Chabad communities in Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia. Activities of early Chabad emissaries and rabbis. Development of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva network in these regions.

### Activities of Rayatz (Pages 31–60)
Period when R' Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz, 6th Lubavitcher Rebbe) was based in Warsaw and Otwock (1933–1939). His leadership of the Chabad movement from Poland. The yeshiva in Otwock.

### Rescue and Aid During WWII (Pages 61–74)
- **Rescue of Rayatz himself**: Escape from Warsaw through Berlin to Riga (2 Tevet 5700 / December 1939), then from Riga to the USA via Stockholm/Gothenburg (24 Adar Rishon 5700 / March 1940), arriving New York 9 Adar Sheni 5700. Diplomatic intervention by US officials and Latvian envoy.
- **Rescue of daughters and sons-in-law**: R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka escaped from Nice/Vichy France to Lisbon, arriving New York 28 Sivan 5701 on ship "Serpa Pinta." Tragic failure to rescue Rebbetzin Sheine and R' Menachem Mendel Horenstein, who perished in Treblinka.
- **Rescue of secretaries**: R' Moshe Leib Rothstein (Zusia Shifrin), R' Shmuel Zalmanow, R' Yitzchak Dov Aushpal escaped via Vilna→Riga→Russia→Japan→San Francisco, arriving New York Cheshvan 5701. R' Yechezkel Feigin HYD perished when Nazis burned the synagogue in Riga, 10 Kislev 5702.
- **Vaad Pidyon Shvuyim**: Relief fund established by Rayatz upon arrival in USA. R' Mordechai Chafetz in Riga coordinated distribution. Lists of ~100 families in Lithuania and 163 persons in Nazi Poland aided.
- **Flight via Vilna and Trans-Siberia**: Yeshiva students and families fled via Vilna, then Trans-Siberian railroad to Kobe, Japan, many continuing to Shanghai.
- **Shanghai yeshiva**: Tomchei Temimim yeshiva operated in Shanghai during the war years.
- **Radom ghetto yeshiva**: Yeshiva operated clandestinely in the Radom ghetto.

### Lubavitch Library History (Pages 75–82)
- **Founding**: Rayatz purchased R' Shmuel Weiner's bibliographic collection in Leningrad in 5685 (1924) for $5,000.
- **Growth**: Library grew to ~13,000–23,000 volumes, including rare manuscripts and early printed books.
- **Locations**: Leningrad → Riga → Warsaw → Otwock
- **Rescue**: 100 boxes of books sent via Stockholm to New York (arrived Sivan 5701 / 1941). 17 boxes remained in Warsaw and disappeared. 3 boxes of manuscripts deposited at US Embassy in Warsaw, ultimately lost. Post-war recovery efforts by R' Shlomo Zilberberg and R' Pinchas Klein.
