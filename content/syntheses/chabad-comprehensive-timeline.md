# Comprehensive Chabad Timeline (1698–2024)

*Sources: ChabadPedia timeline (3,716 Hebrew events), Curated English Chabad history (206 events), Zachor Memoirs of R' Israel Jacobson*

*Last updated: 2026-05-17*

---

## The Pre-Chabad Background: Baal Shem Tov Era (1698–1772)

### 1698
- **18 Elul**: Birth of Rabbi Yisrael ben Eliezer — the Baal Shem Tov (Besht) — in Okopy, Podolia (modern Ukraine)

### ~1700
- Baal Shem Tov orphaned at a young age; raised in the forests of Podolia

### 1716
- Baal Shem Tov marries Leah Rachel

### ~1720
- The hidden years: Baal Shem Tov retreats to the Carpathian mountains for spiritual development

### 1734
- Baal Shem Tov studies under the heavenly mentor Ahiya HaShiloni (mystical tradition)

### 1736
- **18 Elul**: Baal Shem Tov publicly reveals himself as a spiritual leader in Medzhibozh

### 1740
- Baal Shem Tov's teaching circle (Chevre Kaddisha) takes shape in Medzhibozh, Podolia

### 1742
- Baal Shem Tov writes his famous letter to his brother-in-law Rabbi Gershon Kitover describing his mystical ascent

### **1745**
- **18 Elul**: Birth of Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi — future founder of Chabad — in Liozna, Belarus

### 1750
- Baal Shem Tov's inner circle formalized

### 1753
- Publication of "Toldos Yaakov Yosef" — the first Chassidic book — by R' Yaakov Yosef of Polnoye

### 1756
- First rabbinic ban (cherem) against the Chassidim issued

### **1760**
- **6 Sivan (Shavuot)**: Passing of the Baal Shem Tov
- Rabbi Dov Ber of Mezeritch (the Maggid) assumes leadership of the Chassidic movement

---

## The Alter Rebbe Era — Founder of Chabad (1764–1812)

**Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi** — 1st Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch

### 1764
- Rabbi Shneur Zalman (age 19) travels to study under the Maggid of Mezeritch

### 1765
- The Maggid assigns Rabbi Shneur Zalman to write a new halachic code (Shulchan Aruch HaRav)

### 1767
- Rabbi Shneur Zalman marries Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah

### 1772
- **19 Kislev**: Passing of the Maggid of Mezeritch
- Great Vilna Ban against Chassidim renewed

### **1773**
- **9 Kislev**: Birth of the Mitteler Rebbe (R' Dovber Schneerson) — future 2nd Rebbe of Chabad

### 1775
- Alter Rebbe begins writing the Tanya (fundamental Chabad philosophical work)

### 1777
- Alter Rebbe travels to the Holy Land (with Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Horodok) — later returns

### 1781
- Second major ban against Chassidim

### 1788
- Alter Rebbe establishes his court in Liozna; Chabad (acronym: Chochmah, Binah, Da'as) is born as a distinct intellectual-mystical school

### **1789**
- **29 Elul**: Birth of R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson — future Tzemach Tzedek (3rd Rebbe of Chabad)

### 1792
- Alter Rebbe moves his court to Liadi

### **1796**
- First printed edition of the Tanya published

### 1797
- Third renewal of the ban against Chassidim

### **1798**
- **19 Kislev**: Alter Rebbe arrested and imprisoned in St. Petersburg (the "Miteler Arrest")
- **19 Kislev**: Liberation of the Alter Rebbe — celebrated ever since as "Chag HaGeulah" (Festival of Liberation); 19 Kislev becomes the Chabad new year for Chassidus

### 1799
- Second edition of the Tanya published

### 1800
- Alter Rebbe arrested a second time

### 1804
- Alter Rebbe sends emissaries throughout Russia to spread Chassidus

### 1810
- Alter Rebbe's Igeret HaTeshuva published

### 1812
- Napoleon invades Russia — Alter Rebbe prays for Czar Alexander's victory (against Napoleon)
- Alter Rebbe flees east ahead of Napoleon's advancing army

### **1812**
- **24 Tevet**: Passing of the Alter Rebbe in Piena, Ukraine

---

## The Mitteler Rebbe Era (1813–1827)

**Rabbi Dovber Schneerson** — 2nd Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch

### 1813
- Mitteler Rebbe assumes leadership in Lubavitch
- Schism: Rabbi Aharon of Staroselye refuses to accept him; breaks away and establishes his own school

### 1814
- Mitteler Rebbe publishes his first major Chassidic work

### 1818
- Mitteler Rebbe expands the Lubavitch yeshiva

### 1820
- Chabad pioneers settle in Hebron, Eretz Yisrael — establishing the first Chabad community in the Holy Land

### 1821
- Mitteler Rebbe delivers the famous "Torah Or" discourses

### 1823
- Mitteler Rebbe briefly arrested by Russian authorities

### **1826**
- **9 Kislev**: Passing of the Mitteler Rebbe in Niezhin

---

## The Tzemach Tzedek Era (1827–1866)

**Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson** — 3rd Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch

### 1827
- Tzemach Tzedek (R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson, grandson of the Alter Rebbe) assumes leadership

### **1834**
- **2 Iyar**: Birth of the Maharash (R' Shmuel Schneerson) — future 4th Rebbe

### 1837
- Torah Or and Likkutei Torah (major Chabad Chassidic anthologies) published

### 1840
- Russian government forces attendance at Haskalah (Enlightenment) schools; Tzemach Tzedek resists

### 1843
- St. Petersburg Rabbinical Conference — Tzemach Tzedek attends and represents traditional Judaism
- The Tzemach Tzedek gives his famous response when asked his name: "My name is Menachem Mendel Schneerson and my city is Lubavitch"

### 1845
- Tzemach Tzedek sends emissaries to defend Chabad schools from government interference

### 1856
- Tzemach Tzedek writes letters to his son on the concept of shlichus (emissaryship)

### **1860**
- **20 Cheshvan**: Birth of R' Shalom Dovber Schneerson — future Rebbe Rashab (5th Rebbe)

### **1866**
- **13 Nissan**: Passing of the Tzemach Tzedek

---

## The Maharash Era (1866–1882)

**Rabbi Shmuel Schneerson** — 4th Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch

### 1866
- Maharash assumes leadership after his father the Tzemach Tzedek

### 1869
- Maharash travels to Western Europe to advocate for Jewish communities

### 1871
- Maharash issues responsa on Torah study methods; coins concept "lechatchila ariber" (go over obstacles)

### 1875
- Maharash establishes major charitable networks across Russia

### **1880**
- **12 Tammuz**: Birth of R' Yosef Yitzchak Schneerson — future Frierdiker Rebbe / Rayatz (6th Rebbe)

### **1882**
- **13 Tishrei**: Passing of the Maharash in Lubavitch

---

## The Rebbe Rashab Era (1882–1920)

**Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneerson** — 5th Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch

### 1882
- Rebbe Rashab assumes leadership of Chabad

### 1884
- Rebbe Rashab begins his famous series of discourses on inner Torah (nigleh and nistar)

### 1890
- Rebbe Rashab founds the first formal Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in Lubavitch (some sources say 1897)

### 1893
- Rebbe Rashab writes his famous letter opposing Zionism

### **1897**
- **Elul**: Tomchei Temimim Yeshiva officially founded in Lubavitch (flagship institution of Chabad education)
- Theodor Herzl convenes First Zionist Congress in Basel
- Frierdiker Rebbe (R' Yosef Yitzchak, age 17) begins working at Tomchei Temimim

### 1898
- Rebbe Rashab writes his famous letter on the importance of mikveh

### 1900
- Rebbe Rashab begins his monumental Hemshech Samech-Vov (5666) — a multi-year series of profound discourses

### **1902**
- **11 Nissan**: Birth of Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson — the future 7th Lubavitcher Rebbe — in Nikolaev, Ukraine

### 1903
- Kishinev Pogrom — Rebbe Rashab responds with pastoral guidance and relief efforts

### 1905
- Russian Revolution of 1905 — Rebbe Rashab responds to the upheaval

### 1906
- Tomchei Temimim graduates its first class of shluchim (emissaries)
- Children's division of TT opened (~5666) with R' Menachem Mendel Lyader as first teacher

### **1908**
- Rebbe Rashab's discourse on the mission of Chabad
- **2 Marcheshvan**: Israel Jacobson arrives at Tomchei Temimim for the first time; enters yechidus with Rebbe Rashab
- **Winter 5668**: Fire in the small hall (Kleiner Zal) of TT; students fight the fire; Rashab begins giving maamarim in large hall

### 1910
- Rebbe Rashab opens additional branches of Tomchei Temimim in other cities

### 1912
- Rebbe Rashab writes against Agudat Yisrael

### **1914**
- WWI disrupts Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch

### **1915**
- Rebbe Rashab flees Lubavitch; court and yeshiva move to Rostov-on-Don; Lubavitch as Chabad headquarters effectively ends

### **1917**
- Bolshevik Revolution — Chabad faces existential threat from Soviet anti-religion campaign

### 1918
- Rebbe Rashab writes profound letters on the threat of Communism to Torah Judaism

### **1920**
- **2 Nissan**: Passing of the Rebbe Rashab in Rostov-on-Don

---

## The Frierdiker Rebbe Era — Soviet Russia Phase (1920–1939)

**Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneerson** — 6th Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch

### 1920
- Frierdiker Rebbe (Rayatz) assumes leadership of Chabad upon his father's passing
- Israel Jacobson moves to Homel (Gomel); begins founding local Chabad yeshiva

### 1921
- Underground yeshivos and mikvaos established across Soviet Russia under Frierdiker Rebbe's direction

### **1922**
- Soviet anti-religion campaign intensifies; Yevsektsia (Jewish section of Communist Party) targets Jewish institutions

### **1923**
- Future 7th Rebbe (Menachem Mendel Schneerson, age 21) meets the Frierdiker Rebbe in Rostov — pivotal encounter

### **1924**
- Chabad's Soviet underground network at its peak — secret yeshivos in many cities
- Frierdiker Rebbe moves from Rostov to Leningrad (St. Petersburg)

### 1925
- Frierdiker Rebbe establishes secret printing operation in Leningrad
- Israel Jacobson departs Homel for the USA (passing through Riga); settles in New York

### **1925 — Founding of Agudas HaTmimim (New York)**
- **Motzaei Shabbat, Shemini Atzeret 5686 (Oct. 1925)**: Israel Jacobson and ~20 TT alumni found Agudas HaTmimim at R' Eliyahu Simpson's home, 137 W. 116th St., New York

### 1926
- Frierdiker Rebbe's network members arrested by Soviet authorities
- Israel Jacobson active in Agudas Chasidei Chabad in New York

### **1927**
- **Spring**: Frierdiker Rebbe arrested by Soviet secret police (GPU/OGPU)
- Sentenced to death; sentence commuted to exile
- Future 7th Rebbe helps organize international defense campaign
- **12–13 Tammuz**: Frierdiker Rebbe released (celebrated as "Chag HaGeulah" — 12-13 Tammuz)
- **Tammuz**: Frierdiker Rebbe expelled; goes to Kostroma, then permitted to leave USSR
- Frierdiker Rebbe's diary of his imprisonment published (Reshimas HaMaasar)

### **1928**
- **14 Kislev**: Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (future 7th Rebbe) marries Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka (daughter of the Frierdiker Rebbe) in Warsaw

### **1929 — Frierdiker Rebbe's First Visit to the United States**
- **13 Elul 5689 (Sept. 1929)**: Frierdiker Rebbe arrives in the USA
- Stays through **20 Tammuz 5690 (July 1930)**
- Prays for High Holidays 5690 at "Anshei Lubavitch" synagogue, Brownsville, Brooklyn (195 Watkins St.)
- Works intensively to organize and strengthen American Chabad

### 1930
- **20 Tammuz**: Frierdiker Rebbe departs USA; later visits Eretz Yisrael

### 1933
- Future 7th Rebbe studies engineering in Berlin
- Frierdiker Rebbe moves court from Riga to Warsaw

### 1934
- Future 7th Rebbe studies at École Polytechnique in Paris
- Tomchei Temimim branches established in Europe (Poland, Latvia, etc.)

### 1936
- Frierdiker Rebbe warns about Nazi Germany's existential danger

### 1938
- Frierdiker Rebbe organizes emigration aid for European Jews

### 1939 — WWII Begins
- Nazi invasion of Poland — Frierdiker Rebbe trapped in Warsaw
- Dramatic escape from Warsaw via Berlin
- Frierdiker Rebbe in Riga, then Stockholm

## The Frierdiker Rebbe Era — War and America (1940–1950)

### **1940**
- **9 Adar II 5700**: Frierdiker Rebbe arrives in the United States
- **770 Eastern Parkway**, Crown Heights, Brooklyn established as Chabad world headquarters
- Israel Jacobson was centrally involved in the rescue operation from Warsaw

### 1941
- Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch (Central Organization for Jewish Education) founded
- Machne Israel (social services) founded
- Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (future 7th Rebbe) appointed head of Merkos and Kehos
- **28 Sivan**: Rebbe (Menachem Mendel) and Rebbetzin escape to America

### 1941
- Tomchei Temimim reestablished in Brooklyn

### 1942
- Kehos Publication Society founded
- Machne Israel founded
- Rebbe's systematic letter-writing campaign begins

### 1943
- Frierdiker Rebbe's annual letters on Jewish holidays published
- Kfar Chabad established in Eretz Yisrael

### 1945
- Frierdiker Rebbe sends emissaries to Displaced Persons camps in Europe

### 1946
- Operation to rescue Torah scrolls from Europe

### 1947
- Frierdiker Rebbe advocates for the establishment of the State of Israel

### 1948
- Frierdiker Rebbe responds to Israel's establishment

### 1949
- Frierdiker Rebbe's health declines severely

### **1950**
- Frierdiker Rebbe delivers his final maamar: "Basi L'Gani" (I Have Come to My Garden) — 10 Shevat
- **10 Shevat 5710**: Passing of the Frierdiker Rebbe

---

## The 7th Rebbe Era — Early Years (1950–1967)

**Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson** — 7th Lubavitcher Rebbe

### 1950
- Rebbe mourns and refuses to formally accept leadership for a year

### **1951**
- **10 Shevat 5711**: The Rebbe officially accepts the nesius (leadership) of Chabad
- First farbrengen as Rebbe; continuation of "Basi L'Gani" maamar begins annual tradition

### 1953
- Rebbe's vision for Jewish women's education (Nshei Chabad founded; Beth Rivkah schools expanded)

### 1955
- Rebbe begins responding to science and modernity questions in his letters

### 1956
- First summer camps established by Chabad

### 1957
- Rebbe's letters on mental health and Torah perspective on psychology
- **11 Marcheshvan 5718**: Israel Jacobson enters yechidus with the Rebbe; Rebbe instructs him to write his memoirs

### 1958
- First Chabad House opens in Morocco — beginning of global shlichus network

### 1960
- Rebbe meets with Israeli PM David Ben-Gurion in New York

### 1961
- Rebbe establishes Chabad in Australia

### 1962
- Rebbe's sicha on Jewish education in public schools

### 1963
- Rebbe mourns passing of his mother, Rebbetzin Chana

### 1965
- Rebbe's letters on the uniqueness of every Jewish soul

### 1966
- Rebbe dispatches first shluchim to the Soviet Union (covertly)

---

## The 7th Rebbe Era — Global Expansion (1967–1988)

### **1967**
- **Six Day War**: Rebbe predicts Israeli victory; launches Tefillin Campaign — over 500,000 Jewish men put on tefillin during the war; campaign continues afterward
- Chabad establishes presence at the Western Wall in Jerusalem

### 1969
- First Chabad House on a university campus opens at UCLA

### 1970
- Rebbe launches "Mitzvah Tanks" — mobile outreach vehicles

### 1971
- Rebbe sends shluchim behind the Iron Curtain for covert Jewish support

### 1972
- Rebbe's 70th birthday — major celebration at 770 Eastern Parkway

### 1973
- **Yom Kippur War**: Rebbe mobilizes Chabad for emergency support of Israel

### 1974
- The "Mivtzoim" — Rebbe launches seven mitzvah campaigns: Tefillin, Shabbat Candles, Torah Study, Mezuzah, Tzedakah, Taharas HaMishpacha, Jewish Education

### 1975
- **17 Sivan 5735**: Passing of Israel Jacobson (author of the Zachor memoirs)
- Tzivos Hashem (Children's Army of God) organization founded

### 1976
- Rebbe advocates for a moment of silence in US public schools

### 1977
- Rebbe establishes Chabad in South America

### 1978
- Rebbe's letter on the inviolability of Jewish life (in context of peace negotiations)

### 1980
- Chabad opens centers in China, Japan, and the Far East
- Children's Sefer Torah campaign
- Rebbe's campaign for observance of the Seven Noahide Laws by all humanity

### **1981**
- Rebbe begins "Dollar Distributions" — giving a dollar for tzedakah to each of hundreds/thousands of visitors each Sunday

### 1982
- Rebbe responds to the Lebanon War

### **1983**
- Rebbe institutes the daily Rambam study cycle (Maimonides' Mishneh Torah) — completing the entire work over ~3 years

### **1984**
- Likkutei Sichot (Collected Talks) — the 39-volume Torah commentary — published
- Rebbe establishes Chabad in Sub-Saharan Africa

### 1985
- Chabad's Soviet aid network at peak operation

### 1987
- Chabad surpasses 1,000 centers worldwide

### **1988**
- **22 Shevat**: Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka Schneerson passes away
- Rebbe intensifies Moshiach awareness campaign

---

## The 7th Rebbe Era — Final Years (1988–1994)

### **1989**
- Fall of Communism — Rebbe's decades of work behind the Iron Curtain vindicated; mass Jewish revival begins

### **1990**
- Chabad rapidly expands across the former Soviet Union
- **Gulf War**: Rebbe publicly assures that Israel is safe; missiles fall but casualties minimal

### **1991**
- Operation Exodus — Chabad aids mass Soviet Jewish emigration to Israel
- Public Chanukah Menorahs become a worldwide phenomenon
- Rebbe receives Ellis Island Medal of Honor

### **1992**
- **27 Adar I 5752**: Rebbe suffers a major stroke; remains non-verbal but continues to be seen
- Rebbe's continuing presence behind 770 Eastern Parkway; millions continue to seek his blessing

### 1993
- US Congress formally recognizes Rebbe's 91st birthday

### **1994**
- **3 Tammuz 5754**: Passing of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson

---

## Contemporary Chabad (1994–2024)

### 1994
- Chabad continues without appointing a new Rebbe
- The Ohel (Rebbe's resting place in Queens, NY) becomes a world-famous pilgrimage site

### 1995
- Legal victory: Rebbe's library recognized as property of Agudas Chasidei Chabad

### **1997**
- Rebbe awarded Congressional Gold Medal posthumously

### 1998
- Chabad establishes 2,000th center worldwide

### 2001
- **September 11**: Chabad chaplains among first responders at Ground Zero

### 2003
- Chabad sends chaplains to Iraq and Afghanistan with US troops

### 2004
- Chabad responds to the Indian Ocean tsunami

### 2006
- Chabad House in Kathmandu, Nepal becomes famous stop for Israeli trekkers

### **2008**
- **26 Cheshvan**: Mumbai terror attack — the Chabad House (Nariman House) targeted; Rabbi Gavriel and Rebbetzin Rivka Holtzberg and four others murdered hy"d

### 2009
- New Chabad House opens in Mumbai — "We are not afraid"

### 2010
- Chabad responds to Haiti earthquake

### 2012
- Chabad revives the Jewish community in Cuba

### 2013
- Kinus HaShluchim (annual shluchim conference) draws 4,000 rabbis from around the world to Brooklyn

### 2015
- Chabad establishes presence in 100+ countries

### 2017
- Chabad responds to Las Vegas mass shooting

### 2018
- Pittsburgh synagogue shooting — Chabad responds

### 2019
- Poway, California Chabad synagogue shooting on last day of Passover

### **2020**
- COVID-19 pandemic — Chabad pivots to virtual outreach; delivers Passover packages during lockdowns

### 2021
- Chabad celebrates 70 years of the 7th Rebbe's leadership

### **2023**
- **October 7**: Hamas terror attack on Israel — Chabad responds across Israel and worldwide

### 2024
- Chabad operates 5,000+ centers in 100+ countries worldwide
