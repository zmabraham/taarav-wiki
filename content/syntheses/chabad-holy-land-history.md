# Chabad in the Holy Land: History and Institutions (1777–1950)

A synthesis of Chabad's history in the Land of Israel from the first Chassidic aliya in 1777 through the establishment of the State of Israel, based on *Toldos Chabad beEretz HaKodesh* by Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin.

---

## The First Aliya (1777): Establishment in Tiberias

In **5537 (1777)**, a group of approximately **300 Chassidim** led by **[[menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]]** made the first major organized Chassidic aliya to the Holy Land. They settled in **[[tiberias|Tiberias]]** (and later **[[safed|Safed]]**).

Key figures of the first aliya:
- [[menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]] — principal leader; died in Tiberias 5548 (1788)
- [[avraham-of-kalisk|R. Avraham of Kalisk]] — co-leader; took over after R. Menachem Mendel's death
- [[yisrael-of-polotsk|R. Yisrael of Polotsk]] — among the early settlers
- [[tzvi-hirsh-baal-shem|R. Tzvi Hirsh Baal Shem]] — arrived 5539 (1779)
- [[tzvi-hirsh-segal-harky|R. Tzvi Hirsh Segal Harky]] — arrived with wife winter 5544 (1784)

The [[alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] was meant to join but remained in Russia to manage the movement. He organized financial support for the Holy Land community from Russia.

### The Kalisk Controversy

A significant controversy arose between the Alter Rebbe and [[avraham-of-kalisk|R. Avraham of Kalisk]] over theological and organizational matters. This controversy affected the distribution of charitable funds and strained relations between the diaspora leadership and the Holy Land community for decades.

## Kolel Chabad: The Financial Structure

### Pre-Separation (1777–1805)
Support was organized informally through the Alter Rebbe's leadership.

### Formal Establishment (5565 / 1805)
A **separate Kolel Chabad** (distinct from the general Chassidic kolel) was established with:
- **Nasi**: The Alter Rebbe
- **First Gabai**: [[yaakov-of-smilian|R. Yaakov of Smilian]]

This formalized Chabad's financial independence in the Holy Land. See [[kolel-chabad]] for the full organizational history.

---

## Hebron as Chabad Center (1821–1929)

In 5581 (1821), the [[mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] directed Chassidim to **relocate from Safed and Tiberias to [[hebron|Hebron]]** (Chevron). Hebron became the primary Chabad center for the next century.

Key Hebron-era figures:
- [[lapidot-chalavna|Lapidot Chalavna]] — Chabad artisan/settler (two men with this name)
- [[leib-baal-hayisurim|R. Leib Baal HaYisurim]] — moved from Safed to Hebron; died 15 Marcheshvan 5597
- [[uri-ornstein|R. Uri Ornstein]] — emissary from Hebron to Russia
- [[eliezer-shimon-kazernovsky|R. Eliezer Shimon Kazernovsky]] — Chabad figure in Hebron 1906
- [[shlomo-yehuda-eliezrov|R. Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezrov]] — Chabad rabbi of Hebron

The Tzemach Tzedek sent family members to Hebron to reinforce the community. Administrative control passed through the Tzemach Tzedek era assisted by:
- [[chaim-avraham-schneersohn|R. Chaim Avraham Schneersohn]] (son of Alter Rebbe)
- [[menachem-nachum-schneersohn-b|R. Menachem Nachum Schneersohn]] (son of Mitteler Rebbe)

### Moses Montefiore's Endowment

[[moses-montefiore|Sir Moses Montefiore]] visited Hebron and established an endowment that provided ongoing support to the Chabad community. His visits helped connect Western Jewish philanthropy with the Chabad settlement.

---

## The 1837 Earthquake

The earthquake of **1 Shevat 5597 (January 1, 1837)** devastated Safed and Tiberias. Chabad casualties among those who had remained in Safed included:
- [[shemiya|R. Shemiya]] — killed in Safed
- [[lapidot-chalavna|Lapidot Chalavna]] (the father) and his son **Shalom Aharon** — killed in Safed

This earthquake was seen as confirming the wisdom of the Mitteler Rebbe's directive to relocate to Hebron.

---

## Jerusalem Chabad

Chabad established a presence in [[jerusalem|Jerusalem]] over time. The **Tzemach Tzedek Synagogue** in the Old City became a center of Chabad life. The Tzemach Tzedek era saw significant expansion of Chabad's Jerusalem presence.

---

## Yeshiva Torat Emet (1921 onward)

In 5681 (1921), the [[rayatz|Rayatz]] sent seven select students from Tomchei Temimim in Russia to found **[[yeshiva-torat-emet|Yeshiva Torat Emet]]** in Hebron. This became the flagship Chabad educational institution in the Holy Land.

**Key figure**: [[alter-shimchovitch|R. Alter Shimchovitch]] — mashpia at the yeshiva for 12 years (died 5696/1936)

After the 1929 Hebron Massacre, the yeshiva relocated to Jerusalem.

---

## Tel Aviv-Jaffa Chabad (1924 onward)

Chabad presence in Tel Aviv began with a branch of Yeshiva Torat Emet:
- **First period (1924–1929)**: "Cheider Bnei Temimim" / "Yeshiva Achei Temimim"
- **Second period (1938–1951)**: Included adult division "Yeshiva Tomchei Temimim"

---

## Kolel Administration in the Rayatz Era

The [[rayatz|Rayatz]] reorganized Kolel Chabad in 5693 (1933) with a formal constitution (*tochnon*) requiring elections and a **Va'ad Pekidei Kolel** (Committee of Administrators). This modernized the governance structure while preserving the Rebbe's overall oversight.

---

## Kfar Chabad (1949)

**[[kfar-chabad|Kfar Chabad]]** — a Chabad village — was established in 1949 near Tel Aviv, becoming a center of Chabad life in the new State of Israel.
