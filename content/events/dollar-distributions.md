# Sunday Dollar Distributions

Beginning in 1986, the 7th Rebbe stood every Sunday for hours distributing dollar bills to thousands of people. Each recipient was expected to give the dollar to tzedakah. The distributions became an iconic practice, drawing people from around the world.
