# Chabad Soviet Underground

From the 1920s through the 1980s, Chabad maintained an organized underground resistance to Soviet religious persecution. This included secret yeshivas, mikvaot, matzah bakeries, and a network of mashpiim and activists who risked imprisonment and death.

## Key Facts

- **Period**: 1920s–1980s
- **Scope**: Across the entire USSR
