# Yud-Tes Kislev

The 19th of Kislev (Yud-Tes Kislev) commemorates the Alter Rebbe's liberation from prison in 1798. Known as the "Rosh Hashanah of Chassidus," it celebrates the spread of Chassidic teachings and is observed by Chabad communities worldwide with farbrengens and study.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: 19 Kislev 5559 (November 1798)
