# WWII Refugee Crisis

World War II created a massive refugee crisis for Chabad communities. Chassidim fled across the Soviet Union to Central Asia ([[entities/places/samarkand|Samarkand]], [[entities/places/tashkent|Tashkent]]), while others escaped via the Far East.
