# Berlin Wall Prophecy (1989)

The 7th Rebbe predicted the fall of the Berlin Wall and the collapse of Soviet communism. His statements, made when the USSR appeared permanently entrenched, proved prescient when the Wall fell in November 1989.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: Predictions made throughout the 1980s; Wall fell November 9, 1989
