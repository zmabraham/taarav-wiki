# Rayatz Arrives in America (1940)

On 9 Adar II 5700 (March 19, 1940), the Rayatz arrived in New York after escaping war-torn Europe. He established Chabad-Lubavitch headquarters at 770 Eastern Parkway in Crown Heights, Brooklyn, transforming a small American Chabad presence into the foundation for global expansion.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: 9 Adar II 5700 (March 19, 1940)
- **Location**: [[entities/places/crown-heights|Crown Heights, Brooklyn]]
- **Key Figures**: [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]], [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|7th Rebbe]]

## Significance

The Rayatz's arrival in America marked the beginning of Chabad's transformation from a primarily Russian movement to a global force. Within months he launched campaigns for Jewish education, publishing, and outreach that laid the groundwork for the 7th Rebbe's worldwide shlichus network.
