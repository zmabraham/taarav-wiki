# Founding of Tomchei Temimim (1897)

In 5657 (1897), the Rashab founded Yeshivat Tomchei Temimim in the town of Lubavitch. It was the first centralized Chabad yeshiva, designed to produce chassidim who were trained in both the revealed Torah (nigleh) and the inner Torah (chassidus). The Rashab called its students "temimim" — wholehearted ones.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: 15 Elul 5657 (1897)
- **Location**: [[entities/places/lubavitch|Lubavitch]]
- **Key Figures**: [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]], [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]

## Significance

Tomchei Temimim became the central institution of Chabad education for decades. Its students formed the backbone of Chabad's underground resistance during the Soviet era and its post-war revival worldwide.
