# Czarist Persecution of Chabad

Under Czarist rule, Chabad faced multiple waves of persecution including the Alter Rebbe's two arrests (1798, 1800), the Mitteler Rebbe's arrest (1826), the Cantonist decrees forcing Jewish children into the army, and various restrictions on Jewish settlement and religious practice.
