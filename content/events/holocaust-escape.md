# Holocaust Escape Routes

During WWII, Chabad chassidim used multiple escape routes to survive: through the Soviet Union to [[entities/places/samarkand|Samarkand]] and [[entities/places/tashkent|Tashkent]], via [[entities/places/kovno|Kovno]] to [[entities/places/shanghai|Shanghai]], and across Europe to Palestine.

## Key Facts

- **Period**: 1939–1945
- **Key Routes**: Poland → USSR → Central Asia; Lithuania → Japan → Shanghai
