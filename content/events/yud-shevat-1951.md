# Yud Shevat 5711 (1951)

Yud Shevat 5711 — the first anniversary of the Rayatz's passing and the day the 7th Rebbe formally accepted the leadership of Chabad-Lubavitch. He said the maamar Basi LeGani.

- **Date**: 10 Shevat 5711 (January 17, 1951)
