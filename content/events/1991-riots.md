# Crown Heights Riots (1991)

In August 1991, tensions in Crown Heights, Brooklyn erupted into riots after a car in the Rebbe's motorcade accidentally struck and killed a Guyanese-American child. Days of unrest followed, with anti-Jewish violence and the murder of Yankel Rosenbaum.

- **Date**: August 19–22, 1991
