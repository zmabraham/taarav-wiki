# י"ט כסלו — Yud-Tes Kislev: The Alter Rebbe's Liberation (1798)

**Date**: 19 Kislev 5559 (November/December 1798)  
**Location**: St. Petersburg, Russia  
**Significance**: Liberation of the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] from the Peter and Paul Fortress; founding Chabad holiday of liberation

---

## Overview

On **19 Kislev 5559 (1798)**, [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi]] (the Alter Rebbe, founder of Chabad) was released from imprisonment in the **Petropavlovskaya Fortress** (Peter and Paul Fortress) in St. Petersburg. This release — known as **Yud-Tes Kislev** (the 19th of Kislev) — became the primary annual Chabad holiday of liberation and spiritual renewal.

---

## Background: The Arrest

In **Tishrei 5559 (1798)**, the Alter Rebbe was arrested on charges filed by opponents — likely Mitnagdim (opponents of Chassidism) who used Czarist legal channels — accusing him of seditious activity, including collecting money for the Land of Israel and other charges. He was brought to St. Petersburg and imprisoned in the fortress.

See [[events/alter-rebbe-arrest-1798|Alter Rebbe's First Arrest (1798)]] for the arrest account.

---

## The Liberation

After approximately 53 days of imprisonment and investigation, Czarist authorities determined there were insufficient grounds for conviction and released the Alter Rebbe on **19 Kislev 5559**. He was able to return to his followers and continue leading Chabad.

---

## Theological Significance in Chabad Tradition

In Chabad tradition, 19 Kislev is not merely a historical anniversary but carries deep spiritual meaning:

- It is the **Rosh HaShanah (New Year) of Chassidus** — the day when the inner dimensions of the Torah were liberated along with the Rebbe
- The Alter Rebbe was reportedly told during his imprisonment by the spirit of the [[entities/people/maggid-of-mezeritch|Maggid of Mezeritch]] (his deceased teacher) that his release would come on 19 Kislev — the Maggid's own yahrzeit (death anniversary)
- The date is seen as divine confirmation of the legitimacy of Chassidic teaching

---

## How It Is Observed

Chabad communities worldwide observe 19 Kislev with:

- **Farbrengens** (Chassidic gatherings) with Torah study, Chassidic stories, and song
- Study of the Tanya and other Chabad works
- The greeting "_L'chaim u'livracha_" — a toast to life and blessing
- Reflection on the themes of liberation and the spread of Chassidic teaching

---

## Place in the Chain of Liberation Holidays

Yud-Tes Kislev is the first in a chain of Chabad liberation holidays:

1. **19 Kislev** — Alter Rebbe's liberation (1798) — _Rosh HaShanah of Chassidus_
2. **9-10 Kislev** — Mitteler Rebbe's liberation (1826)
3. **12-13 Tammuz** — Rayatz's liberation (1927)
