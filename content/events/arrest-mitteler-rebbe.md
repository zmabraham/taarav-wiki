# Arrest and Liberation of the Mitteler Rebbe (1826)

**מאסר וגאולה — The Defining Drama of Second-Generation Chabad**

---

## Overview

On the eve of Tishrei 5586 (October 1825), Czarist authorities arrested Rabbi DovBer Schneuri, the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]], on charges filed by informants against the Chabad movement. His liberation on 9-10 Kislev 5587 (late 1826) is celebrated as one of the most important Chabad holidays.

The full documentary record is preserved in [[summaries/masar-vegeulat-hamtza|מאסר וגאולת אדמו"ר האמצעי]], translated from the original Russian state archives.

## The Charges

Three denunciations accused the Rebbe of:

1. Collecting _Eretz Yisrael_ funds under false pretenses (the government suspected these funds went to enemies of Russia — the Ottoman Empire controlled Palestine)
2. Running an unauthorized religious organization
3. Maintaining suspicious foreign correspondence

---

## The Rebbe's Defense

The interrogation transcripts reveal the Rebbe defending himself with exceptional composure:

- He explained the nature of Chassidism as a devotional religious movement with no political character
- He described the _ma'amad_ (community fund) as voluntary charitable giving
- He documented the legitimate religious purposes of correspondence with communities in the Land of Israel

His responses were delivered in October 1825 over four separate sessions — composed, detailed, and theologically grounded. He is referred to in Russian documents as "Berkeh Shniur" (transliteration of Ber, son of Shneur).

---

## Parallel to the Alter Rebbe

This arrest replicated (and consciously echoed) the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe's]] two arrests in 1798 and 1800. In both cases:

- The trigger was denunciations by internal Jewish opponents
- The charge involved the Palestine fund
- The resolution was liberation
- The liberation date became a Chabad holiday

This pattern — arrest → liberation → holiday — became a recurring template in Chabad consciousness, culminating in the [[rayatz|Rayatz's]] arrest and liberation in 1927.

---

## 9-10 Kislev as Holiday

The Mitteler Rebbe's liberation date (9-10 Kislev) is his birthday and his yahrzeit — the convergence of all three dates on Kislev 9-10 makes it uniquely significant. The holiday is called _Yud-Tet/Yud Kislev_ and is marked by Chassidic gatherings (_farbrengens_), the study of his teachings, and celebration.
