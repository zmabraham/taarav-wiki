# Warsaw Ghetto Uprising (1943)

The Warsaw Ghetto Uprising in April–May 1943 was the largest Jewish revolt during the Holocaust. While not specifically a Chabad event, it occurred in the city where the Rayatz had maintained his headquarters from 1933–1939.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: April 19 – May 16, 1943
