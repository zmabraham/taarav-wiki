# Chabad in North Africa

The 7th Rebbe dispatched shluchim to North Africa in the early 1950s, notably Rabbi Shlomo Matusof to Morocco. The mission focused on strengthening Jewish education and religious life in communities that had ancient roots but faced challenges from modernization and emigration.

## Key Facts

- **Period**: 1950s onward
- **Locations**: Morocco, Tunisia, Algeria
- **Key Figures**: [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|7th Rebbe]], [[entities/people/matusof-shlomo|R' Shlomo Matusof]]
