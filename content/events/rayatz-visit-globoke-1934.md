# Rayatz's Visit to Globoke 1934

**The Rayatz Visits the Vilna Region Including Globoke**

**Date**: Sivan 5694 (June 1934)
**Route**: Included [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]] and [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]]
**Significance**: One of the last Rayatz visits to this region before WWII; strengthened communities later destroyed in the Holocaust

---

## Overview

In the summer of 1934 (Sivan 5694), the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] undertook a journey to the Vilna region that included stops in both [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]] and [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]]. This was one of the Rayatz's last significant visits to the eastern Jewish communities before the onset of World War II. By this time the Rayatz had relocated his headquarters from [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] to [[entities/places/warsaw|Warsaw]] (1933), making this a journey from Warsaw into the Vilna region.

---

## Background

### The Rayatz in Warsaw (1933–1939)

By 1934:

- **Warsaw headquarters**: The Rayatz had moved from Riga to Warsaw in 1933
- **Polish base**: Warsaw's 350,000 Jews gave him a much larger constituency
- **Travel**: He continued to visit communities across Poland and Lithuania
- **Last visits**: The mid-1930s were increasingly his last opportunities to personally visit communities in the region

### Globoke's Significance

[[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]] merited a Rayatz visit because:

- **Four synagogues**: The community had four Chabad synagogues
- **Dedicated rabbi**: The Chabad rabbi ([[entities/people/menachem-mendel-koperstock|R' Menachem Mendel Koperstock]]) was in place to receive him
- **Regional importance**: Globoke was an important node in the Vilna-region Chabad network

---

## The Visit

### Vilna

The journey began or included [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]]:

- **Chabad community**: The Vilna Chabad community received the Rayatz
- **Historical weight**: Visiting Vilna — the historic enemy of Chassidism — was always symbolically significant
- **Leadership meeting**: Met with [[entities/people/anshil-aranovich|R' Anshil Aranovich]] and other Vilna Chabad leaders

### Globoke

From Vilna, the Rayatz traveled to [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]]:

- **Community reception**: The large Chabad community (four synagogues) gave an enthusiastic welcome
- **Maamarim**: The Rayatz delivered Chassidic discourses
- **Individual meetings**: Met with community members for personal guidance
- **R' Koperstock**: The Chabad rabbi [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-koperstock|R' Koperstock]] was his host and facilitator

---

## Significance

### Last Major Visit to the Region

In retrospect, this 1934 visit was among the last times the Rayatz would personally visit these communities:

- **WWII approaching**: The Holocaust would begin just 5 years later
- **Last blessing**: For many who met the Rayatz in 1934, this was their only personal encounter with the Rebbe
- **Communities destroyed**: Globoke's Jewish community was annihilated in 1942; Vilna's in 1941–1943

### Spiritual Impact

For the communities:

- **Spiritual uplift**: A personal visit from the Rebbe was an extraordinary event
- **Strengthened identity**: Renewed the communities' sense of connection to Chabad
- **Practical guidance**: The Rayatz's guidance helped the communities navigate the challenges of the 1930s
