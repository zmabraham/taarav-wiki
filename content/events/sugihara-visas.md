# Sugihara Visas

Chiune Sugihara, the Japanese consul in Kovno, Lithuania, issued thousands of transit visas to Jews in 1940 against his government's instructions. Among the recipients were Lubavitcher chassidim who used the visas to travel through the Soviet Union and Japan.
