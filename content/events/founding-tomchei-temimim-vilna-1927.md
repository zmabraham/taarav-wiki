# Founding of Tomchei Temimim Vilna Branch 1927

**Establishment of a Tomchei Temimim Yeshiva in the Historic Mitnagdic Capital**

**Date**: 1927 (approximately, 5687)
**Location**: [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]]
**Context**: Connected to Rayatz's arrival in [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] after expulsion from Soviet Union
**Significance**: A Chabad yeshiva in the city that had historically been most hostile to Chassidism

---

## Overview

Around 1927, a branch of [[concepts/tomchei-temimim|Tomchei Temimim]] — the Chabad yeshiva system — was established in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]]. The timing coincided with the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]'s arrival in nearby [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] (1927) after his dramatic expulsion from the Soviet Union. From his Riga base, the Rayatz moved immediately to establish and strengthen Chabad educational institutions across Eastern Europe, and Vilna — despite being the historic center of Mitnagdic opposition — was included in this program.

---

## Background

### Tomchei Temimim

The Chabad yeshiva network:

- **Founded**: 1897 by the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]] in Lubavitch
- **Mission**: Combine rigorous Talmudic study with intensive study of Chassidus
- **Expansion**: The Rayatz worked continuously to establish new branches
- **Soviet persecution**: The Soviet Union had destroyed or suppressed Tomchei Temimim branches in Russia; the Rayatz was rebuilding outside the USSR

### Rayatz's Educational Program

From Riga (1927–1933), the Rayatz:

- **Network rebuilding**: Worked to reestablish the Chabad network that had been suppressed in Russia
- **New branches**: Opened Tomchei Temimim branches across Latvia, Lithuania, and Poland
- **Vilna inclusion**: Despite Vilna's Mitnagdic character, established a branch there

### Vilna's Changed Atmosphere

By 1927:

- **Over 130 years**: The Vilna Gaon had died in 1797 — more than 130 years before
- **Softened opposition**: The extreme anti-Chassidic stance had moderated significantly
- **Multiple groups**: Vilna now hosted all major Jewish streams — Mitnagdim, various Chassidic groups, secular Jews
- **Small but established Chabad community**: The Chabad presence had existed for generations

---

## The Vilna Branch

### Establishment

The Tomchei Temimim branch in Vilna:

- **Student body**: Young men studying Torah and Chassidus
- **Faculty**: Mashpiim and teachers experienced in the Chabad educational system
- **Curriculum**: The standard Tomchei Temimim curriculum — Talmud, Halacha, and Chassidus
- **Location**: Within the Chabad community infrastructure in Vilna

### Significance

Establishing Tomchei Temimim in Vilna:

- **Historical statement**: A Chabad yeshiva in the city that had once excommunicated Chassidism
- **New generation**: Training a new generation of Lithuanian Chabad leaders
- **Network node**: Making Vilna a more active part of the Chabad educational network

---

## Impact

### Short-term

The Vilna Tomchei Temimim:

- **New students**: Attracted young men from Vilna and the region to intensive Chabad study
- **Teachers produced**: Some students became teachers and leaders in other communities
- **Community strengthening**: Raised the level of Torah and Chassidus knowledge in Vilna Chabad

### Long-term Fate

The Vilna Tomchei Temimim branch:

- **Interwar operation**: Functioned through the 1920s and 1930s
- **Holocaust**: Destroyed with the rest of Vilna Jewry in 1941–1943
- **Legacy**: Some students may have emigrated before the war, carrying what they learned
