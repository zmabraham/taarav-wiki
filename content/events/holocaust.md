# Chabad and the Holocaust

The Holocaust devastated Chabad communities across Europe. Chassidim in Poland, Lithuania, and the Soviet-occupied zones faced systematic destruction. Key survival stories include the Shanghai group, escape routes through the USSR to Samarkand, and the post-war Great Escape from Soviet territory.
