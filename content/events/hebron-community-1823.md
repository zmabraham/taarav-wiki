# Chabad Hebron Community Founded (1823)

**Date**: 1823 (5583)  
**Location**: [[entities/places/eretz-yisrael|Hebron]], Eretz Yisrael (then Ottoman Palestine)

---

## Overview

In 1823, the first formal Chabad community in Hebron was established — one of the four holy cities of Judaism and home to the Cave of Machpelah (burial place of the Patriarchs and Matriarchs). This community, which would persist through devastating riots in 1834 and 1929, represented Chabad's most significant organized presence in the Land of Israel before the twentieth century.

---

## Background: Earlier Chassidic Presence

Chabad's presence in Eretz Yisrael predates 1823. The [[events/chabad-eretz-yisrael|1777 aliyah]] of [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]] and [[entities/people/avraham-of-kalisk|Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk]] had established Chassidic communities in Tiberias and Safed. But the Hebron community of 1823 represented a more formally organized, institutionally grounded Chabad presence.

---

## The Community's Character

The Chabad Hebron community was organized around:

- A synagogue with Chabad prayer customs
- A _kolel_ (stipend-supported scholars) framework
- Regular Torah study
- Fundraising networks (_shdarus_) — emissaries sent to Russia and Europe to collect _chalukah_ (charitable donations) for the impoverished Eretz Yisrael community

The community maintained a formal agreement with the existing Sephardic Jewish community of Hebron — an arrangement that allowed Chabad newcomers to establish their own synagogue and practices while respecting the existing communal infrastructure.

---

## The Yeshiva: Magen Avos / Toras Emes

The central learning institution of the Hebron Chabad community was the yeshiva known as **Magen Avos** (later renamed **Toras Emes** after the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rashab]] purchased Beit Romano and formalized it in 1911).

The continuous learning at this yeshiva — maintained through poverty, periodic violence, and the challenges of Ottoman-era Palestine — exemplified [[concepts/mesirus-nefesh|mesirus nefesh]] in its distinctly Eretz Yisrael form: not resisting Soviet persecution but simply enduring in an inhospitable environment to maintain a Jewish presence in the holy city.

---

## Significance

The Hebron community's founding in 1823 predates the [[events/menucha-rochel-aliyah-1845|arrival of Rebbetzin Menucha Rochel Slonim]] in 1845, which gave the community dynastic legitimacy. Together, the institutional founding of 1823 and the arrival of the Rebbe's granddaughter in 1845 created a community of remarkable depth and resilience.
