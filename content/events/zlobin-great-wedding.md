# The Great Wedding in Zlobin

**The celebrated "Grosse Chasene" (Great Wedding) in Zlobin that united the Alter Rebbe's family with the family of R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev; a legendary event in Chabad-Chassidic tradition**

## Background

The Alter Rebbe (R' Shneur Zalman of Liadi) and R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev (1740–1810) were both leading disciples of the Maggid of Mezeritch. They were among the most prominent Chassidic leaders of their generation and maintained a close relationship. According to Chassidic tradition, they became in-laws through a marriage between their families.

## The Wedding

The wedding was held in Zlobin — a town chosen for its central geographic location between Liadi (the Alter Rebbe's residence) and Berditchev. The Shusterman memoir, written by a native of Zlobin, notes that "the Alter Rebbe and R' Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev were mechutanim [co-parents-in-law]" and that this famous wedding was held in Zlobin. Many notable Chassidim from the surrounding areas attended, including:

- R' Yitzchak Aizik Epstein ("R' Eizl Homeler")
- R' Baruch Mordechai of Bobruisk
- Other leading Chassidim from the Homel, Bobruisk, and White Russian regions

The wedding generated numerous legendary stories and traditions that circulated in Chabad for generations. The town's elders still recounted these stories in the early 20th century.

## Significance

The Zlobin wedding represents the social and spiritual connections between the different streams of early Chassidism. The alliance between the Liadi (Chabad) and Berditchev (Polish/Ukrainian Chassidism) movements through a marriage was a significant event, symbolizing the unity of the emerging Chassidic world. The choice of Zlobin as the location placed this small White Russian town permanently on the Chassidic map.
