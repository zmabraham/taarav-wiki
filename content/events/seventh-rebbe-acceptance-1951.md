# Seventh Rebbe Accepts Leadership (1951)

On Yud Shevat 5711 (January 17, 1951), Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson formally accepted the leadership of Chabad-Lubavitch, exactly one year after the Rayatz's passing. He delivered the maamar "Basi LeGani," declaring "LeAlter LeGeulah" — immediately to redemption.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: 10 Shevat 5711 (January 17, 1951)
- **Location**: [[entities/places/770|770 Eastern Parkway]]
- **Key Figures**: [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|7th Rebbe]]

## Significance

This was the formal beginning of the 7th Rebbe's 43-year leadership, during which Chabad grew from a small post-Holocaust community into the largest Jewish outreach organization in the world.
