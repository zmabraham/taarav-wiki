# Rayatz Arrest (1927)

In the summer of 1927, the OGPU (Soviet secret police) arrested the Rayatz in Leningrad. He was imprisoned in the Shpalerka prison and initially sentenced to death, commuted to exile in Solovki (Arctic labor camp), and ultimately expelled from the USSR.
