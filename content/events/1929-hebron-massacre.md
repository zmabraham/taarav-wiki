# 1929 Hebron Massacre

The 1929 Arab riots included a devastating massacre in Hebron on 17 Av 5689 (August 24, 1929), killing 67 Jews. The Chabad community in Hebron, which had maintained a presence since the late 18th century, was among those affected.
