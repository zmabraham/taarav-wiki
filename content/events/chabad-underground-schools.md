# Chabad Underground Schools

After the Bolshevik Revolution and the Soviet ban on religious education, Chabad established a vast network of underground yeshivas that operated secretly across the USSR. These schools preserved Torah learning and chassidic training at enormous personal risk to their students and teachers.

## Key Facts

- **Period**: 1920s–1970s (peak 1920s–1940s)
- **Locations**: [[entities/places/moscow|Moscow]], [[entities/places/kherson|Kherson]], [[entities/places/mogilev|Mogilev]], [[entities/places/homel|Homel]], and many other cities
- **Key Figures**: [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]], [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|7th Rebbe]]

## Significance

The underground schools ensured the survival of Chabad through the Soviet period. Many students were arrested and sent to labor camps. The network demonstrated the extraordinary mesirus nefesh (self-sacrifice) that characterized Chabad under communist rule.
