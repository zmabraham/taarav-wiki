# Ponary Massacres

The Ponary (Paneriai) massacre site near Vilna was where Nazi forces and local collaborators murdered approximately 100,000 people, mostly Jews, during the Holocaust. The Vilna Jewish community, which had historical connections to Chabad, was largely destroyed.

## Key Facts

- **Period**: 1941–1944
- **Location**: [[entities/places/ponary|Ponary]], near [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]]
