# Tanya Vilna Edition — Rashab's Corrected Critical Edition (1900)

## The Standard Reference Text of the Tanya Established

**Date:** תר"ס (1900)
**Published by:** Tomchei Tmimim, Lubavitch
**Supervised by:** [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rabbi Shalom Dov Ber Schneersohn]] (the Rashab), fifth Rebbe of Chabad
**Significance:** Established the authoritative, corrected text of the Tanya used as the standard reference to this day

---

## Overview

By the late nineteenth century, the Tanya — first printed in Slavuta in 1796/7 — had gone through dozens of editions printed in various cities across Eastern Europe. Each printing introduced its own variants, errors, editorial modifications, and typesetting differences. After more than a century of copying and reprinting, the accumulated textual variants had become a significant problem: scholars and Chassidim studying the Tanya could not always be confident they were working from an accurate text.

## The Rashab's Editorial Project

The Rashab (Rabbi Shalom Dov Ber Schneersohn, 1860–1920), the fifth Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch, undertook a systematic editorial project to produce a corrected, authoritative edition of the Tanya. The project involved:

1. **Collating existing editions**: The Rashab and his editors examined the various existing editions of the Tanya, noting the textual variants among them.
2. **Consulting early manuscripts**: Where original or very early manuscript versions of Tanya sections were available, these were consulted to establish the most accurate readings.
3. **Editorial review**: The Rashab personally reviewed the text to produce a critically corrected edition.

The result was published in תר"ס (1900), concurrent with the founding of the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Lubavitch. Its publication at this time was not coincidental — the establishment of a new yeshiva system required a reliable text for study.

## Significance

The תר"ס Rashab edition became the standard reference text for all subsequent Tanya publications. Its importance is comparable to the establishment of a critical edition in biblical or classical scholarship: it provides a reliable base text against which other versions can be compared. The sources in Toldos Chabad BeRussia note this edition as a key milestone in the Tanya's printing history, situating it as the culmination of a century-long process from the first 1796/7 Slavuta printing through the accumulated proliferation of editions.

The edition also reflects the Rashab's broader commitment to establishing Chabad institutions on a firm intellectual and textual foundation at the dawn of the twentieth century — a period of intense social, political, and ideological pressures on Russian Jewry.
