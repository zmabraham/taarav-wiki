# Mitnagdic Opposition to Chabad

The Mitnagdim, led by the Vilna Gaon, opposed the early Chassidic movement including Chabad. This opposition included bans (herem), social ostracism, and in some cases informing to the authorities — most notably the arrests of Chabad chassidim in Vilna in 1798.
