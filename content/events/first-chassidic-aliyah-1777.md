# עליית החסידים הראשונה לארץ ישראל

## The First Chassidic Aliyah (1777)

**Date**: 1777 (5537)  
**Who**: A group of Chassidim — some Chabad-adjacent, some from other early Chassidic circles  
**Destination**: Primarily [[entities/places/tzfas|Tzfas (Safed)]] and [[entities/places/tiberias|Tiberias]] in the Galilee  
**Significance**: Established the first Chassidic presence in Eretz Yisrael; predates Chabad's formal crystallization

---

## Overview

In **1777 (5537)**, a group of Chassidim made aliyah to the Land of Israel — one of the earliest organized Chassidic settlements in Eretz Yisrael. This aliyah is documented in the _Toldot Chabad BeAretz HaKodesh_ (Chabad History in the Holy Land) as the beginning of Chabad's presence in the land.

---

## Context: Early Chassidism and Eretz Yisrael

The aliyah of 1777 occurred while the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] was still establishing Chabad as a distinct movement (he had left the circle of the [[entities/people/maggid-of-mezeritch|Maggid of Mezeritch]] and was beginning to develop his distinctive approach). The movement of Chassidim to Eretz Yisrael in 1777 thus predates the crystallization of Chabad as a separate school.

Earlier, in 1764 and 1777, [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]] (who was close to both the Maggid and early Chabad circles) led aliyot to Eretz Yisrael. The group that eventually settled included both those who remained associated with what became Chabad and others who aligned with different Chassidic currents.

---

## The Settlers' Destinations

The 1777 olim settled primarily in:

- **Tzfas (Safed)**: The mystical capital, associated with the Ari (Rabbi Yitzchak Luria) and Kabbalistic tradition — a natural magnet for mystically-oriented Chassidim
- **Tiberias**: On the Sea of Galilee, another of the four holy cities

Their settlement initiated the Chassidic presence in the Galilee that continued for generations.

---

## Significance for Chabad

The _Toldot Chabad BeAretz HaKodesh_ begins the story of Chabad in the Holy Land with this early aliyah, framing Chabad's Eretz Yisrael presence as predating the formal movement. This aliyah represents:

- The earliest organized Jewish settlement in Eretz Yisrael from the Chassidic world
- The beginning of the Chassidic-Eretz Yisrael connection that later produced the [[events/hebron-community-1823|Hebron community (1823)]] and [[events/menucha-rochel-aliyah-1845|Menucha Rochel's aliyah (1845)]]
