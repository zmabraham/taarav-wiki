# Gimmel Tammuz 5754 (1994)

3 Tammuz 5754 (June 12, 1994) — the day the 7th Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, was nistalek after a stroke suffered the previous year. This date became a defining moment for Chabad-Lubavitch, which continued its global mission without a successor.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: 3 Tammuz 5754 (June 12, 1994)
- **Location**: [[entities/places/crown-heights|Crown Heights, Brooklyn]]
