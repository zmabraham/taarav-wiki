# Chabad Berlin Renewal (1996)

Rabbi Yehuda Teichtel and Rabbi Shmuel Segel established the Chabad-Lubavitch Zentrum in Berlin in 1996, renewing the Chabad presence that had existed in the city during the Rashab's visits and the Rayatz's stay in the late 1920s.
