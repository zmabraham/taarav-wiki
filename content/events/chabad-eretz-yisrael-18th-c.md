# Early Chabad in Eretz Yisrael (Late 18th–19th Century)

**The establishment and early development of Chabad presence in the Land of Israel, beginning with the first Chassidic aliyah of 1777 through the 19th century**

## Background

The first major Chassidic immigration to Eretz Yisrael occurred in 1777, when R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and R' Avraham of Kalisk led approximately 300 Chassidim to settle in the Galilee. This group established itself primarily in Tiberias and Safed (Tzfat).

## Pre-Chabad Background (1777–1812)

These early settlers were not yet Chabad Chassidim in the specific sense, as Chabad was still being formed as a distinct movement under the Alter Rebbe. However, R' Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and R' Avraham of Kalisk had been students of the Maggid of Mezeritch alongside the Alter Rebbe, making them part of the same general Chassidic world. Their community in Tiberias maintained active correspondence with the Alter Rebbe about theological and practical matters.

## Emissaries and Financial Ties

The Eretz Yisrael communities depended heavily on financial support (chalukah) from Diaspora communities. They sent regular emissaries (shluchim) to collect funds:

- **Yoel Smolianir** — an 18th-century emissary from Tiberias who traveled to Russia, documented in Toldos Chabad BeEretz HaKodesh
- Other unnamed shluchim who traveled regularly to Chabad communities

These emissaries maintained the crucial link between the Galilee settlers and their brethren in White Russia.

## Hebron Community

By the 19th century, a distinct Chabad community established itself in Hebron, one of the four holy cities. The Hebron community (documented in other wiki pages) had its own character and leadership.

## Jerusalem Community

A Chabad presence developed in Jerusalem as well, with the community there maintaining its own character and institutions.

## 19th-Century Aliyot

Throughout the 19th century, additional Chabad Chassidim made aliyah, particularly:
- Under the Tzemach Tzedek, who sent many students to Eretz Yisrael
- Individual Chassidim who settled in the Holy Land for spiritual reasons

## Growth to Formal Organization (1941)

The Chabad presence in Eretz Yisrael grew steadily through immigration until 1941, when it was formally organized as Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael (see [[events/agudas-chassidei-chabad-eretz-yisrael-1941]]).
