# שנת תער"ב (1901–02)

**חלק ראשון (Part 1) — The Founding Year**

---

## Overview

The year **תער"ב** (1901–02) is the opening year of the Hemshech Taarav — its **17 maamarim** lay the entire architectural foundation on which the next two decades of discourse are built. The Rashab begins at Shavuot (the festival of Torah reception) and does not choose his opening arbitrarily: the question of how Israel received Torah at Sinai is the hemshech's first lever for prying open the structure of Keter.

This is the year the Rashab establishes the hemshech's two most fundamental claims:
1. **Keter has two inward faces** — Ta'anug (divine delight) and Ratzon (will) — which precede the structured emergence of the ten Sefirot
2. **Or Yashar and Or Chozer** — the direct downward flow of divine light and the reflected ascending light — are the structural metaphor that will govern the entire discourse

Every subsequent year builds on what תרע"ב establishes.

---

## Conceptual Arc of This Year

### Opening: Keter as Dual Intermediary (Maamarim 1–3, Shavuot)

The hemshech opens with the Talmudic account of the two crowns the angels bound on Israel at Sinai — one for *Naaseh* and one for *Nishma*. Why two separate crowns, not one? The Rashab uses this question to establish that Keter itself contains two inner dimensions: **Ta'anug** (pure delight, pre-relational, analogous to Neshama) and **Ratzon** (will, the first outward turning). These are not two faces of a single Sefirah but two pre-Sefirot strata within Keter — the hemshech's load-bearing discovery.

Maamar 2 (Ps. 104:2, *He wraps Himself in light as a garment*) advances this by showing that Or Mekif (encompassing light = will = Ratzon) and Or Pnimi (internalized light = intellect, emotion) map onto the two crown-strata of Keter. Maamar 3 consolidates: the unity of Israel at Sinai (*כאיש אחד בלב אחד* — as one person with one heart) expresses how Or Chozer transforms dispersed or-pnimi into a unified whole.

### Summer–Fall: Or Yashar / Or Chozer in the Sefirot (Maamarim 4–11)

From Parshat Naso through Parshat Shoftim, the Rashab works through the structure of the Sefirot as vessels and lights. The key advance: Or Yashar flows down and produces differentiation (each Sefirah has its own light and vessel), while Or Chozer ascends and produces integration. The *Leviim* in Parshat Naso carrying the components of the Mishkan becomes a detailed allegory for how each Sefirah is "carried" by a specific combination of inner and encompassing lights.

**Pivotal maamar**: *ש"פ חקת* (Parshat Chukat) — the Rashab introduces the concept of Chukat (divine decree beyond reason) as the paradigm for Atzmus: divine essence that does not "reason" itself into being but simply *is*. This seeds the hemshech's later climax that physical mitzvot (themselves Chukim in structure) are the vessel for Atzmus.

### Elul–Rosh Hashana Period: The Kav and the Reshimu

The Devarim-season maamarim (Parshat Devarim through Parshat Nitzavim) turn to the Tzimtzum. Having established Keter's structure *before* the Tzimtzum, the Rashab now accounts for what happens at and after the Tzimtzum: the Reshimu (impression remaining after the contraction), the Kav (the line drawn into the Makom HaPanui), and how these set the stage for Or Yashar and Or Chozer to operate inside creation. Parshat Nitzavim is the year's closing: *Atem Nitzavim* — you stand today — is read as describing the soul's capacity to stand in the divine presence precisely through its Or Chozer quality.
