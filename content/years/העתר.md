# שנת העת"ר (1909–10)

**חלק שני (Part 2) — The Mature Synthesis**

---

## Overview

The year **העת"ר** (1909–10), with its **58 maamarim**, is the hemshech's culminating year and all of **Part 2** (*Chelek Sheni*). Delivered nearly a decade after the hemshech's opening year, with all of Part 1's accumulated framework fully absorbed, these 58 discourses are the Rashab's mature synthesis — applying the Or Yashar/Or Chozer/Keter architecture comprehensively to the complete annual cycle of Torah readings, from Rosh Hashana through Parshat Nitzavim.

Part 2's distinctive character: where Part 1 built the framework *conceptually* (establishing Keter's structure, Or Mekif, the hidden Sefirot, Tohu/Tikkun, Ta'anug, Teshuva), Part 2 *inhabits* that framework — working through each week's parashah and each festival as an application of the now-complete system. The result is what could be called the hemshech's *Atlas*: every major event in Israel's sacred calendar and every major section of Torah is shown to express a specific moment in the Or Yashar / Or Chozer dynamic.

Three conceptual advances are distinctive to Part 2:
1. **Prayer vs. Blessing** (*tefillah* vs. *bracha*) as the two modes of divine-human connection, corresponding to Or Chozer from below and Or Yashar from above
2. **The Two Names of Havaya in Shema** — the two occurrences of the Tetragrammaton in *Shema Yisrael* correspond to Or Mekif (*Havaya d'leila*) and Or Pnimi (*Havaya d'ltata*), with Shem Elokim as the Tzimtzum between them
3. **Collective standing (*Nitzavim*)** — the hemshech's final teaching: that the Or Chozer of all of Israel unified — from the tribal head to the wood-cutter — constitutes the highest form of Or Chozer, surpassing even the most elevated individual ascent

## Conceptual Arc of This Year

### Rosh Hashana: The Sealed Shofar (Maamarim 77–80)

Part 2 opens with *תקעו בחדש שופר בכסה* — the same verse as תרד"ע's Rosh Hashana — but the analysis is now architecturally complete. The *bakesseh* (the hidden/sealed moon) is now explicated through the full Keter framework: the sealed dimension is not merely Ta'anug as distinct from Ratzon (as in תרד"ע) but specifically **the Ta'anug that precedes even the distinction between Ta'anug and Ratzon** — Atzmus itself in its most internal mode.

The shofar's *kol* (voice-without-words, sound beyond speech) is now read as the direct channel to this pre-Ta'anug/Ratzon Atzmus: it is the one human act that bypasses the entire structured hierarchy of dibur/machashavah/ratzon and reaches *before* Keter's own internal differentiation.

**Shabbat Teshuva and Ha'azinu**: The autumn cluster includes the Ha'azinu maamar, which develops the song-structure of *Ha'azinu* as the paradigm for how Or Chozer functions in the collective: a *shira* (song) is produced by *kol* voices sounding together — the collective Or Chozer of Israel's return to God has a qualitatively different resonance than individual return.

### Sukkot and Shemini Atzeret (Maamarim 81–83)

Part 2's Sukkot maamarim develop a new angle not present in Part 1: the **Arba'at haMinim** (four species) as a structural model for how Or Chozer works across different soul-types. The etrog (internal fragrance and taste), lulav (tall stalk without taste), hadas (fragrance without taste), and aravah (neither) correspond to four registers of the divine-human interface:
- Those with Torah knowledge AND practical deed
- Those with Torah knowledge alone
- Those with practical deed alone
- Those with neither — yet all are bound together (*atem kulchem*)

The binding of all four into a single unit is the Or Chozer of inclusion — the "collective standing" that will culminate in Nitzavim.

### The Winter Months: Bereishit Through Vayigash (Maamarim 84–95)

Part 2's greatest structural innovation: for the first time in the hemshech, **the weekly parasha is the sustained vehicle** for each maamar. Every Shabbat from Bereishit (October) through Vayigash (January) receives a dedicated discourse, making this the most systematically Torah-anchored section of the entire hemshech.

Key maamarim in this sequence:
- **Bereishit**: The divine *ta'anug* in creation — the world arose from Atzmus's essential delight (*Ta'anug haAtzmi*), not from purposive Ratzon
- **Vayetze**: Yaakov's ladder (*sulam mutzav artza v'rosho magia hashamayma*) — the ladder as the structural diagram of Or Chozer: rooted in earth, ascending to heaven, with divine messengers moving both directions simultaneously
- **Vayishlach**: Yaakov's wrestling match with the angel — the night-combat as Or Chozer engaged in the resistant darkness; Yaakov's limp as the mark of the descent required to ascend
- **19 Kislev** (maamar 92, *פדה בשלום נפשי*): The **prayer/blessing distinction** — prayer is Or Chozer from below (anyone can pray); blessing is Or Yashar from above (requires standing higher than the one being blessed). *Chassidim* in the verse "your pious ones will bless You" are identified as those capable of *bracha*, whose Or Chozer has reached the level of Or Mekif. The 13 Attributes of Mercy = pnimiyut haKeter, drawn down through Birkat Kohanim

### Chanukah and Winter (Maamarim 93–95)

Part 2's Chanukah maamar (*ש"פ מקץ, ש"ח*) deepens the Part 1 Chanukah analysis: the oil that is *asur lehishtamesh bo* (forbidden for use) is now read not merely as Or Mekif but specifically as **Keter's Ta'anug dimension** — the sealed pleasure-light that cannot be instrumentalized. The Greek assault was an assault on the Ta'anug dimension itself: Greece's philosophy sought to replace the non-instrumental divine delight with purposive rationality (*chochmah*, reason-oriented beauty). The Chanukah victory was the revelation that even after the assault, the Ta'anug-light remained — *matza po* (they found there) the single sealed flask.

### The Shema Analysis (Maamarim 100–101)

Two consecutive maamarim on *Parshat Beshalach* and *Yitro* develop Part 2's most architecturally significant insight about the Shema:

The verse *שמע ישראל ה' אלקינו ה' אחד* contains two instances of the divine Name. These correspond to:
- **First Havaya** (*Havaya d'leila*) = Or Ein Sof in its encompassing mode (Sovev Kol Almin) = the Name as it precedes Tzimtzum
- **Shem Elokim** (*Elokeinu*) = the Tzimtzum itself — the divine concealment that makes creation possible, and also the concealment by which Havaya's first instance hides within the structured world
- **Second Havaya** (*Havaya d'ltata*) = Or Ein Sof as it manifests within the world-system (Memaleh Kol Almin) = the Kav after its entry into the vacated space

The declaration of Shema is thus not merely an affirmation of divine unity but a precise *cosmological act*: the worshipper draws the two modes of Or Ein Sof (pre- and post-Tzimtzum) into alignment through the mediating acknowledgment of Shem Elokim.

### Shavuot: The Deepened Elokim Analysis (Maamarim 117–119)

Part 2's Shavuot returns to the *Vayedaber Elokim* question from תרד"ע but with the Shema-analysis now available:

Why Shem Elokim at Matan Torah? Because Torah-giving is structurally the same as the Shema-declaration in reverse: at Sinai, God "declared" to Israel from the side of the Second Havaya (the Kav, Or Yashar descending) through the Elokim-channel. Torah is the act by which the infinite — specifically the *Havaya d'leila* level — translates itself through the Elokim-Tzimtzum into a receivable form. The "letters of the Torah" are the Reshimu's articulation, not the Or itself.

This explains *Anochi Hashem Elokeinu*: the very first word of the Ten Commandments (*Anochi* = "I, My innermost") and the last two words (*Hashem Elokeinu*) map directly onto the Shema's structure. Torah is the Shema *said from the divine side*.

### Summer–Elul: The Closing Synthesis (Maamarim 120–134)

The summer and Elul maamarim work through the remaining Torah readings (Naso through Nitzavim), accumulating toward the year's culminating insight in the final maamar.

Notable:
- **Shelach** (maamar 122): The spies' failure as a failure of Or Chozer — they descended into the land but could not generate the Or Chozer of "ascent" from within it, remaining trapped in Or Yashar's descending perspective (*eretz ochelet yoshveha*)
- **Chukat** (maamar 124): Returns to the Chukat paradigm established in תרע"ב — Chukat as the structure of Atzmus that does not "rationalize" itself. Part 2's treatment deepens this: Chukat is the paradigm for the *entire Torah*, not just specific statutes; the Torah's "rationality" is never its ultimate ground, which is always Atzmus
- **Va'etchanan** (maamar 128): The Shema analysis in its fullest form, linking the prayer-declaration to the cosmological structure developed in maamarim 100-101
- **Re'eh** (maamar 130): "See, I set before you today blessing and curse" — the binary of Or Yashar (blessing, descending) and Or Chozer (curse transformed into blessing through ascent)

### The Closing: Atem Nitzavim (Maamar 134)

The final maamar of the hemshech (Part 2) takes the verse *אתם נצבים היום כולכם לפני ה' אלקיכם* — "You stand today, all of you, before the Lord your God" (Nitzavim).

"Today" (*hayom*) = Rosh Hashana — the year completes its arc by returning to its own opening. Israel "stands" before God on Rosh Hashana — but it is specifically *kulchem* (all of you), from *rasheihem* (heads of tribes) through *chotev etzecha* (the wood-cutter) — the entire range of the nation in every social and spiritual level.

This *kulchem* is the closing statement of the hemshech's central teaching: Or Chozer is not the achievement of the spiritual elite alone. The wood-cutter's Or Chozer — ascending from the lowest, most physical labor — is essential to the collective "standing." Without the wood-cutter, the tribal head cannot stand before God; without the tribal head, the wood-cutter has no coherent direction. The hemshech ends with the image of **total integration**: Or Yashar descending through all levels, Or Chozer ascending from all levels, meeting in the singular "standing" of Israel as one before Atzmus.
