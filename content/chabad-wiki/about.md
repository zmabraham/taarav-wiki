# About This Wiki

## What Is This?

This is an AI-maintained knowledge wiki for Chabad-Lubavitch Chassidic literature. It provides structured access to 77,000+ pages of primary texts from seven generations of Chabad Rebbes.

## How It Works

1. **Source texts** come from [chabadlibrary.org](https://www.chabadlibrary.org/) (77K+ pages) and [dach.dev](https://www.dach.dev/) (Likkutei Sichos)
2. **Wiki pages** are generated and maintained by an AI agent using the "Karpathy wiki method" — the LLM reads new content and incrementally updates pages
3. **Daily lint** runs an AI review to check for inconsistencies, missing cross-references, and opportunities to deepen coverage
4. **Search** is powered by Quartz's built-in full-text search

## Structure

- `authors/` — Biographical pages for each Rebbe
- `books/` — Overview pages for each major work
- `concepts/` — Key Chassidic concepts and terms
- `likkutei-sichos/` — Indexed content from Likkutei Sichos

## Technology

- [trycloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/do-more-with-tunnels/trycloudflare/) — Tunnel for public access
