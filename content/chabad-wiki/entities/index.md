# Entities — People, Practices, and Texts

Supporting figures, practices, and texts referenced throughout the Chabad wiki.

## Classical Commentators

- [[entities/rashi|Rashi]] — Rabbi Shlomo Yitzchaki, the foremost Torah commentator (1040–1105)
- [[entities/rambam|Rambam]] — Maimonides; halachic authority and philosopher (1138–1204)
- [[entities/ramban|Ramban]] — Nachmanides; kabbalist and Torah commentator (1194–c. 1270)

## Foundational Chassidic Figures

- [[entities/baal-shem-tov|Baal Shem Tov]] — Founder of Chassidism (1698–1760)
- [[entities/maggid-of-mezritch|Maggid of Mezritch]] — Primary disciple and successor of the Baal Shem Tov (c. 1704–1772)

## Chabad Practices and Institutions

- [[entities/farbrengen|Farbrengen]] — Chassidic gathering for inspiration, song, and Torah
- [[entities/niggun|Niggun]] — Chassidic melody; wordless song as spiritual expression
- [[entities/avodah|Avodah]] — Divine service; the inner work of the soul

## Spiritual Concepts and Challenges

- [[entities/histapkus|Histapkus]] — The danger of spiritual complacency and self-satisfaction
- [[entities/moshiach|Moshiach]] — The Messiah; the Chabad teaching on imminent redemption

## Primary Texts

- [[entities/hayom-yom|Hayom Yom]] — Daily calendar of Chassidic teachings compiled by the Rebbe
- [[entities/igros-kodesh|Igros Kodesh]] — The Rebbe's collected correspondence
- [[entities/reshimos|Reshimos]] — The Rebbe's private journals and notes
