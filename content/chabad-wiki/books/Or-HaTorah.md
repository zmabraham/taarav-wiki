# Or HaTorah

**Author:** [[authors/maggid-of-mezritch|Maggid of Mezritch]]
**Library ID:** `400000000`

## About This Work

Torah light — a collection of the Maggid of Mezritch's teachings as recorded by his disciples. Covers the weekly Torah portions with deep Kabbalistic and Chassidic insights.
