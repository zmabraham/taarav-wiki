# Hayom Yom

**Author:** [[authors/the-rebbe|The Rebbe]]
**Library ID:** `3700000000`

## About This Work

From Day to Day — a perpetual calendar compiled by the Rebbe in 1942–43 at the request of his father-in-law, the Rayatz. Each day of the Hebrew calendar has a Chassidic teaching, custom, or aphorism drawn from Chabad tradition. One of the most widely-read Chabad texts.
