# Torah Or

**Author:** [[authors/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]]
**Library ID:** `3900000000`

## About This Work

Torah Light — Chassidic discourses on the weekly Torah portions of Bereishit through Shemot, plus Chanukah. A primary collection of the Alter Rebbe's *maamarim*.
