# Or HaTorah (Tzemach Tzedek)

**Author:** [[authors/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]]
**Library ID:** `1800000000`

## About This Work

An encyclopedic Chassidic commentary by the Tzemach Tzedek spanning the Torah, Prophets, Hagiographa, and more. Considered one of the most comprehensive Chassidic works ever produced.
