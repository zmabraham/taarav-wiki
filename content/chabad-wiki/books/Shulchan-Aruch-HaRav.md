# Shulchan Aruch HaRav

**Author:** [[authors/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]]
**Library ID:** `3100000000`

## About This Work

The Shulchan Aruch of the Rav — a comprehensive halachic code written by the Alter Rebbe. Based on the classic Shulchan Aruch, it incorporates Kabbalistic considerations and is the authoritative halachic source for Chabad Chassidim. Covers all areas of Jewish law.
