# Keter Shem Tov

**Author:** [[authors/baal-shem-tov|Baal Shem Tov]]
**Library ID:** `300000000`

## About This Work

A collection of the Baal Shem Tov's teachings compiled by Rabbi Aharon of Apt, drawn primarily from the writings of Rabbi Yaakov Yosef of Polnoye. The title means "Crown of the Good Name." This is one of the primary sources for the Baal Shem Tov's teachings, covering topics of divine service, prayer, Torah study, and the nature of the soul.
