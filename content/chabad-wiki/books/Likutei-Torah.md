# Likutei Torah

**Author:** [[authors/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]]
**Library ID:** `4000000000`

## About This Work

Gathered Torah — Chassidic discourses on the portions from Vayikra through Devarim, plus Shir HaShirim and the High Holidays. A companion to Torah Or, together these two works contain the Alter Rebbe's major discourses on the entire Torah.
