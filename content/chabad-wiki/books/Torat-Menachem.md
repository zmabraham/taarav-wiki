# Torat Menachem

**Author:** [[authors/the-rebbe|The Rebbe]]
**Library ID:** `1400000000`

## About This Work

The Torah of Menachem — over 90 volumes of the Rebbe's transcribed talks (*sichos*) and formal discourses from farbrengens, covering 44 years of his leadership (1950–1992).
