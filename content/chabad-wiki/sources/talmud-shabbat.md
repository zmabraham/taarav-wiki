# Tractate Shabbat — Key Teachings in Chassidus

**Hebrew:** מַסֶּכֶת שַׁבָּת

Tractate Shabbat is among the most cited Talmudic tractates in Chabad literature.

## Key Teachings Frequently Referenced

### Shabbat 88a — *Na'aseh veNishma*
> *"When Israel said 'we will do' before 'we will hear,' a Divine voice went forth and said: who revealed to My children this secret that the ministering angels use?"*

The Alter Rebbe devotes extensive analysis to this, explaining *kabbalat ol* (acceptance before understanding) as the highest form of Divine service. See [[concepts/chabad-intellect|Chabad Intellect]].

### Shabbat 10a — Torah and the World
> *"If someone studies Torah, the Holy One, Blessed Be He, forgives all his sins."*

Used in Chabad to explain the unique power of Torah study — it doesn't merely refine, it *transforms*.

### Shabbat 55b — The Seal of God is Truth
> *"The seal of the Holy One, Blessed Be He, is truth (emet)."*

Cited in Chabad discussions of *emes* (truth) as the fundamental Divine quality and the goal of *Daat* (deep knowing).

### Shabbat 31a — Hillel's Principle
> *"What is hateful to you, do not do to others — this is the entire Torah; the rest is commentary. Go and learn."*

Connected in Chabad to the primacy of *ahavat Yisrael* — the Golden Rule as the summary of Torah.
