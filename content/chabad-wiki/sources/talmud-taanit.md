# Tractate Taanit — Key Teachings in Chassidus

**Hebrew:** מַסֶּכֶת תַּעֲנִית

## *Jacob Our Father Did Not Die* (Taanit 5b)

> *"Just as his descendants are alive, so too he is alive."*

One of the most foundational teachings about the *tzaddik* who transcends death. Chabad extends this to all *tzaddikim* who live entirely for the spiritual reality — their life-force continues to animate the world.

This is the basis for the Chabad teaching about the continued presence and influence of the Rebbe after his passing (*histalkus*) in 1994.
