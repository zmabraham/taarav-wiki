# Wedding: Rabbi Yosef Goldstein was one of the early students in the Warsaw

> Wedding event from Chabad history — Year: 1922

## Overview

**Type:** Wedding
**Year:** 1922
**Locations:** Warsaw, Riga, Otwock
**People involved:** Rabbi, Rebbe

## Description

Rabbi Yosef Goldstein was one of the early students in the Warsaw yeshivah, enrolling as a sixteen-year-old in 1922. In 1931, now twenty-four and newly married, he visited the Rebbe in Riga for the High Holidays. In the midst of a farbrengen, he was surprised when the Rebbe called him up, placed his right hand on his shoulder, and urged him to open a yeshivah in Chmielnik, a town in southern Poland with a population of twelve thousand, eighty percent of whom were Jews.[[65: Interview with Yankel ---
