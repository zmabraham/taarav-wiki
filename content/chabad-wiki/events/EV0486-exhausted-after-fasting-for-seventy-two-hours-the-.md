## Description

Exhausted after fasting for seventy-two hours, the Rebbe returned to his cell. Finally, on Friday,[[38: Friday, June 17, before sunset.]] after two nights and three days, a Jewish guard brought him his tefillin and books. The Rebbe told the officer that he refused to eat the non-kosher meals provided by the prison, and he asked for food from home.
