## Description

They even targeted the town of Lubavitch, sending Enlightenment activist Mordechai Ben Hillel to explore the possibilities of opening a school there in 1897. He reported that because the community was the bastion of chasidic life, a school there would have a large impact. “Lubavitch is visited by thousands annually.
