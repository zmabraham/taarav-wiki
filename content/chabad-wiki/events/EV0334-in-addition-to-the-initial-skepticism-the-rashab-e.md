# Establishment: In addition to the initial skepticism the Rashab encountered from his own

> Establishment event from Chabad history — Year: 1899

## Overview

**Type:** Establishment
**Year:** 1899
**Locations:** Lubavitch
**People involved:** Rashab, Rayatz, Rabbi

## Description

In addition to the initial skepticism the Rashab encountered from his own Chasidim, opposition arose from the competing Chabad courts in Kopust and Liadi, whose members feared that the new yeshivah would strengthen “the dominance of the Lubavitch stream in the Chabad community,”[[53: Luria, Milchamot Lubavitch, chap. 2. See also a letter that the Rayatz wrote to Rabbi Schneur Slonim on 21 Tevet, 5660 (December 11, 1899) [according to the Julian calendar then in use in czarist Russia], published ---
