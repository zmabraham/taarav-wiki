## Description

When the Rebbe evacuated Lubavitch in 1915, he combined his personal collection with that of his father, and the bulk of it was placed in storage in Moscow. They retained some priceless manuscripts, including valuable chasidic writings dating back centuries, and a select number of books, taking them to Rostov.
