## Description

The Rebbe also encouraged Chasidim in Europe to immigrate to the US. One Chasid, Yochanan Gordon, had three brothers who moved to America. When the Rebbe returned to Riga, he asked Gordon, “Why don’t you join your brothers? They will arrange a visa for you.” He responded, “I don’t want my children to go off the derekh (stop observing the Torah),” explaining that his brothers’ children were no longer religious.
