## Description

For the first few months in America, the Rebbe resided in the Greystone Hotel in Manhattan, except for an interlude for the holiday of Passover in Lakewood, New Jersey. The Chasidim began searching for a permanent home for the Rebbe in the summer of 1940.[[43: A committee was formed by Agudas Chassidei Chabad. It included Chaim Kramer, Avraham Dovber Kramer, Dovber Chaskind, and Rabbis Shlomo Aharon Kazanofsky, Shmuel Levitin, Moshe Dovber Rivkin, Eli Simpson, Yisrael Jacobson, and Menachem Mend
