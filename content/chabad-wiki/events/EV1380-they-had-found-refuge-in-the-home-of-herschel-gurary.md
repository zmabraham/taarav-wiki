# Historical Event: They had found refuge in the home of Herschel Gurary,

## Description

They had found refuge in the home of Herschel Gurary, a distant cousin of the Rashag. The family had heard that “the SS was looking for Schneersohn,” recalls Berke Gurary. The apartment was not near the main Jewish area, and the Rebbe stayed there for a few weeks. While chaos reigned outside, the Rebbe was always productive. “He would sit at the dining room table to write maamarim.”
