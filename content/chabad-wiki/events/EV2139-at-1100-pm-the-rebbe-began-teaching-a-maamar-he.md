## Description

At 11:00 p.m., the Rebbe began teaching a maamar. He paused an hour later for the celebratory meal. The dancing continued throughout the night, and at 4:00 a.m., the Rebbe resumed the maamar for another half-hour. The meal concluded at 6:00 in the morning.
