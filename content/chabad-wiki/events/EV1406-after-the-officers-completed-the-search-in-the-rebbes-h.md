## Description

After the officers completed the search in the Rebbe’s home, they requested that he sign a form indicating that the search had been conducted legally. The Rebbe refused, warning the officers, “I believe that the arrest will result in highly negative publicity, and you should proceed with caution until the truth is clarified – if you are actually seeking the truth.
