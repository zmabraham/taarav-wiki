## Description

In April 1891, when Yosef Yitzchak was ten years old, the Rashab contracted pneumonia. He was sick for six weeks. “There were days that he was dangerously ill, yet his mind and speech were clear.” At one point, the doctors attending the Rashab gave a “fearful prognosis.”
