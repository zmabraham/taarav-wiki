## Description

It was a bright summer day in the town of Lubavitch, a village nestled in the rolling hills and forests of White Russia. From surrounding villages and farms, people streamed into Lubavitch, doubling its population of two thousand for yarmarka, the two-day market in late June of 1891. The streets were festive, bustling with farmers, craftsmen, and other vendors, all vying for customers.
