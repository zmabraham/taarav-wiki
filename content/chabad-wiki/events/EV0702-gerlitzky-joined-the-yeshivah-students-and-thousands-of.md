## Description

Gerlitzky joined the yeshivah students and thousands of Warsaw’s Jews at the gates of the yeshivah’s courtyard to welcome the Rebbe before Shabbat. It was an inspirational few days with the Rebbe. He spent much of Shabbat day in prayer, not ending till 4:00 p.m. Then the Rebbe taught two maamarim. Yeshivah student Aryeh Leib Kramer recalled, “This is the first maamar that I heard from the Rebbe.
