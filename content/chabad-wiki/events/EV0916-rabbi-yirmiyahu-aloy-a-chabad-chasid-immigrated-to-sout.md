## Description

Rabbi Yirmiyahu Aloy, a Chabad Chasid, immigrated to South Africa from Lithuania before the War. In 1948, he met with the Rebbe in New York. The Nationalist Afrikaner Party had just been victorious in the elections. “Everyone thought it would be disastrous for the Jews.
