## Description

After the holiday, Shterna Sara sold a dress she owned and donated the profits to charity. Ten months later, she gave birth to her son, Yosef Yitzchak. Her father-in-law, Rabbi Shmuel, was given the honor of sandek (the one who holds the baby during the circumcision). When the baby started to cry, Rabbi Shmuel remarked, “Why are you crying? When you grow up, you will be a Rebbe and say chasidut (chasidic discourses) with clarity.”[[17: Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, Reshimat HaMaasor, Likkute ---
