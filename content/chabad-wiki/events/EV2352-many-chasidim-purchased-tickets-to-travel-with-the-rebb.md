## Description

Many Chasidim purchased tickets to travel with the Rebbe until the last station before the border. At a number of stations along the route, crowds gathered to bid farewell, and many boarded the train. As it drew closer to the Russian border, the cars rang with the sound of chasidic melodies marking the Russian Chasidim’s last moments with the Rebbe.
