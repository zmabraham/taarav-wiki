## Description

It was a summer morning as the Rashab and his son walked down the path from Lubavitch through a marsh to the cemetery and the edge of town. Rows of stones, reminders of generations past, some with inscriptions weathered from hundreds of years of winter frost, stretched toward the forest. In the middle of it all was a small synagogue adjacent to the Ohel, the stone structure surrounding the graves of Rabbi Menachem Mendel and Rabbi Shmuel. It was the Rayatz’s fifteenth birthday.
