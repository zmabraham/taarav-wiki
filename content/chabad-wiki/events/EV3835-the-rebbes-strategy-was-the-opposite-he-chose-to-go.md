## Description

The Rebbe’s strategy was the opposite; he chose to go on the offensive. His proclamation, “America iz nisht aderesh – America is no different,” challenged the prevailing attitude. He didn’t want to just bolster the small religious community, but to fundamentally change the direction of American Jewish life.
