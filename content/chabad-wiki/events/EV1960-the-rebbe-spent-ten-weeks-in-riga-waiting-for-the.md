## Description

The Rebbe spent ten weeks in Riga waiting for the issuance of the visas. The ordeal in Warsaw had impacted his fragile health, and he rested in a nearby sanatorium. While in Riga, the Rebbe made repeated attempts to secure visas for Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin and other Chasidim. He also wrote to leaders in Eretz Yisrael asking them to expedite certificates that would permit immigration there.
