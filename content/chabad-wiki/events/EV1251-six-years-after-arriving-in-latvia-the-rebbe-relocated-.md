## Description

Six years after arriving in Latvia, the Rebbe relocated to Poland. But Poland too would only provide a home for a few years, until the outbreak of World War II compelled the Rebbe to escape to New York. It was only there that the wandering ended, with the Rebbe establishing Chabad’s permanent base in the iconic Gothic-style building at 770 Eastern Parkway. He lived there for the last decade of his life, until his passing in 1950.
