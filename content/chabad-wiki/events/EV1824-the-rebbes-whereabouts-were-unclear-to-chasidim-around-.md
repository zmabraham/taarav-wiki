## Description

The Rebbe’s whereabouts were unclear to Chasidim around the world. On the first night of Rosh HaShanah in Tel Aviv, Rabbi Michoel Dvorkin bellowed in an anguished voice as he came to the words of the prayers “The deliverance of the righteous is from the Lord.” His friend Pinchas Althaus explained, “From the beginning of the war, we don’t know where the Rebbe is, and that’s why he is crying out.”[[47: Psalm 34, recited at the beginning of the service on Erev Rosh HaShanah. Rabbi Mordechai Ashkena
