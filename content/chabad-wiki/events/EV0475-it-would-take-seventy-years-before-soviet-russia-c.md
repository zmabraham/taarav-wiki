# Establishment: It would take seventy years before Soviet Russia collapsed. Still, says Russian

> Establishment event from Chabad history — Year: 1942

## Overview

**Type:** Establishment
**Year:** 1942
**Locations:** Lubavitch
**People involved:** Not specified

## Description

It would take seventy years before Soviet Russia collapsed. Still, says Russian Jewish historian Nissan Rupo, “After twenty-two years, the Russian government curbed its active persecution of religion.” During World War II, in the early 1940s, the Russian Orthodox Church was permitted to operate publicly again. Also, says Rupo, “In 1942, a Lubavitch yeshivah opened in Samarkand.” ---
