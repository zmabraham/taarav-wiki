## Description

The last holiday the Rebbe celebrated with his Chasidim in Russia was Simchat Torah in October 1927. It was the final opportunity for Russian Chasidim to see the Rebbe, and despite the risk of arrest, Chasidim flocked to Leningrad. Zalman Bronstein recalls, “We all wanted to see the Rebbe before he left. The synagogue, whose normal capacity was six hundred, swelled with Chasidim who packed the room.
