## Description

After five years of marriage, Rebbetzin Shterna Sara was distressed.[[13: Rebbetzin Shterna Sara Schneersohn retold the story in Rostov to Rabbi Rafael Kahn. See Shemuot VeSippurim, 1st edition, vol. 1.]] “I had not yet had a healthy child, and it bothered me. I was also quite young and distant from the home of my parents.” Those feelings were compounded on Simchat Torah in 1879, when her father-in-law, Rabbi Shmuel, blessed each member of the family and overlooked her. ---
