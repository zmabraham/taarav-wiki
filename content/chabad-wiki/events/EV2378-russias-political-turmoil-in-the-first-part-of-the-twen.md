## Description

Russia’s political turmoil in the first part of the twentieth century, culminating in the Russian Revolution of 1917, was imbued with anti-Semitism. As historian Eli Rubin writes, the government blamed the Jews for all their failings. “The ills of Russia were the result of the Jewish-led efforts to erode Russian nationalism, never the result of government corruption and incompetence.” In 1913, this rose to a new level with the infamous Beilis affair.
