# Historical Event: That night in 1920, the Rashab entrusted his son with

> Historical Event — Year: 1920 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.1: Lubavitch

## Overview

**Event Type:** Historical Event
**Year/Date:** 1920
**Location(s):** Not specified
**People mentioned:** Rashab, Yosef Yitzchak

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter 2 - Tomchei Temimim

## Description

That night in 1920, the Rashab entrusted his son with the sacred mission of combatting the Communists’ plan to extinguish religious life in Russia. He commanded him, “Yosef Yitzchak, for the sake of strengthening Torah, reverence of Heaven, and support for Judaism, you will have to act with actual mesirat nefesh (self-sacrifice), risking your life.”
