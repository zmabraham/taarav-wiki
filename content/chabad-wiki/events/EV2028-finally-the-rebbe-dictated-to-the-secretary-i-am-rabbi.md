## Description

Finally, the Rebbe dictated to the secretary, “I am Rabbi Schneersohn, son of the celebrated Rebbe of Lubavitch, and I am preoccupied with religious study. Like all religiously observant Jews, I have no connection to politics.” He told her where he was born, where he lived, his title, and where he studied. The secretary took down his statement word for word on the form, and the Rebbe signed it.
