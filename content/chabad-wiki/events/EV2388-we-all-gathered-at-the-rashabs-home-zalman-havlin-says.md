# Gathering & Farbrengen: “We all gathered at the Rashab’s home,” Zalman Havlin says,

## Description

“We all gathered at the Rashab’s home,” Zalman Havlin says, “and at first they told us the celebration would be brief, just for a few hours.” The Rebbe’s wife had notified the Chasidim that the celebration would go only until curfew, after which they would disperse, unlike in Lubavitch, where the farbrengen lasted through the night.
