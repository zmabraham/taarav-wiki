## Description

The JDC had curtailed its funding to the Rebbe’s underground educational system because it could put them at odds with the Communist regime. Now in Riga, the Rebbe would be able to meet JDC leaders more easily and restore critical funding for Russian Jewry. Some in the JDC dissented with the decision to cut funding for the Rebbe’s work.
