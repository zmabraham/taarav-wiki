## Description

Four days later, just a few hours before the holiday of Sukkot was about to begin, the Polish army in Warsaw surrendered to the Germans. Still, skirmishes raged around the city. As sunset neared, the Rebbe requested that a sukkah be erected by the building manager. Quite surprised by the request, he asked the Rebbe how he could sit in a sukkah in the middle of a war.
