## Description

The fear that the Rebbe might move to the US spurred the European Chasidim who had flocked to Riga for the holidays to take action. They convened Saturday evening after Rosh HaShanah to formally establish Agudas Chassidei Chabad (Association of Chabad Chasidim) of Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia.[[2: The meeting was held in the Rashag’s home in Riga.
