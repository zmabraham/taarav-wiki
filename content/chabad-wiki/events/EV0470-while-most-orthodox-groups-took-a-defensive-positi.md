## Description

While most Orthodox groups took a defensive position, concerned with the preservation of the observant core and ignoring the outside, the Rebbe courageously went on the offensive, taking a broader, more inclusive stance, the potential dangers notwithstanding. His philosophy delicately navigated between the extremes of insularity and integration. His approach was one of “principled engagement,” sharing in the bounty of the freedoms of Western society but staunchly retaining the values of Judaism ---
