## Description

The Rebbe dispatched a series of emissaries to Europe to help the refugees. The first was the Rebbe’s secretary, Dr. Nissan Mindel, sent to England a few months after the war’s end in September of 1945. His mandate was to “collect detailed information on the economic and spiritual status of our fellow Jews in liberated Europe”[[36: The Rebbe sent a series of letters to Jewish leaders and the Chabad community in England asking them to assist Dr. Mindel in his mission.
