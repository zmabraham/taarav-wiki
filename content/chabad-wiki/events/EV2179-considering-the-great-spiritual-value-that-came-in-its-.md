# Communication & Correspondence: Considering the great spiritual value that came in its wake,

## Description

Considering the great spiritual value that came in its wake, it is appropriate not just to mark the days and nights, but also the hours and minutes [of arrest and imprisonment], for every hour and moment of suffering, mortification, and affliction brings tremendous reward and infinite fortitude of mind – even a weak person is transformed into the mightiest of the mighty.
