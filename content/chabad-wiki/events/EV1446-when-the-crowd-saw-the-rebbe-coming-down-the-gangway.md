## Description

When the crowd saw the Rebbe coming down the gangway, they cried out, “Shalom aleikhem!” Cantor Shmuel Kantaroff joyously began to sing the Shehechiyanu blessing. Seized with excitement, the crowd burst into a lively chasidic tune. Risya Kazarnovsky says the singing was a moment of personal affirmation for her, a girl who went to public school whose family always seemed different from those of her peers. “These were the Chabad melodies I heard at home,” she says.
