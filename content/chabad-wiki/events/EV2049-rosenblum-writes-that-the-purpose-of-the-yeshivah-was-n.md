## Description

Rosenblum writes that the purpose of the yeshivah was not just to produce scholars. “There is no lack of great students of Torah.” Tomchei Temimim’s mission was to create a different kind of scholar, one rooted in profound values, “where the Giver of the Torah was embedded in them.” It was to nurture a cadre of students who would be willing to stand up for Jewish principles “with all their strength and vitality.” According to Luria, the Rashab viewed the yeshivah’s role as “producing leaders.” M
