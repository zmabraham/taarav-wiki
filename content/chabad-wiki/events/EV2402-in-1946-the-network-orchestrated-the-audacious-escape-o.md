## Description

In 1946, the network orchestrated the audacious escape of one thousand Chasidim across the border to Poland on false passports. They and their children provided the leadership necessary to create a global Jewish renaissance, and they were an inspiration to a new generation of Chasidim growing up in the postwar era.
