## Description

The Rebbe did not march off the ship as he had done on his visit to America eleven years earlier. He came down the gangway in a wheelchair, guided by one of the ship’s officers, bedecked by a shtreimel, which is customarily worn on Shabbat and holidays. His countenance made an impression on those present.
