## Description

“Those who excelled in their studies,” Tenenbaum explained, “received special privileges.” They were selected to be part of a small group of students permitted to attend the Rebbe’s recitation of a maamar on Shabbat (the Rebbe’s health prevented him from speaking to large crowds).
