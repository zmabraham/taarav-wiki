## Description

That year, in 1884, Yosef Yitzchak began his formal education. On his first day of school, his father and his uncle Rabbi Zalman Aharon were there to celebrate, tossing candies and telling him that they were coming from the angel Mikhael – just as their grandfather, Rabbi Menachem Mendel, had done when they started cheder.
