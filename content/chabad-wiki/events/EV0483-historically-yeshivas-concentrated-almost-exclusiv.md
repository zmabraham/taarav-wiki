## Description

Historically, yeshivas concentrated almost exclusively on Talmud study, which focuses on the legalistic tradition of Judaism. There was a modicum of attention given to the mystical dimension of Judaism. At the meeting on the day of the Rayatz’s wedding, the Rashab outlined his vision for a groundbreaking educational institution whose curriculum would not only focus on Talmud but also integrate the study of Chasidism, formally and systematically. ---
