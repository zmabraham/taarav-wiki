# Historical Event: On Sunday, June 28, the Rebbe travels to Paris for

> Historical Event — Year: 8 Elul | Source: Undaunted — Ch.11: A Global Vision

## Description

On Sunday, June 28, the Rebbe travels to Paris for medical treatment, staying in the Sanitorium in Ville-d’Avray, a suburb right outside Paris. He remains in Paris until the end of August. Before departing, he says a maamar in the chasidic community in Paris on August 26 (8 Elul).
