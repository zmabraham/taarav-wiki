## Description

Within a few days of arriving in Riga, the Rebbe began working on saving the students of the yeshivah in Vilnius, telegramming Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson to find an avenue of rescue for the students. The Rebbe followed up with letters to Jacobson and the Kramer family, as well as other prominent rabbis such as Dr. Leo Jung, asking for help in arranging visas and financing to transport the students. “The students are in grave danger,” he told Jung.
