## Description

When the Rebbe moved to Poland in 1933, he came with few Chasidim and limited resources. The well-established chasidic courts of Ger, Belz, and others had hundreds of thousands of followers and dominated religious life in Poland. The Rebbe realized the future was the youth. He began with one yeshivah with 157 college-age students in 1929.
