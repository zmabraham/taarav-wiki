## Description

During the interlude in Riga, the Rebbe had an opportunity to hold yechidut, private meetings, with many Chasidim, some of whom even traveled from Lithuania to see him. One of those was Shraga Feivel Zisman and his son Leibel, who came from Kovno. “Hundreds were milling around and waiting to see the Rebbe,” Leibel recalls.
