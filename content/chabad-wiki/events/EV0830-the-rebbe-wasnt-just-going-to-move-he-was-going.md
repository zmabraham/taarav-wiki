## Description

The Rebbe wasn’t just going to move, he was going to change his focus. He told Jacobson, “I am entering my sixth decade. The last time I was in the US [referring to his 1929–30 visit], I was involved in strengthening traditional Judaism, and thank G-d, I was successful.
