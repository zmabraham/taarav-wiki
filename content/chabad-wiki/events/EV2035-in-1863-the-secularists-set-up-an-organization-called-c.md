## Description

In 1863, the secularists set up an organization called Chevrah Mefitzei Haskalah (Society to Promote the Enlightenment), known by its acronym, Chamah. It was backed by some of Russia’s wealthiest Jews.[[84: These included Baron Joseph Gunzburg, the organization’s president, his son Horace (Naftali Hertz) Gunzburg, the vice president, Abraham Brodsky, Lev Rosenthal, and others.
