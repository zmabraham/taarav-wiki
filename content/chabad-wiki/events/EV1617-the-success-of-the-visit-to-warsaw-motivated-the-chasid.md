# Travel & Movement: The success of the visit to Warsaw motivated the Chasidim

## Description

The success of the visit to Warsaw motivated the Chasidim of Riga to mobilize. “The Chasidim in Riga and in Lithuania decided to establish here in Riga a Tomchei Temimim yeshivah,” Feigin wrote. He added that this might cause the Rebbe to remain in Riga, but he remained skeptical. “I don’t see it happening, and if the financial situation would allow the Rebbe to move to Poland, he will.”[[23: It is difficult to determine the exact date that the yeshivah opened in Riga, but by the summer of 1932
