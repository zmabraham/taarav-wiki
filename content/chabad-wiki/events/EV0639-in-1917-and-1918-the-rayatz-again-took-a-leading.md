## Description

In 1917 and 1918, the Rayatz again took a leading part in the assembly of rabbis and laymen in Moscow and Kharkiv. For a quarter of a century, until his father’s passing in 1920, the Rayatz took center stage in Jewish life. There was hardly a major concern among Russian Jewry in which the Rashab was not involved, and as his father’s private secretary, the Rayatz dealt with many of these issues on a day-to-day basis.
