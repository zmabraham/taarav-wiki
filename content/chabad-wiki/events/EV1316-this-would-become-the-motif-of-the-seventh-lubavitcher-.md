## Description

This would become the motif of the seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe: Just as the theme of his first maamar was based on the teachings of the Rayatz, in his tenure as Rebbe, he built on the foundation established by the Rayatz, expanding Chabad into the largest Jewish movement in the world. Time and again, he referred to the teachings and directives of his father-in-law as his guiding light.
