## Description

There was no lack of yeshivas in Eastern Europe. The modern yeshivah began with the establishment of the yeshivah in Volozhin in 1803. With time, yeshivas were established in other communities, such as Slabodka, Mir, Telz, and Novardok. These were led by world-class scholars, including Rabbis Nosson Tzvi Finkel, Naftali Tzvi Yehudah Berlin, Chaim Soloveitchik, and others.
