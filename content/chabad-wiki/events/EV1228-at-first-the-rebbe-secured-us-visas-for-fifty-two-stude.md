## Description

At first, the Rebbe secured US visas for fifty-two students. The plan was for them to come directly to the US, but as the war intensified, travel across the Atlantic became impossible. The only way open was eastward, through Russia and Japan. The Chabad students did not hesitate. Chaim Meir Bukiet recalls, “It was the students of Tomchei Temimim who broke the ice.” The Russian official responsible for permitting travel via Russia was named Schlossberg.
