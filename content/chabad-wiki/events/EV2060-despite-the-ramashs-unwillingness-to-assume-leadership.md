## Description

Despite the Ramash’s unwillingness to assume leadership, the Chasidim were still determined that he become the next Rebbe. They had witnessed the years of mentorship that the Rayatz had given the Ramash. Since his arrival in America in 1941, the Rayatz had entrusted him with important projects and responsibilities.
