## Description

Throughout October, Bloch and his soldiers searched the Jewish sections of Warsaw, looking for the Rebbe. They tried asking local Jews if they knew his whereabouts. The Rebbe was well-known, and no doubt this inquiry alarmed those they asked, who could not imagine anything but nefarious intent. Bloch tried visiting Jewish gathering points and asking for the Rebbe, to no avail. Finding the Rebbe in a city of millions, much of it ruined and its residents fearfully secretive, would be difficult.
