# Teaching & Publication: Ultimately, it was the success of the yeshivah in Warsaw

> Teaching & Publication — Year: 1933 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.8: Poland - Starting Anew

## Overview

**Event Type:** Teaching & Publication
**Year/Date:** 1933, 1929, 26 Sivan, 6 Tishrei
**Location(s):** Warsaw
**People mentioned:** Ramash

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter Eight

## Description

Ultimately, it was the success of the yeshivah in Warsaw that lured the Rebbe to Poland. The Rebbe confided to the Ramash: “The spiritual state of the yeshivah in Warsaw is outstanding, and with G-d’s help there is an opportunity for the yeshivah to expand.…When I make a personal assessment, I feel a deep inner longing to be in the ohel (atmosphere) of Torah, where there are scholars full of knowledge who strive to serve
