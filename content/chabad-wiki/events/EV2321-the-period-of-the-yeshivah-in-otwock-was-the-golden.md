## Description

The period of the yeshivah in Otwock was the golden era of Tomchei Temimim in Poland. The Rebbe’s presence there made it the spiritual center of Chabad until the Nazi invasion in 1939. Tragically, many of the students lost their lives in the Holocaust. The network of Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim was an incubator for a new generation of Chabad followers.
