## Description

The Chasidim continued their efforts to free the Rebbe from exile. A group in Moscow led by Madam Peshkova met with Soviet officials, who had been inundated with telegrams and messages from around the world. The head of the GPU in Leningrad, Jewish Communist Stanislav Messing, opposed the Rebbe’s release. At the same time that the Rebbe was arrested, other religious leaders had been detained.
