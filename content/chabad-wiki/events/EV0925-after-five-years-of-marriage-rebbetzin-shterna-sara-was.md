# Wedding: After five years of marriage, Rebbetzin Shterna Sara was ...

> Wedding — Year: 1879 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.1: Lubavitch

## Overview

**Event Type:** Wedding
**Year/Date:** 1879
**Location(s):** Rostov
**People mentioned:** Schneersohn

**Section/Chapter:** Introduction

## Description

After five years of marriage, Rebbetzin Shterna Sara was distressed. “I had not yet had a healthy child, and it bothered me. I was also quite young and distant from the home of my parents.” Those feelings were compounded on Simchat Torah in 1879, when her father-in-law, Rabbi Shmuel, blessed each member of the family and overlooked her.
