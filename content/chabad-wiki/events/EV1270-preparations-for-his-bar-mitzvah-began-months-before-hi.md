## Description

Preparations for his bar mitzvah began months before his birthday. “I had to memorize and master three maamarim.” Two of them were long; one was short. Two he repeated publicly as part of the bar mitzvah celebration, but at the time, “no one was to know of the third maamar.” The Rashab taught Yosef Yitzchak the three maamarim himself.
