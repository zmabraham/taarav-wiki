## Description

The setting was designed to terrorize, but the Rebbe was not intimidated. Instead, he began to tell a story of a Maskil (a proponent of the enlightenment) who suffered a serious illness that lasted for three months, prompting his repentance and return to observance. The message was clear to the Jewish interrogators. But instead of taking it to heart, they responded with anger, mocking the Rebbe.
