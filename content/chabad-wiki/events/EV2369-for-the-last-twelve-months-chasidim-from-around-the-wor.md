## Description

For the last twelve months, Chasidim from around the world had sent letters to New York urging the Ramash to become Rebbe, signing a ktav hitkarut, a pledge of loyalty to him. Until now, the Ramash had repeatedly rebuffed these entreaties. According to chasidic theologian Rabbi Yoel Kahn, “The Ramash was very reserved, a private person. Public leadership was the opposite of his character.”
