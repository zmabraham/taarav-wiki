## Description

News of the Rebbe’s visit electrified the Warsaw yeshivah students and those in the branches in Vilnius and Lodz. Moshe Gerlitzky,[[18: Moshe Eliyahu Gerlitzky (1914–2020) was originally from Lodz and studied in Chabad yeshivas in Poland, Lithuania, and Shanghai. He was one of the nine students who founded the Chabad yeshivah in Montreal in 1941. Mordechai Lightstone, “From Lodz to Montreal: The Life of Rabbi Moshe Eliyahu Gerlitzky,” available at: https://www.lubavitch.com/from-lodz-to-montreal
