## Description

He had just barely finished donning the tefillin when the guard turned and saw what he had done. Enraged, he struck the Rebbe, pushing him down a ladder. “I realized that the metal buckle of my belt had broken and that it had apparently cut a gash into my stomach,” the Rebbe wrote. “My heart contracted with pain; I felt that in another instant I would faint.” The tefillin were confiscated, but the Rebbe still had his tallit.
