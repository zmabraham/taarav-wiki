# Historical Event: Later in the morning, when the prisoners were offered bread, the Rebbe

## Description

Later in the morning, when the prisoners were offered bread, the Rebbe demanded to speak with the prison warden. When one of the senior officers came to the cell, the Rebbe insisted that his tefillin be returned to him and also requested a visit from a doctor. The official told the Rebbe he could see a doctor no earlier than the next day, and concerning the tefillin: “Forget about that foolishness.” The Rebbe responded by going on a hunger strike until his tefillin were returned. ---
