# Conflict & Opposition: The Rebbe is rescued by the German Army and departs

> Conflict & Opposition — Year: 1940 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.11: A Global Vision

## Overview

**Event Type:** Conflict & Opposition
**Year/Date:** 1940, 1940, 21 Kislev, 25 Kislev, 24 Adar, 27 Adar
**Location(s):** Warsaw, Riga
**People mentioned:** Not specified

**Section/Chapter:** Afterword

## Description

The Rebbe is rescued by the German Army and departs Warsaw on December 14 (21 Kislev), arriving in Riga on Sunday, December 18 (25 Kislev). He departs Riga on March 4, 1940 (24 Adar Alef) for Stockholm, Sweden, boarding a ship in Gothenburg on March 7 (27 Adar Alef) and finally arriving in the United States on March 19, 1940 (9 Adar Bet).
