## Description

The Chasidim gathered in the Rebbe’s home early on the morning of the arrest, and by 10 a.m., the meeting moved to the Jewish Community Center. In attendance were representatives of every Jewish group except the Communists. They decided to concentrate their efforts within Russia. Tensions between Russia, Poland, and England caused them to fear that an international campaign might exacerbate the situation. Rebbetzin Nechama Dina, the Rebbe’s wife, took the lead.
