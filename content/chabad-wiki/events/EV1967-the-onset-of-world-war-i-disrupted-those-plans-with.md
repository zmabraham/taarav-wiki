# Conflict & Opposition: The onset of World War I disrupted those plans. With

> Conflict & Opposition — Year: 1920 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.1: Lubavitch

## Overview

**Event Type:** Conflict & Opposition
**Year/Date:** 1920, 1922
**Location(s):** Rostov
**People mentioned:** Rashab

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter 2 - Tomchei Temimim

## Description

The onset of World War I disrupted those plans. With Russia and the Ottoman Empire on opposite sides of the conflict, the Russian students sent by the Rashab were considered enemy aliens in Eretz Yisrael, which was under Ottoman rule. Along with Rabbi Havlin, they were forced to return to Russia. Before his passing in Rostov in 1920, the Rashab instructed Rabbi Havlin to return to Eretz Yisrael as soon as possible. In 1922, he did so and reconstituted the Toras Emes yeshivah in Jerusalem. Today
