## Description

For the next two months, the young couple stayed in Riga. Afterward, they headed to Berlin, where they resided for three years as the Ramash continued his university education. In the spring of 1933, as the Nazi threat began to grow, they left Berlin and after a brief interlude in Riga, resettled in Paris. There the Ramash pursued a degree in engineering.
