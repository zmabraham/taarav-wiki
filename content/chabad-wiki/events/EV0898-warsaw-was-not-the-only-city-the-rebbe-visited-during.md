## Description

Warsaw was not the only city the Rebbe visited during this period. Among other cities in the region, the Rebbe traveled to Vilnius three times, where he was greeted by crowds of thousands at the train station. On his first trip in 1928, which lasted one week, he met with Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski to discuss Russian Jewry.
