## Description

The Rashab was alarmed by reports that the Russian government was seizing Jewish leaders near the front lines and accusing them of spying. With the Germans creeping closer, the Rashab deliberated and then made the decision to abandon Lubavitch, hoping to return at the end of the conflict. For 102 years, four Chabad Rebbes had called Lubavitch home, making it a hub for Russian Jews.
