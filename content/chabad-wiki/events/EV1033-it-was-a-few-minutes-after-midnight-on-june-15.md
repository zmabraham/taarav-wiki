# Gathering & Farbrengen: It was a few minutes after midnight on June 15,

> Gathering & Farbrengen — Year: 1927 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.1: Lubavitch

## Overview

**Event Type:** Gathering & Farbrengen
**Year/Date:** 1927, 14 Sivan
**Location(s):** Leningrad
**People mentioned:** Not specified

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter Four

## Description

It was a few minutes after midnight on June 15, 1927, and the Rebbe and his family had gathered for a late supper in his Leningrad apartment. For the Rebbe, it had been a long day. He received people for private audiences until 11:30 p.m. and then prayed the evening service. Now he was finally sitting down for dinner with his family. He was weary.
