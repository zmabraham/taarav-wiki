## Description

That turned out to be fortuitus. A religious Jew found them a haven in a small, ramshackle guesthouse a short distance away. There, Greenberg met a classmate from New York’s Yeshivah Torah Vodaath. He asked Greenberg if the group could take a large sum of money to America – the exact amount needed for train tickets to Riga. On Sunday morning, Greenberg’s friend purchased the tickets to Riga, where they were welcomed by the Chabad community
