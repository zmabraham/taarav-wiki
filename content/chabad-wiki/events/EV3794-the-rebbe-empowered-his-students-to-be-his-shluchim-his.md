## Description

The Rebbe empowered his students to be his shluchim, his emissaries. They would either return to their hometowns or be dispatched to cities around Poland and Lithuania to work with the local communities to establish yeshivas. By the eve of the war, a broad system of yeshivas spanned Poland and nearby Lithuania.
