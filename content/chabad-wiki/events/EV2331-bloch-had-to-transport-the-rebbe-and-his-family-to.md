## Description

Bloch had to transport the Rebbe and his family to safety. It seemed the best option was to depart from the functioning train station at the edge of Warsaw to Berlin and then on to Riga, though this meant heading west and doubling back east to reach Riga. The whole journey would be incomprehensible to bystanders: German soldiers escorting religious Jews into the heart of anti-Semitic Germany in the midst of the war. It was a risky move.
