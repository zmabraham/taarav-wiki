## Description

Toward the end of that Simchat Torah evening in 1930, the Rebbe’s tone turned ominous. In an uncharacteristically serious fashion, he demanded that those unwilling to lay tefillin daily or observe other basics of tradition not stay to dance with him that night. Those attending were taken aback by the Rebbe’s unusually harsh tone, and some indeed left before the traditional Simchat Torah dancing.
