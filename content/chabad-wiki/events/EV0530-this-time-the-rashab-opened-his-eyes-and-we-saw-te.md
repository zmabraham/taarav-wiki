## Description

“This time, the Rashab opened his eyes and we saw tears.” Then they heard the Rashab whispering. It was difficult to decipher what he was saying, but they soon realized that he was reciting the words “bekhol levavkha uvekhol nafshekha – with all your heart and all your soul” of the Shema prayer. Then suddenly, “he turned to look at us and his soul ascended to heaven.”[[135: Ibid.]] ---
