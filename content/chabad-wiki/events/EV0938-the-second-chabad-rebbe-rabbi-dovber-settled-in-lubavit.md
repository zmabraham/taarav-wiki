## Description

The second Chabad Rebbe, Rabbi Dovber, settled in Lubavitch in the summer of 1813. The previous year, his father, Chabad’s founder, Rabbi Schneur Zalman, known as the Alter Rebbe, passed away while fleeing from Napoleon’s invading army. Rabbi Dovber would be the first of four Chabad Rebbes to reside in Lubavitch. For 102 years, Lubavitch was the capital of the Chabad chasidic movement.
