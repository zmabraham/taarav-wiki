## Description

After the bombing, they moved yet again, this time to Nalevki street. That too became the target of German bombing, and the Rebbe crowded into a shelter with hundreds of others. Not only were bombs falling from above, but as German troops drew closer to Warsaw, an artillery bombardment of the city began. During the attacks, says Wineberg, “The Rebbe would sit in the room writing maamarim, and his hand would shake from the bombing. He would tell those around him, ‘I am not afraid.’”
