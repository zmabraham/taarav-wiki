# Death: Rabbi Moshe Rosenblatt visited Lubavitch during this time...

> Death — Year: 1914 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.1: Lubavitch

**Event Type:** Death
**Year/Date:** 1914, 1990
**Location(s):** Lubavitch
**People mentioned:** Rashab

**Section/Chapter:** Introduction

## Description

Rabbi Moshe Rosenblatt visited Lubavitch during this time. He met with the Rashab and presented a tzetel, a written request, as is customary when a Chasid has yechidut, a private meeting with a Rebbe. The Rashab read the note and told him, “For this, one needs a Rebbe. If there were a place where one could access a Rebbe, I would also travel there.”[
