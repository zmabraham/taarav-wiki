## Description

Schneersohn wrote that they came primarily from White Russia, but there were also guests from other regions, including “Poland, Eretz Yisrael, Germany, and even America.” Yeshivah student Shmaryahu Sassonkin recalls the excitement he felt when he came to Lubavitch over a century ago. “As you got close to the edge of the town, you could see the rising structures of the chatzer, the Rebbe’s court, that reached up a few stories high.” ---
