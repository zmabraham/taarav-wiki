## Description

Polish Jewry was also undergoing major changes. In 1919, seventy-five percent of Poland’s Jews were Shabbat observant, but by the 1930s the numbers had dropped to fifty percent, though many were still very traditional. As Jews moved to the cities, they encountered varied ideas and cultures.
