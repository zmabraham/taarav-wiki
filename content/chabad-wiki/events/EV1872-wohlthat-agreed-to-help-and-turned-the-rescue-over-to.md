## Description

Wohlthat agreed to help and turned the rescue over to Admiral Wilhelm Canaris, head of the Abwehr, Germany’s naval intelligence. Goering himself may have very well endorsed Wohlthat’s efforts. German historian Professor Wilfred Meyer says, “I think Goering knew about the rescue operation since Wohlthat was one of his closest associates.” Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson wrot
