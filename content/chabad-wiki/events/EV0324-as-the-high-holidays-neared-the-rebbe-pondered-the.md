## Description

As the High Holidays neared, the Rebbe pondered the idea of leaving Russia. He made his final decision after embarking on a spiritual pilgrimage to pray at the grave of his father, the Rebbe Rashab, in Rostov.[[82: He was accompanied by his daughter Chaya Mushka.
