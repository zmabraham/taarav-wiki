## Description

At the time, a typhus epidemic had devastated the chasidic community, and famine ravaged Rostov. The yeshivah was forcibly closed by the Yevesektzia, and the Rebbe was being harassed by the authorities. Before Chana’s wedding, the secret police broke into the Rebbe’s home, seizing much of his property, including the wedding dress. With limited financial resources, the celebration was very modest.
