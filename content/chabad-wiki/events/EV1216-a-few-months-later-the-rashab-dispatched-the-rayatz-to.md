## Description

A few months later, the Rashab dispatched the Rayatz to represent him at a rabbinic meeting. He asked the Rashbatz, the Rayatz’s teacher, who was now in his seventies, to accompany him, telling him that despite his son’s young age, he should allow him to begin to play a leadership role, and “you should mix in as little as possible.”
