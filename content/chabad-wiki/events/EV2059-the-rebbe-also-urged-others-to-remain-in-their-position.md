## Description

The Rebbe also urged others to remain in their positions of leadership. Rabbi Meir Chaikin was appointed as a rabbi in Stockholm, Sweden. Disillusioned with the lackluster religious community, he wanted to relocate. The Rebbe rejected that idea. “You should consider your arrival in Stockholm a great honor that divine providence has chosen you to illuminate with the light of Torah in an unplanted field,” the Rebbe wrote to him.
