## Description

Rivkin writes that this was a transformational moment for the Rayatz. “From that point on, he was different. He never had the natural expression of becoming anxious.” In the years to come, “he approached everything with gadlut hamochin (guided by his intellect).” Later, even when the Rayatz was arrested and investigated, he never allowed himself to be intimidated, always maintaining control even when being threatened with bodily harm or death. ---
