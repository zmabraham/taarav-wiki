## Description

After the conclusion of Shabbat, plans were made to transfer the Rebbe and his family to nearby Warsaw. Before the war, dozens of trains ran daily between Otwock and Warsaw, but “now there was nothing,” says the Rashag. The roads were under bombardment, and travel was dangerous.
