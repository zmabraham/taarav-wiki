## Description

One of those who crossed the border was Rebbetzin Chana Schneerson, mother of the Ramash. She was placed in a German DP camp and finally made her way to Paris. Her husband was jailed and exiled in 1939 to a remote village in Kazakhstan near the Chinese border. She followed him and was at his bedside when he passed away in Alma Ata in 1945.
