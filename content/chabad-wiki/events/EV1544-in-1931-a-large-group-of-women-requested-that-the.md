## Description

In 1931, a large group of women requested that the Rebbe speak for them during his visit to Rokiškis, Lithuania. Rabbi Feigin writes, “In the wake of the request, the Rebbe spoke to a gathering of women.” Historically, chasidic Rebbes only spoke before men in their public addresses; this was unprecedented.
