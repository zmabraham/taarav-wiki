## Description

At one point, the Ramash feared being detained by the Bolsheviks, and so in the summer of 1926, he found refuge in the home of Rabbi Chanoch Henoch Etkin in Luga, on the outskirts of Leningrad. Though Etkin was a rabbi, he also had regular employment, and the government did not harass him.
