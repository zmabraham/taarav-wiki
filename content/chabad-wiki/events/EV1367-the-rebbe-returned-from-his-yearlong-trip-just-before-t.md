## Description

The Rebbe returned from his yearlong trip just before the High Holidays of 1930. As the holiday season concluded in Riga and the Chasidim returned home, the Rebbe stood at a crossroads. To the east lay millions of Jews locked behind the Iron Curtain, subject to rising totalitarianism and the beginnings of Stalin’s reign of terror. To the west was Poland, the center of European religious life, with the task of reinvigorating Chabad’s yeshivah system so it could birth new generations of Chasidim.
