## Description

In September of 1930, the Rebbe returned to Europe from his long trip abroad. Instead of going directly home to Riga, he made a stop. “I was weak from the trip and traveled to Marienbad.”[[150: Igrot Kodesh, vol. 4, letter dated 18 Tamuz, 5768 ( July 17, 1938). In a long, self-reflective letter, the Rebbe confides in Rabbi Dovid Rabinowitz of Boston about his medical history and the challenges he was facing in Poland at the time.
