## Description

Berel Gurewicz’s wife, who had made it out of Russia, wanted to return to try to free her husband, who had been arrested for trying to escape and locked up in a local jail in Lvov. The Rebbe advised her against going. “It is preferable that he come here rather than her going there,” he said. Gurewicz and two other Chasidim, Berke Chein and Tzipa Kozliner, had been detained by local police who did not realize they were part of the large-scale organized escape.
