## Description

There were three others: creating a new chasidic historiography, broadening the dissemination of chasidic teachings beyond the chasidic community, and empowering women in the realm of Jewish study. In all five arenas, the Rebbe created a series of initiatives that with time would be key to reconstituting Chabad in Western democratic countries.
