## Description

The Rebbe was now operating in a new set of circumstances that posed a major challenge. The freedoms in Western countries like Latvia offered Jews choices that were unprecedented. Could they survive the influence and temptation of the outside world? Lubavitch had been a spiritual haven, sheltered from external influences. In Riga there was a diverse community, parts of which were antagonistic to traditional Judaism. Could chasidic life flourish in a Western liberal society?
