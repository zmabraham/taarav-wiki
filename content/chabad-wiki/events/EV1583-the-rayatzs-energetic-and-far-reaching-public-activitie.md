# Arrest & Imprisonment: The Rayatz’s energetic and far-reaching public activities...

> Arrest & Imprisonment — Year: 1911 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.1: Lubavitch

**Location(s):** Lubavitch, St. Petersburg
**People mentioned:** Rayatz

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter 2 - Tomchei Temimim

## Description

The Rayatz’s energetic and far-reaching public activities, his watchful defense of the rights of Russian Jewry, and his constant fight against the local and central authorities aroused the displeasure of the czarist regime. Between 1911 and 1916, the Rayatz was arrested on four occasions. The first time was a result of accusations by leaders of the Enlightenment school in Lubavitch. The next incident was in December 1906, when the Rebbe was investigated due to riots in Lubavitch by radical youth
