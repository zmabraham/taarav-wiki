# Historical Event: On Sunday, December 27, the Rebbe departs Otwock for Vienna

> Historical Event — Year: 1937 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.11: A Global Vision

## Overview

**Event Type:** Historical Event
**Year/Date:** 1937, 21 Tevet, 7 Adar, 11 Nisan
**Location(s):** Otwock, Vienna, Paris
**People mentioned:** Not specified

**Section/Chapter:** Afterword

## Description

On Sunday, December 27, the Rebbe departs Otwock for Vienna (Perchtoldsdorf) for medical treatment, remaining there about a week. The Rebbe then travels to Paris, arriving on January 4, 1937 (21 Tevet) and staying till February 18 (7 Adar). The Rebbe then returns to Perchtoldsdorf, traveling home to Otwock on Tuesday, March 23 (11 Nisan).
