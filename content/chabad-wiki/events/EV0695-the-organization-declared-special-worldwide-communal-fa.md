## Description

The organization declared special worldwide communal fasts to plead for heavenly intervention regarding the oppression of the Russian Jews; it also included special prayers for their welfare in the Yom Kippur services the following year. In the coming months, the Rebbe dispatched the Rashag to London, Paris, and Berlin to meet with local chief rabbis and top Jewish officials to request assistance for Russian Jewry.
