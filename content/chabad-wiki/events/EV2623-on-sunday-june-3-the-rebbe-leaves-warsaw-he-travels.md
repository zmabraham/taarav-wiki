# Conflict & Opposition: On Sunday, June 3, the Rebbe leaves Warsaw. He travels

## Description

On Sunday, June 3, the Rebbe leaves Warsaw. He travels to Glubok in Lithuania, stopping along the way in Vilna. He remains in Glubok till Tuesday, June 19 (6 Tamuz). This was a very inspiring visit for the Chasidim of the region. He returns to Warsaw, traveling on July 5 (22 Tamuz) to Marienbad.
