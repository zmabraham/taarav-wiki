## Description

The Rashab was taken aback and said to his son, “Go to your mother and tell her that I am feeling good, my headache is gone, and there is no pain in my heart. She can rest assured that I feel fine.” The boy climbed back over the crowd, made his way to his mother, and gave her the message. “She kissed me and said, ‘Wonderful!’”
