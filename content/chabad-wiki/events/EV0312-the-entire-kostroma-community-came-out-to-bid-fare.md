# Arrest: The entire Kostroma community came out to bid farewell to the Rebbe.

> Arrest event from Chabad history — Year: 1927

## Overview

**Type:** Arrest
**Year:** 1927
**Locations:** Leningrad, Kharkiv

## Description

The entire Kostroma community came out to bid farewell to the Rebbe. “They gave him a grand escort,” remembered Melamed. Two days later,[[74: July 14, 1927.]] the Rebbe arrived in Leningrad, despite concerns that he could be arrested once again. News of the Rebbe’s liberation could not be suppressed, and it spread quickly to Jewish communities across the Soviet Union.
