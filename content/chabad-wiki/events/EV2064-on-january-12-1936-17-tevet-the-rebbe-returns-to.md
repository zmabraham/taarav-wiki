# Conflict & Opposition: On January 12, 1936 (17 Tevet), the Rebbe returns to

> Conflict & Opposition — Year: 1936 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.11: A Global Vision

## Overview

**Event Type:** Conflict & Opposition
**Year/Date:** 1936, 17 Tevet, 24 Shevat, 26 Shevat
**Location(s):** Warsaw
**People mentioned:** Gurary

**Section/Chapter:** Afterword

## Description

On January 12, 1936 (17 Tevet), the Rebbe returns to the sanatorium in Perchtoldsdorf for medical treatment. He returns to Warsaw on February 17 (24 Shevat), attending the bar mitzvah of his grandson, Shalom Ber (Berke) Gurary, on February 18–19 (25–26 Shevat).
