## Description

New legislation introduced in Russia in 1929 created another major economic stumbling block for Shabbat-observant Jews. If they were not officially employed, they were liable to pay five to ten times as much for rent. The law targeted clergy of all religions, curbing their ability to serve congregations in Russia.
