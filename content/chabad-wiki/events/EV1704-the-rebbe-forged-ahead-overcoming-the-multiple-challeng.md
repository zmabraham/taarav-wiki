## Description

The Rebbe forged ahead, overcoming the multiple challenges of raising funds and securing the Russian government’s permission, as well as the logistics of producing matzah. He launched a diplomatic initiative in Latvia, enlisting the help of Mordechai Dubin, who met with the Russian Ambassador in Riga. He promised to do all he could to help the shipment go through.
