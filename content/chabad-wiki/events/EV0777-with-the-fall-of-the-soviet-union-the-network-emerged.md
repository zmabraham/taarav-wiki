## Description

With the fall of the Soviet Union, the network emerged from the shadows. Starting in 1990, the seventh Rebbe (who was living in New York) dispatched a series of young rabbis and rebbetzins to rekindle Jewish life in Russia. They linked up with the activists on the ground, who had been operating clandestinely for decades. That year, the first public menorah lighting took place in Moscow’s Red Square.
