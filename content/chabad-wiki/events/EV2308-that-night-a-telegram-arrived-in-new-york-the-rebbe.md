## Description

That night, a telegram arrived in New York: “The Rebbe is free.” Yisrael Jacobson ran outside and “did handsprings on the sidewalk, shouting with delight.” He could not contain his joy; there was a huge farbrengen in his house that night. The next day he placed a transatlantic call and spoke to the Rebbe, who thanked him for the unprecedented effort of the Chabad community in the US on his behalf.
