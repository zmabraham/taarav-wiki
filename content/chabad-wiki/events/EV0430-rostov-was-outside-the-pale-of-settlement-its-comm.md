## Description

Rostov was outside the Pale of Settlement. Its community was founded by Jewish Cantonist soldiers who, after being drafted as children and serving twenty-five years in the Russian army, were permitted to reside anywhere in Russia. It was augmented by Jewish businessmen who had received permission to live in the city.
