# Historical Event: On March 8, 1932 (30 Adar Alef), the Rebbe travels

## Description

On March 8, 1932 (30 Adar Alef), the Rebbe travels to Lithuania, visiting the communities of Glubok and Pastov, among other townlets. He returns to Riga on March 17 (9 Adar Bet).
