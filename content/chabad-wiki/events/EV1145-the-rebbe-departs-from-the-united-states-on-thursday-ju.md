# Historical Event: The Rebbe departs from the United States on Thursday, July

> Historical Event — Year: 1930 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.11: A Global Vision

## Overview

**Event Type:** Historical Event
**Year/Date:** 1930, 15 Elul
**Location(s):** Berlin, Riga, Marienbad
**People mentioned:** Not specified

**Section/Chapter:** Afterword

## Description

The Rebbe departs from the United States on Thursday, July 17, 1930 (21 Tamuz 5690), arriving in Breman, Germany, on July 23 (27 Tamuz). From there he travels to Berlin for Tisha B’Av on August 3. Before returning to Riga, he stops in Marienbad for two weeks to recover from the long and arduous overseas trip. He then heads home, stopping in Berlin for a few days, and arrives in Riga on September 8 (15 Elul).
