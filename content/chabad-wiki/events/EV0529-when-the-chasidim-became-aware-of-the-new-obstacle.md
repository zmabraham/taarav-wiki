# Persecution / Raid: When the Chasidim became aware of the new obstacle, they rushed to

**Locations:** Not specified

## Description

When the Chasidim became aware of the new obstacle, they rushed to Madam Peshkova and asked her to intervene. She met with Soviet Premier Alexei Rykov, who called Vyacheslav Menzhinsky, head of the secret police, who agreed to let the Rebbe remain in prison until Sunday morning, after which the authorities permitted him a short visit home.[[60: Zigelbaum, Sippur shel Chag, chap. 12.]] ---
