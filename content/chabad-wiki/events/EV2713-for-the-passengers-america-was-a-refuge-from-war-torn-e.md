## Description

For the passengers, America was a refuge from war-torn Europe. For the Rebbe it was a new spiritual frontier with immense potential. He wanted to create a robust, self-assured Jewish life, and reinvigorating Jewish education was the foundation of his strategy to remold American Jewry. He intended to go on the offensive, motivated by his pride in uncompromising standards of Judaism. This was, he believed, his next mission, orchestrated by divine providence.
