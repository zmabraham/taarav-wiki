## Description

Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin took matters into his own hands. Secretly writing to Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson in New York, he implored his old friend for help. “We must put our heads together and find a solution,” he wrote. The Rebbe had not told him explicitly how serious the financial straits were, but it seemed clear to him. “In the end, a wedding must be made.
