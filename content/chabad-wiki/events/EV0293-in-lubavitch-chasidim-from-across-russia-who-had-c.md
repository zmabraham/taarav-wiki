# Establishment: In Lubavitch, Chasidim from across Russia who had come for the holidays

> Establishment event from Chabad history — Year: 1898

## Overview

**Type:** Establishment
**Year:** 1898
**Locations:** Lubavitch
**People involved:** Rabbi, Rayatz, Rashab, Yosef Yitzchak

## Description

In Lubavitch, Chasidim from across Russia who had come for the holidays encountered the students of Tomchei Temimim. The cadre of young, passionate, and intellectually gifted students impressed the guests, and the skepticism that had greeted the idea of a yeshivah just a year earlier evaporated. It was replaced by enthusiasm and prompted the Chasidim to discuss the yeshivah’s future and the need for strong leadership.
