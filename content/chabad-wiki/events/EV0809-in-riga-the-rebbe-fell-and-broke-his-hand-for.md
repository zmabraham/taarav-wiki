## Description

In Riga, the Rebbe fell and broke his hand. “For three weeks I had a cast, and then in the fourth week a regular bandage,” he wrote to his daughter in Paris. The injury was difficult for the Rebbe. “I was unable to write until a few days before our departure.” The Rebbe’s mother, Rebbetzin Shterna Sara, also faced a health challenge during those weeks in Riga.
