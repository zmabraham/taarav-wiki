## Description

The sergeant ordered a nearby police officer to arrest Yosef Yitzchak. “He accused me of ripping off the copper badge from his uniform and preventing him from fulfilling his duty to enforce the law.” No one noticed the young Yosef Yitzchak being escorted by police down the busy streets. “There was a lot of commotion because of the crowds, wagons, horses, and livestock.” After crossing the plaza in the center of the marketplace, they came to the volost, the local police station.
