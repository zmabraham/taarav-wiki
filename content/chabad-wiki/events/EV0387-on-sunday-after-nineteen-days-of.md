## Description

On Sunday,[[61: July 3, 1927 (3 Tamuz, 5687).]] after nineteen days of imprisonment, the Rebbe was permitted to return home for a six-hour visit. Two of his Chasidim, Zalman Duchman and Yitzchak Minkowitz, met the Rebbe in his apartment. The Rebbe asked them if they had studied Chasidut while he was in prison. “We recited psalms,” they answered.
