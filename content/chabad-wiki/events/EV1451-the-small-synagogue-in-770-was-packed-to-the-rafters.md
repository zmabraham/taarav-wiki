# Gathering & Farbrengen: The small synagogue in 770 was packed to the rafters

## Description

The small synagogue in 770 was packed to the rafters when the Ramash finally entered at 9:45 p.m. for the farbrengen. The Chasidim sang a melody, after which the Ramash began to speak about strengthening the bond between the Rebbe and his Chasidim. “Each of us must continue the task the Rebbe entrusted us with.” He then explored the concept of ahavat Yisrael, the central mitzvah of the responsibility to love every Jew.
