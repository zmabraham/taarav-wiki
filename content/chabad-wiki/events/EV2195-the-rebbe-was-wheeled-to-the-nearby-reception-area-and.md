## Description

The Rebbe was wheeled to the nearby reception area, and the crowd ebbed and flowed as everyone strained to see him. Government officials greeted the Rebbe, and he thanked everyone for the warm welcome. The Rebbe’s voice was low, though “occasionally his determination [overcame] his fatigue and his voice would grow stronger,” reported the Brooklyn Eagle.
