## Overview

**Type:** Establishment
**Year:** 1911
**Locations:** Lubavitch
**People involved:** Rashab, Rabbi

## Description

In 1911, the Rashab sent a group of outstanding students, led by noted scholar Rabbi Zalman Havlin, to Hebron to establish the yeshivah. The new yeshivah, named Toras Emes, would be the first chasidic yeshivah in the Jewish homeland and was considered a branch of Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch. ---
