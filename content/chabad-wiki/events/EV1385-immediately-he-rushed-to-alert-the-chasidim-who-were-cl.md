## Description

Immediately he rushed to alert the Chasidim who were closest to the Rebbe, waking up Althaus and running to the home of Rabbi Chaim Lieberman, the Rebbe’s secretary. Knocking on the window, they warned Lieberman to destroy any financial records and documents in his possession related to the secret school system. They feared the GPU would search his house next.
