## Description

Late into his first night back in the US, Jacobson was “sitting and worrying,” wondering what else he could do. “I remembered that the lawyer Asher Rabinowitz had arranged for the Rebbe to meet President Hoover when he visited in 1929.” Despite the late hour, Jacobson called Rabinowitz, telling him, “Come to New York at once.” Asher’s father, Rabbi Dovid Rabinowitz, was a prominent Lubavitcher follower in Boston and a confidant of the Rebbe.
