## Description

The doctors’ consensus was that the Rebbe was weak from years of suffering. “You cannot imagine how much I have endured in the last five and a half years since I was exiled [in 1927],” he said. The Rebbe wrote that he was distraught from the hardships of his Chasidim and disheartened that despite major efforts his fundraising success had been marginal.
