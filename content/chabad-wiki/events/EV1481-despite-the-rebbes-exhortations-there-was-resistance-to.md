## Description

Despite the Rebbe’s exhortations there was resistance to the change in mentality. The transition would take time, gaining momentum when the children of the refugees became yeshivah students during the era of the Rebbe’s successor, the seventh Rebbe. He would expand the vision of his father-in-law of creating a global cadre of shluchim that would transform world Jewry.
