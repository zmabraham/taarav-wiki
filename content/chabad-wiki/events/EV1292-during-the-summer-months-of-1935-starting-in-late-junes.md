**People mentioned:** Not specified

**Section/Chapter:** Afterword

## Description

During the summer months of 1935 (starting in late June/Sivan), the Rebbe stays in the countryside next to Warsaw in the Otwock area. He returns to Warsaw in mid-August (around Rosh Chodesh Elul). On Thursday, September 19, (21 Elul) the Rebbe relocates permanently to Otwock. The main yeshivah in Warsaw is also transferred there.
