## Description

Around the world, Chasidim initiated their own grassroots efforts. In Kharkiv, Ukraine, Yehuda Chitrik had received an invitation for the wedding. “I realized right away there were major expenses.”[[31: Yehuda Chitrik, Reshimot Devarim, 440.]] He collected a thousand rubles and sent it to the Rebbe. Chasidim in other cities did the same. Somehow, through support in the US and Europe and through loans the Rebbe secured, the necessary funds were accumulated. ---
