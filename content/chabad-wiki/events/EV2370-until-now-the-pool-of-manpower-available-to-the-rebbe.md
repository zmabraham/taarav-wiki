## Description

Until now, the pool of manpower available to the Rebbe had been limited. That changed with the arrival of the two sets of refugees – they built the foundations for a dramatic revolution in the last five years of the Rebbe’s life. They filled key leadership positions in the postwar era, and their children would fill the ranks of the seventh Rebbe’s army of emissaries, eventually transforming the landscape of Judaism across the world.
