**Section/Chapter:** Introduction

## Description

Not included were two of the Rebbe’s daughters and their husbands, Rabbi and Mrs. Mendel and Sheina Horenstein, and Rabbi Mendel and Chaya Mushka Schneerson. Eventually, the Ramash and his wife Chaya Mushka were able to escape Paris after the German occupation, arriving in Vichy, France. From there they made their way to neutral Spain and arrived in the US in March 1941. Tragically, the Horensteins were captured by the Nazis and lost their lives in Treblinka.
