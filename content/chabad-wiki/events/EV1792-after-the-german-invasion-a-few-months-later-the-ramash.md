## Description

After the German invasion a few months later, the Ramash and his wife made a harrowing escape from Paris via Vichy, France, and Portugal, arriving in the US in March 1941. At that point, his involvement in the Rebbe’s affairs expanded greatly. In 1941, the Rebbe established Kehot Publication Society, a publishing house for the works of Chasidism and Judaism in general, and he appointed the Ramash as its chairman.
