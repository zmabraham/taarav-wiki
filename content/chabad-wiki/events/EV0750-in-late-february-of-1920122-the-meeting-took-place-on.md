## Description

In late February of 1920,[[122: The meeting took place on Friday, February 27, 1920 (8 Adar, 5780). While on a mission to Moscow, he was harassed by the Yevesektzia. In a diary entry dated 10 Adar Alef, 5787 (February 12, 1927), he writes that he woke up at 3 a.m. feeling troubled by events in Moscow. After reflecting on this meeting with his father, “I went back to sleep, and my soul was relaxed.” Sefer HaSichos, 5680–87 (Brooklyn, NY: Kehot Publication Society, 1992), 132; Likkutei Dibburim, v
