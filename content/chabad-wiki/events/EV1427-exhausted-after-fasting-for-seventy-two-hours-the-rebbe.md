## Description

Exhausted after fasting for seventy-two hours, the Rebbe returned to his cell. Finally, on Friday, after two nights and three days, a Jewish guard brought him his tefillin and books. The Rebbe told the officer that he refused to eat the non-kosher meals provided by the prison, and he asked for food from home. The officer mocked him for his request, but just before sunset, three loaves of uncut challah from home were delivered to his cell.
