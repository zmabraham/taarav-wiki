## Description

He stays in Riga for most of the year. From Wednesday, February 18, 1931 (Rosh Chodesh Adar) until February 26 (9 Adar) the Rebbe visits Rakshik in Lithuania. The purpose of this visit was to give an opportunity for the Chasidim in the surrounding area to visit the Rebbe. On Wednesday, June 24 (9 Tamuz), the Rebbe departs from Riga.
