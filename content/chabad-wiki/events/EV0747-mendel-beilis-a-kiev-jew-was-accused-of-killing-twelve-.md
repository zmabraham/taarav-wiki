## Description

Mendel Beilis, a Kiev Jew, was accused of killing twelve-year-old Andrei Yushchinksy after the boy’s body was discovered in a cave near the brick factory in Kiev that Beilis managed. It was a typical blood libel – prosecutors resurrected the myth of ritual murder, accusing Beilis of killing the boy to use his blood in the Passover matzahs.
