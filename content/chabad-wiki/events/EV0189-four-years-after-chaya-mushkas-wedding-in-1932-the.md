## Description

Four years after Chaya Mushka’s wedding, in 1932, the Rebbe’s youngest daughter, Sheina, married Rabbi Mendel Horenstein in the city of Landwarow,[[4: At the time, Landwarow was under Polish rule. Today the city is known as Lentvaris and is located some fifteen miles from Vilnius in Lithuania.]] Poland, just over the border, not far from Vilnius.[[5: The Rebbe wrote in 1938 that they chose this city for the wedding location because of visa issues.
