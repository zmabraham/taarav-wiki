## Description

The Rashab predicted to his son that the Communist Jews who had formed the Yevesektzia, the anti-religious Jewish section of the Communist Party, would be the greatest threat to their fellow Jews. But he also foresaw their eventual fate. “The Jewish group that combats Judaism will be broken and their leaders killed.
