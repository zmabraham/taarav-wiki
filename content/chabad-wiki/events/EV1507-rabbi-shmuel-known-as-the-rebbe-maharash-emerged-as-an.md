## Description

Rabbi Shmuel, known as the Rebbe Maharash, emerged as an important leader of Russian Jewry. Chasidim flocked to Lubavitch for inspiration, to hear his profound maamarim (discourses), and to seek his advice. Rabbi Shmuel exhibited strong leadership for Russian Jewry, intervening in the capital, St. Petersburg, to avert anti-Semitic decrees. His first wife died within months of their marriage.
