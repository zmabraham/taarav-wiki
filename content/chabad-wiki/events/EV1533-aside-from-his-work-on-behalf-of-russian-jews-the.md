## Description

Aside from his work on behalf of Russian Jews, the Rebbe continued to reach out to communities across the globe during the 1930s. Many Jews had emigrated from the chasidic heartland in Russia to new communities around the world. Some were ardent Chasidim; others merely had a nostalgic connection to Chabad.
