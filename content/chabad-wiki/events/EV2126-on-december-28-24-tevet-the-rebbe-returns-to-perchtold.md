# Historical Event: On December 28 (24 Tevet), the Rebbe returns to Perchtold...

**People mentioned:** Not specified

**Section/Chapter:** Afterword

## Description

On December 28 (24 Tevet), the Rebbe returns to Perchtoldsdorf, stays until March 9 (6 Adar Alef), and then departs for Paris. Three days after his departure from Austria, the Germans annex Austria. In Paris, the Rebbe stays at the Sanitorium in Ville-d’Avray until April 13 (12 Nisan), returning to Otwock in time for Passover.
