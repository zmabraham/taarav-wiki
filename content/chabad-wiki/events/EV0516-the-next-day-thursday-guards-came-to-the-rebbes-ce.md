## Description

The next day, Thursday, guards came to the Rebbe’s cell and ordered him to stand to hear his sentence. He refused and was severely beaten. They returned two more times, and each time the Rebbe refused to adhere to their orders and was beaten. Finally, the guards took him to an office, where Lulov informed him that he would be released from prison and exiled for three years to Kostroma.
