# Teaching & Publication: On Shabbat, the paper reported, “the hotel loses its mundane

## Description

On Shabbat, the paper reported, “the hotel loses its mundane weekday state.” On Shabbat afternoons, “people flood the hotel” to hear the Rebbe recite a maamar. “His voice becomes stronger and more flowing, words are streaming from his mouth, quotes from the Bible, Talmud, and Zohar woven and seasoned with parables, ideas, and interpretations.”
