## Description

The students headed east by train to Moscow, where they expected to receive their visas at the US Embassy. Tzvi Kotlarsky says that until that time, only individuals had trickled through Moscow; they were the first large group. “The US ambassador had never had a request for such a large number of visas.” Indeed, few people ever left Russia. “He said it was impossible to process them in the three short days we were in Moscow.” Instead
