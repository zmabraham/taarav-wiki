## Description

For the High Holidays in 1930, Chasidim from Poland and Lithuania came to celebrate with the Rebbe. A group of twenty-one Chasidim convened in the home of the Rashag, who suggested establishing Agudas Chassidei Chabad (a Union of the Chabad Chasidim) in Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia. With the Rebbe’s approval, the organization took on the mandate of strengthening the Chabad community in the region.
