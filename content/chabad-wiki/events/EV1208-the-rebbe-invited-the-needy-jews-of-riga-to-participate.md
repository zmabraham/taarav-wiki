## Description

The Rebbe invited the needy Jews of Riga to participate in special free dinners in honor of the wedding. “The Lubavitcher Rebbe, in honor of the wedding of his daughter on November 27, is providing meals for free on Sunday and Monday,” read the advertisement in Riga’s paper, the Frimorgen.
