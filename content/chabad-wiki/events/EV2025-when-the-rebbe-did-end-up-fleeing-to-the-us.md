## Description

When the Rebbe did end up fleeing to the US after the 1939 Nazi invasion of Poland, he viewed it as an orchestration of divine providence. Reflecting then on his hesitation to relocate a decade earlier, the Rebbe asserted that he would not have been effective at that time. He bemoaned the attitude of US Jewish leaders who were failing to uphold classic Jewish values. “Even rabbis” rationalized making compromises, he said. “This is the way of the world.”[[5: Igrot Kodesh, vol. 6, letter to Rabbi
