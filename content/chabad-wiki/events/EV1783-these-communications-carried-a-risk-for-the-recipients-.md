## Description

These communications carried a risk for the recipients. In 1930, The Detroit Jewish Chronicle reported that sixteen Jewish leaders were arrested in Minsk for the counterrevolutionary crime of corresponding with the Rebbe. “They had been hounded by the secret police for no other reason,” stated the paper, “other than being in contact with the Lubavitcher Rebbe.”
