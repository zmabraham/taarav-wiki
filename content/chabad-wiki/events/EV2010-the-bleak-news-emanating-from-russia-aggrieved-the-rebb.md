# Teaching & Publication: The bleak news emanating from Russia aggrieved the Rebbe....

> Teaching & Publication — Year: 1933 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.8: Poland - Starting Anew

## Overview

**Event Type:** Teaching & Publication
**Year/Date:** 1933, 24 Tevet
**Location(s):** Moscow, Leningrad, Kharkiv
**People mentioned:** Feigin, Jacobson

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter Eight

## Description

The bleak news emanating from Russia aggrieved the Rebbe. Feigin wrote of the Rebbe’s emotional state, “What gives him no peace are the reports from expulsions in Moscow, Leningrad, and Kharkiv.” The Rebbe’s spirit was broken, he said, as “the situation in Russia affects the essence of his soul.”
