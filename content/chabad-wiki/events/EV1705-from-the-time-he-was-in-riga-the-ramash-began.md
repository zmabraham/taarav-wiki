## Description

From the time he was in Riga, the Ramash began to record his interactions with his father-in-law in a detailed private diary. It included a wealth of chasidic lore, history, and customs, with anecdotes of all the Rebbes, tracing back to the founder of the chasidic movement, Rabbi Yisrael Baal Shem Tov.
