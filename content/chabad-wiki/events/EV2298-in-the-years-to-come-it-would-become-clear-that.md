## Description

In the years to come, it would become clear that the Rebbe’s seven-year ordeal under the Communist regime was but one chapter in their conflict. He continued to orchestrate and support the spiritual resistance in the decades that followed, and the battle he waged had far-reaching historical repercussions both in the Soviet Union and beyond. The clandestine system started by the Rebbe Rayatz in the early 1920s endured, despite the harassment, imprisonment, and even execution of many key activists
