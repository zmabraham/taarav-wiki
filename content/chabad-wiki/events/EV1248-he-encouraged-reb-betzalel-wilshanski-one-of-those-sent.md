## Description

He encouraged Reb Betzalel Wilshanski, one of those sent to Australia, to become an activist. He was known for his scholarship; as a teenager he had studied in the yeshivah in Lubavitch. Now at age fifty-two, he was learning to adapt to a new world. The Rebbe exhorted him to reach out to Australian youth. “Even those who apparently don’t want to study, you should draw them closer by giving them reading material in their language. Don’t get disillusioned in your efforts to influence them.”[[45: I
