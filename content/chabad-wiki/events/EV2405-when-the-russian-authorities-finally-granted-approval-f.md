## Description

When the Russian authorities finally granted approval for the Rebbe and his family to depart Russia for Riga in October 1927, the Rebbe insisted that his future son-in-law also be permitted to leave. Russian officials challenged him. “Can’t you find another son-in-law?” they asked. The Rebbe’s legendary response was, “A son-in-law like him I can’t find there.”
