## Description

His passing was a shock to Chasidim, and the future of Chabad remained in doubt. There were sons, but they were still quite young, causing many to wonder if they would step into their father’s place. Though still reeling from the loss, Chabad elders and rabbis convened in Lubavitch a few weeks after Rabbi Shmuel’s passing.[[34: The meeting took place on Shabbat, October 21, 1882 (8 Cheshvan, 5643, Parashas Lekh Lekha). Participants included prominent rabbis such as Rabbi Zalman Pinsker of Kherso
