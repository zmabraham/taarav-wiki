## Description

The date and location for the government-rabbinic conference was set for St. Petersburg in 1910. The traditional rabbis, representatives of the crown rabbis, and community leaders were all invited to seek a consensus. There had been other rabbinic conferences in the past, but a meeting of this kind, with full government sanction, was unprecedented. The Rashab was named as one of the conference organizers. His son was at his side throughout the process.
