## Description

On the Sunday before the wedding, the Rebbe departed to Warsaw. At the Riga train station, he was greeted by thousands of Chasidim, acquaintances, and well-wishers who came to see him off. Two rows of men stretching some five hundred feet stood as an honor guard as the Rebbe entered the station.
