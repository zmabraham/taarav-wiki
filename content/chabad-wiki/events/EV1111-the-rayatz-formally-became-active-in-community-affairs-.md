## Description

The Rayatz formally became active in community affairs at the age of fifteen in 1895, and from then on, he served as his father’s private secretary. For a quarter of a century, until the passing of the Rashab in 1920, he was intimately involved in almost all of his father’s communal activities. As his secretary, he managed much of the Rashab’s personal correspondence with Jewish leaders on issues of communal concern.
