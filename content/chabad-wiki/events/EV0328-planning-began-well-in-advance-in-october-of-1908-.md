## Description

Planning began well in advance. In October of 1908, the Rashab asked the Rayatz to organize a confidential meeting of a small group of rabbis closely aligned with Chabad to strategize a way forward and develop a consensus. There were a variety of issues to address before the St. Petersburg conference. Because of the political disruption in Russia, the czar had agreed to an election for the first time.
