## Description

News of the Rebbe’s approval and reports of the success of individual escapees spread amongst the Russian Chasidim. Some of the elders worried that if all the Chasidim departed, there would be no one left to direct the Jewish educational underground. In the end, single students were given a green light while others were told to make their own choice. Many decided to try, and from all over the country, Chasidim began streaming to Lvov, the departure point for transports to Poland.
