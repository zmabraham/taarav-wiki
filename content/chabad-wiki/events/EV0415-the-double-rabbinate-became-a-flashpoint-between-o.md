## Description

The double rabbinate became a flashpoint between observant Jews and Enlightenment activists. Communities saw the crown rabbis as a tool to marginalize traditional rabbis and advance the secularists’ agenda backed by government authority. Judah Leib Katzenelson justified the institution of the crown rabbis in a piece he wrote for the secular paper Voskhod, where he maintained that the unregulated traditional rabbinate was “abnormal and required immediate and fundamental reform.”[[99: Voskhod, no. ---
