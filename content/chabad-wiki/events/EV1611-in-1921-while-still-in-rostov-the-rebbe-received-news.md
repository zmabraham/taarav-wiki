## Description

In 1921, while still in Rostov, the Rebbe received news of the opening of a yeshivah in Warsaw. He referred to this time as “days of light and days of joy.” At the time, the yeshivah in Rostov was being forcibly closed, and the Chabad educational network in Russia was going underground.
