# Historical Event: It was a few minutes after midnight on June 15, 1927,[[2: June

> Historical Event event from Chabad history — Year: 1927

## Overview

**Type:** Historical Event
**Year:** 1927
**Locations:** Leningrad
**People involved:** Rebbe

## Description

It was a few minutes after midnight on June 15, 1927,[[2: June 14 and the beginning of June 15 (14 Sivan, 5687).]] and the Rebbe and his family had gathered for a late supper in his Leningrad apartment. For the Rebbe, it had been a long day. He received people for private audiences until 11:30 p.m. and then prayed the evening service. Now he was finally sitting down for dinner with his family. He was weary. ---
