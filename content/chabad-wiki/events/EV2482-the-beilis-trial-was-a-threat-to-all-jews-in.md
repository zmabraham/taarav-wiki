## Description

The Beilis trial was a threat to all Jews in Russia and the chasidic community in particular. The Rashab dispatched the Rayatz to enlist Oscar Gruzenberg, one of Russia’s leading attorneys, to spearhead the defense. Gruzenberg came from a traditional family but was not personally observant.
