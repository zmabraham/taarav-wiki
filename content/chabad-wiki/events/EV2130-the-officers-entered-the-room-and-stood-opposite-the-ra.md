# Historical Event: The officers entered the room and stood opposite the Rashab.

> Historical Event — Year: 1989 | Source: Undaunted — Ch.2: Tomchei Temimim

## Overview

**Event Type:** Historical Event
**Year/Date:** 1989
**Location(s):** Brooklyn
**People mentioned:** Rashab, Schneersohn, Shalom Dovber

**Section/Chapter:** Chapter 2 - Tomchei Temimim

## Description

The officers entered the room and stood opposite the Rashab. “He turned his face to the Chasidim” as if the officers were not there. “We have to say chasidut, and they will be batul (nullified, as if they don’t exist).” The Rashab began saying a maamar that explored the concept of evil being a false reality, with the true essence of all creation being godly
