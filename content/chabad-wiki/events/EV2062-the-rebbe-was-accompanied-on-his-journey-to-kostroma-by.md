## Description

The Rebbe was accompanied on his journey to Kostroma by his unmarried daughter Chaya Mussia, his son-in-law Rabbi Gurary, and Rabbi Eliyahu Althaus. Rabbi Dvorkin arrived Sunday night and started preparing for the Rebbe’s arrival. He repaired the local mikveh and opened a cheder for the children of the one hundred members of the local Jewish community; he also arranged housing for the Rebbe and his entourage at the home of the town shochet.
