## Description

Since the Bolshevik revolution in 1917, the US had refused to recognize the new regime in Russia. In 1933, the newly elected Franklin Roosevelt was determined to change that policy. US recognition was important to Russia, and so Russian Foreign Minister Maxim Litvinov traveled to Washington to personally negotiate with US officials.[[123: Levin, Toldot Chabad BeRusia HaSovietit, chap. 42.]] As with so many other Communist officials, Litvinov was Jewish, born as Meir Finkelstein in 1876 in Bialys ---
