## Description

Since the time of Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi (1745–1812), Chasidim had made regular voluntary contributions called maamad,[[23: The second Rebbe, Rabbi Dovber, known as the Mitteler Rebbe, and his disciple Reb Aharon Horowitz organized the annual collection known as maamad. In 1948, the Ramash wrote that this tradition followed the talmudic teachings of supporting a Torah scholar, making the Chasidim partners in the Rebbe’s work. “Giving maamad,” available at: chabad.org/2281850.
