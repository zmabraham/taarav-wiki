## Description

The Rashab selected that excerpt from the Tanya because of its powerful message, says Rabbi Paltiel. He foresaw that during his son’s leadership, the Rayatz would face many challenges and need to stand up for his principles. He was telling him that “mesirat nefesh, self-sacrifice for G-d and His Torah, is this way and no other way, no compromises, no matter what it costs.” As the Rayatz reflected fifty years later, “It was about resilience, that in matters of principle one stands firm.”
