# Historical Event: After his father’s passing, the Rashab was absent from Lu...

## Overview

**Year/Date:** 1884
**Location(s):** Lubavitch
**People mentioned:** Rashab, Yosef Yitzchak

**Section/Chapter:** Introduction

## Description

After his father’s passing, the Rashab was absent from Lubavitch for long stretches of time seeking medical treatment in Russia and abroad for repeated health issues. He made one such trip in 1884. “When he finally came home, I did not recognize him,” recalled Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak. “My grandmother, Rebbetzin Rivkah, told me, ‘This is your father.’”
