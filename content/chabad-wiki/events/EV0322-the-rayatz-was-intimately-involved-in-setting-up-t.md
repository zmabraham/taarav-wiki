# Teaching / Publication: The Rayatz was intimately involved in setting up the program in Eretz

> Teaching / Publication event from Chabad history — Year: 1913

## Overview

**Type:** Teaching / Publication
**Year:** 1913
**Locations:** Not specified
**People involved:** Rayatz, Rabbi

## Description

The Rayatz was intimately involved in setting up the program in Eretz Yisrael, giving detailed instructions for the curriculum and the acceptance of students. However, he expected that Rabbi Havlin would ultimately take full responsibility. In 1913, he wrote to him,[[68: Igrot Kodesh, vol. 1, letter to Rabbi Havlin dated 3 Kislev, 5674 (December 3, 1913).]] “Our approach is to appoint individuals and entrust them with the management, and they are responsible for the projects given over to them.” ---
