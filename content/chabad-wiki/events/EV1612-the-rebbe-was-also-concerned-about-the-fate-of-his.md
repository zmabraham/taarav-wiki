## Description

The Rebbe was also concerned about the fate of his precious library. The most valuable manuscripts had stayed at his side during the siege in Warsaw, but the bulk of the collection was still in Poland. Feigin asked Jacobson to arrange the transfer of the library that remained in Otwock. “There are 127 cases of books and three cases of handwritten manuscripts,” he wrote.
