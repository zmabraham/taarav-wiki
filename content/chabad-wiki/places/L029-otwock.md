# Otwock

> City in Poland — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Town near Warsaw where the Rayatz had his court in exile. Site of yeshiva.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Otwock** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
