# Eretz Yisrael (Palestine/Israel)

> Region in Middle East — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

The Holy Land. Chabad had a long presence; the Rashag traveled there; many Chabad settlements established.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Eretz Yisrael (Palestine/Israel)** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
