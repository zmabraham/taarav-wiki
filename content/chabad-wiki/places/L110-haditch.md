# Haditch

> City in Ukraine — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Town where the Alter Rebbe (Rabbi Schneur Zalman) passed away in 1813 while fleeing Napoleon.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Haditch** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
