# Malachovka

> Temporary refuge for the Rayatz outside Moscow during final Soviet years

## Summary

Malachovka was a settlement near Moscow where Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz) sought temporary refuge during the final years of his residence in the Soviet Union (1927-1928). It served as a base of operations while he arranged his departure from Russia.

## Significance

**Temporary Refuge:**
- Provided relative safety from immediate G.P.U. harassment
- Allowed continued leadership activities
- Base for arranging escape from Soviet Union
- Connection point for Chasidim

**Strategic Location:**
- Near Moscow but outside direct city control
- Accessible for visitors from Jewish communities
-相对 removed from immediate Soviet surveillance
- Transition point before leaving USSR

**Final Soviet Period:**
- 1927-1928: Period of residence
- Arranged departure from Soviet Union
- Maintained underground connections
- Prepared for global leadership role

## Related Events

- **1927-1928:** Rayatz stays in Malachovka after liberation
- **1928:** Final arrangements to leave Soviet Union
- **1928:** Departure from Russia
