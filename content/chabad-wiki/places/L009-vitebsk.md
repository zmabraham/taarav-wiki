# Vitebsk (Віцебск; Witebsk)

> Major Jewish community in White Russia and site of early Soviet anti-Jewish trials

## Summary

Vitebsk was a significant city in the Vitebsk Governorate of White Russia (now Belarus) with a large and historic Jewish community. It played a notable role in early Soviet anti-religious campaigns, being the site of one of the first trials targeting Jewish education in 1921.

## Significance

**Jewish Community:**
- Major Jewish population center in White Russia
- Active religious life with numerous synagogues
- Strong educational institutions (chedarim, yeshivos)
- Center of Torah scholarship and Jewish culture

**Soviet Persecution:**
- Site of one of the first anti-Jewish trials in 1921
- **Vitebsk cheder trial** - Early example of Soviet anti-religious campaign
- Targeting of Jewish religious education
- Part of broader Yevsektzia efforts to suppress Judaism

**Historical Context:**
- Located in Pale of Settlement
- Part of Russian Empire's Jewish territory
- Significant Chabad presence
- Cultural and economic hub for Jewish life

**1921 Trial:**
- First wave of anti-Jewish prosecutions
- Cheder (religious school) targeted by Soviet authorities
- Teachers and administrators arrested
- Set pattern for future persecutions

## Related Events

- **Pre-1920:** Active Jewish religious life
- **1921:** Vitebsk cheder trial - early anti-Jewish prosecution
- **1920s:** Continued Soviet anti-religious campaigns
- **1941-1944:** Nazi occupation and Holocaust
