# Spalerno Prison

> Notorious GPU prison where the Rayatz was incarcerated for 18 days in 1927

## Overview

Spalerno Prison (Спалерная тюрьма) was a notorious prison in Leningrad operated by the GPU (Soviet secret police). It was used primarily for political prisoners and those accused of "counter-revolutionary" activities.

## Significance in Chabad History

### 1927 Imprisonment

- **Arrest Date:** 15 Sivan 5687 (June 14, 1927)
- **Duration:** 18 days, 11 hours, 15 minutes
- **Cell:** Small prison cell with harsh conditions
- **Interrogation:** Daily intense interrogations by GPU officials

### Prison Conditions

- **Isolation:** Cut off from family and followers
- **Intimidation:** Threats of execution and torture
- **Humiliation:** Degrading treatment to break spirit
- **Surveillance:** Constant monitoring by guards

### Spiritual Victory

Despite the conditions, the Rayatz achieved spiritual heights:

- **Maamar:** Wrote profound discourse of Chassidus on Rosh Chodesh Tammuz
- **Faith:** Declared "I have not feared them, I do not fear them, and I shall not fear them"
- **Perspective:** Called these "luminous days" that elevated his spiritual level
- **Torah Study:** Memorized and reviewed Mishnah during imprisonment

### Famous Incidents

- **Midnight Arrest:** Taken from home at 2:15 AM
- **Interrogation Tactics:** Attempted to extract confession of "anti-Soviet activities"
- **Mass Executions:** Twelve religious leaders executed while Rayatz was imprisoned
- **Spiritual Defiance:** Maintained dignity and faith throughout

## Prison History

- **Location:** Central Leningrad
- **Reputation:** Known for harsh treatment of political prisoners
- **GPU Operations:** Center of Soviet political repression
- **Historical Note:** Many religious and political figures suffered here

## Liberation

- **Date:** 3 Tammuz 5687 (July 3, 1927)
- **Outcome:** Exile to Kostroma instead of expected execution
- **Return:** Eventually completely liberated on 12 Tammuz
- **Historic Impact:** 12-13 Tammuz became Chabad holiday

## Legacy

Spalerno Prison became a symbol of Jewish spiritual resistance against oppression. The Rayatz's conduct there exemplified mesirus nefesh (self-sacrifice) and inspired generations.
