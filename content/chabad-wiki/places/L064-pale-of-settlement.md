# Pale of Settlement

> Region in Russia — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Area of Imperial Russia where Jews were legally permitted to reside. Encompassed most early Chabad communities.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Pale of Settlement** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
