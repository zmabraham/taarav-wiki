# Spalerno (Shpalerka)

> Prison in Russia — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Leningrad political prison. The Rayatz was imprisoned here in 1927.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Spalerno (Shpalerka)** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
