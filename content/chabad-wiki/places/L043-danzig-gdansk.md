# Danzig (Gdansk)

> City in Poland/Germany — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Free city; pivotal in Nazi Germany's territorial claims. The Rayatz fled through here in 1939.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Danzig (Gdansk)** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
