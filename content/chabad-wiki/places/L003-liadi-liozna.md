# Liadi (Ляды; Liozna)

> Residence of the Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi (c.1804-1812)

## Summary

Liadi (also known as Liozna) was a small town in the Vitebsk Governorate of White Russia. It served as the residence of Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi, the founder of the Chabad movement and author of the Tanya and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, from approximately 1804 to 1812.

## Significance

**Founding Period:**
- Rabbi Shneur Zalman settled in Liadi after leaving Liozna
- Established Chabad Chasidus as a distinct movement
- Composed major works including portions of Tanya
- Center of early Chabad activity and scholarship

**Historical Context:**
- Located in Pale of Settlement where Jews were allowed to live
- Part of the Russian Empire's Jewish territory
- Experienced both French invasion (1812) and Russian persecution

**Leadership:**
- First permanent residence of the Alter Rebbe
- From here, Chabad Chasidus spread throughout White Russia
- Early community of Chasidim formed around the Alter Rebbe

## Related Events

- **c.1804:** Alter Rebbe moves to Liadi
- **1812:** French invasion forces Alter Rebbe to flee
- **1812:** Alter Rebbe's arrest and imprisonment by Russians
- **1813:** After Alter Rebbe's passing, Mitteler Rebbe settles in Lubavitch
