# Kiev

> Major Jewish community center in Ukraine

## Summary

Kiev, the capital of Ukraine, has been home to a significant Jewish community for centuries. Located on the Dnieper River, it was an important center of Jewish life, scholarship, and culture in Eastern Europe.

## Significance

**Jewish Community:**
- Large and historic Jewish population
- Major center of Torah learning
- Active religious institutions
- Cultural and economic hub

**Historical Context:**
- Part of Russian Empire, then Soviet Union
- Site of both Jewish prosperity and persecution
- Experienced Soviet anti-religious campaigns
- Devastated during Holocaust

**Regional Importance:**
- Major city in Jewish Pale of Settlement
- Commercial and cultural center
- Connection point for Jewish communities
- Influence on surrounding towns
