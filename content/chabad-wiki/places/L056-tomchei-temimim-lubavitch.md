# Tomchei Temimim (Lubavitch)

> Yeshiva in Russia — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Flagship Chabad yeshiva founded by the Rashab in 1897 in Lubavitch. Training ground for Chabad leaders.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Tomchei Temimim (Lubavitch)** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
