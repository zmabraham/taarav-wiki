# The Ohel

> Shrine in USA — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Burial site (Ohel) of the Rayatz and the 7th Rebbe in Queens, NY. Major pilgrimage site.

## Significance in Chabad History

**The Ohel** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
