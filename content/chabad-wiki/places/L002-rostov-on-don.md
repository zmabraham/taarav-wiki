# Rostov-on-Don (Ростов-на-Дону)

> Final residence of Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rashab), 1915-1920

## Summary

Rostov-on-Don is a city in southern Russia, located on the Don River approximately 30 miles from the Sea of Azov. It served as the residence of the fifth Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rashab), during the final years of his life from 1915 to 1920.

## Significance

**Rebbe's Exile:**
- World War I front lines approached Lubavitch in 1915
- Rashab was forced to flee his lifelong home
- Chose Rostov for its relative safety and established Jewish community
- Remained here until his passing in 1920

**Leadership Transition:**
- Rayatz visited his father in Rostov during the difficult war years
- Critical period of leadership transition occurred here
- Last years of Rashab's teachings and guidance to Chasidim

**Soviet Period:**
- Fell under Bolshevik control during Russian Civil War
- Experienced anti-religious campaigns
- Site of ongoing religious persecution despite communist authorities

## Related Events

- **1915:** Rashab flees to Rostov as WWI front approaches Lubavitch
- **1915-1920:** Rashab resides in Rostov, continuing leadership
- **1920:** Passing of Rashab in Rostov on 2 Nissan
- **1920s:** Continued Chabad presence despite Soviet restrictions
- **1921:** First anti-Jewish trials targeting Rostov yeshivah
