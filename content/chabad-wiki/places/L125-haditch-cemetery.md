# Haditch Cemetery

> Cemetery in Ukraine — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Burial site of Rabbi Schneur Zalman (Alter Rebbe) who died there in 1813.

## Significance in Chabad History

**Haditch Cemetery** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
