# Minsk (Мінск; Минск)

> Capital of Belarus and major Jewish community center

## Summary

Minsk is the capital and largest city of Belarus, historically a major center of Jewish life in Eastern Europe. It served as the capital of the Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic and has been home to a significant Jewish community for centuries.

## Significance

**Jewish Community:**
- Large and historic Jewish population
- Major center of Torah scholarship
- Active religious institutions
- Cultural and economic hub

**Political Center:**
- Capital of Byelorussian SSR
- Administrative center for region
- Site of Soviet anti-religious policies
- Government oversight of Jewish communities

**Regional Importance:**
- Central location in White Russia
- Transportation and commercial hub
- Connection point for Jewish communities
- Influence on surrounding towns
