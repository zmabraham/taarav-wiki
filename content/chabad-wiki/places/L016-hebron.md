# Hebron

> Ancient holy city and location of Toras Emes Yeshiva (founded 1911)

## Summary

Hebron is one of the four holy cities of Judaism and the site of the **Toras Emes Yeshiva**, founded in 1911 by Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rashab). The city holds profound significance in Jewish tradition as the burial place of the patriarchs and matriarchs.

## Significance

**Holy City:**
- One of Judaism's four holy cities (along with Jerusalem, Tiberias, Safed)
- **Cave of Machpelah** - Traditional burial place of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Sarah, Rebecca, Leah
- Ancient Jewish community dating back to biblical times
- Site of continuous Jewish presence for millennia

**Toras Emes Yeshiva:**
- Founded in 1911 by Rashab
- Major Chabad institution in Palestine
- Center of Torah learning in Holy Land
- Part of Rashab's global educational vision

**Jewish Community:**
- Ancient community with periods of interruption
- **1929 Hebron Massacre** - Devastating attack on Jewish community
- Post-1967 reestablishment of Jewish presence
- Modern Jewish community with religious significance

## Related Events

- **1911:** Founding of Toras Emes Yeshiva
- **1929:** Hebron Massacre - destruction of ancient community
- **1967:** Reestablishment of Jewish community after Six-Day War
- **Modern era:** Continued Jewish presence

## Toras Emes Yeshiva

**Establishment:**

**Significance:**
