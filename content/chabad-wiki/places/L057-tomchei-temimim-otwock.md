# Tomchei Temimim (Otwock)

> Yeshiva in Poland — significant in Chabad history

## Overview

Polish branch of Tomchei Temimim established by the Rayatz during his Warsaw exile.

**Tomchei Temimim (Otwock)** plays a role in the history of Chabad Chasidism as documented in the primary sources (Undaunted, A Prince in Prison, The Heroic Struggle, Toldot Chabad).
