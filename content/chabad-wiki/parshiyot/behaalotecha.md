# Behaalotecha — When You Raise Up

**Hebrew:** בְּהַעֲלֹתְךָ | **Book:** Bamidbar (Numbers)

## Summary
Aaron lights the Menorah; the silver trumpets; Passover Sheni; the quail; Miriam and Aaron speak against Moses.
