# Ki Tetze — When You Go Out

**Hebrew:** כִּי תֵצֵא | **Book:** Devarim (Deuteronomy)

## Summary
Laws of warfare; diverse laws (lost property, parents, Shabbat, tzitzit, marriage laws).
