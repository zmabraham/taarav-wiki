## 🕍 The Seven Lubavitcher Rebbes

| Rebbe | Years | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi | 1745–1812 | Founder of Chabad, author of the Tanya |
| Rabbi Dovber Schneuri | 1773–1827 | Second Rebbe, settled Chabad in Lubavitch |
| Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn | 1789–1866 | Third Rebbe, prolific halachic authority |
| Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn (Maharash) | 1834–1882 | Fourth Rebbe |
| Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rashab) | 1860–1920 | Fifth Rebbe, founded Tomchei Temimim |
| Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rayatz) | 1880–1950 | Sixth Rebbe — faced Czarist, Soviet, and Nazi persecution |
| Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Ramash) | 1902–1994 | Seventh Rebbe, built global Chabad movement |

## 🗺️ Key Places

- **Lubavitch** — Capital of Chabad for 102 years
- **Liadi** — Home of the Alter Rebbe
- **Rostov-on-Don** — Where the Rashab spent his final years
- **Leningrad** — Site of the Rayatz's 1927 arrest
- **Spalerno Prison** — Where the Rayatz was held
- **Warsaw** — The Rayatz's Polish exile
- **Brooklyn, NY** — World Chabad headquarters from 1940
- **Hebron** — Site of Chabad yeshiva and 1929 massacre
