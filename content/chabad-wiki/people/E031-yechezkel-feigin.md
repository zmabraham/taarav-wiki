# Rabbi Yechezkel Feigin

## Overview

Chabad chassid who served as an emissary and secretary, involved in the Rebbe's work from Russia through the periods of exile in Riga and beyond.

## Key Facts

- **Role:** Emissary (shaliach), secretary, administrator
- **Era:** Early-mid 20th century
- **Primary association:** Sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe (Rayatz)

## Contributions

### Emissary Work

- **Exile coordination:** Involved in coordinating the Rebbe's activities during exile in Latvia
- **Underground network:** Participated in maintaining the underground Jewish educational and religious network
- **Community visits:** Traveled to various communities as the Rebbe's representative

### Administrative Support

- **Secretarial duties:** Assisted with correspondence and documentation
- **Logistical coordination:** Helped organize the Rebbe's schedule and activities

## Historical Context

Feigin was active during several major transitions in Chabad history:
- The period of Soviet persecution in Russia
- The Rebbe's exile to Riga (1927-1934)
- The relocation to Warsaw (1934-1940)
- The eventual move to America
