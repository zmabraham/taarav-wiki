# Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski

## Overview

Prominent rabbinic leader and dayan (judge) who coordinated closely with the Rashab (fifth Lubavitcher Rebbe) on matters of Jewish education and communal affairs.

## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1863
- **Died:** 1940
- **Title:** Dayan (chief rabbinical judge)
- **Location:** Vilna (Vilnius), Lithuania
- **Era:** Late 19th - early 20th century

## Contributions

### Rabbinic Leadership

- **Communal advocacy:** Worked with the Rashab to create strategies for maintaining Jewish education in the face of secularization and government pressure
- **Education policy:** Debated the question of secular education for community rabbis, arguing that a modicum of Russian language knowledge was necessary
- **Coordination:** Partnered with the Rashab and other rabbinic leaders to protect Jewish religious life

### Historical Context

Grodzinski was active during a period of significant challenge for European Jewry:
- Rise of secularism and enlightenment movements
- Increasing government involvement in Jewish education
- World War I and its aftermath
- The interwar period in independent Lithuania

## Notable Interactions

### Collaboration with the Rashab

- Participated in rabbinic assemblies to coordinate responses to challenges facing traditional Judaism
- Worked to balance the need for protecting Torah education with practical necessities of communal leadership
