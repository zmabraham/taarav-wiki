# Rabbi Dovber Schneuri (The Mitteler Rebbe)

> Second Lubavitcher Rebbe who settled in Lubavitch and authored profound Chabad philosophy

## Overview

Rabbi Dovber Schneuri (1773-1827), known as the Mitteler Rebbe ("Middle Rebbe"), was the son of Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi (the Alter Rebbe) and the second leader of the Chabad movement. He authored profound philosophical works that systematized Chabad Chassidus.

## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1773 (9 Kislev 5534), Liozna, White Russia
- **Died:** 1827 (9 Kislev 5588)
- **Father:** Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe)
- **Succeeded:** 1813 upon father's passing
- **Predecessor:** Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi
- **Successor:** Rabbi Menachem Mendel (Tzemach Tzedek)

## Major Achievements

### Settlement in Lubavitch (1813)

- **Year 1813:** Moved from Liadi to Lubavitch
- **Reason:** Established Lubavitch as Chabad headquarters
- **Significance:** Lubavitch remained Chabad capital for 102 years
- **Impact:** Created stable center for movement

### Literary Works

Authored profound Chabad philosophical texts:
- **Sha'ar HaYichud V'HaEmunah** (Gate of Unity and Faith)
- **Sha'arei Teshuvah** (Gates of Repentance)
- **Sha'arei Orah** (Gates of Light)
- **Biurei HaZohar** (Commentary on Zohar)
- **Maamarim** (Discourses) - Published in multiple volumes

### Leadership Challenges

- **Napoleonic Wars:** Guided chassidim through chaotic period
- **Internal Struggle:** Dealt with opponents of Chabad
- **Persecution:** Faced arrest and interrogation by Russian authorities
- **Building:** Established institutions for future growth

## Character and Style

- **Depth:** Extremely profound and analytical approach
- **Complexity:** Wrote sophisticated philosophical explanations
- **Systematization:** Organized Chabad teachings systematically
- **Leadership:** Guided movement through difficult transition period

## Legacy

- **Philosophy:** His works remain foundational texts in Chabad study
- **Lubavitch:** His decision to settle there defined Chabad geography
- **9 Kislev:** His passing (and birth) is celebrated as Chabad holiday
- **Influence:** Shaped Chabad's intellectual and spiritual approach

## Family

- **Son:** Rabbi Menachem Mendel (Tzemach Tzedek) succeeded him
- **Grandchildren:** Included the Rebbe Maharash and other leaders
- **Descendants:** Continues through Chabad leadership line
