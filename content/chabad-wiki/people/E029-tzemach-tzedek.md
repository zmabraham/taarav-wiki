# Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn (The Tzemach Tzedek)

> Third Lubavitcher Rebbe who authored extensive responsa and led during tumultuous period

## Overview

Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn (1789-1866), known as the Tzemach Tzedek ("Righteous Sprout"), was the third Lubavitcher Rebbe. A grandson of the Alter Rebbe, he authored voluminous works on Jewish law and Chassidus, leading Chabad through a period of growth and challenge.

## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1789 (29 Elul 5549), Liozna
- **Died:** 1866 (13 Nissan 5626), Lubavitch
- **Father:** Rabbi Shalom Shachna
- **Mother:** Devorah Leah (daughter of Alter Rebbe)
- **Became Rebbe:** 1827 (succeeded Mitteler Rebbe)
- **Children:** Seven sons, including Rebbe Maharash

## Major Achievements

### Leadership Expansion

- **Growth:** Expanded Chabad's influence throughout Russia
- **Institutions:** Strengthened yeshivas and communal organizations
- **Correspondence:** Guided thousands through letters
- **Unity:** Maintained unity among diverse Jewish communities

### Literary Works

Authored extensive works including:
- **Tzemach Tzedek:** Responsa on Jewish law (8 volumes)
- **Sefer HaMaamarim:** Chassidic discourses
- **Biurim:** Explanations on Tanya and other texts
- **Halachic Decisions:** Clear rulings on complex legal questions

### Historical Context

Led through major historical events:
- **Decembrist Revolt (1825):** Political upheaval in Russia
- **Crimean War (1853-1856):** Conflict affecting Jewish communities
- **Jewish Emancipation:** Period of changing Jewish legal status
- **Enlightenment (Haskalah):** Response to modernist movements

### Character and Approach

- **Scholarship:** Tremendous breadth in Torah and Jewish law
- **Leadership:** Balanced wisdom with accessibility
- **Compassion:** Deep concern for every Jew
- **Practical Guidance:** Clear halachic decisions for daily life

## Family Dynasty

- **Seven Sons:** All became scholars and leaders
- **Successor:** Rabbi Shmuel (Maharash) became fourth Rebbe
- **Grandchildren:** Many distinguished rabbis and leaders
- **Descendants:** The Schneersohn family lineage

## Legacy

- **Legal Authority:** His responsa remain studied and cited
- **Chabad Structure:** Built institutions that sustained movement
- **Leadership Model:** Exemplified balanced, compassionate leadership
- **13 Nissan:** His passing is commemorated annually
