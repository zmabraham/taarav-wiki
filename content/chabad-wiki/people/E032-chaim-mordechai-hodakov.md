# Rabbi Chaim Mordechai Aizik Hodakov

## Overview

Chabad chassid and educator who was cultivated as an important personality during the Rebbe's period in Riga. Born to a Chabad family, he became a significant figure in the educational work of the movement.

## Key Facts

- **Full name:** Chaim Mordechai Aizik Hodakov
- **Role:** Educator, community leader
- **Location:** Riga, Latvia (during Rebbe's exile)
- **Era:** Early-mid 20th century

## Contributions

### Educational Work

- **Riga cultivation:** Recognized as one of the important personalities developed during the Rebbe's sojourn in Riga
- **Youth education:** Involved in educational initiatives for Jewish children
- **Community leadership:** Played a role in organizing Jewish communal life in Latvia

### Legacy

- **Long-term impact:** Decades after the Rebbe's time in Riga, the inspiration of his work was still felt in the community
- **Educational influence:** Contributed to the development of Chabad educational methodology

## Historical Context

Hodakov was part of the group of Chabad leaders who emerged during:
- The Rebbe's exile in Riga (1927-1934)
- The period of rebuilding and expansion in the 1920s-1930s
- The transition period before the Rebbe's move to Warsaw
