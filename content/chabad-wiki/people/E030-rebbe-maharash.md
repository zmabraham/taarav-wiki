# Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn (The Rebbe Maharash)

> Fourth Lubavitcher Rebbe known for bold approach to overcoming obstacles

## Overview

Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn (1834-1882), known as the Maharash (acronym for **M**oreinu **Ha**Rav **Sh**muel), was the fourth Lubavitcher Rebbe. Famous for his revolutionary approach "Lechat Chit Ara" ("straight to the heart of the matter"), he led Chabad with boldness and innovation.

## Key Facts

- **Born:** 1834 (2 Iyar 5594), Lubavitch
- **Died:** 1882 (13 Tishrei 5643)
- **Father:** Rabbi Menachem Mendel (Tzemach Tzedek)
- **Became Rebbe:** 1866 (succeeded father)
- **Age When Becoming Rebbe:** 32
- **Children:** Seven sons, including Rabbi Shalom Dovber (Rashab)

## Famous Philosophy

### Lechat Chit Ara

The Maharash's revolutionary approach:
- **Concept:** When the world says you can't go through, go over!
- **Meaning:** Don't accept barriers as final; find ways to transcend obstacles
- **Application:** Spiritual, communal, and personal challenges
- **Legacy:** Became foundational Chabad approach to challenges

## Major Achievements

### Leadership Style

- **Boldness:** Took unprecedented actions for Jewish needs
- **Innovation:** New approaches to old problems
- **Activism:** Engaged directly with Russian authorities
- **Vision:** Saw beyond conventional limitations

### Community Building

- **Expansion:** Spread Chabad influence throughout Russia
- **Institutions:** Strengthened educational and communal organizations
- **Public Presence:** Represented Jewish interests to government
- **Leadership:** Guided through period of change and challenge

### Historical Context

Led through significant events:
- **Jewish Emancipation:** Changing status of Jews in Russia
- **Haskalah Movement:** Response to Enlightenment challenges
- **Pogroms (1881):** Outbreak of anti-Jewish violence
- **Emigration:** Beginning of mass Jewish emigration

## Character

- **Energy:** Tremendous vitality and drive
- **Directness:** Straightforward, no-nonsense approach
- **Spirituality:** Deep mystical connection
- **Leadership:** Charismatic and inspiring

## Family

- **Seven Sons:** All became Torah scholars
- **Successor:** Rabbi Shalom Dovber (Rashab), second son, became fifth Rebbe
- **Youngest Son:** Rabbi Yisrael Noach (died young)
- **Grandchildren:** Including the Rayatz

## Legacy

- **13 Tishrei:** Passing commemorated annually
- **Lechat Chit Ara:** Philosophy continues to inspire Chabad approach
- **Leadership Model:** Example of bold, visionary leadership
- **Twenty Volumes:** His published teachings and writings
