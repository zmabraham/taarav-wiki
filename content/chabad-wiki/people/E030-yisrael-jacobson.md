# Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson

## Overview

Prominent Chabad chassid who served as an emissary and representative for the Rebbe in America, particularly involved in rescue and fundraising efforts during the critical period of the 1920s-1940s.

## Key Facts

- **Role:** Emissary (shaliach), fundraiser, community organizer
- **Location:** United States (New York)
- **Era:** 1920s-1940s
- **Primary association:** Sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe (Rayatz)

## Contributions

### Rescue and Advocacy

- **Visa advocacy:** Camped out in the offices of the Kramer brothers in New York, pleading for visas to help the Rebbe and others escape
- **American representation:** Represented Chabad interests in the United States during difficult times
- **Rescue efforts:** Worked to secure safe passage for Jewish individuals fleeing persecution

### Fundraising and Support

- **Financial campaigns:** Helped organize funding for the Rebbe's underground educational network and other Chabad institutions
- **Community organizing:** Worked to build support for Chabad activities in America

## Historical Context

Jacobson was active during several major crises:
- The 1927 arrest and exile of the Rayatz
- The rise of Nazi Germany
- World War II and the Holocaust
- The early years of the Rebbe's settlement in America

## Notable Activities

### New York Advocacy

- Maintained persistent presence in legal and governmental offices to secure necessary documentation
- Worked with prominent attorneys like Samuel Kramer to advocate for visas

### Communication with Europe

- Served as a critical link between American Jewish communities and the Rebbe during his exile in Riga and Warsaw
- Helped coordinate rescue efforts for European Jews
