# Likkutei Sichos

**Author:** [[authors/the-rebbe|The Rebbe]] | **Volumes:** 39 | **Total sichos:** 1193

Likkutei Sichos is the Rebbe's primary Torah work — 39 volumes of edited talks on the weekly Torah portion and Jewish holidays, covering the entire cycle of the Torah multiple times with ever-deepening analysis.
