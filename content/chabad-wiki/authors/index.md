# Authors

The Chabad dynasty spans seven generations of Rebbes, each building upon the previous:

- [[baal-shem-tov|The Baal Shem Tov (BeShT)]] (1698–1760)
- [[maggid-of-mezritch|The Maggid of Mezritch (The Great Maggid)]] (c. 1704–1772)
- [[alter-rebbe|The Alter Rebbe (Elder Rebbe) — First Chabad Rebbe]] (1745–1812)
- [[mitteler-rebbe|The Mitteler Rebbe (Middle Rebbe) — Second Chabad Rebbe]] (1773–1827)
- [[tzemach-tzedek|The Tzemach Tzedek — Third Chabad Rebbe]] (1789–1866)
- [[maharash|The Maharash — Fourth Chabad Rebbe]] (1834–1882)
- [[rashab|The Rashab — Fifth Chabad Rebbe]] (1860–1920)
- [[rayatz|The Rayatz (Frierdiker Rebbe) — Sixth Chabad Rebbe]] (1880–1950)
- [[the-rebbe|The Rebbe — Seventh Chabad Rebbe]] (1902–1994)
