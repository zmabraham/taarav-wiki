# Emperor Paul I of Russia

## Overview
Emperor Paul I (Pavel Petrovich) ruled Russia from 1796 to 1801. He played a direct role in the Alter Rebbe's first arrest and liberation in 1798-1799.

## Details
After the Alter Rebbe was arrested and brought to Petersburg in 1799 for interrogation, Emperor Paul personally reviewed the case. After the interrogations and testimony were translated into Russian and submitted to him, he wrote an order on 19 Kislev 5559 (1798):

"Did not find in the behavior of the Jews who founded the sect of the Karliners anything harmful to the state, nor any corruption or anything else that disturbs the general peace, and ordered all of them to be released."

The Alter Rebbe received his official release certificate (svobodny propusk) the following day, 20 Kislev 5559 (November 17, 1798). The certificate read: "He who has this certificate is the Jew Rabbi Zalman son of Baruch, who was here for a special purpose, and is now released to his home in Belarus, and must appear to the governor of Vitebsk, the advisory councilor Zhegulin."

Paul I was assassinated on the night of 10 Nisan 5561 (April 1801), during the Alter Rebbe's second imprisonment. His son Alexander I succeeded him and issued the final release certificate.
