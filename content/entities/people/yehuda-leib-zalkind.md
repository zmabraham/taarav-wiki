# Rabbi Yehuda Leib Zalkind

Rabbi Yehuda Leib Zalkind (died 21 Kislev 5618 / 1858, Dvinsk) was one of the greatest disciples of the Alter Rebbe, the Mitteler Rebbe, and the Tzemach Tzedek. He served as rabbi of the Chassidic community in Vilkomir and later as rabbi of Dvinsk (Daugavpils). He was known as "R' Leib Batlan" — the title "batlan" (one who leaves worldly affairs) used in that era to describe a person who abandoned temporal life and devoted himself entirely to higher spiritual matters.

## Biography

Son of Rabbi Shlomo Zalkind. He served as rabbi of the Chassidic community in Vilkomir together with Rabbi Aharon of Vilkomir until 5578 (1818), after which he served as rabbi of Dnenborg (Dvinsk) for 40 years until his death.

He was known as a man of enormous sharpness (charifus) combined with exceptional holiness and ascetic piety beyond the norm.

According to Beit Rabbi, he was a close companion and friend of the Tzemach Tzedek — they had studied together in their youth, and later maintained halachic correspondence. Another source records that the Tzemach Tzedek studied under him briefly in his youth.

His responsa were published as "Zecher Yehuda" (Berdichev 5649), with a new edition with additions published in Kfar Chabad 5751 (1991), with an introduction by Rabbi Yehoshua Mondshine.

## Connection to Chabad

As a disciple across three generations of Chabad — from the Alter Rebbe through the Tzemach Tzedek — Zalkind represents the continuity of the first generation of Chabad Chassidim. His personal relationship with the Tzemach Tzedek, both as former fellow-student and as halachic correspondent, was particularly close.
