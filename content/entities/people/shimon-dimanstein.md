## Biography

Head of the Yevsektsiya, the Jewish Section of the Soviet Communist Party, in its early years. A former yeshiva student who became a Bolshevik, he oversaw campaigns against Jewish religious life and institutions. The Yevsektsiya under his leadership targeted Chabad and other religious Jewish organizations.
