# Shlomo Freydes of Shklov

**Lived**: early 19th century, Shklov, Russia  
**Role**: Senior Chabad chassid of Shklov; witness in the Mitteler Rebbe's 1825 investigation

---

## Overview

R' Shlomo Freydes (also spelled Freidman, Fridman, or Preydes in Russian documents) was a prominent Chabad chassid of Shklov and one of the three witnesses appointed by the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] to oversee the distribution of the family relief fund in 1814. He was summoned from Shklov to Vitebsk to testify before investigators during the 1825 investigation.

The footnotes of the Masar book confirm his identity: "The famous chassid R' Shlomo Freydes (see Beis Rebbi, 71a)." He appears in the documents variously as "Preydin," "Fridman," and "Shlomo Freidman, merchant of Mogilev, resident of Shklov."

---

## Role in the 1825 Investigation

R' Shlomo, like [[entities/people/zalman-slonim-shklov|R' Zalman Reizes Slonim]], was brought from Shklov to testify as a non-accused witness. He was described as age 43, married with children.

His recorded testimony states:

- The Rebbe arrived in Shklov sometime after the French were driven out and likely solicited support from local residents for his expenses.
- He probably did the same in other places along his route from Kremenchug to Shklov.
- Freydes heard that residents of Shklov and Lubavitch had committed to giving funds that the Rebbe would distribute to relatives and keep the larger share for himself.
- He was not aware of the specific distribution document shown to the governor.
- He admitted it is possible that people regularly support the Rebbe financially; he himself gives 15–25 rubles at such assemblies.
- The Rebbe may approach Jews himself or use intermediaries for these collections.

He signed: "Shlomo Fridman, merchant of Mogilev, resident of Shklov."

---

## Note on Names

The document notes that in the original distribution letter, R' Shlomo Freydes was named alongside R' Pinchas Shik and R' Zalman Reizes as a witness. Because [[entities/people/pinchas-shik|R' Pinchas Shik]] had died before the investigation, the remaining two witnesses — Zalman and Shlomo — were called to testify.

The Mitteler Rebbe's own answers noted he had appointed R' Shlomo as a witness "for the future" but that R' Shlomo "was sick at the time" of the actual distribution and may not have been present.
