# Polkovnik Loginov

**Active**: 1825, Vitebsk gubernia, Russia  
**Role**: Russian military officer (Polkovnik = Colonel); one of Governor Khavanski's two primary investigating officers in the 1825 Mitteler Rebbe case

---

## Overview

Polkovnik (Colonel) Loginov was one of the two senior officers dispatched by [[entities/people/governor-khavanski|Governor-General Prince Khavanski]] in October 1825 to investigate the denunciations filed by [[entities/people/simcha-kissin|Simcha Kissin]] against [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Rabbi DovBer Schneursohn]] (the Mitteler Rebbe). He worked alongside [[entities/people/markov-investigator|Court Counselor Markov]] and a third officer, [[entities/people/morozov-investigator|Major Morozov]].

---

## The Investigation

The investigation order of 2 October 1825 directed Loginov, Markov, and Morozov to travel from Vitebsk to Lubavitch, then through Babinovich, Shklov, and back to Vitebsk, questioning witnesses along the way.

Loginov and Markov appear together throughout the documents as the primary interrogating pair. They conducted the initial interrogation of the Mitteler Rebbe in Vitebsk (5–9 October 1825), questioning him about:
- The Hebrew distribution document seized from the estate of the late [[entities/people/pinchas-shik|Pinchas Shik]]
- The nature and amounts of the chassidic financial support system
- The alleged illegal arbitration courts (beit din) conducted by the Rebbe

They also summoned [[entities/people/zalman-slonim-shklov|R' Zalman Reizes Slonim]] and [[entities/people/shlomo-freydes-shklov|R' Shlomo Freydes]] from Shklov to Vitebsk for witness testimony.

The joint report by Loginov and Markov was submitted to Governor Khavanski by 17 November 1825 (document number 5850), which led to the transfer of the case to the Mogilev gubernia court system.

---

## Status and Role

Loginov, as a Polkovnik (full colonel), was senior in rank to Markov (a court counselor) and Morozov (a major). He appears in the official summary documents as a trusted officer of the governor's special missions staff.
