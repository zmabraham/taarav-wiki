# R' Chaim DovBer Vilnski

**Chabad Chassid and mashpia associated with the Kremenchug community; disciple of the earlier Chabad masters**

## Biography

R' Chaim DovBer Vilnski was a significant Chabad Chassid and mashpia active during the eras of the Mitteler Rebbe and the Tzemach Tzedek. He was associated with the Kremenchug community in the Poltava region of Ukraine, one of the most important Chabad centers in southern Russia.

Kremenchug was home to a major Chabad concentration, and figures like R' Chaim DovBer played essential roles in maintaining the spiritual life of the community between visits from the Rebbe's court. The mashpia served as the primary ongoing spiritual guide for Chassidim in a given city, teaching Chassidus, conducting farbrengens (Chassidic gatherings), and providing individual spiritual direction.

R' Chaim DovBer is identified in Toldos Chabad BeRussia as part of the chain of Chabad teachers in the Kremenchug region, where he influenced subsequent generations of Chassidim including R' Baruch Tamrish and through him R' Asher Grotsman of Nikolaev.

## Significance

R' Chaim DovBer Vilnski illustrates the geographic spread of Chabad into the Poltava and Kherson regions of Ukraine during the middle period of Chabad history. His role in Kremenchug as a transmitter of Chassidus connects the earlier Chabad masters to the figures of the late 19th century. The chain from R' Chaim DovBer to R' Baruch Tamrish to R' Asher Grotsman represents a documented three-generation transmission of Chabad spiritual direction in southern Russia.
