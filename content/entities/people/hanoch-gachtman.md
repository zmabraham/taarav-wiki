# Rabbi Hanoch Gachtman

Rabbi Hanoch Gachtman (born 18 Av 5744 / 1984, Haifa) is a Chabad shaliach in Gedera and a researcher of Chassidic and Soviet-era Jewish history. He is known for his work at Yad L'Achim's anti-missionary division and for stepping in to serve as temporary shaliach in Mumbai following the 2008 terrorist attack.

## Biography

Born in Haifa, Rabbi Gachtman was appointed as shaliach in Gedera and simultaneously headed the anti-missionary division at Yad L'Achim, the organization dedicated to combating missionary activity among Israeli Jews.

Following the devastating terrorist attack at the Mumbai Chabad House on 26 Cheshvan 5769 (November 2008), in which shluchim Rabbi Gavriel and Rebbetzin Rivka Holtzberg were murdered along with other victims, Rabbi Gachtman flew with his family to Mumbai to temporarily serve the Jewish community there while permanent replacements were arranged.

He has devoted significant scholarly effort to researching Jewish and Chassidic history, with a particular focus on the Soviet era and the Holocaust period.

In 5780 (2020), he received the "Yakir ha'Ir Gedera" (Honored Citizen of Gedera) award in recognition of his community service during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Connection to Chabad

Rabbi Gachtman's willingness to uproot his family and travel to Mumbai in the immediate aftermath of the attack exemplifies Chabad's commitment to maintaining shluchim posts even in the face of tragedy. His research activities contribute to preserving the history of Chabad in challenging environments.
