# Chava Bilienky

Maternal grandmother of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin; wife of R. Yisrael Bilienky (shochet of Dashov). Mother of Leah Beila (the author's mother) and at least six other children.

## Description
When her husband R. Yisrael Bilienky died young, Chava was left a widow with seven children. The oldest, a son, was eighteen and was appointed shochet in Dashov, supporting the family from that income. Leah Beila was eventually married off to R. Efraim Shmuel Levin of Zhivotov.
