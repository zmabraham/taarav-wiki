# R' Shmuel Yitzchak Luria (ר' שמואל יצחק לוריא)

**Chairman of the Homel Jewish community during Israel Jacobson's residence there (1920–1925).**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Chairman/head of the Homel Jewish community |
| **Period** | ~5680–5685 (1920–1925) during Jacobson's Homel years |
| **City** | Homel (Gomel), Belarus |

## Overview

R' Shmuel Yitzchak Luria was the chairman of the organized Jewish community in Homel during the early Soviet period. He worked with Israel Jacobson in communal matters and in the founding of the local Chabad yeshiva. His role as community chairman during the difficult early Soviet years required navigating between maintaining Jewish life and the pressures of the new regime.
