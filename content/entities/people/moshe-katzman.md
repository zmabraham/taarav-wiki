# Moshe Katzman

A Chabad chassid who arrived in Samarkand during WWII with his wife and two sons, Zelig and Hillel. Both he and his wife died in the street from disease during the terrible epidemic that struck Samarkand in winter 5702 (1941-42), leaving their two sons as orphans.
