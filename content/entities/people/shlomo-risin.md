# R' Shlomo Risin (ר' שלמה ריסין)

**Mashgiach on Chassidus in the large hall of Tomchei Temimim; later rabbi in Avaravichy near Homel.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Rogatchev |
| **Role** | Mashgiach on Chassidus in the Ulam Gadol (large hall) at TT |
| **Later role** | Rabbi in Avaravichy, near Homel |
| **Called** | "Shlomo ben Dovid" (his father was named Dovid) |

## Overview

R' Shlomo Risin from Rogatchev was the mashgiach on Chassidus (Supervisor of Chasidic studies) in the large hall of TT when Israel Jacobson first arrived in 5668 (1908). He had a gentle temperament (described as "ish rach hateva" — a gentle-natured man).

He was later replaced in this role by R' Elchanan Dov from Rozov mid-winter of 5668.

He subsequently became a rabbi in the town of Avaravichy, near Homel — which means he would have been in the same region as Israel Jacobson when Jacobson lived in Homel (5680–5685).
