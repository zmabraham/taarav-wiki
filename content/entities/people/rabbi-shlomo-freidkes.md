# Rabbi Shlomo Freidkes (of Shklov)

## Overview
Rabbi Shlomo Freidkes was one of the leading Chassidim of the Alter Rebbe, based in Shklov. He was a renowned recorder of the Alter Rebbe's discourses and possessed a large collection of manuscripts.

## Details
He was among the prominent (muflagim) disciples of the Alter Rebbe. He possessed an extensive collection of manuscript notebooks — approximately forty volumes — containing the Alter Rebbe's discourses that he had meticulously recorded.

These forty notebooks were later transferred to the Tzemach Tzedek on the occasion of the marriage of the Tzemach Tzedek's son Rabbi Yehuda Leib to the Freidkes family in 1825. Beit Rebbi describes this as the dowry (nedunyah) — the entire collection was passed to the Tzemach Tzedek, who then corrected and annotated them extensively.

Sadly, most of these notebooks were later destroyed in the great fire of Lubavitch. One discourse with the Tzemach Tzedek's corrections was later printed in Or HaTorah.

The Mitteler Rebbe also wrote to Rabbi Shlomo Freidkes requesting manuscript copies of the Alter Rebbe's discourses for the preparation of the Siddur im Da"ch (1822) and the Biurei HaZohar.
