# Rabbi Mordechai Chafetz

Chabad activist in Riga, Latvia. Key coordinator of wartime relief efforts for Jews trapped in Nazi-occupied Europe.

## Relief Coordination

During WWII, R' Mordechai Chafetz served in Riga as the primary local coordinator for the [[entities/organizations/vaad-pidyon-shvuyim]] (Committee for Captive Redemption), the relief fund established by [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) upon his arrival in the USA.

R' Chafetz coordinated:
- Distribution of funds to Jews in need
- Facilitation of currency transfers between Riga and the USA through a currency exchange arrangement
- Worked together with R' Yehoshua ben R' Kalman Mizrach, a wealthy man who helped transfer funds

Food packages were also sent via Lisbon to those in need.
