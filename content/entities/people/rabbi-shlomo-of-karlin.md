# Rabbi Shlomo of Karlin

## Overview
Rabbi Shlomo of Karlin was a major disciple of the Maggid of Mezeritch who served as the Rebbe of Karlin from 1772 until he was expelled to Ludmir (Volhynia) by the Mitnagdim around 1784. He was killed in Ludmir in 1792.

## Details
He led the Karlin Chassidic dynasty, which was one of the major Chassidic movements in Belarus. The Mitnagdim succeeded in expelling him from Karlin, and he relocated to Ludmir in Volhynia.

He sought the Alter Rebbe's agreement to settle in Byeshinkowitz (a town the Alter Rebbe had passed through after returning from Mogilev). The Alter Rebbe agreed on three conditions, but Rabbi Shlomo only accepted two of them. The third condition — that he should not teach that the Tzaddik must "carry" the flock (i.e., that followers should not rely entirely on the Tzaddik) — was rejected. This disagreement reflects a fundamental difference between the Karlin approach and Chabad's emphasis on individual spiritual effort.

After his expulsion from Belarus to Volhynia, he no longer competed with the Alter Rebbe for leadership of Chassidim in Belarus.

He was killed in Ludmir in 1792 (5552), as noted in the book's account of the post-release period.
