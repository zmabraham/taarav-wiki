# R. Yisrael Noach Belenetsky

A senior Chabad elder in **[[samarkand|Samarkand]]** during the WWII period. He is described in the memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* as one of the "elder Chassidim" (*di eltereh chassidim*) in Samarkand, whose authority guided community decisions.

## Role in Samarkand

During WWII, [[samarkand|Samarkand]] (in Uzbekistan, Central Asia) became a gathering point for thousands of Jewish refugees who fled eastward from the advancing German army. Among these were many Chabad Chassidim. R. Yisrael Noach Belenetsky was one of the established elders of this refugee community.

## The Decision to Leave (c. 5706/1946)

The memoir describes a pivotal community gathering in the summer of 5706, concerning whether Chassidim should emigrate westward (to Poland and then beyond) using Polish passports. The debate took place in the courtyard of R. Yisrael Noach's dwelling — indicating his home was a community gathering place. He sat at a table together with [[nachum-shmarya-sasankin|R. Nachum Shmarya Sasankin]] (the community's *mara d'atra*, local rabbinic authority) to discuss the decision.

The decision reached was: those who had already begun traveling should continue, but new travelers should not start. This guidance structured the community's response to the emigration question.
