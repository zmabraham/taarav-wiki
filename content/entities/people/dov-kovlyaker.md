# Dov Kovlyaker (Levertov)

A Chabad chassid, also known by the name "Levertov" (apparently his place of origin). Mentioned in the memoir at Simchas Torah 5696 (1935) in Moscow, where he said that the NKVD had already called him in and he did not know if he would be taken. He was subsequently arrested in Moscow and died in prison. His son Sholom Levertov later came to Samarkand.
