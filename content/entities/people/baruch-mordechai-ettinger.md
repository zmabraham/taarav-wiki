# R' Baruch Mordechai Ettinger — ר' ברוך מרדכי עטינגר

**Rabbi of the Vilna Chabad Minyan; Later Served in Bobroisk**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Baruch Mordechai Ettinger
**Dates**: Late 18th–early 19th century
**Positions**: Rabbi of the Chabad minyan in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]]; later rabbi in Bobroisk
**Affiliation**: Early Chabad-Lubavitch

---

## Overview

Rabbi Baruch Mordechai Ettinger was one of the early rabbinical leaders of the Chabad community in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]], serving as the rabbi of the Chabad minyan there during the period of intense Mitnagdic opposition. His name "Ettinger" connects him to a well-known rabbinical family. He later moved to serve in Bobroisk (in present-day Belarus), a town with a significant Chabad community.

---

## Background

### The Ettinger Family

The Ettinger family name was associated with distinguished rabbinical scholarship in the region:

- **Rabbinical family**: Connected to the broader Ettinger rabbinical dynasty
- **Chabad affiliation**: Despite coming from or marrying into a distinguished family, he aligned with the Chassidic movement
- **Early period**: Active in the first generation of Chabad in Vilna

---

## Service in Vilna

### Rabbi of the Chabad Minyan

R' Baruch Mordechai served as the formal rabbinical authority for the Vilna Chabad minyan:

- **Halachic authority**: Provided rabbinical rulings according to Chabad tradition
- **Leading prayers**: Supervised the Nusach Ari prayers
- **Community management**: Helped manage the affairs of the small Chabad community
- **Distinction from lay leadership**: While [[entities/people/meir-raphael-yafeh|R' Meir ben Rephael Yafeh]] led the community as rosh (head), R' Baruch Mordechai served specifically as the rabbinical authority

### Under Persecution

His service in Vilna coincided with the most intense period of Mitnagdic opposition:

- **Vilna Gaon's era**: The Gaon was alive and active in opposing Chassidism
- **Communal pressure**: The Chabad community faced constant social and religious pressure
- **Courage**: Serving openly as rabbi of a Chabad minyan in Vilna required real courage

---

## Move to Bobroisk

After his Vilna service, R' Baruch Mordechai Ettinger moved to serve in Bobroisk:

- **Bobroisk**: A city in present-day Belarus with a significant Chabad community
- **Chabad stronghold**: Bobroisk (Babruysk) was one of the more receptive areas for Chabad, unlike Vilna
- **New chapter**: His move represented a transition from hostile Mitnagdic territory to more welcoming ground
- **Continued service**: Continued his rabbinical work in the Chabad tradition

---

## Legacy

R' Baruch Mordechai Ettinger represents the early Chabad rabbis who provided formal authority to the nascent Chassidic communities in hostile Lithuanian territory. His willingness to serve openly in Vilna — the center of opposition — was a statement of religious conviction.
