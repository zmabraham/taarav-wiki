# Rabbi Yitzchak Luria (Arizal)

Rabbi Yitzchak Luria Ashkenazi (1534–1572), known as the Arizal or Ari Hakadosh, was the foremost kabbalist of Tzfat. His teachings form the basis of Lurianic Kabbalah, which deeply influenced Chabad's intellectual approach to Chassidus.

## Connection to Chabad

Chabad Chassidus, while developing its own distinct path, builds on the Arizal's framework of tzimtzum, sefirot, and the purpose of creation. The Alter Rebbe's Tanya and subsequent Chabad texts engage extensively with Lurianic concepts.
