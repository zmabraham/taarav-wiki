# Rabbi Baruch Portugali-Batlan

Rabbi Baruch Portugali-Batlan was the father of the Rebbetzin Rachel, who was the maternal grandmother of the Alter Rebbe (Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi). His story appears in the Sefer HaZikronot (Book of Memoirs).

## Biography

His family name was "Portugali," indicating descent from Jews exiled from Portugal. The nickname "Batlan" was given as a compliment — meaning that all the city's prominent and honored citizens would subordinate themselves ("mevatlim") before him, owing to his elevated character and noble personality.

The study of Kabbalah formed an inseparable part of the traditional learning among Portuguese exile communities, and Rabbi Baruch was raised accordingly.

After his marriage he established a gemilut chasadim (charitable loan) fund and would lend to merchants without any personal benefit. Beyond monetary charity, he also performed acts of kindness with his person, laboring for others' welfare.

He was educated by students of Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem of Worms, and he himself became a follower of Rabbi Yoel, the Baal Shem of Zamoshti, and was the leader of the group of Rabbi Yoel's Chassidim in Pozna.

He never did anything — in business or in his life and home — without consulting his teacher, the Tzaddik Rabbi Yoel Baal Shem.

Of the nine children he had, seven died young. Two survived and both merited old age. This was because when Rabbi Yoel Baal Shem once visited Pozna, Rabbi Baruch brought his two surviving children, Rachel and Binyamin, before him, and the Baal Shem blessed them with long life — a blessing both fulfilled.

Rabbi Baruch taught: "It is good to be withdrawn from people while being among them" — meaning one should walk the middle path, not isolated from others, but also not frivolous; engaged with people as needed, while maintaining inward detachment.

## Connection to Chabad

Through his daughter (or granddaughter) Rebbetzin Rachel, he is a direct ancestor of the Alter Rebbe, founder of Chabad Chassidus.
