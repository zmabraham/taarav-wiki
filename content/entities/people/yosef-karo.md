# Rabbi Yosef Karo (Beit Yosef)

Rabbi Yosef Karo, known as "Maran" and "the Beit Yosef" (after his great halachic work), and "HaMechaber" (the Author, referring to the Shulchan Aruch), was one of the greatest halachic decisors in Jewish history. He is primarily known for composing the Shulchan Aruch, which became the foundational code of Jewish law for all communities for the past 500 years.

## Biography

Born in Spain to Rabbi Ephraim Karo. His family was expelled with the Spanish Jews in 1492, eventually settling in Istanbul after brief stays in Portugal and other locations. After his father's death, he lived with his uncle Rabbi Yitzchak Karo.

He settled in Nikopol (Bulgaria) where he opened a yeshiva and began his monumental commentary on the Tur, known as the Beit Yosef. He received Torah from a heavenly Maggid (divine teacher) who instructed him to move to Eretz Yisrael. Upon arriving in Tzfat, he became a disciple of Rabbi Yaakov Berav and was the first of four scholars to receive renewed semicha (rabbinic ordination).

In Tzfat he also had a close relationship with the Ramak (Rabbi Moshe Cordovero) — serving as his teacher in revealed Torah while being his student in Kabbalah — and with Rabbi Shlomo Alkabetz. The ARI (Rabbi Yitzchak Luria) was his son-in-law.

## Major Works
- **Beit Yosef** — exhaustive commentary on the Tur, surveying all halachic opinions
- **Shulchan Aruch** — concise halachic code, the standard reference for Sephardic (and much of general) Jewish practice
- **Kesef Mishneh** — commentary on Maimonides' Mishneh Torah, answering the Raavad's objections
- **Maggid Meisharim** — Kabbalistic teachings received from his heavenly teacher
- **Avkat Rochel** — responsa

## Connection to Chabad
The Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch (Shulchan Aruch HaRav) is based on the Beit Yosef. The Alter Rebbe cites the Beit Yosef hundreds of times. The Tzemach Tzedek likewise references him extensively. The Rebbe stated: "By their light [the Beit Yosef and the Rema] we shall walk until Mashiach."
