# Rabbi Baruch Moyazin

A hidden tzaddik (nistar) who became a student of the Baal Shem Tov. His story appears in the Sefer HaZichronot (Book of Memoirs) of the Frierdiker Rebbe.

## Biography

Born in Ilya near Vilna, Baruch was recognized as a prodigy from youth. He studied at the yeshiva in Slutsk where he was nicknamed "the silent one" (the Shtikel). He adopted the practice of not benefiting from others' charity, working for his living.

After wandering through Vilna and Viazyn (where the yeshiva of Rabbi Shaul was located), he worked for a peasant named Eliyahu whom he discovered to be a hidden Torah scholar. He traveled to Brisk (Brest-Litovsk) to study with Rabbi Shlomo and became part of his inner circle.

In the town of Smila he worked as a woodchopper while studying, where he met Rabbi Nachum, a hidden tzaddik. Rabbi Nachum arranged his marriage to the orphaned daughter of Rabbi Yosef Buna the stove-builder (also a hidden tzaddik). After becoming wealthy in business, Baruch built a study hall near Smila with ten permanent Torah scholars whom he supported.

After fifteen years he moved to Slutsk, where he established another academy with the goal of reducing opposition to the hidden tzaddikim (the nistarim). When Rabbi Adam Baal Shem (head of the hidden tzaddikim) visited Slutsk, the opposition had largely dissipated.

At age 76, when the Baal Shem Tov was appointed leader of the nistarim, Baruch traveled to meet him in Tlust, where the Baal Shem Tov told him to immigrate to the Land of Israel. He did so and fulfilled various missions there for the Baal Shem Tov.

His sons Rabbi Yosef and Rabbi Aharon continued his work in Slutsk and invited the Baal Shem Tov to visit there. Among those who came to see the Baal Shem Tov during that visit was Rabbi Nissan the teacher from Harki, whose son Rabbi Yitzchak Shaul told this story to Baruch — the father of the Alter Rebbe (Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi).
