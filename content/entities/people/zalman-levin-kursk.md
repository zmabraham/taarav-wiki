# Zalman Levin (of Kursk)

Zalman Levin of Kursk was a Chabad yeshiva student who studied together with R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin in the underground yeshiva in Kyiv. He is mentioned as a study companion during the period when the author was learning in Kyiv.

## Description
Zalman Levin of Kursk was among the group of Chabad yeshiva students learning in the underground network in Kyiv in the late 1920s/early 1930s. The Zichronos memoir mentions him as a chavrusah (study partner) or fellow student during this period. Like other students in the underground network, he navigated the dual dangers of Soviet anti-religious enforcement and the need to maintain Torah study.
