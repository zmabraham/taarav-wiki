# Attorney Asher Rabinowitz

American attorney involved in the diplomatic effort to rescue [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) from Nazi-occupied Warsaw in late 1939.

## Rescue Efforts

Together with Attorney Sam Kremer, R' Rabinowitz worked in the United States to obtain diplomatic and political support for Rayatz's rescue. He helped mobilize US government officials, politicians, and judges for the effort.
