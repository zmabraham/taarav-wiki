# Moshe Aharon Geisinsky

A younger Chabad student in the Kyiv underground, educated by R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin and his colleagues. He was detained by the NKVD in the raid on the Kyiv synagogue. He later wrote his own memoirs of this period, published in "Kfar Chabad" journal no. 379 (28 Nisan 5749 / 1989), pp. 16ff.
