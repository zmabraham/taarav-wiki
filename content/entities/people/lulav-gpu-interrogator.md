## Biography

Soviet GPU interrogator who conducted sessions with Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak during his imprisonment. During one famous exchange, when Lulav threatened the Rebbe with a pistol, the Rebbe gave his celebrated response about one who has two worlds fearing nothing. Lulav reportedly converted to Judaism after his encounters with the Rebbe.
