# Rabbi Yaakov Rosenheim

Rabbi Yaakov Rosenheim was a leader of Agudas Yisrael who corresponded with the Rashab on matters of Orthodox Jewish policy. He was involved in inter-Orthodox diplomacy between the Chassidic and non-Chassidic worlds.
