# Rabbi Yehuda Leib, Great-Grandson of the Maharal

Rabbi Yehuda Leib (5371–5464 / 1611–1704) was the son of Rabbi Shmuel, son of Rabbi Betzalel Charif, son of the Maharal of Prague. Through his son Rabbi Moshe of Pozna, he is the ancestor of the Alter Rebbe's family, making him a notable figure in the genealogy of Chabad.

## Biography

Born in Prague to Rabbi Shmuel, showing exceptional gifts from childhood and becoming a great Torah scholar. He studied under Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem of Worms, who had settled in Prague and opened a branch of his yeshiva there.

He lost two wives who died leaving young children. After his father (who had served as community head in Prague) died, the community elected him to succeed his father, but he declined. He married Sara, daughter of Rabbi Chaim of Pozna, and in 5420 (1660) moved to Pozna where their son Moshe was born. In Pozna he engaged in Torah discussions with the scholars of the city and served as Av Beit Din.

He died in Tishrei 5464 at age 93, and was buried in Prague.

His son Rabbi Moshe of Pozna was the great-great-grandfather of the Alter Rebbe — providing the direct lineage from the Maharal to the Alter Rebbe.

## Connection to Chabad

Rabbi Yehuda Leib is notable as a link in the chain connecting the Maharal of Prague to the Alter Rebbe. The Frierdiker Rebbe's Memoirs (Sefer HaZikronot) document this lineage, placing Rabbi Yehuda Leib in the spiritual genealogy of Chabad leadership.
