# Alexander Kirk

US Ambassador to Berlin during 1939–1940. Played a key role in facilitating the transit of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) through Germany from Warsaw to Riga.

## Role in Rescue

Kirk coordinated with Latvian envoy Alfred Bilmanis and US State Department officials ([[entities/people/robert-pell]], [[entities/people/cordell-hull]]) to arrange Rayatz's safe passage through Berlin.
