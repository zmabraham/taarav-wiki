# Baal Shem Tov (Rabbi Yisrael ben Eliezer)

Founder of the Hasidic movement and head of the hidden tzaddikim (anusim). Born 18 Elul 1697 in Tlust (Talost), passed away 6 Sivan 1760 (first day of Shavuot) in Medzhybizh (Mezhibuzh). Considered the spiritual progenitor of all Hasidic dynasties, including Chabad.

## Biography

Rabbi Yisrael was orphaned young; his father Eliezer's final words to him were "Fear nothing but God and love every Jew unconditionally." As a youth he secretly joined the circle of hidden tzaddikim under Rabbi Adam Baal Shem of Ropshitz. At age 14 he entered the secret society and became its leader at 18.

He was revealed publicly in Tlust on 17 Elul 5494 (1734), at age 36, at the instruction of his teacher Achiyah HaShiloni. He traveled through Horodenka, Kitov, Nemirov, Sharigrad, Polnoe, Zaslav, and Berditchev healing the sick, redeeming captives, and spreading Torah.

In 1740 (5500) he moved to Medzhybizh, which became the center of the Hasidic movement. Thousands of disciples gathered around him, including great Torah scholars.

### Ascent to the Heavenly Palace (Rosh Hashanah 5507)
The Baal Shem Tov described an ascent of his soul to the heavens where he met the Messiah. When he asked when the Messiah would come, the answer was "when your wellsprings are spread outward" — making the dissemination of Hasidic teachings central to Messianic redemption.

### Victory over the Frankists (1759)
On 26 Tammuz 5519, the Baal Shem Tov defeated the Frankist sect in Lvov (Lemberg). He and his disciples declared this a festive day.

## Teachings and Philosophy

- **Divine Providence (Hashgacha Pratit)**: Even a falling leaf reflects divine supervision
- **Power of thought**: "Where a person's thoughts are, there the person is entirely"
- **Elevation of evil**: No true evil exists — apparent evil serves as a throne for good
- **Love of simple Jews**: Emphasized that a simple Jew's sincere blessing has value equal to the devotions of great tzaddikim

- Rabbi Pinchas of Koritz
- Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev

## Family
- Son Rabbi Tzvi
- Daughter Adel (mother of Rabbi Moshe Chaim Ephraim of Sudilkov and Rabbi Baruch of Medzhybizh)
- Great-great-grandson Rabbi Nachman of Breslov (through daughter Adel)

## Chabad Connection
The Alter Rebbe (Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi) was a spiritual grandson of the Baal Shem Tov through the Maggid of Mezeritch. Chabad traces its lineage directly to the Baal Shem Tov. The Rebbe (Menachem Mendel Schneerson) frequently quoted the Baal Shem Tov in his teachings.
