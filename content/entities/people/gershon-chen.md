# R' Gershon Chen (ר' גרשון חן)

**Tomchei Temimim student; later Chabad activist; relative and mentor to Israel Jacobson.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Senior student at TT who supervised young Jacobson |
| **Family connection** | His father's mother and Jacobson's mother's uncle R' Baruch Meir Malkav married his mother's sister from Rogatchev |
| **Brothers** | R' Peretz Chen, R' Gershon Chen, R' Yosef Chen |

## Overview

When Israel Jacobson's father brought him to Lubavitch as a young boy (around age 12), he entrusted him to R' Gershon Chen for supervision and guidance, owing to their family connection.

Jacobson recalls one winter episode: he fell ill (caught a cold), and went to R' Gershon for help. R' Gershon took him to the student R' Yaakov Zecharya Moskalik, who told him: "Yisrael is sick? Go home, drink two cups of tea with milk and sugar, then go to sleep."

R' Gershon's brother R' Yosef Chen later settled in Eretz Yisrael and wrote to Jacobson: "I am Yosef son of R' Dovid Chen from Rogatchev... I studied together with you in TT in Lubavitch. I am the brother of Gershon Chen, rabbi in Alexandria."
