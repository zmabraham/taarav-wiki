# Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson

Seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe (1902–1994). Son-in-law of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz), married to [[entities/people/chaya-mushka-schneersohn]].

## Escape from Europe (1941)

During WWII, R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson and Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka were in France. As Germany occupied France and the Vichy regime controlled the south, their situation became increasingly dangerous.

They escaped from Nice/Vichy France to Lisbon, Portugal, and from there boarded the ship **"Serpa Pinta"** to the United States. They arrived in New York on **28 Sivan 5701** (summer 1941).

## Leadership

After the passing of Rayatz on 10 Shevat 5710 (1950), R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson became the Seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe. He led the movement from 770 Eastern Parkway, Brooklyn, until his passing on 3 Tammuz 5754 (1994).
