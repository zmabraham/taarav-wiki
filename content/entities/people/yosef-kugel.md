# R' Yosef Kugel of Pleshchenitsy

**Distinguished Chabad Chassid of Pleshchenitsy; elder brother of R' Hendl (Chanoch) Kugel; described as an even greater Chassid than his famous brother**

## Biography

R' Yosef Kugel was a prominent Chabad Chassid who lived in Pleshchenitsy (Pleszczenice) in the Minsk gubernia of White Russia. He was the older brother of R' Chanoch Hendl Kugel, who later became famous as a mashpia in Lubavitch. Remarkably, those who knew them personally described R' Yosef as having been even greater in Chassidus than his more celebrated younger brother R' Hendl.

R' Yosef was part of the remarkable Chassidic circle in Pleshchenitsy that included the Razenboim family and later R' Avraham Chaim Razenboim. The R' Shmuel Dov Ber of Borisov (a significant Chabad Chassid) corresponded with R' Yosef and sent him personal greetings in letters, indicating his stature.

R' Yosef died sometime around 1876 (Adar 5636), at which point R' Shmuel Dov Ber of Borisov wrote: "My heart melted within me and I remained as if bewildered... woe and alas, sorrow upon sorrow..." — a poignant expression of the loss felt among Chabad Chassidim at his passing.

## Significance

R' Yosef Kugel represents the extraordinary concentration of Chassidic greatness found in the small town of Pleshchenitsy. The fact that he is described as surpassing even his famous brother R' Hendl in Chassidic attainment — yet left almost no written record — illustrates how much of Chabad's true spiritual wealth existed in the oral and personal realms, transmitted through living example rather than books. His early death meant that the mantle of leadership passed to his son R' Gershon Leib and then to R' Hendl.
