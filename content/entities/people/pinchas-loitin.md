# Pinchas Loitin

A Chabad chassid (of blessed memory) who became the author's business partner in Samarkand. They jointly purchased a wooden weaving machine (stanok) and worked together in their living quarters to produce woven goods. He is mentioned with the notation "ע"ה" indicating he has passed away.
