# R. Betzalel Vilshansky

A Chabad Chassid and **shochet** (ritual slaughterer) who worked in **Paris** after World War II alongside the narrator of *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu*. He worked at the **Pletzel** — the Jewish quarter market in Paris — providing kosher-slaughtered meat to the post-war Jewish refugee community.

## Work at the Pletzel

The Pletzel (פלעצעל — "little square") in Paris's 4th arrondissement was the historic Jewish market in the Marais district. After the war, it resumed as a center of Jewish commercial and community life. R. Betzalel and the narrator worked there as shochtim (ritual slaughterers), providing an essential religious service to the Jewish community.

## Post-War Paris

During the summer of 5707 (1947), Paris hosted a large community of Jewish refugees and Holocaust survivors, many awaiting immigration documents for America, Israel, or other destinations. The Chabad presence in Paris included the [[lishka-le-refugees|Lishka leRefugees]] (Refugee Office) under [[binyamin-gorodetsky|R. Binyamin Gorodetsky]].

## Family Connection

R. Betzalel is likely related to [[refael-vilshansky|R. Refael Vilshansky]], who served as Secretary of the Lishka leRefugees in Paris — both sharing the Vilshansky surname.
