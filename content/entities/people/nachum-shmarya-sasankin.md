# R. Nachum Shmarya Sasankin

The **Mara d'Atra** (local rabbinic authority) of the Chabad refugee community in **[[samarkand|Samarkand]]** during World War II. He served as the de facto spiritual leader of the Chabad Chassidim who gathered in Samarkand during the wartime period (5701–5707 / 1941–1947).

## Role in Samarkand

When thousands of Jews fled eastward to Central Asia during WWII, Samarkand became a major refugee center. Among the Chabad community there, R. Nachum Shmarya Sasankin served as the rabbinic authority, helping resolve halachic questions and providing community guidance.

## The Emigration Decision

The memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* describes a critical community gathering (c. 5706/1946) on the question of whether Chassidim should emigrate to Poland and beyond. R. Nachum Shmarya sat at the decision-making table alongside [[yisrael-noach-belenetsky|R. Yisrael Noach Belenetsky]] and others. The assembled elders reached the ruling that new emigrants should not begin the journey, but those already traveling should continue.

## Community Structure

The Samarkand Chabad community had a clear internal structure even in exile:
- **Mara d'Atra**: R. Nachum Shmarya Sasankin
- **Senior elders**: R. [[yisrael-noach-belenetsky|Yisrael Noach Belenetsky]], [[nissan-nemenov|R. Nissan Nemenov]] (mashpia)
- **Community members**: Hundreds of Chabad families
