# Sholom Dov Ber Levin

Son of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin (the memoir author) and Ita Dvora Levin. He is a Chabad historian and authored "Toldos Chabad B'Russia HaSovietit" (Kehot, 5749/1989), in which portions of his father's memoirs were first published. He edited and prepared his father's complete memoirs for publication, with the assistance of his own son Yosef Yitzchak Keller.

## Description
The memoir's introduction notes that Sholom Dov Ber first published excerpts from his father's memoirs in "Toldos Chabad B'Russia HaSovietit." He then received the complete memoirs in summer 5755 (1995) and, together with his son Yosef Yitzchak Keller, prepared them beautifully for publication. The author expresses deep gratitude to both of them.
