# Judge Louis Brandeis

Former US Supreme Court Justice (retired by 1939) who was among the prominent American figures who intervened in the rescue of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) from Nazi Warsaw in 1939–1940.
