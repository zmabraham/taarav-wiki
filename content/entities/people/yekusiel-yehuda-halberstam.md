# Rabbi Yekusiel Yehuda Halberstam (Klausenberger Rebbe)

Rabbi Yekusiel Yehuda Halberstam (4 Shevat 5665 – 9 Tammuz 5754 / 1905–1994) was the first Rebbe of the Sanz-Klausenburg Chassidus. He led the Chassidus from 5706 (1946) until his death in 5754 (1994). A Holocaust survivor who lost his wife and ten children at Auschwitz, he became a symbol of Jewish rebuilding from destruction. He founded Kiryat Sanz in Netanya and the Laniado Medical Center.

## Biography

A child prodigy who knew the entire Talmud by age 13. He studied primarily under "the Rav from Dokla" and others, and became deeply immersed in the Sanz Chassidic tradition. He married a relative of the Satmar Rebbe's family.

At age 21 he was appointed rabbi of the "Sephardic" (Chassidic) community of Klausenburg. After the Nazi invasion of Hungary in 5704 (1944), he was sent to forced labor. His wife and ten of his eleven children were murdered at Auschwitz.

After the Holocaust he stayed in the Fernwald DP camp and began establishing Torah institutions ("Sheiris HaPleita") in DP camps. In 5706 (1946) he came to America to raise funds and established institutions in Mexico and New York for Holocaust survivors. He established schools throughout Israel (Safed, Be'er Sheva, Kfar Ozer, and others) and a yeshiva in Williamsburg.

He established a new family with Chaya Nechama (daughter of Rabbi Shmuel David Ungar of Nitra). He entered yechidus with the Frierdiker Rebbe and received a special blessing letter for his second marriage.

In 5715 (1955) he first visited Israel and decided to build the Chassidic town "Kiryat Sanz" in Netanya. Fulfilling a vow made during the Holocaust — that if he survived he would always try to save Jewish lives — he established the Laniado Medical Center. For its founding he consulted extensively with the Rebbe, who instructed that it be called a "medical center" (not "hospital").

In 5732 (1972) he returned to America and resided in Union City. He passed away on 9 Tammuz 5754.

## Relationship with the Rebbe

From the time the Rebbe assumed leadership, a warm friendship existed between him and the Klausenberger Rebbe. The Rebbe sent messages and blessings for the establishment of institutions and Laniado Medical Center.

The Klausenberger Rebbe stood publicly with the Rebbe on Jewish identity issues, speaking forcefully against those who sought to delay the "Who is a Jew" legislation. Though he stated he was not himself a Lubavitcher Chassid, he declared: "The Rebbe of Lubavitch is absolutely right. He is truly the Prince of Torah and a true Tzaddik."

He said he prayed for the Rebbe daily because "all the forces of evil fight against him over the Jewish identity issue." On Land of Israel, he similarly supported the Rebbe's position.

He expressed great admiration for the Rebbe's work: "How I envy the Lubavitcher Rebbe's work, his broad shoulders, the divine pleasure he creates..." He instructed a chassid going to visit the Rebbe: "The Lubavitcher Rebbe is a very great person" and encouraged the visit.

When asked about the claim that the Rebbe was the Mashiach, he waved his hand dismissively to a critic and replied: "What do you care who it is?! The main thing is that he comes already!"

He also praised the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch and ruled that in cases of halachic disputes, one should follow the Shulchan Aruch HaRav.
