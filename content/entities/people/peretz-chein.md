# Peretz Chein Family

## Notable Early Chabad Family (18th–19th centuries)

**Also known as:** The Chein family, Peretz Chein and descendants
**Era:** 18th–19th centuries (Alter Rebbe through Tzemach Tzedek periods)
**Region:** Belarus, particularly around Vitebsk and Lubavitch
**Role:** A notable early Chabad family that produced multiple generations of devoted chassidim

---

## Overview

The **Peretz Chein family** was a distinguished family in early Chabad history, known for producing multiple generations of devoted chassidim who served the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]], the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]], and the [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]]. The family exemplifies how Chabad became embedded in particular family lines, with commitment passing from parents to children across generations.

## Family Origins

### Peretz Chein — The Patriarch

**Peretz Chein** was the patriarch of this family line, active during the Alter Rebbe's era. Details about his life are limited in available sources, but he appears to have been:

- A respected figure in the Belarusian Jewish community
- Connected to the emerging Chabad movement from its early days
- Someone whose children and grandchildren continued his Chabad commitment

### Geographic Base

The family was associated with:

- **Vitebsk region** of Belarus
- Proximity to **Lubavitch** (Chabad headquarters from 1813)
- Other areas where early Chabad communities formed

## Relationship with the Rebbes

### The Alter Rebbe Era

During the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe's]] leadership (c. 1770s–1812), the Chein family:

- Became connected to the fledgling Chabad movement
- May have been among the earliest families to embrace the Alter Rebbe's teachings
- Provided support and service to the Alter Rebbe's court

### The Mitteler Rebbe Era

Under the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] (1812–1827), the family:

- Participated in establishing Lubavitch as Chabad's headquarters
- Served the Mitteler Rebbe's court in various capacities
- Helped organize the community's infrastructure

### The Tzemach Tzedek Era

During the long leadership of the [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]] (1827–1866), the family:

- Continued their service across the longest period of stability
- Saw the next generation take on responsibilities
- Benefitted from Chabad's expansion into a mass movement

## Historical Significance

### Family Dynasties in Chabad

The Chein family exemplifies a broader phenomenon:

- **Chabad became embedded in particular family lines**
- Commitment passed from parents to children
- Certain families became associated with service to the Rebbe
- These families formed the social infrastructure of Chabad communities

### Social Infrastructure

Families like the Cheins provided:

- **Continuity**: Across periods of upheaval and persecution
- **Institutional memory**: Preserving practices and traditions
- **Human resources**: People to serve communal needs
- **Economic support**: Financial resources for communal institutions

### Marriage Networks

Through strategic marriages, families like the Cheins:

- Created networks of interconnected Chabad families
- Strengthened communal bonds
- Facilitated communication across regions
- Consolidated Chabad's social structure
