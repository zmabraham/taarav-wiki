## Biography

Mashpia (spiritual mentor) and leading Chassid in the Rebbe's inner circle. Active in the underground Jewish educational network in Soviet Russia. Known for his deep knowledge of Chassidus and his role in transmitting Chabad teachings under dangerous conditions.
