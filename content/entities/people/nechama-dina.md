# Rebbetzin Nechama Dina Schneersohn

**Born**: c. 1880  
**Died**: c. 1970s  
**Role**: Wife of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] (sixth Rebbe); mother of Rebbetzin Moussia

---

## Overview

Rebbetzin Nechama Dina Schneersohn was the wife of the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe, [[entities/people/rayatz|Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn]], and mother of his daughters including [[entities/people/rebbetzin-moussia|Chaya Mushka]] (who married the seventh Rebbe) and [[entities/people/sheina-horowitz|Sheina]] (who was killed in the Holocaust).

---

## Marriage

Nechama Dina Schneersohn was a second cousin of the Rayatz (her marriage to him was thus within the Schneersohn family). Their engagement was in 1896. The [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]] (the Rayatz's father) bore all wedding expenses.

The wedding itself took place on **September 10, 1897 / 13 Elul 5657** — the same event at which the Rayatz made his famous request for the founding of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva. The following day (15 Elul), the Rashab convened fifty guests and announced the yeshiva. Thus Nechama Dina's wedding was the occasion for one of the most consequential decisions in Chabad institutional history.

See [[events/tomchei-temimim-founding-1897|Tomchei Temimim Founding (1897)]].

---

## Life Through the Exile Years

Nechama Dina accompanied the Rayatz through his extraordinary journey: the Soviet period, the arrest of 1927, exile to Riga, the Warsaw years, and ultimately America. She was a constant presence through decades of upheaval and reconstruction.

Her daughter Sheina's murder in the Holocaust was a devastating personal loss shared by both the Rayatz and Nechama Dina.
