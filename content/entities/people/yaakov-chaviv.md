# Rabbi Yaakov Chaviv

Rabbi Yaakov Chaviv (born 10 Sivan 5735 / 1975, Paris) is an Av Beit Din and halachic authority, presiding judge of the "Ittud HaRabbanim" rabbinical court of France, and a member of Machon Halacha Chabad.

## Biography

Born in Paris to his father Rabbi Massoud Chai, a Chabad Chassid who served in governmental and communal roles within the French Jewish "Consistoire" and established communities in Nice, Strasbourg, Paris, and later Montreal.

Rabbi Chaviv descends from the celebrated Chaviv rabbinic family, which includes the Nimukei Yosef, the Maharalbach (chief rabbi of Jerusalem), Rabbi Maharim Chaviv (author of "Get Pashut"), and Rabbi Avraham Chaviv (Av Beit Din of Tripoli).

As a youth he studied in Chabad schools in Nice under Rabbi Yosef Pinson, then entered Tomchei Tmimim in Brunoy (1988), then Tomchei Tmimim in Kiryat Gat, and then the central Tomchei Tmimim in Brooklyn. He received rabbinical ordination in New York and completed advanced ordination at the kolel in Kiryat Gat.

In 5759 (1999) he married Nechama Dina Rachel of the Savag family (prominent Chabad family of Paris). He served as dayan in Paris rabbinical courts and in the "Yuru Mishpatecha LeYaakov" beit din of France.

In 5759 (1999) he co-founded the "Ittud HaRabbanim" beit din of France with Rabbi Yaakov HaLevi Poltork. This court serves the French-speaking Jewish world on all aspects of daily halachic life: Jewish identity, kashrut, marriage and divorce, and financial matters. A kashrut certification body was added in 5774 (2014).

In 5777 (2017) he joined the dozens of rabbis of Machon Halacha Chabad answering practical halachic questions.

## Works
- **She'ut Reach HaSadeh** — responsa on all four sections of the Shulchan Aruch
- **Cham HaShemesh** — series on laws of mourning, kashrut, marriage, and Sifrei Torah
