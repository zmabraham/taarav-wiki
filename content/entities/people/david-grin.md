# David Grin

A student from Kamenets (Kamenets-Podolsk) who traveled with R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin and Avrahamele Kvash from Kyiv to Vitebsk in spring 5688 (1928). He is the father of the Rabbi Yekatiel Grin (mentioned in a memoir footnote).
