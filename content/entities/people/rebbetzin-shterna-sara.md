# Rebbetzin Shterna Sara

**Born**: c. 1860  
**Died**: c. 1942 (in Nazi-occupied territory)  
**Role**: Wife of the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]]; mother of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]

---

## Overview

Rebbetzin Shterna Sara was the wife of the fifth Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Shalom DovBer Schneersohn ([[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rashab]]), and mother of the sixth Rebbe, [[entities/people/rayatz|Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn]]. She played a foundational role in shaping the Rayatz's character through her devotion to traditional values and her direct influence on his childhood in Lubavitch.

---

## Marriage and Family

Shterna Sara married the Rashab in Lubavitch and bore him one son — Yosef Yitzchak, born 12 Tammuz 5640 (1880). The Rayatz's biography describes her as deeply devoted to Torah values and instrumental in creating the home environment that would shape her son's extraordinary character.

The Rayatz held his mother in profound reverence. Her commitment to Jewish tradition complemented the Rashab's intense spiritual and intellectual world, providing the Rayatz with a rounded upbringing.

---

## Later Years

After the Rashab's death in Rostov-on-Don in 1920 and the Rayatz's exile from the Soviet Union in 1927, Shterna Sara remained behind in Soviet territory — a devastating separation from her son. The fate of many Chabad family members left in Soviet Russia during this period was tragic. Soviet conditions and ultimately Nazi invasion during WWII claimed the lives of many who could not escape.
