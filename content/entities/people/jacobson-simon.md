# Rabbi Simon Jacobson

**Born**: 1956, Brooklyn, New York  
**Role**: Chabad teacher, author, and media personality; founder of the Meaningful Life Center

---

## Overview

Rabbi Simon Jacobson is one of the most prominent translators of Chabad Chassidic philosophy for a general English-speaking audience. A student of the [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|Seventh Rebbe]], he is best known as the author of **"Toward a Meaningful Life: The Wisdom of the Rebbe"**, one of the most widely read Chabad books in English. Through his writing, radio show, and the Meaningful Life Center, Jacobson has brought the Rebbe's teachings to hundreds of thousands of readers and listeners who might never encounter them in their original Yiddish or Hebrew form.

---

## Education and Formation

Born and raised in Brooklyn, Jacobson studied in Chabad yeshivos and was a close student of the [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|Seventh Rebbe]]. He served as a member of the team that transcribed and edited the Rebbe's talks (farbrengens), giving him deep familiarity with the Rebbe's thought and style of expression.

---

## "Toward a Meaningful Life"

Published in 1995, **"Toward a Meaningful Life: The Wisdom of the Rebbe"** organized the Seventh Rebbe's teachings thematically — covering topics such as birth, love, death, work, relationships, suffering, and joy — and presented them in accessible English prose. The book became a bestseller and introduced the Rebbe's ideas to a broad audience far beyond the Chabad community.

The book's success demonstrated that the Rebbe's teachings had universal resonance and could speak to contemporary seekers regardless of their religious background.

---

## The Meaningful Life Center

Jacobson founded the **Meaningful Life Center**, which produces:

- **Radio programming** — Jacobson has hosted a weekly radio show discussing the application of Chassidic wisdom to contemporary life
- **Website and digital content** — articles, classes, and resources applying the Rebbe's teachings
- **Books and publications** — continuing the work of translating Chassidic concepts for a general audience

---

## Significance

Jacobson represents a critical function in the modern Chabad enterprise: the translation of deep Chassidic philosophy into language and frameworks accessible to contemporary English speakers. While the Rebbe's original talks were delivered in Yiddish with extensive references to classical Chassidic and Kabbalistic texts, Jacobson's work demonstrates that the underlying ideas have broad human relevance and can be communicated without requiring specialized knowledge.
