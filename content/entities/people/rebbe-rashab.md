# Rebbe Rashab — R' Shalom Dovber Schneerson (אדמו"ר הרש"ב)

**Fifth Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch (1882–1920); founder of Tomchei Temimim yeshiva.**

## Biography

R' Shalom Dovber became the fifth Chabad Rebbe after the passing of his father, the Maharash, in 5643 (1882). He is most famous for founding Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in 5657 (1897) in Lubavitch, which became the flagship yeshiva of Chabad and produced generations of devoted Chasidim and shluchim.

He was known for his profound intellectual discourses (maamarim), many delivered on Shabbat in the "kleiner zal" (small hall) of the Lubavitch beis medrash, standing in the southeast corner. After a fire damaged the small hall (winter 5668/1908), he began delivering maamarim in the large hall.

He opposed Zionism strongly (letter of 5653/1893), and resisted pressure from the Russian government regarding Haskalah education. In 5675 (1914/1915), during WWI, he fled Lubavitch and moved the court and yeshiva to Rostov-on-Don.

He passed away on 2 Nissan 5680 (1920) in Rostov.

## In the Zachor Memoirs

Israel Jacobson first saw the Rashab on Shabbat Parshas Noach, 2 Marcheshvan 5668 (1907) — his first Shabbat in Lubavitch. He describes the Rashab entering the small hall, facing east, wearing a shtreimel, and delivering a deep discourse. Jacobson recalls the profound impression without retaining the content, being too young to follow it.

He later witnessed the Rashab at Shavuot discourses, the fire episode, and many other occasions. He describes the Rashab's physical setting, his manner, and the atmosphere he created.

A memorable story recounted: the Rashab once told a butcher who had heard his discourse on "neshamos d'atzilus" (souls of Atzilus) that souls need warm shawls — the butcher thereafter stopped rushing the shochet, insisting he sharpen the knife perfectly.
