# Rabbi Avraham Chaim Naeh

Prominent Chabad rabbi and halachic authority, known as "Baal HaShiurim" (Master of Measurements) for establishing the standard halachic measurements used in modern Jewish practice. Rosh (head) of Kollel Chabad in Jerusalem, dayan of the Edah HaChareidis, and leading Chabad posek in the Land of Israel.

## Biography

Born into a distinguished Chabad family in Hebron — a 15th-generation rabbinical lineage. As a young child learned with Rabbi Chaim Chizkiya Medini (author of Sdei Chemed) in Hebron, who gave him access to his entire library.

At age 21 (1911) sent by Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (the Rashab) as an emissary to Samarkand, Uzbekistan, replacing his uncle Rabbi Shlomo Yehuda Leib Eliezrov. During this mission he dressed in Bukharian clothes and published a halachic booklet in Hebrew and Bukharan. He later visited Lubavitch where the Rashab offered him the position of Rosh Yeshiva, which his father declined on his behalf.

During World War I, was exiled with other Jews to Egypt, where he founded a yeshiva for exiles.

After the war, became secretary and right-hand man of Rabbi Yosef Chaim Zonnenfeld (Gaavad of the Edah HaChareidis). Served as Sofer (secretary) of the Edah HaChareidis rabbinical court, member of its governing board and slaughter committee. Despite close ties to the Edah HaChareidis, he was a dedicated Chabad Chassid throughout his life.

In 1938 (5698) became a founding member of the Kollel Chabad leadership. In 1949, appointed as co-manager of Kollel Chabad by the Frierdiker Rebbe alongside Rabbi Shlomo Yosef Zevin and others.

## Major Controversy: The Shiurim Debate

Naeh's most lasting impact was establishing "the small measurements" (shiurim ketanim) — precise conversions of halachic measurements to modern metric units. His system became standard in most of world Jewry. The Chazon Ish (Rabbi Avraham Yeshaya Karelitz) opposed these measurements, preferring larger measurements. The dispute was intense and long-running, but the majority of leading poskim — including Rabbi Yosef Tzvi Dushinsky, Rabbi Yitzchak Herzog, Rabbi Tzvi Pesach Frank, and eventually Rabbi Ovadia Yosef — sided with Naeh. The Rebbe said of his measurements: "One can rely on him."

## Relationship with Chabad Rebbes

**With the Rashab**: Visited Lubavitch after his Samarkand mission; the Rashab wanted him as Rosh Yeshiva.

**With the Frierdiker Rebbe**: Accompanied him during his 1929 visit to the Holy Land in Jerusalem and Hebron. The Frierdiker Rebbe wrote a special letter about his book Ketzot HaShulchan.

**With the Rebbe (Menachem Mendel Schneerson)**: Corresponded extensively on halacha and Chabad custom. Was among those who led the acceptance of the Rebbe's leadership in Jerusalem in 1950, proposing the proper formula for the letter of allegiance. Sent a congratulatory telegram to the Rebbe at his formal assumption of the Nesius on 10 Shvat 5711.

## Works
- **Ketzot HaShulchan** (9 volumes) — abridgment and commentary on Shulchan Aruch HaRav
- **Shiurei Torah** — halachic measurements in modern units
- **Shiur Mikveh** — measurements for the mikveh
- **Shiurei Tzion** — responses to critics
- **Kontres HaShulchan**
- **Piskei HaSiddur**
- **Luach Kollel Chabad** — annual calendar of Chabad customs
