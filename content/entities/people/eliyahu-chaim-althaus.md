**Born**: Germany

## Overview

Rabbi Eliyahu Chaim Althaus was a German-born Chabad chassid whose life story reflected the complex connections between German and Russian Jewry during the tumultuous mid-20th century. As a Chabad chassid who found himself in the Soviet orbit, his experience bridged these two Jewish worlds.

## Background in Germany

Reb Eliyahu Chaim was born into Germany's Jewish community during the early 20th century, when German Jewry was one of the most acculturated and sophisticated communities in the world.

**German Jewish Context**: German Jews had made tremendous contributions to European culture — in philosophy, science, literature, and the arts. Many were highly assimilated, viewing themselves as Germans of the Jewish faith. Orthodox communities existed but were a minority.

**Connection to Chabad**: How Reb Eliyahu Chaim came to Chabad is not fully documented in available sources. **Life Under Soviet Rule**: Whether by choice or circumstance, Reb Eliyahu Chaim found himself living under Soviet rule. This experience connected him to the Russian Chabad underground that maintained Jewish life despite Communist persecution.

## The War Years and Beyond

**Survival**: Like many European Jews, Reb Eliyahu Chaim faced extraordinary dangers during the war years. His survival itself was an achievement.
