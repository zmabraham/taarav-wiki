# R' Eliyahu Chaim Althaus

**Mashpia and teacher at Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in Riga, Latvia; key figure in Chabad education in the Baltic region**

## Biography

R' Eliyahu Chaim Althaus served as a mashpia (spiritual mentor and Chassidic teacher) at the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva branch established in Riga, Latvia. He was one of the principal educators shaping the spiritual character of Chabad students in the Baltic region during the interwar period.

After the Rayatz (6th Rebbe) was exiled from the Soviet Union in 1927, he settled in Riga (1927–1933), which became the new center of Chabad leadership. During this period, the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in Riga was a primary institution. R' Althaus as mashpia played a crucial role in the yeshiva's spiritual program — teaching Chassidic texts, conducting farbrengens, and providing individual guidance to the students.

Riga was also home to the Latvian Jewish community, a significant and relatively affluent population that included both Lithuanian-style mitnagdim and Chassidic Jews. R' Althaus's work there helped strengthen the Chabad presence in this cosmopolitan Baltic capital.

## Significance

R' Althaus represents the critical function of the mashpia in the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva system — distinct from the rosh yeshiva (who taught Talmud) and the Rebbe himself, the mashpia was responsible for the Chassidic and spiritual development of the students. His work in Riga during the Rayatz's residence there placed him at the center of Chabad's post-Soviet leadership transition.
