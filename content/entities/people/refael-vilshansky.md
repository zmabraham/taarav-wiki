# R. Refael Vilshansky

**Secretary of the Lishka leRefugees** (לשכה לפליטים — the Refugee Office) in **Paris**, 5707 (1947). The Lishka was founded by the Previous Rebbe (Rayatz, R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn) to assist Jewish refugees in the post-war period, particularly Chabad Chassidim seeking to emigrate from Europe.

## The Lishka leRefugees

The Lishka (Office) was directed by [[binyamin-gorodetsky|R. Binyamin Gorodetsky]] as director, with R. Refael Vilshansky as his secretary/administrator. The office helped coordinate:
- Documentation for emigration
- Housing and support for arriving refugees
- Connection to American and Israeli immigration channels

## Work in Paris

R. Refael Vilshansky's administrative work at the Lishka made him a key figure for Jewish refugees arriving in Paris after WWII. The memoir's narrator interacted with the Lishka upon arriving in Paris before Shavuot 5707 (1947).

## Family Connection

R. Refael likely shares the Vilshansky family name with [[betzalel-vilshansky|R. Betzalel Vilshansky]], a Chabad shochet who also worked in Paris at the same time. The Vilshansky family appears to have had multiple members active in Paris's post-war Chabad community.
