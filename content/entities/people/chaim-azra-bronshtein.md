# Chaim Azra Bronshtein

A Chabad chassid in the Malachovka/Moscow area. He gave the author his home address to use as a receiving address for letters from the Rebbe (since receiving mail from the Rebbe openly was dangerous). He later brought the author a postcard response from the Rebbe (via R. Chaim Lieberman) directing him to apply for a visa to Riga.
