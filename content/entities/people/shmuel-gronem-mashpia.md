# R' Shmuel Gronem Estherman (ר' שמואל גרונם)

**Master mashpia (spiritual mentor) at Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch; renowned for his warmth, depth, and farbrengens.**

## Overview

R' Shmuel Gronem was one of the most influential mashpiyim (spiritual mentors) in Tomchei Temimim during the era of the Rebbe Rashab. He was famous for his farbrengens (Chassidic gatherings), which could last all night — from 11pm until near dawn, sometimes more than once a week.

## In the Zachor Memoirs

Jacobson describes how R' Shmuel Gronem urged senior students to study the works of the Mitteler Rebbe (Admor HaEmtzai — R' Dovber of Lubavitch), specifically "Imrei Binah," for 12 hours a day and more.

A vivid anecdote: at a farbrengen (possibly Purim Katan 5673), R' Shmuel Gronem was sitting on the narrow ledge around the bimah with students packed in tightly. He turned to a student (from the Arantchik family) and said: "Do you remember when you came to the yeshiva, standing behind the office door, crying? You cried? Your soul cried!" He then elaborated on Divine Providence — if stepping on a finger doesn't happen without heavenly announcement (Talmudic dictum), how much more so does the soul merit to study at TT through special providence. His deliberate, emphatic delivery penetrated the hearts of all present.

Jacobson writes that Shmuel Gronem's farbrengens were among the most spiritually formative experiences of yeshiva life, occurring almost every Friday night and on special calendar dates throughout the year.
