# Rabbi David Moshe Krispin of Lod

Rabbi David Moshe Krispin (born 5710 / 1950, Morocco) is a Chabad mashpia and Torah lecturer in the Chabad community of Lod, known for his expertise in both halacha-kabbalah and Chassidus. He is the grandson of the Moroccan kabbalist Rabbi Ya'ish Krispin (author of "Perach HaShoshana") and the brother of Rabbi Aminadav Makhlouf Krispin, a leading Chabad shaliach in Morocco.

## Biography

Born in Morocco to Rabbi Yisrael Krispin, from a distinguished family. His grandfather Rabbi Ya'ish Krispin was a famous tzaddik and kabbalist. His brother Rabbi Aminadav became one of the leading Chabad figures in Morocco.

In 5721 (1961) the family made aliyah. At age 10, Rabbi David entered Porat Yosef yeshiva — the roshei yeshiva initially hesitated due to his young age, but were so impressed by his encyclopedic knowledge of Talmudic tractates that they accepted him. He became close to Rabbi Shalom Cohen and Rabbi Shimon Badani.

Under a friend's influence he transferred to the vocational school (Beit HaMelachah) in Kfar Chabad, where he learned printing and devoted himself to publishing sacred Chassidic texts. After marriage to Rachel (daughter of Rabbi Chaim Nizri of Lod), he settled in Chabad housing in Lod.

He is known for his extraordinary breadth of Torah knowledge across Talmud, halacha, Kabbalah, and Chassidus, and teaches shiurim on Shelah HaKadosh, Likkutei Sichot, Derech Mitzvotecha, and the Ma'amar Teras"u, drawing listeners from across the spectrum.

He has donated complete sets of Talmud and Mishnah ("Schottenstein") worth thousands of shekels to dozens of synagogues.

When the Krispin family brought the manuscripts of "Perach HaShoshana" to the Rebbe upon their aliyah, the Rebbe asked to keep them in his library. For decades the manuscripts remained under the Rebbe's personal attention until their eventual publication.
