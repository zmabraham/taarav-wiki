# Pyotr Lopukhin (Russian Prosecutor General)

## Overview
Pyotr Vasilyevich Lopukhin served as the Prosecutor General (General Procurator) of Russia under Emperor Paul I and played a central administrative role in handling the Alter Rebbe's first arrest and liberation in 1798-1799.

## Details
Lopukhin was the senior government official who managed the proceedings against the Alter Rebbe. After Emperor Paul I issued his order of release on 19 Kislev 5559 (November 17, 1798), it was Lopukhin who sent the official notification to Semyon Zhegulin, the civil governor of Belarus.

His letter to Zhegulin, dated November 17, 1798 (20 Kislev 5559), reads: "Notification, upon fulfillment of His Majesty's will. To the Second Civil Councilor, Governor of the Province. Hereby notified that Zalman ben Baruch has been released, and for his departure without difficulties he received a certificate."

The letter was numbered 928 and was sent from the capital.

The Alter Rebbe himself mentions in a letter (approximately 5562/1802) that he responded successfully to the government's interrogations and that the Tsar and ministers were favorably impressed.
