## Biography

Second Lubavitcher Rebbe, son of the Alter Rebbe. Also known as the Mitteler (Middle) Rebbe. Moved the center of Chabad to Lubavitch, which remained the movement's home for over a century. Known for his lengthy and elaborate Chassidic discourses. He was the one who established Lubavitch as the iconic center of the movement.
