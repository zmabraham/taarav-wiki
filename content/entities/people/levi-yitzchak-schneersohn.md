## Biography

Father of Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn, the seventh Rebbe. Served as chief rabbi of Yekaterinoslav (Dnepropetrovsk), Ukraine. A renowned Kabbalist and Torah scholar. Was arrested by Soviet authorities in 1939 and died in exile in Kazakhstan. Author of Likkutei Levi Yitzchak, a Kabbalistic commentary.
