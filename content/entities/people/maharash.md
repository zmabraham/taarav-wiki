# Maharash — R' Shmuel Schneerson (אדמו"ר מהר"ש)

**Fourth Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch (1866–1882).**

## Overview

R' Shmuel Schneerson became the fourth Chabad Rebbe after his father the Tzemach Tzedek passed away in 5626/1866. He was known for his approach of "lechatkhila ariber" — going over obstacles rather than under them.

He traveled to Western Europe in 5629/1869 for Jewish advocacy and established major charitable networks. He maintained the Chabad court in Lubavitch until his passing in 5643/1882.

His wife **Rebbetzin Rivka** survived him for many years and became a beloved and revered matriarch at the Lubavitch court during her son the Rashab's era.
