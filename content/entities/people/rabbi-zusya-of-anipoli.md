# Rabbi Zusya of Anipoli

## Overview
Rabbi Meshullam Zusya of Anipoli (Hanipoli) was one of the beloved disciples of the Maggid of Mezeritch, known for his extraordinary humility and love of God. He wrote the preface to the first printed edition of the Tanya (1796-97).

## Details
Rabbi Zusya and his brother Rabbi Elimelech of Lizhensk were famous wandering disciples of the Maggid who traveled the Jewish communities of Eastern Europe.

He wrote the introduction to the Tanya in Elul 1796, before the printing began in Slavita. In his introduction, he explained why the Alter Rebbe agreed to print the work — due to the proliferation of copies with many scribal errors, the Alter Rebbe was compelled to allow printing to produce an accurate text.

The Alter Rebbe stayed in Anipoli while in the area of Rovneh to be near the Maggid of Mezeritch before the Maggid's passing in 19 Kislev 5533 (1772).
