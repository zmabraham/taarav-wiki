# R' Shmaryahu Gurary

**Son-in-law of the Rayatz (6th Rebbe); senior Chabad figure; documented in the context of Chabad activities in Poland and the United States**

## Biography

R' Shmaryahu Gurary (known by his acronym "Rashag") was the husband of Sheina Schneerson, a daughter of the Rayatz (6th Rebbe). He was thus a brother-in-law of the Seventh Rebbe (R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson), who was married to Sheina's sister Chaya Mushka.

R' Gurary was a learned and active figure in Chabad leadership. He is documented in the Toldos Chabad BePolin, Lita, u'Latvia in connection with Polish Chabad activities. He later came to America and continued to be active in Chabad organizational affairs in Brooklyn.

After the Rayatz's passing in 1950 and the Seventh Rebbe's acceptance of leadership in 1951, R' Gurary occupied a senior position within the Chabad community. He and his wife Sheina were part of the Chabad leadership circle in Brooklyn.

## Significance

R' Shmaryahu Gurary represents the family dimension of Chabad leadership succession. As a son-in-law of the Rayatz and brother-in-law of the Seventh Rebbe, he was part of the inner circle of the movement during one of its most critical periods of transition. His documented involvement in Polish Chabad activities places him in the context of the movement before the Holocaust, and his presence in America afterward represents Chabad's successful post-war reconstruction.
