# Manie Dobravsky

A Chabad woman in Samarkand during WWII; daughter of R. Mendel Dobravsky (a notable Chabad rav). She was widowed in Samarkand (her husband died) and also lost a daughter. She had one surviving child, a boy named Yeishke (Yehoshua Dobravsky), who became one of the first students in the underground Tomchei Temimim.

## Description
When R. Mikhael Teitelboim and the author went to visit Manie Dobravsky to enroll her son in the Tomchei Temimim, they found her completely broken by grief, her son lying pale and ill in bed. She was initially reluctant, saying "what are you taking from me, he is sick." But Mikhael convinced her to enroll him, and miraculously the child recovered and began studying. This brought the first Tomchei Temimim cohort to three students.
