# R' Avraham Michel Lis

**Chabad Chassid in Warsaw; member of the Polish Chabad community during the Rayatz's Warsaw years**

## Biography

R' Avraham Michel Lis was a Chabad Chassid in Warsaw, documented in the Toldos Chabad BePolin, Lita, u'Latvia. He was part of the Chabad community in Warsaw during the interwar period, specifically during or around the time the Rayatz resided there (1933–1939).

Warsaw presented Chabad with unique challenges and opportunities. Its large, diverse Jewish population included both traditionally observant and modernizing Jews. The Chabad community navigated this environment while maintaining its distinctive character and supporting the Rayatz's leadership.

## Significance

R' Avraham Michel Lis is one of the Polish Chabad Chassidim whose names are preserved in historical documentation. Alongside figures like R' Alter Shimchovitch and R' Yosef Blumenfeld, he represents the lay Chassidic community that sustained the Warsaw Chabad presence. These names, preserved in Toldos Chabad BePolin, serve as memorials to a community largely destroyed by the Holocaust.
