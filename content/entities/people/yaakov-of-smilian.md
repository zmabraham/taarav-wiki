# Rabbi Yaakov of Smilian

The first **Gabai (administrator) of Kolel Chabad**, appointed by the [[alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] himself. Rabbi Yaakov was the practical administrator of the first organized Chabad charitable fund for the Chassidic community in the Holy Land.

## Role as First Gabai

When the Alter Rebbe served as **Nasi** (president/overseer) of the Chabad community in the Land of Israel, Rabbi Yaakov of Smilian served as his representative on the fundraising side — the Gabai who collected and transmitted the charitable contributions ("ma'amad" and kolel funds) from the diaspora to the Eretz Yisrael community.

This two-tier structure — with a Nasi in the diaspora overseeing the overall effort and a Gabai handling the financial flows — became the model for Kolel Chabad's administration for generations.

## Context: Early Kolel Chabad

The Kolel Chabad (separate from the general Chassidic kolel) was formalized around 5565 (1805), though Chabad's financial support for its Holy Land community predates this. The Alter Rebbe personally organized support for Rabbi [[menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]]'s group, which had made aliya in 5537 (1777).

## Smilian

Smilian (Smilian, likely in Belarus/Ukraine) was a town in the Pale of Settlement. Little is known about Rabbi Yaakov's biography outside his administrative role.
