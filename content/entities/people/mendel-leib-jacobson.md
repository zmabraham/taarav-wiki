# Mendel Leib Jacobson

A Chabad rav from a distinguished anash family who served as the treasurer (gazbr) of the communal funds for the rabbis of anash in the Moscow region. He is described as having a sharp mind and is noted for an endearing scene at Simchas Torah 5696: standing in a corner and not looking at the others, then spontaneously joining in the hakafos dancing after taking a drink.
