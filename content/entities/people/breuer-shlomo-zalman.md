# Rabbi Shlomo Zalman Breuer

Rabbi Shlomo Zalman Breuer was a leader of the German Orthodox community and son-in-law of Rabbi Samson Raphael Hirsch. He was part of the Frankfurt Orthodox establishment that interacted with Chabad during the Rashab's visits to Germany.
