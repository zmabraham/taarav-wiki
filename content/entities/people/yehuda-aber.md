# Rabbi Yehuda Aber הי"ד

Rosh Yeshiva (head) of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva, based in Riga. One of the senior Chabad educators in Latvia.

## Martyrdom

R' Yehuda Aber and his family perished in **Riga** during **Kislev 5702** (approximately November–December 1941), when the Nazis murdered the Jews of Riga. The exact date is recorded as occurring in Kislev 5702.
