# Rabbi Aharon of Strashelya

## Overview
Rabbi Aharon of Strashelya (also known as Reb Aharon HaGadol) was one of the most prominent early Chabad disciples of the Alter Rebbe. He authored the work Sha'arei HaYichud VeHaEmunah and other Chabad texts.

## Details
He was among the very first to attach himself to the Alter Rebbe, as attested by his own statement in Sha'arei HaYichud VeHaEmunah: "close to thirty years that I have gathered the dust at his feet."

After the Alter Rebbe's passing, a dispute arose regarding succession. Rabbi Aharon of Strashelya supported a different approach to Chassidus compared to the Mitteler Rebbe.

After the liberation on 19 Kislev 1799 (release from the first arrest), the Alter Rebbe's freedom led to the spread of both Chabad Chassidus and general Chassidus throughout Belarus and Lithuania. Along with this came the emergence of the Stolin-Lechovitch Chassidic movement (founded by Rabbi Mordechai of Lechovitch and Rabbi Asher of Stolin), which took root in Lithuania and Belarus.
