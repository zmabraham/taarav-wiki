# R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich — ר' ישראל נח חצקביץ'

**Chabad Rabbi of Kupishok, Lithuania; Martyred 1941**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich
**Dates**: (late 19th–1941)
**Position**: Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/kupishok|Kupishok]] (Kupiškis), Lithuania (documented from 1928)
**Affiliation**: Chabad-Lubavitch
**Death**: Martyred in the Holocaust, 1941

---

## Overview

Rabbi Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich was the Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/kupishok|Kupishok]] (Kupiškis) in northeastern Lithuania, documented in his role from at least 1928. He was part of the Lithuanian Chabad network active under the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]'s direction from [[entities/places/riga|Riga]]. He was martyred in 1941 when Nazi forces and their Lithuanian collaborators murdered the Jewish community.

---

## Service in Kupishok

### Rabbinical Role

As Chabad rabbi of Kupishok:

- **Documented from 1928**: His service is documented in the Toldos Chabad source
- **Community leadership**: Provided rabbinical guidance, halachic rulings, and spiritual direction
- **Part of the network**: Connected to the Chabad network coordinated from [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] and [[entities/places/kovno|Kovno]]
- **Lithuanian Chabad**: Maintained Chabad identity in this predominantly Mitnagdic environment

---

## Martyrdom (1941)

When Germany invaded Lithuania in June 1941:

- **Lithuanian Jews targeted**: Lithuanian Jews were among the earliest victims of the Nazi genocide
- **Speed of murder**: Communities were murdered within weeks or months of the German invasion
- **1941**: Kupishok's Jewish community was murdered in 1941
- **R' Chatzkeivich killed**: He was murdered with his community

---

## Legacy

R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich represents the Lithuanian Chabad rabbis who maintained their communities until the very end — giving their lives not by choice but because they were murdered. His documented service in 1928 connects him directly to the period of the Rayatz's Riga headquarters and the network that sustained Lithuanian Chabad in that era.
