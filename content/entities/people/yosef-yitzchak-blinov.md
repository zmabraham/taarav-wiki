# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Blinov

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Blinov (born 10 Elul 5721 / 1961) serves as Rosh Yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim Central in Kfar Chabad, rabbi of the Chabad community in Bnei Brak, and head of the central beit hora'ah "Morashet Moshe" in Bnei Brak.

## Biography

Born in Paris to Sheina Rivka and Rabbi Mordechai Blinov, chairman of the Va'ad Rabbinei Lubavitch in France. He studied at Tomchei Tmimim in Brunoy (France), under the influence of Rabbi Yisrael Noach Belenetsky, then at Tomchei Tmimim Central — 770 in Brooklyn.

In 5743 (1983) he went to serve as chief mashpia at the Chabad Yeshiva Gedola in Casablanca, Morocco. In 5745 (1985) he married Nechama Dina, daughter of Rabbi Moshe Yehuda Leib Landa of Bnei Brak.

He studied at the Tzemach Tzedek kollel in Jerusalem, where he helped edit the journal "Yagdil Torah" and halachic compilations on the Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe. In summer 5746 (1986) he began heading the "Paamei Yaakov" kollel for hora'ah (halachic decision-making), founded in memory of Rabbi Yaakov Landa.

He served as dayan and rabbinic supervisor at the Landa family's beit hora'ah, and managed the kashrut administration of Rabbi Moshe Landa. He has served as rabbi of the Chabad community in Bnei Brak — comprising hundreds of families with educational and charitable institutions — and also serves in the central Chabad synagogue on Rashi Street, Bnei Brak.

In 5779 (2019) he was appointed Rosh Yeshiva of Tomchei Tmimim Central Kfar Chabad, directing the yeshiva with emphasis on deep Talmud study according to the Rebbe Rashab's approach in Kuntres Etz HaChaim.

## Works
- **Sha'arei Mishor** — 4 volumes of responsa and halachic clarifications (5767/2007)
- **Noach HaTena'im** — commentary on the Tzemach Tzedek's betrothal formula used in Chabad (5770/2010)
- Edited posthumous works of his father Rabbi Mordechai Blinov on the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch
