# R' Gedalia Vilenskin — ר' גדליה וילענסקין

**Chabad Rabbi of Dolhinow in the Vilna Region**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Gedalia Vilenskin
**Dates**: (early 20th century)
**Position**: Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/dolhinow|Dolhinow]]
**Affiliation**: Chabad-Lubavitch

---

## Overview

Rabbi Gedalia Vilenskin served as the Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/dolhinow|Dolhinow]] (Dolhinów, now Daŭhinava, Belarus), a community in the Vilna region of eastern Poland during the interwar period. His family name "Vilenskin" (meaning "of Vilna") suggests family origins in or strong connection to the Vilna region. He was part of the network of Chabad rabbis serving communities in the eastern borderlands.

---

## Service in Dolhinow

### Rabbinical Role

As Chabad rabbi of Dolhinow:

- **Community leadership**: Provided rabbinical guidance according to Chabad tradition
- **Vilna region**: Part of the Chabad network in the Vilna/eastern Poland region
- **Halachic guidance**: Rendered decisions for his community
- **Spiritual direction**: Provided Chassidic guidance

### Family Name Significance

The name "Vilenskin":

- **Vilna connection**: Suggests the rabbi or his family had origins in or deep connections to Vilna
- **Regional identity**: Many families in the borderland communities had names indicating their geographic origins

---

## Fate During WWII

The Dolhinow Jewish community was murdered during the Nazi occupation of Belarus in 1942. R' Gedalia Vilenskin's specific fate is documented in the Toldos Chabad source.
