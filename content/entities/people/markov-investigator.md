# Court Counselor Markov

**Active**: 1825, Vitebsk gubernia, Russia  
**Role**: Russian civilian official (Court Counselor = Советник Двора); one of Governor Khavanski's primary investigating officers in the 1825 Mitteler Rebbe case

---

## Overview

Court Counselor Markov (Советник Двора Марков) was one of the two primary officers dispatched by [[entities/people/governor-khavanski|Governor-General Prince Khavanski]] in October 1825 to investigate the denunciations filed by [[entities/people/simcha-kissin|Simcha Kissin]] against [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Rabbi DovBer Schneursohn]]. He worked alongside [[entities/people/loginov|Polkovnik Loginov]] and [[entities/people/morozov-investigator|Major Morozov]].

---

## The Investigation

Markov appears in nearly every document of the investigation as the civilian counterpart to Loginov's military role. Together they signed the formal list of questions put to the Mitteler Rebbe (7 October 1825) and conducted the witness interrogations of [[entities/people/zalman-slonim-shklov|R' Zalman Reizes Slonim]] and [[entities/people/shlomo-freydes-shklov|R' Shlomo Freydes]].

A document signed by Markov alone (13 October 1825) directed the Mitteler Rebbe to re-present the Hebrew text of the disputed distribution document together with a new Russian translation — signed jointly by the Rebbe and his translator [[entities/people/yehuda-yafeh-kapust|R' Yehuda Yafeh]] — with both parties accepting legal responsibility for the accuracy of the translation.

Markov also signed the joint summary report submitted to Khavanski by 17 November 1825, which triggered the transfer of the case to the Mogilev gubernia courts.

---

## Court Counselor Rank

In the Russian Imperial Table of Ranks, "Court Counselor" (Надворный советник) was the 7th rank out of 14, equivalent to a lieutenant-colonel in the military hierarchy. It was a mid-senior civilian rank used for special administrative missions.
