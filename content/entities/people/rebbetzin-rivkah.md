# Rebbetzin Rivkah

**Born**: c. 1833  
**Died**: 1914, Lubavitch  
**Role**: Wife of the [[entities/people/rebbe-maharash|Rebbe Maharash]]; grandmother of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]

---

## Overview

Rebbetzin Rivkah was the wife of Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn, the fourth Lubavitcher Rebbe ([[entities/people/rebbe-maharash|Maharash]]), and the grandmother of the sixth Rebbe, [[entities/people/rayatz|Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn]]. She is remembered in Chabad tradition as a woman of exceptional charitable character and moral stature.

---

## Character and Charitable Work

Rebbetzin Rivkah was renowned for her extensive charitable work in Lubavitch and the surrounding regions. She organized and funded assistance for the poor, widows, and orphans in the community, operating independently within the bounds of traditional Chabad life but with unusual initiative and scope.

The Rayatz's biography notes that when it came to his marriage, Rebbetzin Rivkah (his grandmother) favored a wealthy match — a natural preference given her awareness of the financial burdens Chabad leadership carried. However, the Rashab (the Rayatz's father) chose a match based on spiritual and family considerations rather than financial ones, selecting Nechama Dina Schneersohn as his son's bride.

---

## Legacy

Rebbetzin Rivkah's legacy of charitable activity became a model for Chabad women's engagement in communal life. The pattern of Chabad Rebbetzins combining deep piety with active charitable work — visible across multiple generations — finds one of its early prominent exemplars in her life.

She died in 1914 in Lubavitch, as the Rashab was still leading the movement and the world was on the verge of WWI.
