# R' Mordechai Chefetz (ר' מרדכי חפץ)

**Student at Tomchei Temimim; shochet in Riga; devoted to the Rayatz; martyred by Nazis.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | TT student during Jacobson's time in Lubavitch |
| **Role** | Shochet in Riga |
| **Relationship to Rayatz** | Among his closest and most devoted followers |
| **Death** | Killed by the Nazis, hy"d |

## Overview

When Israel Jacobson first arrived in Lubavitch (5668/1908), R' Mordechai Chefetz was already an older unmarried student studying in the large hall (Ulam). He was among the students who had completed their shechita apprenticeship under R' Shlomo Chaim Kutan and served as an assistant teacher.

He later married into a shochet family in Riga and became the shochet there himself. When Jacobson passed through Riga in late 5685 (1925) on his way to America, he met Chefetz there.

When the Rayatz came to Riga, Chefetz became one of his closest associates and was described as being wholly devoted "heart and soul" to the Rayatz and his household. In 5698 (1938), the Rayatz sent him as a special emissary (shodur) to America to strengthen the community.

He was killed by the Nazis, may God avenge his blood (hy"d). Jacobson notes he intended to write more about him.
