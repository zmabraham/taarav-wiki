# Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah (מרת שטערנא שרה)

**Wife of the Rebbe Rashab (5th Rebbe of Chabad); known for her welfare work on behalf of yeshiva students.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Husband** | Rebbe Rashab (R' Shalom Dovber Schneerson, 5th Rebbe) |
| **Known for** | Providing daily meals for younger TT students ("Rebbetzin's krupnik") |
| **Active** | Lubavitch, during the Rashab era |

## Overview

Rebbetzin Sterna Sarah was the wife of the Rebbe Rashab and mother of the Rayatz. She was known for her charitable activities toward the young students of Tomchei Temimim, particularly those who could not afford regular meals.

She initiated a meal program called the **"Rebbetzins Krupnik"** (Rebbetzin's porridge/soup) — providing a daily hot meal (soup or stew, sometimes with a small piece of meat) for younger students. The meals were arranged at the home of R' Shimon Dov Lipshitz.

Israel Jacobson began benefiting from this program after the end of the first Passover, when his father wrote that he could no longer afford the full board payments.
