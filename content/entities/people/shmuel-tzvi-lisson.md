# R' Shmuel Tzvi Lisson — ר' שמואל צבי ליסאן

**Martyred Mashpia (Spiritual Guide) of Ponevezh**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Shmuel Tzvi Lisson
**Dates**: (early 20th century – 1941)
**Position**: Mashpia (spiritual guide/mentor) in [[entities/places/ponevezh|Ponevezh]], Lithuania
**Affiliation**: Chabad-Lubavitch
**Death**: Martyred in the Holocaust, 1941

---

## Overview

Rabbi Shmuel Tzvi Lisson was a Chabad mashpia (spiritual guide and mentor) who served the Jewish community of [[entities/places/ponevezh|Ponevezh]], Lithuania, during the interwar period. He was murdered in the Holocaust in 1941 together with the rest of Ponevezh Jewry. R' Lisson is remembered as one of the martyred spiritual leaders of Lithuanian Chabad — a man who dedicated his life to nurturing the inner spiritual life of his community and whose work was cut short by the Nazi genocide.

---

## The Role of Mashpia

In Chabad tradition, a mashpia is a unique type of religious leader:

- **Spiritual mentor**: The mashpia guides individual Chassidim in their inner spiritual work (avodas Hashem)
- **Not a halachic authority**: Unlike a posek (halachic decisor), the mashpia focuses on spiritual development
- **Personal guidance**: Gives individual attention to students and community members
- **Teacher of Chassidus**: Explains Chabad philosophy and applies it to personal life
- **Model**: The mashpia embodies the Chassidic values he teaches

## Biography

### Background

- **Training**: Received Chabad education and mashpia training
- **Appointment**: Appointed as mashpia for the Chabad community of Ponevezh
- **Period**: Active in the 1920s and 1930s in Ponevezh

### Service in Ponevezh

In Ponevezh — a city famous as a Mitnagdic stronghold — R' Lisson served the Chabad community:

- **Two Chabad synagogues**: The Ponevezh Chabad community was substantial enough to have two synagogues
- **Spiritual guidance**: Guided individuals in their Chassidic practice and spiritual growth
- **Teaching**: Taught Chassidus to community members
- **Farbrengens**: Led Chassidic gatherings on Shabbos and holidays
- **Community anchor**: Was the spiritual center of the Chabad presence in Ponevezh

### Character

From what can be reconstructed:

- **Spiritual refinement**: Known for personal piety and inner refinement (the hallmarks of a true mashpia)
- **Dedication**: Committed to his community despite the challenging environment of Mitnagdic Ponevezh
- **Teaching quality**: Skilled at bringing Chassidic teachings to life

---

## Martyrdom (1941)

When Germany invaded Lithuania in June 1941:

- **Rapid destruction**: Lithuanian Jews were among the first to be systematically murdered
- **Ponevezh**: The city's Jewish community was murdered in the summer and fall of 1941
- **R' Lisson's death**: He was murdered with his community
- **Loss**: His spiritual guidance, his teachings, and his presence were cut off

---

## Legacy

R' Shmuel Tzvi Lisson is remembered:

- **Among martyred mashpiim**: Listed in Chabad memorial literature as one of the spiritual leaders murdered in the Holocaust
- **Ponevezh Chabad memory**: Represents the Chabad spiritual tradition that flourished in Ponevezh
- **The mashpia ideal**: His life and death exemplify the Chabad mashpia — one who serves with complete dedication
