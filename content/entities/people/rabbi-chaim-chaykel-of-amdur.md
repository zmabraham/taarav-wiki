# Rabbi Chaim Chaykel of Amdur

## Overview
Rabbi Chaim Chaykel of Amdur (Indura) was a disciple of the Maggid of Mezeritch who led the Chassidic community in Amdur (Belarus). He passed away in winter 5547 (1787).

## Details
He was one of the Maggid's disciples active in Belarus alongside the Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch, Rabbi Levi Yitzchak, and Rabbi Shlomo of Karlin.

After his passing, his young son Rabbi Shmuel (around age 20) took his place and began traveling to the Alter Rebbe to learn from him. This is confirmed by the interrogation records from the Alter Rebbe's first arrest in 1799, where one of the 30 Chassidim arrested with the Alter Rebbe — Rabbi Shmuel of Amdur — stated that he had visited the Alter Rebbe three times: nine years before (1790), five years before (1794), and the previous year (1798).
