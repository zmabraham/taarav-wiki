# Attorney Aharon Blum הי"ד

A lawyer in Warsaw who agreed to arrange the export of the Lubavitch Library books from Poland.

## Role in Library Rescue

When [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) needed to get the library out of Europe, Attorney Aharon Blum agreed to arrange the export for a fee of $1,000. He received $500 before Rayatz left Poland, with the remaining $500 to be paid upon completion.

He successfully arranged the shipment of 100 boxes of books via Stockholm to New York (arriving Sivan 5701 / 1941). However, 17 boxes remained in Warsaw and disappeared. Three boxes of manuscripts were left at the US Embassy in Warsaw, which were ultimately lost when the US entered the war.

## Martyrdom

Attorney Aharon Blum perished in the Holocaust. He is designated with הי"ד (may God avenge his blood).
