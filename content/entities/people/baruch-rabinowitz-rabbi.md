# R' Baruch Rabinowitz (הרב ר' ברוך רבינוביץ)

**Rabbi of the Ashkenazic community in Zuravichy; later rabbi in Chicago; teacher of young Israel Jacobson.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Rabbi of the Ashkenazic (non-Chasidic) community in Zuravichy |
| **Later role** | Rabbi in Chicago |
| **Family** | From the Shapiro rabbinical family; descendant of the Volozhiner yeshiva; son-in-law of R' Aryeh Leib Shapiro (rabbi in Yezna and Shrshov) |

## Overview

R' Baruch Rabinowitz was the rabbi of the Ashkenazic congregation in Zuravichy during Israel Jacobson's childhood. Around 5667 (winter, 1906/1907), when Jacobson was about 11 years old, his father entrusted him to R' Baruch to study Tractate Bava Basra. He found the boy talented.

R' Baruch initially recommended Yeshivas Reines in Lida (where secular studies were also taught), writing letters of recommendation for the boy. However, after a chance encounter at a train station (Molatchetzna) where two strangers persuaded them toward Lubavitch, the family chose Tomchei Temimim instead.

R' Baruch Rabinowitz eventually became a rabbi in Chicago.
