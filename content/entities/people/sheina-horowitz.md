# Sheina Horowitz

**Born**: c. 1906  
**Died**: c. 1942–1944, Holocaust (exact date and location unknown)  
**Role**: Daughter of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]; killed in the Holocaust

---

## Overview

Sheina Horowitz (née Schneersohn) was the daughter of [[entities/people/rayatz|Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn]] (the sixth Rebbe) and [[entities/people/nechama-dina|Rebbetzin Nechama Dina]]. She was murdered in the Holocaust along with her husband, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Wolff — a tragedy that left her father in inconsolable grief.

---

## Marriage and Polish Years

Sheina married Rabbi Menachem Mendel Wolff, and the wedding was held in Landwarow, Poland. This was during the Rayatz's Polish period (1933–1939), when he had relocated Chabad's center to Warsaw and Otwock.

---

## The Holocaust

When Nazi Germany invaded Poland in September 1939, the Rayatz was evacuated through extraordinary diplomatic effort. But Sheina and her husband were unable to escape. They were caught in the Nazi net that swept through Polish Jewry with devastating completeness.

Both Sheina and Rabbi Menachem Mendel Wolff were killed in the Holocaust. The exact circumstances — which camp, which date — are not recorded in the sources, reflecting the chaos and horror of mass murder.

---

## Her Father's Grief

The Rayatz never recovered from Sheina's loss. According to [[summaries/rayatz-biography|the biography *Undaunted*]], after the war, the Rayatz sat with photographs of his fallen Chasidim — including Sheina and Rabbis [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin|Feigin]] and others killed by the Nazis — weeping uncontrollably. Only visits from relatives brought him temporary relief from grief.

He carried her memory throughout the final years of his life in Brooklyn.
