# Alfred Bilmanis

Latvian diplomatic envoy (ambassador) to the United States during WWII. Assisted in the rescue of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) by facilitating his entry to Latvia.

## Role in Rescue

Bilmanis used his position as Latvia's representative in the US to help obtain the necessary documents for Rayatz to enter Latvia after escaping Warsaw. He coordinated with US Ambassador [[entities/people/alexander-kirk]] in Berlin.
