# Melech Kaplan (Rav)

A Chabad rav present in the Moscow/Yegarovsk area around Simchas Torah 5696 (1935). He was with the author at a private Simchas Torah farbrengen at a Litvak's (Lithuanian-Jewish) home near the Butyrkha prison, where they spoke about anash members then imprisoned.
