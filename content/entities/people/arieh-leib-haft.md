# Rabbi Arieh Leib Haft

Chabad rabbi and halachic authority; rabbi of Konotop (Kanatop) in Chernigov province and author of the responsa work Divrei Taam.

## Biography

A beloved disciple of the Tzemach Tzedek, who held him in great esteem. He married Frade Rasye from the Vapsi family of Dvinsk.

Served as rabbi in Karop for eighteen years, teaching young Torah students there — among them Rabbi Yehuda Leib Zirelson, who learned the method of studying the Talmud and halachic codes from him. Later served in Kaziletz before settling in Konotop (Kanatop) in Chernigov province, which was a distinctly Chabad town.

In Konotop he composed his halachic responsa, gathering material for his book Divrei Taam, printed in 5664 (1904) with a foreword by the Rashab of Ratsits (a grandson of the Tzemach Tzedek). The book's name encodes his first name: "Divrei" has the same numerical value (gematria) as "Arieh."

Among his responsa are letters exchanged with Rabbi Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (the Rashab) on halachic matters.

## Family
- Son-in-law: Rabbi Avraham Yechezkel Arlosorov
- Son Rabbi Ben Tzion Haft — moreh tzedek in Klinitsa; grandson Rabbi Binyamin Movshowitz — rabbi of Herzliya
- Daughter Aidele (wife of Rabbi Shimon HaKohen Amorai of Teitchin, a faculty member of Volozhin Yeshiva)
