# Moshe Binyamin Kaplan

A younger Chabad student who was being mentored by the author and others in Kyiv. He was present in the women's gallery of a Kyiv synagogue when the NKVD conducted a raid on their study group.

## Description
Moshe Binyamin Kaplan and Moshe Aharon Geisinsky were two younger students being educated by the senior students (including the memoir author) in the Kyiv underground. They were studying Gemara in the women's gallery when two plainclothes agents entered and began questioning them. Most of the seniors managed to escape through the windows, but the younger boys and some others were taken to the NKVD. Moshe Aharon Geisinsky later published his own memoirs of this period in "Kfar Chabad" journal no. 379 (28 Nisan 5749).
