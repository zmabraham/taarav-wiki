# Rabbi David Chai Abuchatzeira

Rabbi David Chai Abuchatzeira (born 24 Elul 5712 / September 14, 1952, Tafilalet, Morocco) is the former Chief Rabbi of Nahariya, son of the Baba Meir and grandson of the Baba Sali. He studied at Chabad institutions in Morocco and Kfar Chabad, and has maintained warm ties with Chabad throughout his life.

## Biography

Born in Tafilalet, Morocco, to Rabbi Meir Abuchatzeira son of the Baba Sali and his wife Simcha. At his birth, the Rebbe sent a letter of blessing to his father, writing: "…and on 24 Elul a male son was born and his name in Israel is David Chai for length of days and good years…and in his days we should merit the complete redemption through David Mashiach Tzidkeinu…"

As a child he studied at Chabad institutions in the town of Arfoud, Morocco. His father sent him at a young age to Tomchei Tmimim in Kfar Chabad, where he came close to Chabad and the Rebbe.

He settled in Nahariya and served as Chief Rabbi of the city. After retiring from that position, he established a large court (chatzer) where many come to receive his blessing.

On 1 Adar I 5768 (2008), the Tanya was printed in his office.

He signed the "Pesak Din" (halachic ruling) declaring the Rebbe to be Melech HaMashiach. When discussing the Rebbe's statement that miracles flow through the Abuchatzeira family "on the right, on the left, and in the middle," he explained: "Because the Rebbe said it, it became so." He added his theology: "All the influence of a generation comes through the tzaddik — everything physical and spiritual, advice, wisdom — all comes through the tzaddik."

## Connection to Chabad

As a member of the Abuchatzeira dynasty who studied at Chabad institutions and maintained a close relationship with the Rebbe, Rabbi David Chai represents the cross-pollination between the Sephardic kabbalistic tradition and Chabad Chassidus. His grandfather Baba Sali had a uniquely warm relationship with the Rebbe.
