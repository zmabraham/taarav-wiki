# Rebbetzin Sterna (Wife of the Alter Rebbe)

## Overview
Rebbetzin Sterna was the wife of Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi (the Alter Rebbe). She was known as an exceptionally wise, modest, and righteous woman who sacrificed greatly for her husband throughout his life.

## Details
She was the daughter of Reb Yehuda Leib Segal of Vitebsk, described as a distinguished and prominent man in the city. Her mother was named Beila.

She was married in Vitebsk, where the Alter Rebbe lived for approximately 17 years before moving to Horodok.

Beit Rebbi praises her: "She was a great, modest, distinguished woman with wisdom and knowledge. Words of wisdom were heard from her. She had great self-sacrifice for the Rebbe from the very beginning when she was in her father's home, and later when they lived in Mohilev (on the Dniester), and then throughout his rabbinic leadership, especially when he was taken to Petersburg."

Her family was intertwined with the rabbinic and lay leadership of Vitebsk. Her father Yehuda Leib Segal was a cousin of the Kissin family — which included:
- Rabbi Yisrael Kissin, Chief Rabbi of Vitebsk (a student of the Vilna Gaon)
- Rabbi Yaakov Kritziver, Rabbi of Vitebsk
- Lipman Kissin, lay leader (parnas) of Vitebsk
