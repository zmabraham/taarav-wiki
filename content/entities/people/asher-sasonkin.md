# Asher Sasonkin

A Chabad chassid who married Freyde Levin (the younger sister of the memoir author) in Samarkand in Tammuz 5704 (summer 1944). The author arranged the match and rented them an apartment. R. Sasonkin worked distributing bread from a kiosk.
