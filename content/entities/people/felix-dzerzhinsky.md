## Biography

Polish-born Bolshevik revolutionary who founded the Cheka, the first Soviet secret police organization (later GPU, OGPU, NKVD). Established the apparatus of terror that would be used to persecute religious communities including Chabad. Although he died in 1926 before the 1927 arrest, his organizational legacy was the instrument of the Rebbe's persecution.
