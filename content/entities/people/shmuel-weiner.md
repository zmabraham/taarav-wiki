# R' Shmuel Weiner

Bibliographer and book collector based in Leningrad (formerly St. Petersburg). His collection of Hebrew books and manuscripts became the foundation of the Lubavitch Library.

## Library Sale

R' Shmuel Weiner assembled a major collection of Hebrew books, manuscripts, and Judaica in Leningrad. In **5685 (1924)**, [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) purchased this collection for **$5,000**. The contract of sale was signed on **8 Marcheshvan 5685**.

The purchase required permission from Soviet authorities to export the collection from Russia. R' [[entities/people/mordechai-dubin]] (Riga) and Dr. A. Cohen (Berlin) helped obtain this permission.

This collection, renamed "Bibliothek Lyubawitch" (Lubavitch Library), traveled with Rayatz from Leningrad to Riga, then to Warsaw, and then to Otwock.
