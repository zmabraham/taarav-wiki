# Rabbi Pinchas HaKohen of Ansbach — ר' פנחס הכהן מאנסבאך

**Role**: Orthodox rabbi of Ansbach, Bavaria; correspondent of the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]]  
**Location**: Ansbach, Bavaria, Germany  
**Period**: Late 19th – early 20th century

---

## Overview

Rabbi Pinchas HaKohen was the rabbi of Ansbach, a city in Franconia (Bavaria), and corresponded with the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]] regarding support for Russian Jewry through Agudas Yisrael. His correspondence is preserved in the Chabad-Germany volume.

---

## Correspondence with the Rashab

The Rashab's letters to Rabbi Pinchas HaKohen focused on the practical matter of supporting Russian Jewish interests through the Agudas Yisrael framework. This correspondence reflects the Rashab's broader effort, during his German sojourns (particularly the [[events/wurzburg-year-1906|Würzburg year of 1906–1907]]), to enlist German Orthodox leaders in the cause of their beleaguered Russian counterparts.

---

## Context: Chabad and Agudas Yisrael

The Rashab's relationship with [[entities/people/yaakov-rosenheim|Rabbi Yaakov Rosenheim]] (president of Agudas Yisrael) and other German Orthodox leaders during this period reflected his conviction that Orthodox Jews across national boundaries needed coordinated action to counter both governmental persecution in Russia and the challenges of the Enlightenment and Zionism.
