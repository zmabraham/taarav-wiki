# Sarah Levin (sister)

Sister of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin (the memoir author); daughter of R. Efraim Shmuel Levin and Leah Beila. Older than the author's youngest sister Rivka, and older than Freyde. Her birthday is on Yom Kippur.

## Description
Mentioned in the letter written by her brother to the Rayatz in Iyar 5694 (1934): "my sister Sarah, may she live, will turn twenty on Yom Kippur." The memoir does not provide further details about her fate.
