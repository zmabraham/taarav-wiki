# Rabbi Menachem Nachum Schneersohn of Niezhin

**Born**: 1820  
**Died**: 1883  
**Father**: [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]]  
**Court**: Niezhin, Ukraine

---

## Overview

Rabbi Menachem Nachum Schneersohn was a son of the [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]] who established the Niezhin branch of Chabad after his father's passing in 1866. Niezhin (also spelled Nezhyn) is a city in the Chernihiv region of Ukraine.

---

## The Niezhin Branch

The Niezhin branch was one of several Chabad offshoots that emerged when the Tzemach Tzedek's seven sons each established independent courts. While smaller than the [[entities/people/yehuda-leib-kopust|Kopust branch]], the Niezhin court maintained the Chabad tradition of combining Talmudic scholarship with deep study of Chassidic philosophy.

Like the other non-Lubavitch branches, the Niezhin line gradually diminished as the main [[entities/places/lubavitch|Lubavitch]] branch — led by the [[entities/people/rebbe-maharash|Maharash]], then the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rashab]], and then the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] — consolidated its position as the central and ultimately sole surviving Chabad movement.
