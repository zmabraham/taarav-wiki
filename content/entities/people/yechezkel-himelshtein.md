# R' Yechezkel Himelshtein (ר' יחזקאל הימלשטיין)

**Mashgiach (supervisor/dean) at Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Mashgiach at Tomchei Temimim |
| **Origin** | Schedrин (same town as R' Yechiel Komisar) |
| **Prior role** | Graduate of TT; then assistant rabbi in Rodnya |
| **Later role** | Mashgiach in the children's section (Chadarim) of TT |

## Overview

R' Yechezkel Himelshtein was the primary mashgiach (administrative supervisor) encountered by Israel Jacobson upon his arrival at Tomchei Temimim in 5668 (1907/1908). He introduced new students to the rebbe melamdim and placed them in appropriate classes based on testing.

When Jacobson first arrived, Himelshtein assigned him to the class of R' Yechiel Komisar after examining him on Motzaei Shabbat. He was a graduate of TT who had served as an assistant rabbi in Rodnya before returning as mashgiach.

Jacobson describes a notable scene where Himelshtein repeatedly deferred transferring him to a higher class — Jacobson had to demonstrate he could independently explain a Tosafos before Himelshtein would consider it. Even then, he was delayed further, frustrating the young student.
