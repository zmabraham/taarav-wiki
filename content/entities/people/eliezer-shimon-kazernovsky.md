# R. Eliezer Shimon Kazernovsky

A Chabad figure in [[hebron|Hebron]] documented in 5666 (1906). He represents the Chabad community in Hebron in the late Ottoman period, in the decades before the 1929 Hebron Massacre that ended the continuous Chabad presence there.

## Historical Context

By 1906, Hebron had been the primary Chabad center in the Holy Land for nearly a century (since the Mitteler Rebbe's directive in 1821). The community had its own yeshiva (Yeshiva Tzemach Tzedek) and kolel structure. The 20th century brought new challenges, including competing ideological movements and eventual Arab violence.

## Hebron Chabad in His Era

The Chabad community in Hebron in 1906 consisted of several leading families and scholars. The community maintained close ties with the Lubavitch court in Russia. Rabbi Kazernovsky was a known figure in this community.

## Family Name

The surname "Kazernovsky" suggests origins in Kazernovka or a similar town in the Russian/Polish Empire.
