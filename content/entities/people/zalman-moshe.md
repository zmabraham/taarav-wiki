# Zalman Moshe (Reb Zalman Moshe)

A senior and deeply spiritual Chabad chassid who was based in or near Malachovka/Moscow in the 1930s. He is described as praying with extraordinary length and intensity, "going beyond the norm in the service of prayer." He had great compassion and encouragement for younger chassidim.

## Description
R. Zalman Moshe appears in the memoir during the difficult period around Pesach 5694, when the author was desolate and unsure of his path forward. The author noted how R. Zalman Moshe prayed in the beis midrash with extraordinary fervor, and then after prayer spoke with the young men, asking after their welfare. The author confided in him about his problems, particularly about the expired passport that needed to be renewed, with the hostile official at Yegarovsk. R. Zalman Moshe encouraged him, gave him a blessing, and directed him to write to the Rebbe for guidance.

When the author showed R. Zalman Moshe subsequent letters from the Rebbe, he encouraged him even more. R. Zalman Moshe's wife also handled financial missions on behalf of Rav Zalman Schneersohn.

His full name is likely Zalman Moshe Eliyashvili or similar — the text simply calls him "ר' זלמן משה."
