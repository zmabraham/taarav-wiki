# Rabbi Yitzchak Horowitz (Masmidam) הי"ד

Chabad rabbi in Riga, Latvia. Known by the appellation "Masmidam" (המסמידם).

## Martyrdom

R' Yitzchak Horowitz was burned alive in a synagogue by the Nazis on **10 Kislev 5702** (approximately December 1941), together with:
- R' [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin]]
- R' [[entities/people/eliyahu-chaim-althoiz]]
