# Rabbi Yitzchak Yaakov Weiss

Rabbi Yitzchak Yaakov Weiss was the Gaavad (head of the rabbinical court) of the Edah HaChareidis in Jerusalem and author of the "Minchat Yitzchak" responsa series. He was a widely respected posek (halachic decisor).

## Connection to the Rebbe

In 5719 (1959) he sent his book to the Rebbe and received a warm response. In his books he mentions the Rebbe with respect and admiration.

In 5736 (1976) at a gathering of eight thousand people in Jerusalem, Chabad emissaries sought to have the Twelve Pesukim (Verses) recited. They approached Rabbi Weiss in the morning, explained their purpose, and showed him the list. He asked about the practice and was told the Rebbe considered these verses and sayings particularly effective against enemies. He arranged for them to be incorporated into the program.

When Rabbi Moshe Weber informed him that the Rebbe had mentioned the "Minchat Yitzchak" favorably at a farbrengen, Rabbi Weiss called for the Chabad emissaries to elaborate on what the Rebbe had said.

In 5733 (1973), on the Rebbe's 70th birthday, Rabbi Weiss sent a letter of blessing, writing: "Fortunate is he and fortunate is his generation in his exalted activities and great influence, bringing back many from the depths and drawing them under the wings of the Divine Presence in all the places of their dispersal. May Hashem continue to grant His Honor strength and good long years to illuminate the multitudes with love of Torah and Chassidus and fear of Heaven until the coming of the righteous redeemer."
