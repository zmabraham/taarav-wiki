# Mordechai Sternberg

Son of Rabbi Shlomo Sternberg (rav of Lipovetsh). Grew up in Lipovetsh during the Soviet period and was close with Yosef Levin (older brother of the memoir author). He subsequently studied at the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in Nevel and in Kharkov, left the USSR in 5706 (1946), and settled in Bnei Brak from 5723 (1963).

## Description
Mordechai was close with Yosef (the author's older brother), and both studied Gemara, Rashi and Tosafos with the same melamed. On the occasion of Mordechai's own bar mitzvah (or that of a sibling), Yosef sat next to him at the celebration. After the Levin family's time in Lipovetsh, Mordechai went on to study in the underground Tomchei Temimim.
