# R' Chaim Yosef Rozenblum

**Chabad leader in Eretz Yisrael; founding committee member of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael (1941)**

## Biography

R' Chaim Yosef Rozenblum was a dedicated Chabad Chassid and communal leader who settled in Eretz Yisrael and became one of the founding figures of Agudas Chassidei Chabad there. He was a member of the 1941 founding committee alongside R' Shaul Dov Zislin and R' Moshe Gourarie.

R' Rozenblum was among the Chabad Chassidim who arrived in Eretz Yisrael from Eastern Europe and built a new communal life in the Land. His involvement in the founding committee of Agudas Chassidei Chabad indicates that he was recognized as one of the community's natural leaders. The organization he helped found became the primary institutional expression of Chabad identity in the Land of Israel.

The wartime context of 1941 — with Europe in flames and massive immigration to Eretz Yisrael — gave the founding of Agudas Chassidei Chabad particular urgency. The organization needed to address both the needs of new refugees and the maintenance of existing Chabad institutions.

## Significance

R' Rozenblum's role in founding the official Chabad organization in Eretz Yisrael represents the transition from informal community to formal institution. This organizational work, though not as dramatic as other chapters of Chabad history, was essential for the long-term survival and growth of Chabad in the Land of Israel. His presence alongside other founding members illustrates the collaborative leadership of the Eretz Yisrael Chabad community.
