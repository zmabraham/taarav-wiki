# Yosef Levin (brother)

Older brother of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin (the memoir author), son of R. Efraim Shmuel Levin and Leah Beila. He died as a martyr (Hei"d — may God avenge his blood) and is mentioned with the memorial abbreviation indicating he was killed.

## Description
In the pogrom chapters of the memoir, the author mentions hiding under a blanket with his brother Yosef as a pogromist entered their home. He is referred to as "אחי יוסף הי"ד" — indicating he was later murdered by enemies of Israel. The exact circumstances of his death are not described in the surviving memoir pages.

Yosef was close with Mordechai, son of Rabbi Shlomo Sternberg. A teacher taught both of them Gemara, Rashi, and Tosafos, and Yosef sat next to the bar mitzvah boy at the Sternberg family's bar mitzvah celebration.
