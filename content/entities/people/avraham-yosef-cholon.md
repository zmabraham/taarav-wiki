# Rabbi Avraham Yosef of Cholon

Rabbi Avraham Yosef (born 25 Tammuz 5709 / 1949, Egypt) is the de facto Mara D'Atra of Cholon and a prominent Sephardic posek. He is the son of Rabbi Ovadia Yosef. He served as Sephardic Chief Rabbi of Cholon and member of the Chief Rabbinate Council of Israel.

## Biography

Born in Egypt to Rabbi Ovadia Yosef and Rebbetzin Margalit. He studied at Porat Yosef, Kol Yosef, and Torah V'Hora'ah yeshivot.

He served in the IDF for 13 years in various rabbinate roles including base rabbi at the Rephidim Air Force Base, district rabbi of Haifa, and deputy Air Force rabbi.

He served as community rabbi in northern Tel Aviv for 30 years. In 5759 (1999) he was elected Sephardic Chief Rabbi of Cholon alongside Rabbi Eliyahu Yochanan Guraria (Ashkenazic Chief Rabbi). He also served as acting Chief Rabbi of Or Yehuda (5768–5771) and as a member of the Chief Rabbinate Council.

He was a candidate for Rishon LeTzion in 5773 but ultimately did not run. In 5777 he was convicted of breach of trust (receiving a suspended sentence), resulting in his resignation from the Chief Rabbinate positions. He continues to serve as rabbi of the Magen David synagogue in Cholon and is widely recognized as the de facto Mara D'Atra. He hosts a weekly call-in halachic question-and-answer program on Radio Kol Chai.

## Connection to Chabad

Rabbi Avraham Yosef has been associated with Chabad events and activities over many years. His key statements include: "Were it not for Chabad — most of Judaism would assimilate." He ruled that Sephardim may recite the blessing on Chabad tefillin. He has participated in Torah gatherings at Tomchei Tmimim Migdal HaEmek, Kfar Chabad, and elsewhere, and visited the Berlin Chabad yeshiva. In 5785 he generated controversy by stating that Chabad Chassidim "fulfill their obligation after the fact" (b'dieved) when praying late due to prior Chassidus study.
