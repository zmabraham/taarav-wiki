# R. Reuven Grazovsky

The **Rosh Yeshiva (dean)** of **Kamenitz Yeshiva** (Kamenets-Litovsk), one of the major Lithuanian yeshivos. He is mentioned in the memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* as having helped students emigrate from Russia, with connections both to Russia and to America.

## Kamenitz Yeshiva

Kamenitz (Kamenets Litovsk, now in Belarus) hosted one of the great Lithuanian yeshivos, founded by Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz (1866–1939). The yeshiva was known for its rigorous Talmudic study and produced many leading rabbinic figures. R. Grazovsky succeeded or served alongside the founding leadership.

## Role in Emigration

During and after WWII, R. Reuven Grazovsky used his position and connections to help students and members of the yeshiva world obtain the documentation needed to emigrate from Russia to America. The memoir's narrator credits him specifically as having helped facilitate emigration.

## Russia-America Connections

His effectiveness stemmed from connections in both:
- **Russia/Eastern Europe**: Knowledge of how to navigate Soviet and Eastern European bureaucracy
- **America**: The American yeshiva world and its advocacy capacity
