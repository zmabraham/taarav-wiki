# R' Yitzchak Eber

**Rosh yeshiva (head) of Tomchei Temimim in the Warsaw region; leader of Chabad education in Poland**

## Biography

R' Yitzchak Eber served as rosh yeshiva of Tomchei Temimim in the Warsaw region of Poland during the late Rayatz era. As rosh yeshiva, he was responsible for the Talmudic curriculum and the overall academic direction of the yeshiva, while mashpi'im like R' Moshe Chefetz and R' Eliyahu Chaim Althaus handled the Chassidic spiritual dimension.

The Warsaw Tomchei Temimim branch was one of the most significant outside of the Riga center during the period of the Rayatz's Polish residence (1933–1939). Warsaw itself had one of the largest Jewish populations in the world, and the Chabad presence there included several significant figures.

R' Eber's work as rosh yeshiva in Poland placed him in one of the most dangerous situations when the German invasion began in September 1939. The fate of Polish Chabad yeshiva leaders during the Holocaust was often tragic — many were killed.

## Significance

R' Yitzchak Eber represents the Talmudic scholarly leadership of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva, complementing the mashpia role. His position in the Warsaw region during the crucial years before the Holocaust makes him a figure who embodies both the vitality of interwar Polish Chabad and its tragic destruction. His work helped maintain Chabad's dual emphasis on Chassidus and Talmud study.
