# Rabbi Moshe of Pozna

Paternal great-grandfather of the [[alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] (Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi). According to Chabad genealogical tradition, Rabbi Moshe of Pozna was descended from the **Maharal of Prague** (Rabbi Yehuda Loew ben Bezalel, 1525–1609), making the Alter Rebbe a descendant of one of the most prominent Ashkenazic rabbinic dynasties.

## Genealogical Line

The chain of descent as documented in "Beit Rebbi" and corroborated by archival records:

1. **Rabbi Moshe of Pozna** — the ancestor
2. His son (name unclear in sources) — settled in Belarus region
3. **Rabbi Schneur Zalman** (grandfather of the Alter Rebbe)
4. **Rabbi Baruch** (father of the Alter Rebbe; born Lozna; died c. 5552/1792)
5. **Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi** (the Alter Rebbe, born 5505/1745)

The genealogy appears in "HaYom Yom" (compiled by the 7th Rebbe from his father's records): "...the R. Moshe. 5) his son the R. Schneur Zalman. 6) his son the R. Baruch. 7) his son... the Alter Rebbe."

## Descent from the Maharal

The claim of descent from the Maharal of Prague is part of Chabad oral tradition and appears in multiple Chabad chronicles. The Toldos Chabad beRussia haTzarit discusses this genealogy extensively, including challenges raised by the book "Igros Baal haTanya" which questions certain details of the genealogical record.

## Historical Context

Pozna (Poznań, in western Poland) was a major center of Jewish learning in the medieval and early modern period. The Maharal himself served in nearby communities. If the genealogical tradition is accurate, Rabbi Moshe would have lived in the 17th century.
