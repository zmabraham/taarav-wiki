# Major Morozov

**Active**: 1825, Vitebsk gubernia, Russia  
**Role**: Russian military officer (Major); third investigating officer in the 1825 Mitteler Rebbe case

---

## Overview

Major Morozov was the third officer assigned by [[entities/people/governor-khavanski|Governor-General Prince Khavanski]] to the 1825 investigation of [[entities/people/simcha-kissin|Simcha Kissin's]] denunciations against the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]]. He appears alongside [[entities/people/loginov|Polkovnik Loginov]] and [[entities/people/markov-investigator|Court Counselor Markov]] in the investigation order of 2 October 1825, signing jointly with them on the formal interrogation questions presented to the Rebbe (7 October 1825).

---

## Role in the Investigation

Morozov signed the 7 October 1825 interrogation document alongside Loginov and Markov, but appears less prominently in subsequent documents. Later administrative documents reference "the late adjutant Volasenko" being sent on a separate mission to Staroselye to bring [[entities/people/aaron-halevi-staroselye|R' Aharon HaLevi of Staroselye]] to Vitebsk for questioning — a mission that appears to have been Morozov's or a separate officer's responsibility.

The primary investigating pair in the surviving archival record is Loginov and Markov, suggesting Morozov may have been reassigned or played a supporting role.
