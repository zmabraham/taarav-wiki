# Rabbi Yisrael Yehuda Levi

**Born**: 7 Tammuz 5672 (1912), Zviitov, Ukraine  
**Died**: c. 1990  
**Role**: Memoirist of the Soviet Chabad underground; subject of _Zichronosai_ (Vol. 8 of _Mesibbv L'Chassidus_ series)

---

## Overview

Rabbi Yisrael Yehuda Levi was a product and survivor of the Chabad underground in the Soviet Union. Over a decade (1927-1938), he moved through twelve different cities as part of the clandestine yeshiva network that kept Torah study alive under Soviet persecution. His memoirs, published as _Zichronosai_, provide an invaluable firsthand account of this underground world and are a primary source for understanding the Chabad experience under Soviet rule.

---

## Early Life

Born on 7 Tammuz 5672 (1912) in Zviitov, Ukraine, Levi grew up during the tumultuous years of World War I, the Russian Revolution, and the establishment of the Soviet state. As a young man, he entered the underground Chabad yeshiva network — the clandestine system of Torah study that operated in defiance of Soviet anti-religious policy.

---

## The Underground Yeshiva Years (1927-1938)

For eleven years, Levi was part of the network of underground yeshivos maintained by Chabad chassidim throughout the Soviet Union. This network — organized under the direction of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] and his representatives — moved constantly to evade the Soviet secret police. Levi passed through **twelve different cities** during this period, studying and teaching Torah under conditions of extreme danger and deprivation.

This period of his life is the central subject of his memoirs and represents one of the most dramatic chapters in modern Jewish history.

---

## Escape from the Soviet Union (1946)

In 1946, Levi was part of the mass escape of Chabad chassidim from the Soviet Union. He used forged Polish identity papers in the name **"Naftali Bat"** to cross the Soviet border, joining the stream of refugees who made their way westward through Poland and eventually to the displaced persons camps in Europe.

See [[events/great-escape-1946|The Great Escape (1946)]].

---

## Life in Kfar Chabad

In 1949, Levi settled in **Kfar Chabad**, the Chabad village in Israel, where he lived for the remainder of his life. He made his first trip to the [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|Seventh Rebbe]] in Brooklyn for Tishrei 5723 (1962).

---

## Legacy

Levi's son, **Rabbi Shalom DovBer Levin**, became the editor of the _Mesibbv L'Chassidus_ series, ensuring that his father's memoirs and those of other Soviet Chabad survivors were preserved and published for future generations.
