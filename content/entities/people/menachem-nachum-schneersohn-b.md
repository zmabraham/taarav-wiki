# R. Menachem Nachum Schneersohn (Son of Mitteler Rebbe)

Son of the [[mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] (Rabbi DovBer Schneuri) and brother-in-law of the [[tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]] (Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn III). He served as one of the Tzemach Tzedek's key assistants in administering **Kolel Chabad** — the central Chabad charitable organization for the Holy Land.

## Role with Kolel Chabad

The Tzemach Tzedek, who succeeded the Mitteler Rebbe as head of Chabad-Lubavitch, took over the administration of Kolel Chabad in Russia. He was assisted by two close family members:

1. **Rabbi Chaim Avraham Schneersohn** — Son of the Alter Rebbe (Tzemach Tzedek's uncle)
2. **Rabbi Menachem Nachum Schneersohn** — Son of the Mitteler Rebbe (Tzemach Tzedek's brother-in-law)

This family structure ensured loyal administration of the important charitable fund.

## Family Context

- Father: [[mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] (Rabbi DovBer Schneuri, 1773–1827)
- Brother-in-law: [[tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]] (who married the Mitteler Rebbe's daughter)
- He was part of the extended Schneersohn family that led various branches of Chabad after the Tzemach Tzedek's six sons spread to different towns

## Distinguished From

This Rabbi Menachem Nachum Schneersohn should be distinguished from:
- **Rabbi Menachem Nachum of Niezhin** — a different Chabad figure
- **Rabbi Menachem Nachum of Chernobyl** — founder of the Chernobyl Chassidic dynasty (unrelated)
