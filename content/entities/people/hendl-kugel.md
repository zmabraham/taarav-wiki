# Rabbi Hendl Kugel

**Lived**: 19th century, Pleszczenice (Minsk gubernia, Russia)  
**Role**: _Mashpia_ (spiritual guide) of the Chabad community in Pleszczenice

---

## Overview

Rabbi Hendl Kugel served as the _mashpia_ (Chassidic spiritual mentor) of the Chabad community in the town of Pleszczenice (Плещеницы) in the Minsk gubernia of the Russian Empire. He is documented as the spiritual guide of [[entities/people/avraham-chaim|Rabbi Avraham Chaim Morosow]], illustrating how the Chabad communal structure extended from Lubavitch's center to towns throughout White Russia (Belarus).

---

## The Mashpia Role

In Chabad communal life, the _mashpia_ played a role analogous to that of the _mashpia_ in Tomchei Temimim yeshiva — a guide focused on spiritual development, internalization of Chassidic values, and the relationship with the Rebbe. In community settings outside the major yeshivas, the mashpia provided:

- Regular classes in Chassidic texts
- Individual spiritual guidance
- Connection to the broader Chabad world and Rebbe

---

## Significance

Rabbi Hendl Kugel's mention in [[summaries/toldot-avraham-chaim|*Toldot Avraham Chaim*]] illustrates how Chabad's institutional structure permeated even small provincial towns — with trained spiritual guides providing organized communal religious life far from the center in Lubavitch.
