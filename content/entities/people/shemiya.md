# Rabbi Shemiya

A Chabad Chassid who resided in [[safed|Safed]] (Tzfat) and was **killed in the great earthquake of 5597 (1837)**. Rabbi Shemiya represents the Chabad presence in Safed even after most Chassidim had relocated to [[hebron|Hebron]] following the Mitteler Rebbe's directive in 5581 (1821).

## The 1837 Earthquake

The earthquake of 1 Shevat 5597 (January 1, 1837) was catastrophic for Safed and Tiberias. The earthquake destroyed much of both cities and killed thousands of residents. According to *Toldos Chabad beEretz HaKodesh*, Rabbi Shemiya (who had remained in Safed) was among the Chabad victims killed in the disaster.

Other Chabad casualties in the earthquake included:
- **R. Lapidot Chalavna** and his son **Shalom Aharon** (also killed in Safed)
- **R. Leib Baal HaYisurim** had moved from Safed to Hebron before the earthquake (he died 15 Marcheshvan 5597, just weeks before the earthquake)

## Why Some Remained in Safed

Despite the general directive to relocate to Hebron, some Chabad Chassidim remained in Safed and Tiberias for various reasons — family ties, economic considerations, or simply the impracticality of relocating. The earthquake served as a tragic confirmation, in hindsight, of the wisdom of the Mitteler Rebbe's directive.
