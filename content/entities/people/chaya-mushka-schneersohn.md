# Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka Schneersohn

Middle daughter of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz). Married [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-schneerson]], the future 7th Lubavitcher Rebbe.

## Escape from Europe

During WWII, she and her husband were trapped in Vichy France (Nice). They escaped to Lisbon and arrived in New York on **28 Sivan 5701** aboard the ship "Serpa Pinta."
