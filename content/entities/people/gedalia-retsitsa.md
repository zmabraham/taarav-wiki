# Gedalia of Retsitsa

A student from Retsitsa who studied at the Tomchei Temimim in Vitebsk before the memoir author. His enthusiastic letters home about the yeshiva — describing the developed learning and the community there — inspired the author and Avrahamele Kvash to travel to Vitebsk.
