# Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka Schneerson

Rebbetzin Chaya Mushka (1901–1988) was the daughter of the Rayatz and wife of the 7th Rebbe. She married Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson in 1928 in Warsaw. Known for her private dignity and deep wisdom, she was a guiding presence throughout the 7th Rebbe's leadership.

## Connection to Chabad

She was the last link connecting the 7th Rebbe to the Rayatz's household and played a quiet but essential role in the transition of leadership.
