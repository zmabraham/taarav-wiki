# Rabbi Mordechai Dubin

Prominent leader of the Jewish community in Riga, Latvia. Member of the Latvian Seim (parliament). A central figure in Chabad affairs in Latvia and a close associate of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz).

## Role

R' Dubin used his political connections to assist Jewish causes, including helping obtain Soviet permission to export R' [[entities/people/shmuel-weiner]]'s book collection from Russia in 1924. He coordinated many of Rayatz's affairs in Latvia.

## WWII Fate

When Soviet forces occupied Latvia in 1940, R' Dubin was arrested by the Soviet secret police (NKVD/KGB). He was later released on **23 Kislev 5702** (approximately December 1941). He survived the war in Saratov, Russia.
