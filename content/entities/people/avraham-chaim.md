# Rabbi Avraham Chaim Morosow

**Primary Source**: [[summaries/toldot-avraham-chaim|תולדות אברהם חיים]] (Vol. 2 of the Mesibbv L'Chassidus series)

---

## Overview

Rabbi Avraham Chaim Morosow is the central figure of the second volume in the Chabad history series. His life story documents the experience of Chabad in the Soviet period through the lens of an individual emissary (_shliach_) — his training, his mission, his persecution, and his survival.

---

## Significance

His biography serves as a representative account of the generation of Tomchei Temimim graduates who maintained Chabad life under Soviet conditions. The detail preserved in _Toldot Avraham Chaim_ illustrates the institutional network the Rayatz built and sustained from afar.
