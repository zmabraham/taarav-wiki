## Biography

Soviet GPU secret police agent who led the operation to arrest Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak in Leningrad on June 15, 1927. The arrest took place at midnight at the Rebbe's residence. Nachmanson conducted the initial phases of the case against the Rebbe.
