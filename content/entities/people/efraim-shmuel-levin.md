# R' Efraim Shmuel Levin

Father of the memoirist R' Yisrael Yehuda Levin; a devoted melamed (Torah teacher) who maintained a traditional *cheder* in Lipovitz during the early Soviet era.

## Biography

Efraim Shmuel Levin was born around 5638 in Zhivitiov, Ukraine. After his father R' Yosef Levin (rabbi) died around 5649 (1889), Efraim Shmuel and his siblings were left as orphans. He studied at the yeshiva in Berditchev, then married Leah Beila Bilienky from Dashov.

In Zhivitiov the family ran a small shop, with the mother managing it during market fairs while the father ran a *cheder*. He was known for his great fear of God.

During the pogroms of winter 5680 (1919–1920), Efraim Shmuel was attacked by a bandit who struck at his head; he defended himself with his arm and was wounded but escaped. The family fled Zhivitiov just before Passover 5680.

In Lipovitz, despite increasing Soviet pressure, he continued to run a *cheder*, teaching children Torah, Chumash with Rashi, writing, Hebrew, Nakh, grammar, and arithmetic. He was publicly mocked by the Yevsektsiya in anti-religion Chanukah campaigns (5681/1920). When the Soviet authorities demanded that parents send children to Soviet schools under threat of arrest, he refused to comply. His family was designated "lishentsyes" (stripped of civil rights) as a result.

He died 15 Shvat 5693 (1933), leaving his children as orphans. His son Yisrael Yehuda was 22 years old at the time (born 7 Tammuz 5672).
