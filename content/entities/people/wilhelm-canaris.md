# Admiral Wilhelm Canaris

Head of the Abwehr (German military intelligence). Despite his senior position in the Nazi military apparatus, Canaris played a role in facilitating the rescue of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) from Warsaw in late 1939.

## Role in Rescue

Canaris used his position to enable Rayatz's passage from Warsaw through Berlin to Riga. Two German officers of partial Jewish ancestry — Klaus Shenk and Ernst Bloch — worked under or through Canaris's influence in facilitating the rescue.

Canaris was later executed by the Nazis in April 1945 for his involvement in anti-Hitler resistance activities, particularly his connections to the July 1944 assassination plot.
