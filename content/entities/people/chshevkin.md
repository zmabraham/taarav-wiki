# Chshevkin

A minor Russian official mentioned in the archival documents of the **Mitteler Rebbe trial** (5585–5588 / 1825–1828). The exact nature of Chshevkin's role is unclear from the available documents — he appears as a deputy or minor official in the proceedings against the [[mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]].

## Context

The trial of the Mitteler Rebbe involved a cascade of Russian officials at multiple levels:
- **Governor [[khovansky|Khovansky]]** of Vitebsk — the primary prosecutor/initiator
- **Colonel [[loginov|Loginov]]** — special investigator
- **[[markov-investigator|Markov]]** — court advisor co-investigating with Loginov
- **[[korbitsky|Korbitsky]]** — police official who traveled to Lubavitch to summon the Rebbe
- **Chshevkin** — minor deputy role (function unclear from surviving documents)
