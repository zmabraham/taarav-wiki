# Rabbi Menachem Mendel Horenstein הי"ד

Son-in-law of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz), husband of [[entities/people/sheina-horenstein]].

## Life and Martyrdom

R' Horenstein was based in Otwock, Poland. When Germany invaded in September 1939 and Rayatz escaped to the USA, R' Horenstein and Rebbetzin Sheine remained trapped. Despite rescue efforts coordinated from the USA, they could not obtain the necessary documents to escape.

They were deported and murdered at **Treblinka** extermination camp. The date is variously recorded as 15 Elul, 3 Tishrei, or 25 Cheshvan 5703 (1942–1943).
