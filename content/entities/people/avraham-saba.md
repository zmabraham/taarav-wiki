# R' Avraham Saba

Medieval rabbinic scholar, author of the Torah commentary **Tzror HaMor** (Bundle of Myrrh). Lived in the late 15th century, was expelled from Spain in 1492.

## Manuscript in Lubavitch Library

A handwritten manuscript by R' Avraham Saba was noted as one of the prized items in the Lubavitch Library when it was housed in Riga. The manuscript was written approximately 440 years before the 1928 article describing it (placing it around 1488), making it a pre-expulsion document.

The manuscript's existence in the library was described in a report in the periodical "HaOlam" (London, 22 Elul 5688 / 1928).
