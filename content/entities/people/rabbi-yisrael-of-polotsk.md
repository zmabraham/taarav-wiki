# Rabbi Yisrael of Polotsk

## Overview
Rabbi Yisrael of Polotsk was one of the three leading disciples of the Maggid of Mezeritch appointed to lead the Chassidim in Russia and Lithuania. He was one of those who made aliyah to Eretz Yisrael in 1777 with Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk.

## Details
He was designated along with the Alter Rebbe and Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch as a leader of the Chassidim in Russia and Lithuania in a letter sent by Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk on 4 Adar 5542 (1782).

He made aliyah in 1777 with Rabbi Menachem Mendel, apparently intending to return to Russia but never did. He passed away in Eretz Yisrael, and according to Beit Rebbi, is buried near the resting place of Rabbi Avraham the Angel (son of the Maggid of Mezeritch) in the town of Fastov.

After his passing (around 1783), the leadership of the Chassidim in Belarus and Lithuania remained with the Alter Rebbe and Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch, until the latter subordinated himself to the Alter Rebbe.
