# R' Pinchas Klein

Chabad survivor based in Warsaw. Participated in post-war recovery of the Lubavitch Library books.

## Post-War Activity

Together with R' [[entities/people/shlomo-zilberberg]], R' Pinchas Klein located surviving Lubavitch Library books in Warsaw after the war. The books were held by the Jewish Central Committee's historical division and some were eventually returned.
