# Rebbetzin Menucha Rochel Slonim

**Born**: 1798, Lyady, Russia  
**Died**: 1888, Hebron, Eretz Yisrael  
**Role**: Granddaughter of the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]]; daughter of [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]]; settled in Hebron 1845; spiritual anchor of the Hebron Chabad community for over 40 years

---

## Overview

Rebbetzin Menucha Rochel Slonim was one of the most remarkable women in Chabad history. A direct descendant of the first two Lubavitcher Rebbes (granddaughter of the Alter Rebbe, daughter of the Mitteler Rebbe), she made aliyah to Hebron in 1845 at the age of 47 and lived there until her death at 90 in 1888 — providing the Chabad community in Hebron with dynastic legitimacy and spiritual authority for over four decades.

---

## Lineage

Menucha Rochel was:

- Granddaughter of [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi]] (Alter Rebbe), founder of Chabad
- Daughter of [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Rabbi DovBer Schneersohn]] (Mitteler Rebbe), second Chabad Rebbe
- Wife of Rabbi Yaakov Culi Slonim

Her presence in Hebron brought the Rebbe's direct lineage to the Eretz Yisrael community.

---

## Aliyah to Hebron (1845)

In 1845, at age 47, Menucha Rochel and her husband made aliyah — a momentous decision. The journey from Russia to Eretz Yisrael in the mid-nineteenth century was arduous and dangerous.

The timing was significant: the [[events/hebron-community-1823|Chabad Hebron community]] had been established in 1823 and survived the devastating [[events/hebron-pogrom-1834|1834 riots]], partially with the help of Sir Moses Montefiore. By the time Menucha Rochel arrived, the community needed renewal and authoritative leadership.

See [[events/menucha-rochel-aliyah-1845|Menucha Rochel's Aliyah to Hebron (1845)]].

---

## Life in Hebron

Menucha Rochel lived in Hebron for over 40 years until her death in 1888. She became the spiritual anchor of the community — a woman of extraordinary moral stature whose Chabad lineage gave the Hebron community a direct connection to the Rebbe dynasty.

She was known for:

- Providing guidance to the community based on the Chassidic tradition she had absorbed from her father
- Fundraising and financial management for the community
- Serving as a living link between the Chabad dynasty in Russia and the Eretz Yisrael community

Her presence in Hebron was itself understood as a spiritual elevation of the community — having a granddaughter of the Alter Rebbe among them was a source of immense pride and blessing.

---

## Legacy

Menucha Rochel died in Hebron in 1888 at age 90 — a remarkable lifespan that allowed her to serve the community through decades of hardship. Her ohel (memorial tomb) in Hebron became a pilgrimage site for Chabad chassidim and others seeking divine blessing.

The Slonim family that she established in Hebron became a foundation of the Chabad community there. Her great-grandchildren and their descendants formed the nucleus of the Chabad presence in Hebron until the [[events/hebron-riots-1929|1929 riots]] forced the community to disperse.
