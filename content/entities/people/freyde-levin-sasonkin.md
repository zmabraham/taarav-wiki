# Freyde Levin (Sasonkin)

Younger sister of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin (the memoir author). The youngest of the Levin siblings. During WWII she was located near Stalingrad, and eventually came to Samarkand. There she married R. Asher Sasonkin in Tammuz 5704 (1944).

## Description
Freyde was the youngest of the Levin children. During WWII she ended up near Stalingrad at a labor camp. The author's wife, Ita Dvora, found her through a Soviet war information bureau in summer 5703 and arranged for her to come to Samarkand. She was housed there and later the author arranged her shidduch with R. Asher Sasonkin, for whom he also rented an apartment. R. Sasonkin found work distributing bread from a kiosk.
