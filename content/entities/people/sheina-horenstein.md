# Rebbetzin Sheine Horenstein הי"ד

Youngest daughter of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz). Married [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-horenstein]].

## Martyrdom

Rebbetzin Sheine and her husband R' Menachem Mendel Horenstein were trapped in Otwock, Poland, when Germany invaded. Despite rescue efforts by Rayatz from the USA, they could not escape. They were murdered at **Treblinka** extermination camp. Various dates are recorded for their murder: 15 Elul, 3 Tishrei, or 25 Cheshvan 5703 (1942–1943).
