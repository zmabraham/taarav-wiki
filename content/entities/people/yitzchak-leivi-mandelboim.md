# R' Yitzchak Leivi Mandelboim — ר' יצחק לייוי מאנדלבוים

**Chabad Rabbi of Globoke; Martyred in the Holocaust**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Yitzchak Leivi Mandelboim
**Dates**: (early 20th century – 1942/1943)
**Position**: Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]]
**Affiliation**: Chabad-Lubavitch
**Death**: Martyred in the Holocaust, 1942/1943

---

## Overview

Rabbi Yitzchak Leivi Mandelboim served as a Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]], a community in the eastern Polish borderlands notable for its four Chabad synagogues. He was martyred in the Holocaust in 1942 or 1943, together with the Jewish community of Globoke. His death represents the destruction of one of the most established Chabad communities in the region.

---

## Service in Globoke

### The Globoke Community

Globoke was a significant Chabad community:

- **Four synagogues**: An unusual number, reflecting deep Chassidic roots
- **Dedicated rabbi**: Having its own Chabad rabbi (rather than relying on the general community rabbi)
- **Rayatz's visit**: The Rayatz visited in 1934, highlighting the community's importance

### R' Mandelboim's Role

As Chabad rabbi of Globoke:

- **Halachic authority**: Provided rabbinical rulings according to Chabad tradition
- **Community leader**: Served all four Chabad synagogues
- **Spiritual guide**: Also functioned as mashpia for the community
- **Post-Koperstock**: Likely succeeded [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-koperstock|R' Menachem Mendel Koperstock]] who moved to Israel

---

## Martyrdom

### The Holocaust in Globoke

The German occupation reached the eastern borderlands in 1941:

- **Ghetto**: Jews in Globoke were concentrated in a ghetto
- **Liquidation**: The Globoke ghetto was liquidated and the Jews murdered in 1942
- **R' Mandelboim's death**: He perished with his community

### The Meaning of His Death

- **End of a community**: His death marked the end of a community with centuries of Jewish history
- **Four synagogues silenced**: The four Chabad synagogues he had served were destroyed
- **Lost leadership**: His rabbinical guidance, his knowledge, and his spiritual leadership were destroyed

---

## Legacy

R' Yitzchak Leivi Mandelboim is remembered among the martyred rabbis of Polish-Lithuanian Chabad — one of the many dedicated leaders who gave their lives, not by choice but by murder, in the Holocaust.
