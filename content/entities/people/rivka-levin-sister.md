# Rivka Levin (sister)

Sister of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin (the memoir author); daughter of R. Efraim Shmuel Levin and Leah Beila. Younger than Sarah and older than Freyde. Her birthday is in the middle of summer.

## Key Facts
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|

| Age (in 5694/1934) | ~18 years old |

## Description
Mentioned in the letter written by her brother to the Rayatz in Iyar 5694 (1934): "my sister Rivka, may she live, will turn eighteen in the middle of the summer." The memoir does not provide further details.
