# R' Shaul Dov Zislin

**Chabad leader in Eretz Yisrael; member of founding committee of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael (1941)**

## Biography

R' Shaul Dov Zislin was a prominent Chabad Chassid and communal leader in Eretz Yisrael during the 1930s and 1940s. He was a member of the founding committee of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael, established in 1941 — the official organizational body that unified and represented the Chabad community in the British Mandate territory.

The founding of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael in 1941 was a significant organizational milestone. The Chabad community in the Land of Israel had grown substantially through immigration from Eastern Europe, and there was a need for a formal body to coordinate communal activities, support new immigrants, and maintain connections with the Rayatz in New York. R' Zislin, as a member of the founding committee, was among those who took the initiative to create this institutional framework.

He worked alongside other founding committee members including R' Moshe Gourarie and R' Chaim Yosef Rozenblum. Together they laid the groundwork for what would become a significant Chabad organizational presence in Eretz Yisrael.

## Significance

R' Zislin's role in founding Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael represents the organizational consolidation of the Chabad community in the Land of Israel during a period of great upheaval — wartime, British Mandate restrictions, and the beginning of mass Holocaust refugees. The formal organization he helped establish provided a framework for receiving immigrants and maintaining communal life.
