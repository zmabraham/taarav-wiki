# Zelig Katzman

Son of R. Moshe Katzman, who died on the streets of Samarkand during the WWII epidemic. After his father's and mother's deaths, Zelig became one of the first three students enrolled in the underground Tomchei Temimim established in Samarkand by Mikhael Teitelboim.
