# Rabbi Avraham Schneersohn of Kishinev

**Born**: 1826  
**Died**: 1893  
**Father**: [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]]  
**Residence**: Kishinev (Chisinau), Bessarabia (modern Moldova)

---

## Overview

Rabbi Avraham Schneersohn was a son of the [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]] who settled in Kishinev (Chisinau) in Bessarabia. After the Tzemach Tzedek's passing in 1866, while several brothers established formal Chassidic courts, Rabbi Avraham moved to Kishinev, a significant Jewish center in the Pale of Settlement.

---

## Kishinev

Kishinev was a major city in the Bessarabia region with a large Jewish population. It would later become infamous for the devastating Kishinev pogroms of 1903 and 1905, events that occurred during the era of the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]] and shook the Jewish world. Rabbi Avraham's earlier presence there reflects the spread of Chabad influence into the southern reaches of the Pale of Settlement.

---

## Context Among the Tzemach Tzedek's Sons

Rabbi Avraham was one of seven sons of the Tzemach Tzedek, several of whom established independent Chassidic courts after their father's passing:

- [[entities/people/baruch-shalom|Rabbi Baruch Shalom]] — remained in Lubavitch
- [[entities/people/yehuda-leib-kopust|Rabbi Yehuda Leib]] — Kopust
- [[entities/people/chaim-schneur-zalman-liadi|Rabbi Chaim Schneur Zalman]] — Liadi
- [[entities/people/rebbe-maharash|Rabbi Shmuel (Maharash)]] — became the primary Lubavitch Rebbe
- [[entities/people/menachem-nachum-niezhin|Rabbi Menachem Nachum]] — Niezhin
- Yisrael Noach — Niezhin (later moved)
- **Rabbi Avraham** — Kishinev
