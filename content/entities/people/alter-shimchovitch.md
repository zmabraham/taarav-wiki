# R' Alter Shimchovitch

**Chabad Chassid in Warsaw; figure in the Polish Chabad community during the interwar period**

## Biography

R' Alter Shimchovitch was a Chabad Chassid in Warsaw, documented in the Toldos Chabad BePolin, Lita, u'Latvia. He was part of the Warsaw Chabad community that existed alongside the larger non-Chassidic Jewish population of Poland's capital.

Warsaw under the Rayatz's leadership (1933–1939) became a central location for Chabad, as the 6th Rebbe relocated from Riga to Warsaw. The presence of the Rebbe in Warsaw attracted Chassidim and created a more vibrant Chabad community. R' Shimchovitch was among the Warsaw Chassidim active during this period.

His fate after the German invasion of September 1939 is unknown, but Warsaw's Jewish population was systematically murdered during the Holocaust.

## Significance

R' Shimchovitch represents the lay Chassidic leadership of the Warsaw Chabad community — the Chassidim who supported and sustained the community during the Rayatz's residence there. Warsaw was an unusual location for Chabad — cosmopolitan, largely non-Chassidic, and with a powerful secular Jewish intelligentsia — making the work of maintaining a Chabad presence there particularly challenging.
