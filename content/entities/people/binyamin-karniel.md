# Rabbi Binyamin Karniel of Gedera

Rabbi Binyamin (Beni) Karniel (born 4 Tevet 5724 / 1964) is the founding shaliach of Beit Chabad Gedera. Under his leadership, the Gedera Chabad House has grown into one of the most active in Israel, with approximately 1,000 worshippers on the High Holidays and a large school. His Chabad school survived a direct Iranian ballistic missile hit on Rosh Hashana eve 5785 (2024).

## Biography

Born to parents who themselves served as shluchim in Kfar Maimon. His family moved to Kfar Chabad when he was about ten years old on the Rebbe's instruction.

He studied at Tomchei Tmimim Lod and Tomchei Tmimim Kfar Chabad. In 5744 (1984) he spent the "Kvutza" year at Tomchei Tmimim 770. In Tishrei 5745, he and Rabbi Yaakov Aryeh Shmulowitz composed a melody for the words "This year shall be the year of Melech HaMashiach," which the Rebbe encouraged during Hakafot and farbrengens.

In 5745 (1985) he married Chana, daughter of Rabbi Yochanan Sparnai of Kfar Chabad. Later that year they went on shlichus to Gedera.

The Gedera Chabad House grew significantly over decades. About 1,000 people pray there on the High Holidays; thousands of shmurah matzah packages are distributed annually; many city residents have taken organized visits to 770. Rabbi Karniel brought additional shaliach families to serve different neighborhoods. In 5780 (2020) the head of the Gedera Council presented the city key to the Rebbe through the shluchim.

In 5782 he founded the Shalhevet Chabad school in Gedera, which became one of the city's most successful schools.

**Missile attack, Rosh Hashana 5785 (2024):** During Iran's major missile attack, a ballistic missile struck the Shalhevet Chabad school directly on the eve of Rosh Hashana. The building was empty due to the hour. By Tzom Gedaliah the following day, students returned to regular classes. His wife Chana, the principal, described it as a great miracle.

He also serves in Gedera's Chevra Kadisha (burial society).
