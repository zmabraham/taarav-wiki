# Robert Pell

Official in the US State Department who participated in the diplomatic effort to rescue [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) from Nazi Warsaw in 1939–1940.

## Role in Rescue

Pell worked with Secretary of State [[entities/people/cordell-hull]] in facilitating Rayatz's exit. He coordinated with [[entities/people/alexander-kirk]] (US Embassy Berlin) and Latvian envoy Alfred Bilmanis.
