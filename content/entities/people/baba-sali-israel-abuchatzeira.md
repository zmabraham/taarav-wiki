# Rabbi Yisrael Abuchatzeira (Baba Sali)

Rabbi Yisrael Abuchatzeira, known as the "Baba Sali" (1 Tishrei 5650 – 4 Shevat 5744 / 1889–1984), was a great rabbi, tzaddik, Av Beit Din, and kabbalist. He was famous for his open ruach hakodesh (divine intuition) and miracles, and maintained a uniquely close relationship with the Rebbe and prominent Chabad Chassidim in Morocco, the Land of Israel, and France.

## Biography

Born in Rissani, Morocco, grandson of the renowned Rabbi Yaakov Abuchatzeira (author of "Abir Yaakov"). After the murder of his brother Rabbi David Abuchatzeira hy"d, he began serving as rabbi and Av Beit Din of Arfoud, Morocco.

In that period, the Rebbe's emissary to Morocco, Rabbi Shlomo Matusof, came to Arfoud and established the initial connection between Chabad and the Baba Sali. Together they built Chabad institutions in Arfoud. The Baba Sali was a full and active partner in establishing the yeshiva.

Between 5714–5724 (1954–1964), the Baba Sali resided in Arfoud and also frequently visited Casablanca and France, where he stayed at the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva in Brunoy, studying Likkutei Torah and maintaining close relationships with the yeshiva's rabbis: Rabbi Yosef Goldberg (Rosh Yeshiva), Rabbi Nissan Nemenov (mashpia), and Rabbi Yisrael Noach Belenetsky.

In 5711 (1951) he emigrated to Israel. The Rebbe wrote him special letters. For a time he returned to Morocco, then in 5724 (1964) permanently settled in Israel — initially in Yavne, then in Netivot, which became his permanent home after he left Yavne due to a dismissive remark made there about the Baal Shem Tov.

He passed away on 4 Shevat 5744, and the Rebbe sent a letter of condolence to his family.

## Relationship with the Rebbe

The Rebbe exchanged extensive correspondence with the Baba Sali (portions published in Igrot Kodesh). Key letters include:

- A letter (26 Tevet 5712) urging him to remain in Israel and strengthen Sephardic Jews' chinuch, addressed with rare honorifics including "chad mibeni aliyah" (one of the completely righteous)
- When the Baba Sali wrote of his plan to emigrate to America (5712), the Rebbe counseled him in a long, well-reasoned letter to remain in Israel where he was needed as a communal leader
- The Rebbe ended one letter to the Baba Sali with the unique formula "with respect, appreciation, and admiration" — unparalleled in all Igrot Kodesh

The Baba Sali supported all the Rebbe's campaigns: Mivtza Tefillin, Ner Shabbat Kodesh, the Jewish identity law campaign against non-halachic conversions, and the Sefer Torah for Children campaign. He co-signed the proclamation against attacks on Chassidim teaching Tanya in Williamsburg (with Rabbi Kaduri, Av 5743/1983).

When shown an album of the Rebbe and asked about the claim that the Rebbe was the Mashiach, the Baba Sali smiled with admiration and said: "Istahal kol shi!" — an Arabic expression meaning "Everything is fitting for him, he deserves it all!"
