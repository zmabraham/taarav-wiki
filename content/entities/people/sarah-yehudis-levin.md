# Sarah Yehudis Levin

Paternal grandmother of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin, wife of R. Yosef Levin (the family rav). Widowed when R. Yosef passed away around 5649 (1889), she was left with three young children.

## Key Facts
| Field | Value |

| Husband | R. Yosef Levin (rav of Zhivotov and other towns) |
| Children | R. Efraim Shmuel Levin, Chaya Bracha, Avraham Yaakov |
| Widowed | ~5649 (1889), when her husband died |

## Description
When R. Yosef died around 5649 (1889), Sarah Yehudis was left a widow with three children: Efraim Shmuel (then about 11), Chaya Bracha, and Avraham Yaakov. Little more is recorded about her in the memoir beyond the fact of her widowhood and her children.
