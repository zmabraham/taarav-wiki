# R' Baruch Tamrish of Kremenchug

**Prominent Chabad mashpia in Kremenchug; teacher of R' Asher Grotsman; key figure in spreading Chabad in southern Russia**

## Biography

R' Baruch Tamrish served as a mashpia and Chassidic leader in Kremenchug, one of the most important Chabad centers in the Poltava region of Ukraine. He was active during the eras of the Tzemach Tzedek and the Maharash.

R' Baruch Tamrish was a significant teacher within the Kremenchug Chabad community. Among his disciples was R' Asher Grotsman, who later served as a shoichet-mashpia in Nikolaev. This transmission from R' Baruch Tamrish to R' Asher Grotsman documents a specific chain of Chassidic teaching in southern Russia.

Kremenchug under figures like R' Baruch Tamrish developed as one of the vibrant secondary Chabad centers outside the heartland of White Russia, demonstrating the movement's successful spread into the Ukrainian steppe.

## Significance

R' Baruch Tamrish represents the crucial role of local mashpi'im in propagating Chabad teaching across a wide geographic area. He was a link in the transmission chain that stretched from the Chabad Rebbes through established Kremenchug leaders like himself to disciples like R' Asher Grotsman who then carried the tradition to even more peripheral communities like Nikolaev.
