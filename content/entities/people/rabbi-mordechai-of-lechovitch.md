# Rabbi Mordechai of Lechovitch

## Overview
Rabbi Mordechai of Lechovitch was a disciple of Rabbi Shlomo of Karlin who established himself in Lechovitch (Belarus) after the liberation of 19 Kislev 1799. He founded the Stolin-Lechovitch Chassidic dynasty.

## Details
He is mentioned in the interrogation records from the Alter Rebbe's 1799 arrest, where one of the arrested Chassidim (Rabbi Meir son of Rephael of Vilna) testified about him as having approximately 100 Chassidim.

After the liberation on 19 Kislev 1799, he settled in Lechovitch and his movement began to spread through Belarus and Lithuania, alongside the Stolin branch led by Rabbi Asher.

The Alter Rebbe's liberation and the subsequent freedom of Chassidus in Russia enabled both Chabad and the Stolin-Lechovitch movement to spread openly in Belarus and Lithuania.
