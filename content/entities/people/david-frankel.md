# R' Dovid Frankel (Husyatin)

Bibliographer from Husyatin (a town known for its Hasidic dynasty). He corresponded with [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) regarding books in the Lubavitch Library's collection, particularly questions about secular books from the Weiner collection.

## Library Connection

In the early period when the library was in Riga (beginning of 5688/1927), R' Frankel wrote to Rayatz asking about various secular books in the library collection. Rayatz replied that the catalog was not yet complete.
