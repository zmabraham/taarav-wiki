# Rabbi Yitzchak Dov Aushpal

Secretary/staff member of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz). Escaped from Europe during WWII.

## Escape via Trans-Siberia

Together with [[entities/people/moshe-leib-rothstein]] and [[entities/people/shmuel-zalmanow]], R' Aushpal escaped via:
- **Vilna** → **Riga** → **Russia** (Trans-Siberian Railroad) → **Japan** (Kobe) → **San Francisco** → **New York**

He arrived in New York on **Parshas Noach, Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan 5701** (autumn 1940).
