# Chaya Bracha Levin (Malkiman)

Paternal aunt of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin; daughter of R. Yosef Levin and Sarah Yehudis. She married a man named R. Malkiman and lived with her family in Dirazne (near Kamenets-Podolsk) until World War II, after which nothing more is known of her fate.
