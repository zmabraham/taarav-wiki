# Rabbi Chaim Lieberman

Librarian of the Lubavitch Library and secretary/staff member of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz).

## Library Work

R' Lieberman served as the primary librarian of the Lubavitch Library, maintaining and cataloging its collection of some 13,000+ volumes. He compiled a typed list of the library's holdings (on long sheets, using a typewriter) which served as an internal working catalog — both to know what was already in the collection and to identify what still needed to be acquired. He accompanied Rayatz during the escape from Warsaw.

## Escape

R' Lieberman escaped from Europe with Rayatz. He is mentioned as accompanying Rayatz during the escape from Warsaw through Berlin to Riga.
