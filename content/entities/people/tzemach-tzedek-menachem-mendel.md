## Biography

Third Lubavitcher Rebbe, grandson and son-in-law of the Mitteler Rebbe. Named after his responsa work Tzemach Tzedek. Led Chabad during the era of Czar Nicholas I's harsh decrees against Jewish life. Championed traditional Jewish education against the Haskalah (Enlightenment) movement. Father of seven sons, each of whom became a Chassidic leader.
