# R. Zusha Paz

A Chabad Chassid in Soviet-era **Moscow** who served as the **shamash (sexton/administrator)** of the **Mariina-Roshcha synagogue** — one of the few functioning synagogues in Moscow during the Soviet era. He is documented in the memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu*.

## Role in Moscow

R. Zusha Paz occupied a critical role in the Moscow Jewish underground:
- **Shamash** at Mariina-Roshcha shul — giving him access to the most important gathering point of Moscow Jews
- **Matchmaker (shadkhn)** — he arranged the marriage of the memoir's narrator, introducing him to his future wife in 5700 (1940)
- A trusted figure in the Chabad underground community

## The Match He Arranged

R. Zusha Paz introduced the unnamed narrator (who had come to Moscow to find religious Jewish work and community) to the daughter of **R. Zusman Pradkin** of Shumyatz, a shochet (ritual slaughterer) and community leader. The tena'im (engagement ceremony) took place in Nisan 5700 (1940).

## Arrest by NKVD

R. Zusha Paz was later **arrested by the NKVD** (Soviet secret police). This was the fate of many observant Jews in Stalin's terror regime, particularly those with visible roles in religious community life. His fate after arrest is not specified in the memoir.

## Family

- **Brother**: [[chaim-chanye-paz|R. Chaim-Chanye Paz]] — who was present at the narrator's tena'im ceremony and was later killed in the Holocaust

## Mariina-Roshcha Synagogue

The Mariina-Roshcha (Maryana Roshcha) neighborhood shul was one of the principal Moscow synagogues that remained functional during the Soviet era, albeit under difficult conditions.
