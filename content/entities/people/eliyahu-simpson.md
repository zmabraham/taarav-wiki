# R' Eliyahu Simpson (ר' אליהו סימפסון)

**Early student at Tomchei Temimim; co-founder and pillar of Agudas HaTmimim in New York.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Founding member of Agudas HaTmimim |
| **Residence** | 137 West 116th Street, New York |
| **Brother** | R' Gershon Simpson (also a pillar of Agudas HaTmimim) |

## Overview

R' Eliyahu Simpson was among the earliest TT students, studying there when the yeshiva first opened a children's section around 5666 (1906). He studied under R' Menachem Mendel Lyader, the first melamed in that section.

He and his brother R' Gershon were described as "pillars" (amudei hatoch) of Agudas HaTmimim. His home at 137 West 116th Street in New York was the site of the founding meeting of Agudas HaTmimim on Motzaei Shabbat Shemini Atzeret 5686 (1925).

## Founding Meeting of Agudas HaTmimim

The meeting was held: Motzaei Shabbat, Shemini Atzeret 5686 (October 1925)  
Location: Home of R' Eliyahu Simpson, 137 W. 116th St., New York  
About 20 members attended, all TT alumni who had studied in the large hall (Ulam) or in the children's classes (Chadarim) at various points.

The organization maintained active correspondence with the Rayatz and was encouraged by him. It faced disruption during WWI (5674/1914 onward), was reconstituted in 5678 (1918), then interrupted again by the Bolshevik revolution.

## Role in Lubavitch Library Acquisitions

R' Simpson was also tasked by [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) with acquiring books in America for the Lubavitch Library, particularly early American Hebrew printings. He was first approached about this in Menachem Av 5696 (1936). He sent three manuscripts as a gift; Rayatz identified two as parts of Siddur HaAri and one as a Machzor from Venice (cataloged as items 1991 and 1996 in the manuscript collection).
