# R' Alter Beylin (ר' אלטר ביילין)

**Shochet; student at Tomchei Temimim; later brother-in-law of Israel Jacobson.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Shochet |
| **Connection to Jacobson** | Classmate at TT; Jacobson's sister Sasha Hinda married him |
| **Father** | R' Yishaaya Beylin (at whose home Jacobson boarded in Lubavitch) |

## Overview

R' Alter Beylin was the son of R' Yishaaya Beylin, at whose home Israel Jacobson stayed in Lubavitch (paying 6 rubles 25 kopeks per month for board). Alter was the same age as Jacobson and studied in the same class at TT.

The two were classmates and close friends. When they were both frustrated at not being promoted to the higher class, they together boycotted the lessons ("banded in the shiurim"), which led to the mashgiach threatening to expel them.

Alter Beylin later married Israel Jacobson's older sister Sasha Hinda, making him Jacobson's brother-in-law. He became a shochet.
