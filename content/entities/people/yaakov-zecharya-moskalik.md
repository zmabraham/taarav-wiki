# R' Yaakov Zecharya Moskalik (ר' יעקב זכרי' מאסקאליק)

**Senior student at Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch; known for nurturing younger students; later rabbi in Zuravichy.**

## Overview

R' Yaakov Zecharya Moskalik was a prominent older student at TT who was beloved for his warmth toward the younger boys. He earned the affectionate nickname "di Mame" (the Mother) for his nurturing care.

When Israel Jacobson fell ill his first winter in Lubavitch, R' Gershon Chen brought the young boy to R' Yaakov Zecharya. He advised: "Yisrael is sick? Go home, drink two cups of tea with milk and sugar, then go to sleep." The practical, fatherly advice made a lasting impression.

His family connection: his maternal grandmother was the daughter of R' Moshe Komisar, brother of R' Chalavna, father of R' Yechiel Komisar (the rebbe melamed). This made him distantly related to the Komisar family.

He later became rabbi in Zuravichy — Israel Jacobson's own hometown — creating an interesting full-circle connection.
