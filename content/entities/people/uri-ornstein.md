# Rabbi Uri Ornstein

A Chabad **emissary (shadran)** from [[hebron|Hebron]] to Russia, sent to collect charitable donations for the Chabad community in the Holy Land. Rabbi Ornstein represents the class of "shadranim" — traveling fundraisers — who were essential to sustaining the Kolel Chabad community.

## Role as Emissary

The Kolel Chabad's financial system depended on periodic emissaries traveling from the Holy Land to the diaspora (primarily Russia and Eastern Europe) to collect contributions. These emissaries would visit communities, present letters of authorization from the Kolel leadership, and return with funds to support the scholars and families in Hebron and later Jerusalem.

Rabbi Uri Ornstein served as one of these emissaries from Hebron, making at least one major fundraising journey to Russia on behalf of the community.

## Historical Context

The system of shadranim was well-established in Chabad by the mid-19th century. Emissaries had to carry official letters (*kol korei*) signed by the Rebbe and the Kolel administration to authenticate their mission and distinguish legitimate collectors from imposters. The journeys were long and arduous, often taking a year or more.
