# Captain Volasenko

**Active**: 1825–1826, Vitebsk gubernia, Russia  
**Role**: Adjutant (aide-de-camp) of Governor Khavanski; sent to bring R' Aharon HaLevi of Staroselye to Vitebsk for questioning

---

## Overview

Leib-Gvardia Captain Volasenko (full name: former adjutant of Governor Khavanski, Leib-Gvardia [Life-Guards] Captain Volasenko) was dispatched by [[entities/people/governor-khavanski|Governor-General Prince Khavanski]] in October 1825 on a special mission to [[entities/places/staroselye|Staroselye]] to bring [[entities/people/aaron-halevi-staroselye|R' Aharon HaLevi of Staroselye]] to Vitebsk for interrogation in connection with the Mitteler Rebbe case.

He traveled with a police officer from the Kapust district (Paved Kapust), a round trip to Staroselye and back to Vitebsk using six horses.

---

## The Travel Expenses Dispute

The travel expenses of 119 rubles and 52 kopecks paid to Volasenko from the imperial treasury became the subject of a prolonged administrative dispute that stretched to 1828. When the case against the Mitteler Rebbe was fully resolved with an acquittal, the question arose: from whom should the treasury recover these travel costs, if no one was found guilty?

The Vitebsk treasury office repeatedly requested guidance from Khavanski's office. The final resolution (September 1828) determined that since the Imperial Senate had found no one criminally liable, the 119 rubles and 52 kopecks would be written off as treasury expenses without recovery — citing the November 1824 Imperial Senate decree on travel expense procedures.

This administrative trail provides documentary evidence of the exact date of Volasenko's mission (19 October 1825 authorization, 24 October 1825 payment) and the resolution of the overall case timeline.
