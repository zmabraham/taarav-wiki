# R' Gershon Leib Kugel

**Chabad Chassid of Pleshchenitsy; son of R' Yosef Kugel; died in Vilna**

## Biography

R' Gershon Leib Kugel was the son of R' Yosef Kugel, a distinguished Chabad Chassid of Pleshchenitsy. He was part of the illustrious Kugel family from Pleshchenitsy that included his father R' Yosef and his uncle R' Chanoch (Hendl) Kugel, who later became a celebrated mashpia in Lubavitch.

R' Gershon Leib died on 18 Elul (Chai Elul) 5637 (1877), just about a year after his father R' Yosef. His passing was recorded by his son Shlomo in handwritten notes: "Day of passing of my father and teacher, R' Gershon Yehuda Leib, on the first day [of the week], after the close of Shabbos, parshas Nitzavim, year 5637... buried in Vilna on 18 Elul after midday." That he was buried in Vilna rather than Pleshchenitsy suggests he was traveling or had relocated near the end of his life.

R' Shmuel Dov Ber of Borisov corresponded with R' Gershon Leib and sent him greetings alongside his father, indicating that both father and son were recognized figures in the broader Chabad network.

## Significance

R' Gershon Leib Kugel represents the second generation of the Kugel Chassidic family in Pleshchenitsy. His early death — coming shortly after his father's — meant that the family's spiritual legacy in Pleshchenitsy passed to his uncle R' Hendl, who eventually settled in Lubavitch. The precise date of his death (18 Elul, which is also the birthday of the Baal Shem Tov and the Alter Rebbe) recorded in family manuscripts provides a rare instance of documentary precision in the history of ordinary Chabad Chassidim.
