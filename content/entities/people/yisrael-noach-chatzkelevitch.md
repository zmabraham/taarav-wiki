# R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkelevitch

**Chabad rabbi who served in Kupishok, Lithuania (documented 1928)**

## Biography

R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkelevitch was a Chabad rabbi who served in Kupishok (Kupiškis), a town in northern Lithuania, around 1928. His tenure in Kupishok is documented in the Toldos Chabad BePolin, Lita, u'Latvia source book as part of the broader Chabad presence in Lithuania during the interwar period.

Lithuania was historically the stronghold of the Mitnagdim — the opponents of Chassidism — with Vilna serving as their intellectual center. Despite this, Chabad maintained communities and rabbinic leadership throughout Lithuania. Rabbis like R' Chatzkelevitch served these communities, navigating the tension between Chassidic identity and the Litvish cultural environment.

His tenure coincided with the Rayatz's residence in Riga (1927–1933), when Chabad leadership was accessible to Lithuanian Chassidim more easily than it had been when the center was inside the Soviet Union.

## Significance

R' Chatzkelevitch represents the Chabad rabbinic presence in Lithuanian communities during the interwar period — an important but understudied dimension of Chabad history that focuses on the communities between the Riga center and the major Polish cities. These Lithuanian Chabad rabbis maintained Jewish observance in smaller communities that were eventually devastated by the Holocaust.
