# Korbitsky

A police official of **Vitebsk gubernia** who was sent to [[lubavitch|Lubavitch]] in September 1825 to formally summon the [[mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] (Rabbi DovBer Schneuri) to Vitebsk for interrogation in the legal case brought by [[simcha-kissin|Simcha Kissin]].

## Role in the Trial

Korbitsky served under **Governor [[khovansky|Khovansky]]** of Vitebsk. After the denunciations by Simcha Kissin were received, Korbitsky was dispatched on the physical task of traveling from Vitebsk to Lubavitch — a journey of considerable distance — to deliver the official summons to the Rebbe.

His travel to Lubavitch in **September 1825** is the first direct government action against the Mitteler Rebbe in this case. The summons required the Rebbe to appear in Vitebsk for questioning.

## Historical Context

The use of a police official to summon a Chassidic Rebbe was significant — it meant the matter had escalated beyond informal inquiry to an official legal proceeding. The Mitteler Rebbe complied and traveled to Vitebsk, where he was interrogated and then released to his home on **10 Kislev 5586** — the date that became the Chassidic holiday of Yud Kislev.
