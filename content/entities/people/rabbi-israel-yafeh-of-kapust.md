# Rabbi Yisrael Yafeh of Kapust

## Overview
Rabbi Yisrael Yafeh was a prominent Chassid who served as a printer in Kapust (Kopust). He was one of the leaders of the Chassidic community and printed important Chabad texts, including the Siddur in 1822.

## Details
He had a personal audience (yechidut) with the Alter Rebbe in which he received spiritual guidance. After the audience, he wrote a summary of the conversation and submitted it to the Alter Rebbe, who wrote him a response in the letter printed in Igros Kodesh (letter 80).

By 1796 (5556), he had become one of the leaders (manhigim) of the Chassidic community and was among the signatories of the renewed Takanot Liozna.

He was involved with the Alter Rebbe's Siddur and was the printer of the Siddur in 1822 (from the approbation he gave). His press in Kapust published many important Chabad works.

He is mentioned in the second-generation sources as the printer who received manuscripts from the Mitteler Rebbe and published Chabad works.
