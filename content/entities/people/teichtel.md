# Rabbi Yehuda Teichtel

**Born**: 1970, Germany  
**Role**: Chabad shliach to Berlin; director of Chabad Berlin (Lubavitch Zentrum Berlin)

---

## Overview

Rabbi Yehuda Teichtel is the Chabad shliach to Berlin and the founder of one of the most symbolically significant Chabad operations in the world. Establishing Chabad's presence in Berlin in 1996, he has built a major Jewish community center in the heart of the German capital — representing the astonishing post-Holocaust return of vibrant Jewish life to Germany.

---

## Establishing Chabad Berlin (1996)

Teichtel established the Chabad presence in Berlin in 1996, just a few years after German reunification. Berlin at that time was in the midst of reinventing itself as a unified capital, and its Jewish community — decimated by the Holocaust — was beginning to grow again, partly through immigration from the former Soviet Union.

The symbolism of a Chabad House in Berlin — the city from which the Holocaust was planned — was not lost on anyone. Teichtel's mission represented the Chabad conviction that Jewish life must not merely survive but flourish, even in the places where it was most brutally destroyed.

---

## The Berlin Jewish Community Center

Under Teichtel's leadership, Chabad Berlin has grown into a comprehensive communal operation:

- **Synagogue** — seating 250 worshippers
- **Jewish school** ("Yudishe Traditsyone Shule Ohr Avner") — located in a building that formerly served as a **Nazi officers' club**, a powerful symbolic transformation
- **Restaurant** — serving kosher food in the heart of Berlin
- **Mikveh**
- **Library and study center**
- **Youth center** — serving young Jewish families and students

---

## Symbolic Significance

The fact that a Chabad school operates in a former Nazi officers' club is one of the most powerful symbols of Jewish resilience in post-Holocaust Europe. Teichtel has spoken publicly about this symbolism: the very building where Nazi officers gathered is now filled with Jewish children studying Torah.

Berlin today has one of the fastest-growing Jewish communities in Europe, and Chabad Berlin under Teichtel's leadership plays a central role in serving that community.
