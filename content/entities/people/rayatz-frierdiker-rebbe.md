# Rayatz — Frierdiker Rebbe — R' Yosef Yitzchak Schneerson (אדמו"ר הריי"צ)

**Sixth Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch (1920–1950); leader during Soviet persecution and WWII rescue.**

## Biography

R' Yosef Yitzchak was in many ways co-administrator of Tomchei Temimim under his father, the Rashab, from his youth. Israel Jacobson describes him managing yeshiva admissions, overseeing students, wearing a shtreimel on Shabbat — and being present at the small hall discourses alongside his father.

After the Rashab's passing in 5680 (1920), R' Yosef Yitzchak assumed leadership. He worked to maintain Chabad institutions under Bolshevik rule, running underground yeshivos and mikvaos. He was arrested by the Soviet secret police in 1927, sentenced to death, then commuted to exile; after international protests he was released and permitted to leave the USSR.

He settled in Riga, then Warsaw. He made his first visit to America from 13 Elul 5689 (1929) to 20 Tammuz 5690 (1930) — a period extensively documented in Jacobson's memoirs. During this visit he prayed in Brownsville's "Anshei Lubavitch" synagogue for the High Holidays and worked intensively on strengthening American Chabad.

After the Nazi invasion of Poland in 1939, he was trapped in Warsaw. Israel Jacobson was directly involved in organizing his rescue and emigration to America, which succeeded and he arrived on 9 Adar II 5700 (1940). He established headquarters at 770 Eastern Parkway and founded Merkos L'Inyonei Chinuch, Machne Israel, and Kehos Publication Society. He passed away 10 Shevat 5710 (1950).
