# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Mishriy

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Mishriy, known as "the Ilui (prodigy) of Shriy," was the uncle by marriage of the Alter Rebbe, a hidden tzaddik (nistar), and head of the yeshiva of Vitebsk. He was a student of the Baal Shem Tov's circle and a figure in the pre-Chassidic network of hidden righteous men.

## Biography

Born in the town of Shriy to Yaakov Krepil and Tzipa Brecha, who had been childless for many years. After a great act of charity by his father — saving a Jewish child from a wicked priest at great personal cost — the Baal Shem Tov blessed them and Yosef Yitzchak was born.

From childhood, his extraordinary gifts were evident — reading at age three, knowing all of Tanach by age five. His father, who maintained ties with the circle of hidden tzaddikim, engaged a nistar (hidden righteous person) as his teacher on the Baal Shem Tov's instruction. After six years, he went to study at the Minsk yeshiva where he was called "the Ilui of Shriy."

The Baal Shem Tov personally organized a six-year curriculum for him and appointed Rabbi Moshe Nissan — head of the Vitebsk nistarim — as his guide. He also studied at the Smorgan yeshiva, a center for Chassidic preparation.

On the Baal Shem Tov's instruction, he married Devorah Leah (who was the aunt of the Alter Rebbe), and was appointed as head of the Vitebsk yeshiva. He was elected as one of two representatives of the yeshiva to the Council of Four Lands (Va'ad Arba Aratzot) in Lublin, where he began hidden missions.

## Connection to Chabad

As the uncle-by-marriage of the Alter Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Mishriy bridges the hidden-tzaddikim tradition and the early Chabad tradition. His story appears in the Memoirs (Sefer HaZikronot) of the Frierdiker Rebbe, which is a primary source for his biography.
