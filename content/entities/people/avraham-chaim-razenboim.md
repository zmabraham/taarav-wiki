# R' Avraham Chaim Razenboim

**Devoted Chabad Chassid of the Pleshchenitsy community; figure in the early Tzemach Tzedek–Maharash era**

## Biography

R' Avraham Chaim Razenboim was a prominent Chabad Chassid associated with the Pleshchenitsy (Pleszczenice) community in White Russia. The Pleshchenitsy community was known for producing particularly devoted Chassidim who maintained strong ties to the Rebbe's court.

R' Avraham Chaim was part of the Razenboim family — a Chabad family in the Pleshchenitsy area that maintained its Chassidic commitments across multiple generations. He likely traveled to Lubavitch for yarchei kallah (extended study periods) and the High Holidays, as was customary for devoted Chassidim of his era.

The Pleshchenitsy community in the Tzemach Tzedek and Maharash eras was a functioning Chabad congregation with its own kloiz (Chassidic prayer house) and local communal infrastructure.

## Significance

R' Avraham Chaim Razenboim exemplifies the devoted lay Chassid who formed the backbone of the Chabad movement — not a rebbe or rosh yeshiva, but a community member whose personal devotion to Chassidus sustained local Chabad life. The Pleshchenitsy community he was part of represents the type of small White Russian towns where Chabad culture was deeply embedded in daily Jewish life.
