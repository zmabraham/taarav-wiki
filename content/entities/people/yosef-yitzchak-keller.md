# Yosef Yitzchak Keller

Grandson of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin; son of Sholom Dov Ber Levin. He assisted his father in editing and preparing the Zichronos memoir for publication, doing so "beautifully and in an organized manner," as noted by the author in his introduction.
