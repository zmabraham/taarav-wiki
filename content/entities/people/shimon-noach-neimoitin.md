# R' Shimon Noach Neimoitin — ר' שמעון נח ניימויטין

**Mashpia for Vilna and the Surrounding Region**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Shimon Noach Neimoitin
**Dates**: (late 19th–mid 20th century)
**Position**: Mashpia (spiritual guide) for [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]] and surroundings
**Affiliation**: Chabad-Lubavitch

---

## Overview

Rabbi Shimon Noach Neimoitin served as the mashpia (spiritual mentor) for the Chabad community in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]] and the surrounding region of Lithuania. His position in Vilna — the center of Mitnagdic opposition to Chassidism — was particularly significant, as he maintained and nurtured Chabad spiritual life in the very city that had historically been most hostile to the movement.

---

## The Challenge of Vilna

### Vilna as Chabad's Symbolic Enemy

For a Chabad mashpia to serve in Vilna meant:

- **Historical weight**: Serving in the city where the [[entities/people/vilna-gaon|Vilna Gaon]] had led the excommunication of Chassidim
- **Continued Mitnagdic culture**: Vilna remained a center of non-Chassidic Torah scholarship
- **Minority status**: Chabad was a minority within Vilna's larger Jewish world
- **Courage**: Maintaining an overt Chabad identity in Vilna required a certain spiritual fortitude

### The Chabad Community He Served

Vilna's Chabad community by the interwar period:

- **Small but established**: Several Chabad synagogues functioning in Vilna
- **Long history**: Chassidim had maintained a presence in Vilna despite early persecution
- **Broader network**: Connected to communities throughout the Vilna region

---

## Role as Mashpia

R' Shimon Noach Neimoitin's work included:

- **Individual guidance**: Personal spiritual mentorship for Chassidim in Vilna and surroundings
- **Teaching**: Classes in Chabad Chassidic philosophy and practice
- **Regional scope**: His responsibility extended beyond Vilna to surrounding smaller communities
- **Farbrengens**: Leading Chassidic gatherings
- **Coordination**: Working with the broader Lithuanian Chabad network

---

## Connection to Chabad Leadership

- **Rayatz's network**: Part of the Lithuanian Chabad network coordinated through [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] and [[entities/places/warsaw|Warsaw]]
- **Reporting**: Kept Chabad leadership informed about conditions in Vilna
- **Implementation**: Implemented directives from the Rayatz at the local level
- **Rayatz's visits**: When the Rayatz visited Vilna (1928), R' Neimoitin would have been a central figure in receiving him

---

## Fate During WWII

Vilna's Jewish community, including its Chabad members, was decimated in the Ponary massacres (1941–1943). R' Shimon Noach Neimoitin's fate during the Holocaust is documented in the Toldos Chabad source.

---

## Legacy

R' Shimon Noach Neimoitin represents the Chabad spiritual leaders who maintained the tradition in Vilna — the Jerusalem of Lithuania that had once persecuted the movement and eventually became home to a vibrant, if small, Chabad community.
