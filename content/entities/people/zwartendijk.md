# Jan Zwartendijk

Jan Zwartendijk (1896–1976) was the Dutch consul in Kovno, Lithuania, who in July 1940 provided end-destination visas to Curaçao, enabling thousands of Jews to escape Nazi-occupied Europe. Among those saved were Lubavitcher chassidim who used the visas to travel through the Soviet Union and Japan.

## Connection to Chabad

Zwartendijk's Curaçao visas, combined with Sugihara's Japanese transit visas, created an escape route that saved the Shanghai group of Lubavitcher chassidim.
