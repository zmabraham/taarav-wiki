# Waldman (Polish Refugee, Samarkand)

A Polish refugee who lived in Samarkand during World War II and provided R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin with Polish identity documents under the name "Naftali Bat." His first name is not recorded in the memoir.

## Description
During the war, many Polish citizens — including Jews — had fled or been deported into Soviet Central Asia. Waldman was such a Polish refugee living in Samarkand. He provided R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin with his Polish identity documents, which allowed R. Yisrael Yehuda to live under the alias "Naftali Bat" and evade Soviet military conscription authorities who were searching for him.

These same documents later facilitated R. Yisrael Yehuda's escape from the Soviet Union in 1946 through the postwar Soviet–Polish repatriation scheme.
