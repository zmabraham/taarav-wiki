# R. Sadia Liberov

A prominent Chabad Chassid present at the **tena'im ceremony** (engagement of the narrator) in Moscow, in **Nisan 5700 (1940)**. He is described in the memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* as a significant figure in the Moscow Chabad underground community.

## The Tena'im Ceremony

The tena'im (formal engagement betrothal) ceremony for the memoir's narrator and his future wife (daughter of [[zusman-pradkin|R. Zusman Pradkin]] of Shumyatz) took place in Moscow. R. Sadia Liberov was one of the prominent guests, indicating his standing in the community.

Others present included:
- [[chaim-chanye-paz|R. Chaim-Chanye Paz]] (brother of the matchmaker [[zusha-paz|R. Zusha Paz]])

## Context

By 1940, the Moscow Chabad community consisted of a small but tight-knit group of Chassidim who maintained religious observance secretly under Soviet rule. Prominent figures at any religious gathering were rare, as attendance itself carried risk.
