# R' Yitzchak Meir Gurary

Chabad rabbi who helped lead the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in Shanghai during WWII.

## Shanghai Yeshiva

After the flight of yeshiva students through Vilna, Trans-Siberia, and Kobe, many arrived in Shanghai where a Tomchei Temimim yeshiva continued operating throughout the war years. R' Gurary was among the senior figures maintaining this institution.
