# Pinchas Reizes Shik of Shklov

**Lived**: late 18th – early 19th century, Shklov, Russia  
**Role**: Senior Chabad chassid; trusted treasurer and administrator for the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] and [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]]; central figure in the 1825 arrest case

---

## Overview

R' Pinchas Reizes Shik of Shklov was one of the most senior and trusted chassidim of the Alter Rebbe and the Mitteler Rebbe. He served as the primary custodian of the Rebbe's family relief fund following the Napoleonic devastation of Lyady (1812), and his death prior to the 1825 arrest contributed directly to the crisis, as sensitive documents from his estate fell into the informer Simcha Kissin's hands.

His family name "Reizes" derived from his mother-in-law, the Rebbetzin Roiza [Berlin], whose maiden name became associated with her sons-in-law. He was a brother-in-law of [[entities/people/zalman-slonim-shklov|R' Zalman Reizes Slonim of Shklov]].

---

## Role as Treasurer

In Tevet 5574 (early 1814), approximately one year after the passing of the Alter Rebbe, the Mitteler Rebbe organized the distribution of 35,000 rubles collected for family relief following the burning of Lyady by Napoleon's retreating forces. The distribution letter, written in the Mitteler Rebbe's own hand, names R' Pinchas Shik as the primary trustee and custodian:

> "All moneys shall be deposited in Pinchas's custody, and against this sum he presents a receipt in his handwriting that the entire distribution amount is in his possession, and he will distribute it to each recipient as he sees fit, with interest accrued through a business partnership arrangement."

R' Pinchas, together with R' Zalman Reizes Slonim and R' Shlomo Freydes, served as witnesses and overseers of the entire distribution. The document was to remain sealed and secret under R' Pinchas's supervision until better times.

A letter from the Mitteler Rebbe to "my friend R' Pinchas of Shklov" confirms this arrangement: "all the money that the emissaries collect should be handed to R' Pinchas of Shklov."

---

## Connection to the 1825 Arrest

R' Pinchas died before the denunciations of 1825. After his death, the distribution letter — a sensitive document in the Mitteler Rebbe's own Hebrew handwriting — came into the possession of [[entities/people/simcha-kissin|Simcha Kissin]], who used it as the centerpiece of his denunciations to [[entities/people/governor-khavanski|Governor Khavanski]]. Kissin argued the document proved illegal collection of large sums.

Because R' Pinchas had died, his two co-witnesses — R' Zalman Reizes Slonim and R' Shlomo Freydes — were summoned by investigators [[entities/people/loginov|Loginov]] and [[entities/people/markov-investigator|Markov]] to testify about the money collections. The Mitteler Rebbe's responses to investigators explicitly noted that R' Pinchas was "the late Pinchas" (המנוח), confirming he had predeceased the investigation.

---

## Historical Significance

R' Pinchas Shik appears throughout the primary sources of the Masar book as the backbone of the Rebbe's financial administration. The fact that his estate documents were accessible to an informer reveals how vulnerable the Chabad leadership structure was to legal attack through ordinary succession and estate matters.
