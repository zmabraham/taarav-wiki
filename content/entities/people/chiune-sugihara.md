# Chiune Sugihara

Japanese Vice-Consul in Kovna (Kaunas), Lithuania. Known as the "Japanese Schindler" for issuing thousands of transit visas to Jewish refugees in 1940, enabling them to escape via the Trans-Siberian Railroad to Japan.

## Role in the Trans-Siberian Escape

In 1940, thousands of Jewish refugees — including many Chabad students and their families — gathered in Vilna and Kovna seeking escape routes. Sugihara issued Japanese transit visas to thousands of Jews, against instructions from his government, enabling them to travel via the Trans-Siberian Railroad through the Soviet Union to Kobe, Japan, and from there to other safe destinations including Shanghai.

The Chabad secretaries R' [[entities/people/moshe-leib-rothstein]], R' [[entities/people/shmuel-zalmanow]], and R' [[entities/people/yitzchak-dov-aushpal]] were among those who used this route.
