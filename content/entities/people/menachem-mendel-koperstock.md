# R' Menachem Mendel Koperstock — ר' מנחם מענדל קאָפּערסטאָק

**Chabad Rabbi of Postavy and Globoke; Survivor Who Settled in Haifa**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Menachem Mendel Koperstock
**Dates**: (early–mid 20th century)
**Positions**: Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/postavy|Postavy]]; later Chabad rabbi of [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]]
**Affiliation**: Chabad-Lubavitch
**Post-war**: Settled in Haifa, Israel

---

## Overview

Rabbi Menachem Mendel Koperstock served as a Chabad rabbi in the eastern borderlands of interwar Poland, first in [[entities/places/postavy|Postavy]] and then in [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]] — a community notable for its four Chabad synagogues and a visit by the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] in 1934. R' Koperstock was among the fortunate Chabad leaders who survived the Holocaust and continued his work in Israel, settling in Haifa.

---

## Career Before the Holocaust

### Service in Postavy

R' Koperstock began his career as the Chabad rabbi of Postavy:

- **Location**: Postavy, Vilna region, eastern Poland (now Belarus)
- **Role**: Provided rabbinical leadership for the Chabad community
- **Period**: Served during the interwar period

### Move to Globoke

From Postavy, R' Koperstock moved to serve the larger and more prominent community in [[entities/places/globoke|Globoke]]:

- **Larger community**: Globoke had four separate Chabad synagogues — a more significant position
- **Expanded role**: Served as the Chabad rabbi for this established multi-synagogue community
- **Rayatz's visit**: Was the Chabad rabbi of Globoke when the Rayatz visited in 1934 (Sivan 5694)

### The Rayatz's Visit (1934)

The [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] visited Globoke in the summer of 1934:

- **R' Koperstock's role**: As the local Chabad rabbi, he was central to receiving and hosting the Rayatz
- **Community celebration**: The visit was a major event for the Globoke Chabad community
- **Personal connection**: R' Koperstock thus had a direct personal connection with the Rayatz

---

## Survival and Post-War Life

### Escape from the Holocaust

R' Koperstock survived the Holocaust — the circumstances of his escape are not fully detailed in available sources but he managed to leave Europe before or during the war.

### Haifa, Israel

After the war:

- **Settlement**: Settled in Haifa, Israel's major port city
- **Continued service**: Continued his rabbinical work in Israel
- **Legacy**: As one of the few survivors, he carried the memory of Postavy and Globoke Chabad

---

## Significance

### Survivor-Witness

R' Koperstock is significant as:

- **Living connection**: One of the few survivors who could personally testify to the Chabad life in Postavy and Globoke
- **Memory preservation**: His survival helped preserve the memory of these communities
- **Continuity**: Demonstrated that Chabad survived the Holocaust and was rebuilt

### Two Communities Served

The arc of his career — from Postavy to Globoke — illustrates how rabbis in this region served multiple communities and how communities were connected through shared personnel.
