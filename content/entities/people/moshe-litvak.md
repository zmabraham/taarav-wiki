# Moshe Litvak

A respected elderly member of the Lipovetsh Jewish community. When the Levin family arrived as refugees from Zhivotov just before Passover 5680 (1920), the local rav and shochtim brought them to Moshe Litvak's home. He had a large apartment and generously hosted the family, also providing matzos for Passover.
