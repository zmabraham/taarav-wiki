# R' Elchanan Dov from Rozov (ר' אלחנן דובער מרוזוב)

**Mashgiach on Chassidus at Tomchei Temimim; long-time gabai to the Rashab and Rayatz.**

## Overview

R' Elchanan Dov spent almost all his life in Lubavitch. At various times he was engaged in manufacturing kosher tallisim (prayer shawls), a small factory he ran; later in tobacco and cigarette trade after WWI. For most of his career, he served as the gabai (personal attendant/secretary) to both the Rebbe Rashab and then the Rayatz.

He was appointed mashgiach on Chassidus in the large hall of TT mid-winter of 5668 (1908). His appointment was announced by R' Eliezer Kaplan who read aloud a letter from the Rashab to the assembled students in the large hall.

The Rashab once said of him at a farbrengen: "He doesn't hear [outwardly], Chanan is an inward person" (ער הערט דאך ניט, חנן איז א פנימי) — suggesting his spirituality was entirely interior rather than outwardly expressed.
