# Leah Beila Levin (née Bilienky)

Mother of the memoirist R' Yisrael Yehuda Levin; a learned and resilient woman who maintained the family's religious life during extreme hardship in the Soviet era.

## Biography

Leah Beila was born in Linitz and raised in Dashov, where her father R' Yisrael Bilienky served as shochet. Her family were descendants of R' Avraham Yehuda of Linitz and traced their lineage through R' Nachman Kassover.

She was unusually educated for a woman of her time and place — having studied in a *cheder* as a girl (a custom in that region). She could read and write Hebrew and taught Tanakh, writing, and arithmetic to her husband's students.

After her husband R' Efraim Shmuel died (15 Shvat 5693/1933), she was widowed with several children and suffered from a heart condition. She wrote a letter to the Rebbe Rayatz (dated Rosh Chodesh Tammuz, summer 5694/1934) describing the family's situation.

She died during the Soviet period; the memoir notes her as HY"D (killed in sanctification of God's name), suggesting she perished in the Holocaust or under Soviet persecution.

## Letter to the Rebbe

A letter written by Leah Beila to the Rayatz (5694/1934) is preserved in the Chabad Library archive. In it she describes: being born in Linitz; her parents; growing up in Dashov; her husband's devotion as a melamed; his death; the children's ages and situation; her own illness (heart disease).
