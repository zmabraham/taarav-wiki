# Rabbi Moshe of Pozna

## Overview
Rabbi Moshe of Pozna was the paternal grandfather of the Alter Rebbe (Rabbi Shneur Zalman of Liadi), serving as the head of the community (Rosh Kahal) in Pozna.

## Details
Rabbi Moshe was the son of Rabbi Yehuda Leib, who was himself a descendant of the Maharal of Prague through the line of Rabbi Shmuel (son of Rabbi Betzalel, the only son of the Maharal). This genealogical connection to the Maharal is discussed at length in the book, based on the Sefer HaZichronos of the Friediker Rebbe and other sources such as Beit Rebbi.

A gravestone inscription from Prague records: "25 Tishrei [5426/1665] Yehuda Leib son of [the head] Shmuel R. [Betzalels]." This confirms the Alter Rebbe's descent from the Maharal of Prague through Rabbi Shmuel.

The book discusses at length scholarly disputes about whether Rabbi Moshe was the av beit din (rabbinical court head) of Pozna or merely the Rosh Kahal (lay community leader). The consensus is that he was Rosh Kahal.
