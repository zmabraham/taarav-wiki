# Emperor Alexander I of Russia

## Overview
Emperor Alexander I (1777–1825) ruled Russia from 1801 to 1825. He succeeded his father Paul I (who was assassinated on 10 Nisan 5561 / April 1801) and issued the final release of the Alter Rebbe from his second imprisonment.

## Details
When Paul I was assassinated in April 1801, Alexander I became emperor while the Alter Rebbe was still imprisoned following his second arrest. Alexander reviewed the case and issued the Alter Rebbe's final release certificate.

The Alter Rebbe received his official release under Alexander on 27 Nisan 5561 (1801), allowing him to return to his home in Liozna/Belarus.

Under Alexander I's reign, the Alter Rebbe settled in Lyady (Liadi) in 1801 after his liberation, where he served as the leader of Chabad Chassidim until the French invasion of Russia in 1812. The Alter Rebbe sided strongly with Russia against Napoleon's invasion, fleeing with his family from Lyady as Napoleon's forces advanced, and passing away during the journey in Piena, on 24 Tevet 5573 (1812).
