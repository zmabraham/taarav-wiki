# Governor Nikolai Khavanski

**Lived**: early 19th century  
**Role**: Russian provincial governor who administered the case against the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] (1825–1826)

---

## Overview

Governor Nikolai Khavanski was the key Russian administrative figure overseeing the investigation and prosecution of Rabbi DovBer Schneersohn — the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] — following the denunciations filed by [[entities/people/simcha-kissin|Simcha Kissin]] of Vitebsk in 1825.

---

## Role in the Case

The documentary collection [[summaries/masar-vegeulat-hamtza|*Maasar V'Geulat Admur HaEmtza'i*]] — which reproduces actual Russian imperial court and police records — identifies Khavanski as the governing authority who administered the case at the provincial level.

As a regional governor in the Russian imperial system, Khavanski had authority over law enforcement, local courts, and the processing of charges through the legal hierarchy. The Mitteler Rebbe's case moved from local authorities in Vitebsk through Khavanski's gubernia administration and ultimately to the Imperial Senate in Petersburg.

---

## Historical Significance

Khavanski represents the impersonal face of Russian imperial power as it intersected with Jewish communal life. The documentary approach of _Maasar V'Geulat Admur HaEmtza'i_ — reproducing actual Russian bureaucratic documents — allows readers to see directly how the imperial system processed a Chassidic religious leader: by his Russian name ("Berkeh Shniur" — a bureaucratic transliteration of "Ber, son of Shneur"), through standard administrative procedures, with no awareness of his spiritual status.
