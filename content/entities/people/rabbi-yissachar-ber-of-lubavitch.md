# Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch

## Overview
Rabbi Yissachar Ber of Lubavitch was one of the three leaders of the Chassidim in Russia and Lithuania appointed by Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk (Horodoker), alongside the Alter Rebbe and Rabbi Yisrael of Polotsk.

## Details
He served as the Maggid (preacher) of the town of Lubavitch. He is mentioned as "כבוד מו"ה ישכר בער סג"ל, מ"מ דק' לובאוויטש" (the honored Rabbi Yissachar Ber Segal, Maggid of Lubavitch) in the famous letter of Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk from 4 Adar 5542 (1782), which appointed the three leaders of Chassidism in Russia.

Over time, as the Alter Rebbe's leadership became preeminent, Rabbi Yissachar Ber subordinated himself to the Alter Rebbe, becoming his disciple. As Beit Rebbi describes: "Over some years he recognized increasingly the greatness of the Rebbe and bowed his head before him to be his disciple, and was his emissary."

He was one of the last of the Maggid's disciples to remain in Belarus and Lithuania; the others had either made aliyah to Eretz Yisrael, been expelled by Mitnagdim, or passed away.

The town of Lubavitch, where he served, would later become the seat of the Chabad Rebbes.
