# Rabbi Hillel Halevi of Paritch

## Overview
Rabbi Hillel Halevi of Paritch was one of the leading disciples of the Mitteler Rebbe (Rabbi Dovber of Lubavitch) and later the Tzemach Tzedek. He was a prominent Chabad rabbi and author of the Pelach HaRimon series of works.

## Details
He was known for recording the discourses he heard from the Mitteler Rebbe, to which he would add his own notes and explanations (within half-circles, marked with segol symbols). After the Mitteler Rebbe's passing in 1828, he continued recording discourses heard from the Tzemach Tzedek.

He also wrote his own discourses, all published together in the series Pelach HaRimon.

His original manuscripts, including several volumes in his own handwriting (MSS 482, 1046, 1228, and part of 756), are preserved in the Lubavitch library, along with dozens of volumes containing copies of his notes and discourses.
