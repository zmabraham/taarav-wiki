# Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk (Horodoker)

## Overview
Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk (also known as the Horodoker) was one of the leading disciples of the Maggid of Mezeritch and a major figure in the spread of Chassidus in Belarus and Lithuania. He made aliyah to Eretz Yisrael in 1777 and settled in Tiberias, where he led the Chassidic community until his passing.

## Details
He established a major Chassidic center in Horodok (near Vitebsk) between 1773-1777 before making aliyah. He was one of three leading disciples appointed by the Maggid to lead the Chassidim in Russia and Lithuania — alongside the Alter Rebbe and Rabbi Yisrael of Polotsk.

Before his aliyah in summer 1777, he passed through Mogilev on the Podolia (near Turkey), where the Alter Rebbe accompanied him. There, the Chassidim urged the Alter Rebbe to remain in Russia to oversee them.

After settling in Eretz Yisrael, he continued to correspond with the Russian Chassidim, ultimately designating the Alter Rebbe as the sole leader of the Chassidim in Russia and Lithuania. His key letters appointing the Alter Rebbe are preserved and quoted extensively in the book.

He passed away on 1 Iyar 5548 (1788).

His disciple Rabbi Avraham of Kalisk continued his work in Eretz Yisrael.
