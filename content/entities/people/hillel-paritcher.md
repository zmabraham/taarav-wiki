# Rabbi Hillel of Paritch

**Born**: 5555 (1795), Paritch, Belarus  
**Died**: 11 Av 5624 (1864), Kherson Province, Ukraine  
**Role**: One of the great Chabad masters of the 19th century; student of the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] and [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]]; legendary chassidic figure

---

## Overview

Rabbi Hillel of Paritch is one of the towering figures of 19th-century Chabad Chassidism. Though he never served as Rebbe, he was widely recognized as a master of Chassidic philosophy and spiritual practice. He spent years studying under the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] and later under the [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]], absorbing the deepest teachings of Chabad thought.

---

## Early Life and Studies

Born in Paritch, a small town in Belarus, Rabbi Hillel was a prodigy from youth. He initially sought to study under the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] but arrived too late — the Alter Rebbe had already passed away in 1812. He then attached himself to the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] in [[entities/places/lubavitch|Lubavitch]], studying under him for years with extraordinary devotion.

After the Mitteler Rebbe's passing (1827), he became a devoted follower of the [[entities/people/tzemach-tzedek|Tzemach Tzedek]].

---

## Spreading Chassidism in Southern Russia

Rabbi Hillel devoted much of his life to spreading Chassidism in the Jewish communities of southern Russia and Ukraine — areas that were traditionally less connected to the Chassidic heartland of Belarus and Lithuania. He traveled extensively, teaching, inspiring, and establishing Chabad communities in the Kherson province and beyond.

---

## Mastery of Prayer and Service

Rabbi Hillel was legendary for his intense prayer and spiritual devotion. Stories of his meditative prayer sessions — lasting hours, with visible rapture — circulated widely. He was considered a living embodiment of the Chabad ideal of _avodas ha'tefillah_ (service of prayer), combining deep intellectual comprehension of Chassidic philosophy with heartfelt emotional engagement.

---

## Literary Works

His major work, **Pelach HaRimon** (_Pomegranate Segment_), is a profound Chabad philosophical text that applies Kabbalistic and Chassidic concepts to the Torah portions. It is studied in Chabad yeshivos and circles to this day.

---

## Death and Legacy

Rabbi Hillel died on 11 Av 5624 (1864) in the Kherson province. His gravesite became a pilgrimage destination. He is remembered as one of the purest exemplars of the Chabad chassidic path — a figure whose life demonstrated that the deepest philosophical understanding and the most heartfelt spiritual devotion are not opposites but complements.
