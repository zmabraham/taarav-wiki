# Avrahamele Kvash

A study companion of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin from Kamenets (Kamenets-Podolsk). Together they traveled from Kyiv to Vitebsk in spring 5688 (1928) to study at Tomchei Temimim, without permission or prior arrangement. He continued as a study partner in Vitebsk.

## Description
Avrahamele Kvash is mentioned in two places: once as a co-traveler to Vitebsk (spring 5688), and later (in a letter to the Rayatz in 5694) as having been the author's study companion from the beginning of his yeshiva period, now located in Riga. A footnote records that after studying in Vitebsk, he illegally crossed the border to Poland and studied at Tomchei Temimim in Warsaw and Otwock, and perished in the Holocaust.
