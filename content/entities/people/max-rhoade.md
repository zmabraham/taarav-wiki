## Biography

American lawyer and advocate who played a pivotal role in securing the release and exile of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak from Soviet imprisonment in 1927. Worked through diplomatic channels and international pressure to commute the death sentence and arrange for the Rebbe's release and eventual exile to Riga.
