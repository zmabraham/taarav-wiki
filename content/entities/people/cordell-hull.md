# Cordell Hull

US Secretary of State under President Franklin Roosevelt. Intervened on behalf of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) during the rescue effort in 1939–1940.

## Role in Rescue

Cordell Hull was among the senior US government officials whose intervention helped secure Rayatz's release and exit from Nazi-occupied territory. He acted together with State Department official Robert Pell.
