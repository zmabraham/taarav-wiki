# R' Yehoshua ben R' Kalman Mizrach

A wealthy man in Riga who assisted with the transfer of relief funds during WWII.

## Currency Transfer Scheme

R' Mizrach helped facilitate the transfer of money between the USA and Riga, working through a currency exchange arrangement. This enabled [[entities/organizations/vaad-pidyon-shvuyim]] funds from Rayatz in the USA to reach Jews in need through [[entities/people/mordechai-chafetz]] in Riga.
