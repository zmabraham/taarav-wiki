# R' Meir ben Rephael Yafeh — ר' מאיר בן רפאל יפה

**Head of Vilna Chabad Community; Arrested in the 1798 Persecution**

**Full Name**: Rabbi Meir ben Rephael Yafeh (known as "Meir Raphael's")
**Dates**: Late 18th century
**Position**: Head (rosh) of the Chabad community in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]]
**Affiliation**: Early Chabad-Lubavitch (Alter Rebbe's generation)
**Key event**: Arrested 1798 during anti-Chassidic persecutions

---

## Overview

Rabbi Meir ben Rephael Yafeh — known in Yiddish as "Meir Raphael's" (son of Rephael) — was the head of the Chabad community in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]] during the critical and dangerous period of the late 18th century. He led the community through the most intense phase of Mitnagdic persecution. In 1798, he was arrested by Russian authorities together with other Lithuanian Chassidim in a wave of persecution directly connected to the same denunciations that led to the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe's]] arrest in Liadi.

---

## Historical Context

### Vilna in the Late 18th Century

During R' Meir's leadership:

- **Vilna Gaon alive and active**: The [[entities/people/vilna-gaon|Vilna Gaon]] (1720–1797) was at the height of his opposition to Chassidism
- **Active persecution**: Vilna's Jewish community actively persecuted local Chassidim
- **Herem**: Chassidim had been excommunicated by Vilna and other communities
- **Government danger**: In the Russian Empire, religious deviations could bring government intervention

---

## Leadership of Vilna Chabad

### Early Community

R' Meir led the Vilna Chabad community in the earliest period:

- **Founding era**: Among the first Chassidim to establish a minyan in Vilna
- **Nusach Ari**: Introduced Chabad's distinct liturgical rite (Nusach Ari) into Vilna
- **Small community**: The community was small, secretive, and vulnerable
- **Connection to Alter Rebbe**: As the community's head, R' Meir maintained the connection to the Alter Rebbe's court

### Under Pressure

His leadership required navigating constant danger:

- **Vilna Gaon's opposition**: The Gaon personally opposed Chassidism and supported measures against Chassidim
- **Social ostracism**: Vilna Chassidim faced communal exclusion
- **Economic pressure**: Mitnagdim could affect the livelihoods of Chassidim
- **Physical danger**: At the extreme, arrest and exile were real threats

---

## The 1798 Arrests

### The Wave of Persecution

In 1798, the anti-Chassidic forces in Vilna took their most aggressive action:

- **Denunciations**: Mitnagdic leaders reported Chassidim to the Russian government as dangerous sectarians
- **Government action**: Russian authorities, responding to the reports, arrested approximately 30 Chassidim from Vilna and surrounding areas, including [[entities/places/vidz|Vidz]]
- **R' Meir arrested**: As head of the Vilna Chabad community, R' Meir ben Rephael Yafeh was among those arrested
- **Simultaneous persecution**: The same wave of denunciations led to the Alter Rebbe's arrest in Liadi
- **Interrogation**: The arrested Chassidim were interrogated about their beliefs and practices

### Outcome

- **Released**: The Chassidim were eventually released — the Russian government found no basis for criminal charges
- **Alter Rebbe's release**: The Alter Rebbe was also released, emerging on 19 Kislev 5559 (1798) — the day celebrated as "Chag HaGeulah" (Festival of Redemption)
- **Legacy**: The episode demonstrated both the dangers facing early Chassidim and the ultimate failure of persecution to destroy the movement

---

## Legacy

R' Meir ben Rephael Yafeh represents:

- **First generation courage**: One of the earliest Chassidim willing to maintain the movement in the most hostile possible environment
- **Vilna Chabad founder**: Part of the founding generation of Chabad in Vilna
- **Persecution narrative**: His arrest links the Vilna story directly to the Alter Rebbe's story
- **Persistence**: Despite persecution, he and others like him ensured Chabad's presence in Vilna survived
