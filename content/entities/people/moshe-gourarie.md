# R' Moshe Gourarie

**Chabad leader in Eretz Yisrael; member of founding committee of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael (1941)**

## Biography

R' Moshe Gourarie was a leading figure in the Chabad community of Eretz Yisrael during the British Mandate period. He served as a member of the founding committee of Agudas Chassidei Chabad in Eretz Yisrael when it was formally established in 1941.

The Gourarie family name is prominent in Chabad — the Gouraries were a distinguished family with Chabad leadership connections. R' Moshe Gourarie's presence on the founding committee reflects the participation of established Chabad families in building the organizational infrastructure of the community in the Land of Israel.

During this period, the Chabad community in Eretz Yisrael was centered primarily in the old established settlements — Hebron (before the 1929 riots), Jerusalem, Tzfat (Safed), and the coastal cities — as well as in newer immigrant communities. The founding of Agudas Chassidei Chabad provided a central address for coordinating activities across these scattered communities.

## Significance

R' Gourarie represents the established Chabad families who settled in Eretz Yisrael and took responsibility for building communal institutions. His presence on the 1941 founding committee alongside R' Zislin and R' Rozenblum shows that the organizational initiative came from a diverse group of community leaders rather than from any single dominant figure.
