# R. Tzvi Hirsh Baal Shem

One of the early Chabad Chassidim who made aliya to the Holy Land as part of the first wave of Chassidic immigration. He arrived in [[tiberias|Tiberias]] in **5539 (1779)**, two years after the main group led by [[menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]] arrived in 5537 (1777).

## Background

- Came from **Vitebsk** (Belarus), a major center of both Chabad Chassidim and the Mitnagdim (opponents of Chassidism)
- His epithet "Baal Shem" (literally "Master of the Name") likely refers to a family tradition of practical Kabbalah, or possibly family connection to the Baal Shem Tov's circle — this was distinct from being a Chabad Rebbe

## Aliya and Settlement

He arrived in Tiberias in 5539 (1779), joining the Chassidic community that had settled there under the leadership of R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and R. [[avraham-of-kalisk|Avraham of Kalisk]]. Tiberias was the initial center of this first Chassidic aliya before some later moved to Safed and Hebron.

## Historical Significance

R. Tzvi Hirsh represents the second wave of Chabad aliya — those who followed the pioneers of 1777 in subsequent years, swelling the community. The *Toldos Chabad beEretz HaKodesh* documents several such individuals, showing that the 1777 aliya was not a single event but a sustained migration.
