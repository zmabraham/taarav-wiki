# R' Yosef Levin (Rabbi of Zhivitiov)

Paternal grandfather of the memoirist R' Yisrael Yehuda Levin; served as rabbi in several Ukrainian towns.

## Biography

R' Yosef Levin was born around 5612 in Letichev, near Derazhne in the Kamenitz-Podolsk district of Ukraine. He served as rabbi in several small Ukrainian towns over the course of his career.

He died around 5649, leaving his wife Sara Yehudis a widow with three young children: Efraim Shmuel (then about 11 years old), Chaya Bracha, and Avraham Yaakov.

After his death, Avraham Yaakov eventually emigrated to the United States, where he died young. Chaya Bracha married a man named Malkhman and lived with her family in Derazhne until WWII, after which her fate was unknown.
