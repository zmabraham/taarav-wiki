# David Eliezrie — דוד אליעזרי

**Role**: Chabad shliach (emissary); author; journalist; Jewish communal activist  
**Known for**: Author of _Undaunted_ — English-language biography of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]  
**Location**: Orange County, California (Yorba Linda/Huntington Beach area)

---

## Overview

David Eliezrie is a Chabad shliach based in Southern California and one of the most prominent English-language writers on Chabad history. His biography of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]], _Undaunted_, is the primary English-language account of the sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe's life — drawing on Chabad archives and documented oral history to present the Rayatz's story for a broad audience.

---

## _Undaunted_ — Biography of the Rayatz

Eliezrie's biography covers the Rayatz's life from childhood in [[entities/places/lubavitch|Lubavitch]] through his death in Brooklyn in 1950, spanning five countries and four decades. The book provides extensive narrative detail not always available in the Hebrew primary sources, including:

- The famous **Akedah ceremony** (1895) in which the Rashab told his 15-year-old son he would be the next Rebbe
- Detailed accounts of the founding of [[events/tomchei-temimim-founding|Tomchei Temimim]]
- The [[events/rayatz-arrest-1927|1927 arrest and liberation]] by the Soviet GPU
- The [[events/escape-from-warsaw-1939|escape from Warsaw]] in 1939
- The [[events/great-escape|Great Escape]] of Chabad chassidim from the USSR (1946–47)
- The Rayatz's declaration upon arrival in America: _"America iz nisht anderesh — America is no different"_

The book is described by Chabad historian Menachem Zigelbaum as documenting how the Rayatz "started from scratch three times."

---

## Significance

_Undaunted_ serves as Source 14 in the Chabad History Wiki, the only English-language primary text in the corpus. It provides biographical and narrative detail that complements the Hebrew institutional histories, making it indispensable for understanding the Rayatz as an individual.
