# Rabbi Yitzchak Yosef

Rabbi Yitzchak Yosef (born 18 Tevet 5712 / 1952) is the former Rishon LeTzion (Sephardic Chief Rabbi of Israel), head of the "Chazzon Ovadia" yeshiva, and author of the encyclopedic halachic series "Yalkut Yosef." He is a member of the Shas party's Moetzet Chachmei HaTorah.

## Biography

Born in Jerusalem as the third son of Rabbi Ovadia Yosef. He studied at Talmud Torah Yavne and then at Yeshivat Porat Yosef and Yeshivat HaNegev in Netivot.

At age 18 he began collecting his father's halachic rulings. In 5733 (1973) he established the "Chazzon Ovadia" kollel, and in 5740 (1980) received rabbinical ordination. In 5752 (1992) he opened the "Chazzon Ovadia" yeshiva.

In 5773 (2013) he was elected Rishon LeTzion alongside Ashkenazic Chief Rabbi David Lau. At the end of his term, he was appointed to the Shas Moetzet Chachmei HaTorah.

## Connection to Chabad

As a child he participated in a Chabad evening class in the Shikunat Chabad neighborhood of Jerusalem, under Rabbi Naftali Roth, and joined the Rebbe's Chitas (daily Torah portions) practice at his father's encouragement.

Over the years he visited dozens of Chabad Houses worldwide, participated in Rambam completion celebrations, and repeatedly praised Chabad's global outreach work. In an interview (5783/2023) he stated: "There is nothing like the Chabad movement in bringing people closer to Judaism."

He praised the Alter Rebbe as "not just any rebbe — a rebbe who was a Torah genius, the author of the Shulchan Aruch HaRav — a remarkable work."
