# R' Moshe Chefetz

**Chabad mashpia who taught at Tomchei Temimim branches in Riga and Poland**

## Biography

R' Moshe Chefetz was a Chabad mashpia who served at Tomchei Temimim yeshiva branches in both Riga (Latvia) and in various Polish locations during the interwar period. He was among the corps of Chabad educators who formed the spiritual backbone of the yeshiva network during the Rayatz's leadership.

The Tomchei Temimim network in the 1920s and 1930s maintained branches across Eastern Europe, with the Rayatz in Riga (1927–1933) and then Warsaw (1933–1939) serving as the central hub. Mashpi'im like R' Chefetz traveled between branches and maintained the distinctive Chabad educational character — intensive study of Tanya and Chassidic texts alongside traditional Talmudic study.

After the Rayatz moved from Riga to Warsaw in 1933, R' Chefetz continued serving in the expanded network in Poland. The outbreak of World War II in 1939 brought catastrophic destruction to these communities.

## Significance

R' Moshe Chefetz illustrates the dedicated corps of Chabad educators who sustained the yeshiva network across Eastern Europe during the interwar period, working between multiple locations and under increasingly difficult political conditions. The Polish Tomchei Temimim branches he served were among the Chabad institutions destroyed in the Holocaust.
