# Chiune Sugihara

Chiune Sugihara (1900–1986) was the Japanese Imperial Consul in Kovno, Lithuania, who in July–August 1940 defied his government's orders and issued thousands of transit visas to Jews fleeing Nazi occupation. His actions saved an estimated 6,000 Jews.

## Connection to Chabad

Sugihara's transit visas enabled Lubavitcher chassidim, including Rabbi Meir Ashkenazi and his community, to travel from Kovno through Vladivostok to Kobe and Shanghai, where they survived the war.
