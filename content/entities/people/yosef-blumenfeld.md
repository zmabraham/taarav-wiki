# R' Yosef Blumenfeld

**Chabad Chassid in Warsaw; figure in the Polish Chabad community during the interwar period**

## Biography

R' Yosef Blumenfeld was a Chabad Chassid in Warsaw, documented in the Toldos Chabad BePolin, Lita, u'Latvia. He was part of the Warsaw Chabad community during the Rayatz's residence there (1933–1939).

Warsaw's Jewish community was the largest in Poland and the second largest in the world. The Chabad presence in Warsaw during this period was centered around the Rayatz's court and the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva branch. R' Blumenfeld was among the Chassidim who formed the lay leadership supporting these institutions.

## Significance

R' Yosef Blumenfeld represents the Warsaw Chabad community during its final flourishing under the Rayatz. The Polish Chabad community was destroyed in the Holocaust, and figures like R' Blumenfeld represent the pre-war vitality of Polish Chassidic life.
