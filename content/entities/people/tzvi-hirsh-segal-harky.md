# R. Tzvi Hirsh Segal Harky

An early Chassidic immigrant to the Holy Land who arrived in [[tiberias|Tiberias]] in **winter 5544 (1784)**, with his wife. He is documented in *Toldos Chabad beEretz HaKodesh* as part of the sustained Chassidic aliya following the initial group of 1777.

## Background

- "Harky" (הארקי) refers to Harki, a town or region in the Russian Empire, possibly in Belarus or Ukraine
- "Segal" (סג"ל) is a Levitic designation — an abbreviation for *Segan Leviyyah* (Deputy Levite), indicating his Levitic lineage
- He made aliya with his wife, indicating a family commitment to settlement rather than just individual study

## Aliya and Period

He arrived in winter 5544 (1784), seven years after the first Chassidic aliya. By this point, the Holy Land community under R. [[avraham-of-kalisk|Avraham of Kalisk]] (who had taken leadership after R. [[menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]]'s death in 5548/1788) was established in Tiberias.
