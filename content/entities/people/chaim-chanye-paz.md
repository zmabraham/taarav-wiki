# R. Chaim-Chanye Paz

Brother of [[zusha-paz|R. Zusha Paz]] of Moscow, and a Chabad Chassid who attended the narrator's **tena'im ceremony** in Moscow in Nisan 5700 (1940). He was **killed in the Holocaust**.

## Background

R. Chaim-Chanye Paz was part of the Moscow Chabad community — the underground circle of observant Jews who maintained religious life despite Soviet repression. He is identified as the brother of R. Zusha Paz, the shamash of the Mariina-Roshcha synagogue.

## The Tena'im Ceremony

He was present at the formal engagement ceremony (tena'im) in Moscow when the memoir's narrator became engaged to the daughter of [[zusman-pradkin|R. Zusman Pradkin]]. His presence at this religious gathering indicated his active participation in the underground Jewish life of Moscow.

## Holocaust Death

The memoir records that R. Chaim-Chanye Paz was **killed in the Holocaust**. This likely occurred during the German advance into Soviet-held territory in 1941–1942, when the Nazis reached many communities in which Jews had remained. His fate contrasts with that of the narrator, who escaped to Samarkand.

## The Paz Family in the Holocaust

The fates of the Paz family illustrate the varied outcomes among Soviet-era Chabad Chassidim:
- [[zusha-paz|R. Zusha Paz]] — arrested by NKVD (Soviet repression)
- **R. Chaim-Chanye Paz** — killed in the Holocaust (Nazi genocide)
