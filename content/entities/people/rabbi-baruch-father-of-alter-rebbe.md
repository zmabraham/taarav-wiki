# Rabbi Baruch (Father of the Alter Rebbe)

## Overview
Rabbi Baruch was the father of Rabbi Shneur Zalman (the Alter Rebbe). He lived in Liozna and later wandered in exile, eventually settling in Sulit (Sulish) in Hungary, where he died and was buried with great honor.

## Details
His full name appears on a gravestone as "Yisrael Baruch son of Avraham" — though the Alter Rebbe's letters consistently refer to his father simply as "Baruch." This discrepancy has been the subject of scholarly discussion. One explanation is that after becoming a disciple of the Baal Shem Tov, he stopped using the name "Yisrael" to avoid calling his master by his own name.

His father's name (the Alter Rebbe's paternal grandfather) is recorded as "Shneur Zalman" in the chain of lineage in Hayom Yom, and is listed as "Avraham" in some sources including the Pinkas of Liozna.

He passed away before 1792 (5552), which is established based on when the Alter Rebbe's grandchildren began being named "Baruch" in his memory.

The Pinkas of Liozna (Lozna) records:
- "15 Kislev 5467: Baruch son of Avraham was accepted into the chevra kadisha"
- "15 Kislev 5504: The child Shneur Zalman son of Rabbi Baruch was accepted into the chevra kadisha"
