## Biography

R' Yisrael Bilienky served as shochet in Dashov. He was descended from the family of R' Avraham Yehuda of Linitz. He was connected by marriage to R' Rabinowitz.

He died young (in the prime of life — "באבי ימיו"), leaving seven orphaned children. The eldest, then eighteen years old, took on the shochet position to support the family. His daughter Leah Beila later married the melamed R' Efraim Shmuel Levin of Zhivitiov.

## Maternal Great-Great Lineage

Leah Beila's letter to the Rebbe (5694) traces: "My father's mother's father [=R' Yosef Yehuda] was shochet of Ir Chadash, descended from R' Nachman Kassover and R' Moshe his son."
