# R. Mendel Neveler (Gribov)

A **Maggid Shiur** (teacher/lecturer) at **Tomchei Temimim** — the underground Chabad yeshiva in [[kremenchug|Kremenchug]] (Ukraine) — during the early Soviet era. He is identified in the memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* both by his hometown "Neveler" (from Nevel, Belarus) and the name "Gribov."

## Career

R. Mendel taught at Tomchei Temimim in Kremenchug, one of the cities where the Chabad yeshiva network maintained a functioning underground operation despite Soviet pressure. He was a mainstay teacher at the Kremenchug branch.

## A Memorable Encounter

The memoir describes the narrator arriving at Kremenchug in the middle of the night after the Kherson yeshiva branch had just been raided and its leadership arrested. R. Mendel, knowing the situation in Kherson had deteriorated, had come to the train station hoping to receive news from arriving travelers. He recognized the narrator at the station.

This vignette illustrates how the underground yeshiva network maintained information flows through personal contacts and chance encounters, given that telephone communications were not secure.

## The Gribov Connection

"Gribov" (Hrybyv?) may refer to a second town or family name. In Soviet-era records, individuals sometimes went by multiple names or place-designations to confuse authorities.
