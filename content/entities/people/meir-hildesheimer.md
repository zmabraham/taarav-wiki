# Rabbi Meir Hildesheimer

**Born**: c. 1864, Berlin, Germany  
**Died**: c. 1934  
**Role**: Berlin Orthodox rabbi; son of [[entities/people/azriel-hildesheimer|Rabbi Azriel Hildesheimer]]; continued his father's relationship with the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]]

---

## Overview

Rabbi Meir Hildesheimer was the son of the renowned [[entities/people/azriel-hildesheimer|Rabbi Azriel Hildesheimer]] and continued his father's leadership in Berlin's Adass Jisroel community. He maintained and deepened the relationship with the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]] that his father had initiated in 1885.

---

## Connection to the Rashab

The Rashab's correspondence, documented in [[summaries/chabad-germany|*Chabad Rebbes and German Jewry*]], includes letters to Rabbi Meir Hildesheimer. The Rashab praised him as "the first to genuinely engage with the situation of Russian Jews" — a significant accolade indicating that of all the German Orthodox rabbis, Meir Hildesheimer showed the most serious commitment to the cause of Russian Jewry.

This relationship was part of the broader Chabad engagement with German Orthodox Jewry that included correspondence with Rabbi Pinchas HaKohen of Ansbach and Rabbi Yaakov Rosenheim, all coordinated through the Rashab's efforts to build an Orthodox front against both the Haskalah (Enlightenment) and growing antisemitism.

---

## Role in Supporting Russian Jews

Rabbi Meir Hildesheimer was involved in the _Vaad Magen UMekhaseh_ (Committee for Protection of Russian Jewry) — one of the organizational frameworks through which European and Western Jewish leaders supported their beleaguered Russian coreligionists.
