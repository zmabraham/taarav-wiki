# R' Eliezer Kaplan (ר' אליעזר קאפלאן)

**Office director (and possibly deputy director) of Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Manager/director of the TT office; possibly also "Deputy Director" |
| **Active** | Lubavitch era (~5668 and surrounding years) |

## Overview

R' Eliezer Kaplan managed the administrative office of Tomchei Temimim. He handled correspondence, student admissions paperwork, stipend distribution, and official communications from the Rebbe Rashab.

A notable moment: when R' Elchanan Dov from Rozov was appointed as mashgiach on Chassidus for the large hall, it was R' Eliezer Kaplan who came to the hall and read aloud the letter of appointment from the Rebbe Rashab to the assembled students.
