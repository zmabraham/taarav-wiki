# R' Yehuda Shmotkin

Chabad activist based in Eretz Yisroel (Land of Israel). Served as an agent for acquiring books for the Lubavitch Library.

## Library Work

R' Shmotkin was tasked by [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) — through correspondence by secretary R' [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin]] — with acquiring books in Eretz Yisroel for the Lubavitch Library. He was instructed to obtain books according to specific lists sent from Otwock, and to solicit donations of books from authors and others.

Rayatz instructed him that the library was interested in anything printed in Hebrew letters in any language and on any Jewish topic, including periodicals, announcements, and Kol Kore pamphlets — though not daily newspapers.
