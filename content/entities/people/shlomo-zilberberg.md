# R' Shlomo Zilberberg

Chabad survivor of the Holocaust based in Warsaw. Participated in post-war recovery efforts for the Lubavitch Library.

## Post-War Activity

After the liberation of Warsaw, R' Shlomo Zilberberg, together with R' [[entities/people/pinchas-klein]], located some of the Lubavitch Library books that had survived the war in Warsaw. These books were held by the Jewish Central Committee's historical division. Eventually, some of the recovered books were returned.
