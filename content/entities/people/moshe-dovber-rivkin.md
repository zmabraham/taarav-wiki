# R' Moshe DovBer Rivkin

**Temim; R"M and mashgiach at Toras Emes Jerusalem; first Chabad-affiliated mashgiach of Yeshivas Torah Vodaath**

## Overview

R' Moshe DovBer Rivkin (abbreviated "Ramad"R") was a graduate of Tomchei Temimim who served as a Rosh Mesivta (R"M) and mashgiach on nigleh (Talmudic studies) at Yeshivas Toras Emes in Jerusalem. In 5688 (1928), he came to America together with R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin on a fundraising trip for Toras Emes, and remained in the United States, eventually becoming the first spiritual director (_menahel ruchni_) of Yeshivas Torah Vodaath in New York.

## At Toras Emes Jerusalem

R' Moshe DovBer served at Toras Emes during its Jerusalem phase (following the yeshiva's reconstitution there after WWI). His role was specifically focused on the nigleh (Talmudic and halachic) curriculum of the yeshiva, as distinct from Chassidic studies — the mashpia for Chassidus was R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin. R' Yisrael Jacobson, who knew him well, described him as "a lamdan and man of experience in yeshiva administration." At Toras Emes he is said to have significantly elevated the spiritual level of the yeshiva.

## Coming to America (1928)

In late spring of 5688 (1928), R' Moshe DovBer Rivkin came to America along with R' Shlomo Zalman Havlin. Their official purpose was to raise funds for Toras Emes, which needed a stable American support base. They stopped in Riga to be received by the Rayatz before continuing to New York.

In America, R' Moshe DovBer became a central figure in the small Chabad community that had coalesced around Agudas HaTmimim and Agudas Chassidei Chabad. R' Yisrael Jacobson hosted the visitors at his own home for several weeks. Negotiations were held between the visitors and the leadership of Agudas Chassidei Chabad about whether Toras Emes should fundraise independently or under the umbrella of the general Chabad organization in America.

R' Moshe DovBer's temperament, Jacobson noted, was that of "a man of books" (_ish hasefer_) — scholarly, precise, and not naturally suited for public fundraising. Nevertheless, he gained a following among Temimim who remembered him from Lubavitch.

## Role in the Maamad

During R' Moshe DovBer's stay in America, Jacobson also involved him in organizing fundraising for the Rebbe's _maamad_ (personal support fund), which was in a severely depleted state. R' Moshe DovBer was made chairman of a special committee for the maamad under the umbrella of Agudas Chassidei Chabad. A notable gathering took place — approximately 12 Temimim assembled, each pledging at least $100 for the maamad (R' Gershon Simpson pledged $150); this represented a significant mobilization of the then-tiny Chabad community in America.

## Mashgiach at Torah Vodaath

R' Yisrael Jacobson introduced R' Moshe DovBer to the leadership of Yeshivas Torah Vodaath, where the position of mashgiach ruchani (spiritual director) had become vacant following R' Shraga Feivel Mendlowitz's departure from that role. R' Nissan Tluskin also advocated for R' Moshe DovBer. He was accepted for the position — Jacobson notes that at the time "there was no one more suitable in America" — and thus became the first Chabad-affiliated figure to serve in a senior role at Torah Vodaath.

## Significance

R' Moshe DovBer Rivkin's trajectory — from R"M at Toras Emes in Jerusalem to mashgiach at Torah Vodaath in New York — illustrates the bridge that Chabad-trained figures sometimes created between Chabad institutions and the broader American Orthodox yeshiva world. His appointment at Torah Vodaath represented an early instance of Chabad educational influence extending beyond the Chabad community, at a time when both institutions were in their formative American phase.
