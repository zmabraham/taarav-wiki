# Semyon Zhegulin (Governor of Belarus)

## Overview
Semyon Zhegulin served as the civil governor (advisory councillor) of Belarus (Belorussia) during the time of the Alter Rebbe's first arrest in 1798. He was the regional official through whom the Alter Rebbe's release was processed.

## Details
Zhegulin was the Vitebsk-based official who oversaw Belarus province. When the Alter Rebbe was arrested in summer 1798, Zhegulin sent the Alter Rebbe's books and papers sealed with his official seal to St. Petersburg along with the Alter Rebbe himself.

His letter dated 30 Tishrei 5559 (1798) to the General-Governor Lopukhin states: "His papers and books were immediately sent in one box and one valise sealed with my seal."

The Alter Rebbe's release certificate (svobodny propusk) from Emperor Paul I, dated 20 Kislev 5559 (November 17, 1798), directed the Alter Rebbe to report to Zhegulin upon returning to Belarus: "He who has this certificate is the Jew Rabbi Zalman son of Baruch... and must appear to the governor of Vitebsk, the advisory councillor Zhegulin."
