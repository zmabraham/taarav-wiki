# Rabbi Shlomo Sternberg

The rav (rabbi) of Lipovetsh in the early Soviet period. He prayed at one of the batei midrash in Lipovetsh along with the shochet R. Levi. The Levin family davened at this beis midrash after their arrival in 1920.

## Description
Rabbi Sternberg was a respected figure in Lipovetsh. After his departure for Kyiv around 5685, the Yevsektsia's pressure on the town intensified, as the remaining acting dayan was described as having poor opinions and was willing to represent religion in a humiliating public debate with the Yevsektsia organizer.

R. Sternberg's son Mordechai was the study partner of the author's brother Yosef. R. Mordechai Sternberg subsequently studied at Tomchei Temimim in Nevel and Kharkov, left Russia in 5706, and settled in Bnei Brak from 5723.
