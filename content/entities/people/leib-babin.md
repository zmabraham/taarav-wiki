# R' Leib Babin (ר' ליב באבין)

**Teacher (melamed) at Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch; known as "the Vieytker teacher."**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Melamed at TT, Lubavitch |
| **Nickname** | "Der Vieytker Melamed" (the teacher from Vieytke/Vietka) |
| **Active** | From 5668 (1908) at minimum |

## Overview

R' Leib Babin was hired as a teacher for Tomchei Temimim in the winter of 5668 (1907/1908). He taught the lower classes (Bava Kamma, Gittin). He was known for his extreme thinness — described as "literally skin and bones" — and needed soft cushioning to sit at a table.

He had encyclopedic knowledge of the tractates Bava Kamma and Gittin by heart (including the letters/words), as well as other tractates taught in smaller yeshivos.

The Rebbe Rashab is reported to have said of him: "Only the teacher remained in him" — meaning his identity had entirely merged with his teaching role.

Israel Jacobson was placed in his class when he first arrived at TT. He quickly advanced from Class 1 (Bava Kamma) to Class 2 (Gittin), where Tosafos were first introduced.

## An Incident

During winter 5668, students noticed sparks flying from the chimney. They told R' Leib about it. He scolded them: "What business is this of yours? Learn!" Within half an hour the building was on fire.
