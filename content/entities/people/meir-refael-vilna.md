# Rabbi Meir Refael of Vilna — ר' מאיר רפאל מווילנא

**Period**: Late 18th century  
**Role**: Chabad chassid in Vilna; among those arrested during the anti-Chassidic campaign of 1798  
**Significance**: Early victim of Mitnagdim persecution of Chabad in Lithuania

---

## Overview

Rabbi Meir Refael was a Chabad chassid in Vilna who was among those arrested during the Mitnagdim's campaign against Chassidim in 5559 (1798–1799). His arrest is documented in the Chabad-Poland-Lithuania volume (_Toldot Chabad BePolin, Lita veLativya_) as part of the broader anti-Chassidic legal actions that accompanied the [[events/the-herem-1796|Vilna Gaon's Herem of 1796]].

---

## The Hoshana Raba Arrests

In the period following the Vilna Gaon's most severe ban against Chassidism, Mitnagdim in Vilna took the unprecedented step of using Russian imperial legal channels against Chassidic leaders in their community. Rabbi Meir Refael and other Chabad chassidim in Vilna were arrested on **Hoshana Raba 5559 (1798)** — a date the _Toldot Chabad BePolin_ volume documents as the beginning of organized anti-Chassidic legal persecution in Lithuania.

This pattern — using secular/governmental courts to persecute religious opponents — was the inverse of what would later happen to the Alter Rebbe (1798), the Mitteler Rebbe (1826), and the Rayatz (1927), where Russian and Soviet authorities used their legal systems against Chabad leadership.

---

## Historical Significance

The arrests of Chabad chassidim in Vilna in 1798 represent the transition from religious denunciation (herem/ban) to legal persecution. They document how deeply the Mitnagdim-Chassidim conflict could cut — willing to involve the Czarist legal system in internal Jewish religious disputes.
