# Yehoshua (Yeishke) Dobravsky

Son of Manie Dobravsky and grandson of Rav Mendel Dobravsky. He was ill and bedridden when Mikhael Teitelboim and the author came to enroll him in the Samarkand underground Tomchei Temimim. He recovered miraculously after being enrolled, becoming one of the first three students.
