# Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson

Rabbi Yisrael Jacobson was an early Chabad emissary to America who laid the groundwork for Chabad's American presence before the Rayatz's arrival in 1940. He was involved in establishing Chabad institutions in New York.

## Connection to Chabad

Jacobson was among the first Chabad representatives in America, preparing the infrastructure that would become critical when the Rayatz established his headquarters in Crown Heights.
