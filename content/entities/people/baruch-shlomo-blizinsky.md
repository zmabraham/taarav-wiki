# Rabbi Baruch Shlomo Blizinsky

Rabbi Baruch Shlomo Blizinsky (born 5 Tevet 5724 / 1964, Bnei Brak) is a rabbi and mashpiah at the "Rabbi Mendel's Shul" synagogue in Kfar Chabad, a rosh yeshiva at Tomchei Tmimim Kfar Chabad, and a member of Machon Ohalei Shem for editing Chabad texts.

## Biography

Born in Bnei Brak to Rabbi Shneiur Zalman Blizinsky and Chana, grandson of the Chassidic mashpiah Rabbi Meir Shalom Blizinsky.

In his youth he studied at Tomchei Tmimim Kfar Chabad, and in 5744 (1984) traveled to Tomchei Tmimim Central at 770 in Brooklyn for his "year of the group." After his return he received rabbinical ordination.

After marrying Sarah (daughter of Rabbi Mordechai Gorodetsky of Nachalat Har Chabad), he settled in Kfar Chabad and served as rosh midrasha at Achei Tmimim Rishon LeTzion yeshiva (which was then based in Kfar Chabad), later becoming its head until it closed in 5765 (2005). He then became rosh yeshiva at Tomchei Tmimim Kfar Chabad.

In 5756 (1996) the Tzeirei Chabad website was established and he was invited to join its team of rabbis answering halachic questions online. He also signed the ruling that year declaring the Rebbe as Melech HaMashiach.

When the "Rabbi Mendel's Shul" synagogue was established in the northern Levi Yitzchak neighborhood of Kfar Chabad (named after Rabbi Menachem Mendel Brook), he was appointed rabbi and mashpiah.
