# Berel (Grinfeld) Kornitser

A brilliant Chabad talmid chacham and mashgiach, described as "the ornament of the temimim from Rostov." He had exceptional talents in both Nigleh (Talmud) and Chassidus (Dach), and was known for his genuine humility despite his greatness — preferring to live like other young students on three rubles a day rather than accept the large stipend that R. Zalman Schneersohn would have given him. He was mashgiach in Tomchei Temimim in Kharkov, Warsaw, and Otwock. He perished in the Holocaust.

## Description
R. Berel Kornitser appears in the memoir in the Malachovka section (Pesach 5694). He arrived late at night at R. Zalman Moshe's home, sent on a financial errand from R. Zalman Schneersohn. The author was already there for a Yud-Gimmel Nissan (yahrzeit of the Tzemach Tzedek) farbrengen and was overjoyed when R. Berel joined. Then R. Yaakov Maskalik (Zhuravitser) also arrived, and the three of them held a warm farbrengen.

The memoir notes he once repeated a maamar in Rostov from memory that had not been written down, having heard it from the Rebbe. Despite his great stature, he would not accept special treatment and preferred to receive the same three rubles a day as ordinary students.
