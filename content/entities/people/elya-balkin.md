# Elya Balkin

A Chabad rav present in the Malachovka/Moscow area in the 1930s. Known for a remarkable saying: "What is a Rebbe? A Rebbe is the Ein Sof [infinite Divine] clothed in a body." He would arrive at the beis midrash in the early morning to warm up near the stove and speak with the young men.
