# Rabbi Esriel (Azriel) Hildesheimer

Rabbi Esriel Hildesheimer (1820–1899) was a leader of German Orthodox Jewry and founder of the Hildesheimer Rabbinical Seminary in Berlin. His approach combined strict Orthodoxy with modern scholarship.

## Connection to Chabad

Hildesheimer represented the German Orthodox world that interacted with Chabad through the Rebbes' visits to German-speaking territories.
