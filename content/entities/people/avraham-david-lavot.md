# R' Avraham David Lavot

**Rabbi of Nikolaev; author of Kav Naki; ancestor of the Seventh Rebbe**

## Biography

R' Avraham David Lavot served as the rabbi of Nikolaev, a major city in the southern Kherson gubernia of the Russian Empire (present-day southern Ukraine). He was a Chabad Chassid and a significant halachic authority whose rulings guided the large Jewish community in this port city.

He is the author of Kav Naki (כב נקי), a halachic work dealing with practical Jewish law. The title, drawn from biblical and rabbinic idiom suggesting precision and purity, reflects the nature of the work — a careful, clear presentation of halachic decisions.

R' Avraham David Lavot is particularly notable in Chabad genealogy as an ancestor of the Seventh Rebbe, R' Menachem Mendel Schneerson. This ancestral connection makes him part of the extended Schneersohn family network that shaped Chabad's leadership dynasty.

## Significance

R' Lavot exemplifies the combination of Chabad Chassidic affiliation with serious halachic scholarship. His presence in Nikolaev helped anchor a significant Chabad community in the Black Sea region of southern Russia. The Kav Naki represents the rabbinic literary output of provincial Chabad rabbis who contributed to the broader Orthodox responsa literature. His genealogical connection to the Seventh Rebbe gives him special significance in Chabad history.
