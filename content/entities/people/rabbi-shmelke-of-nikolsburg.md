# Rabbi Shmelke of Nikolsburg

## Overview
Rabbi Shmuel Shmelke Horowitz of Nikolsburg was a prominent disciple of the Maggid of Mezeritch and brother of Rabbi Pinchas (Baal HaHaflaah). He served as Chief Rabbi of Nikolsburg (Moravia).

## Details
He and his brother Rabbi Pinchas traveled together to visit the Maggid of Mezeritch at Rovneh, before taking their respective rabbinical appointments in Frankfurt am Main and Nikolsburg (Germany). During that visit they saw the Alter Rebbe's Shulchan Aruch manuscript and praised it enthusiastically.

The family genealogy shows that their election process began in spring 1771: Rabbi Pinchas was chosen for Frankfurt, and Rabbi Shmelke was chosen for Nikolsburg. Rabbi Shmelke arrived in Nikolsburg on the Friday eve of 27 Sivan 5533 (1773).
