# Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem of Worms

Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem of Worms (born c. 5297/1537, Krakow) was a head of a Kabbalistic yeshiva, a master Kabbalist, and the founder of the circle of hidden tzaddikim (nistarim). His circle prepared the spiritual ground for the emergence of the Baal Shem Tov and Chassidic movement. Rabbi Baruch Batlan, great-great-grandfather of the Alter Rebbe, was one of his students.

## Biography

Born in Krakow to Rabbi Yosef Yuzpa, himself described as a hidden holy man and descendant of Spanish exiles. According to the Frierdiker Rebbe's Memoirs (Sefer HaZikronot), Rabbi Yosef Yuzpa lived as an ascetic in Krakow for many years refusing to marry, until a miraculous chain of events — including a deceased husband appearing to his widow in a dream — led to his marriage, from which Rabbi Eliyahu was born. He was named for the Prophet Elijah.

Orphaned at 13, he wandered and eventually settled in Worms in 5350 (1590), establishing a Kabbalistic yeshiva there. He became famous as a baal shem and wonder-worker.

Due to opposition from the scholars of Speyer led by Rabbi Pinchas Zelig, who objected to his Kabbalistic activities, he moved his yeshiva to Prague in 5384 (1624). In Prague he was supported by Rabbi Shmuel, grandson of the Maharal, and together with another man built a courtyard complex for the yeshiva.

He lived over 118 years according to the Sefer HaZikronot.

## The Nistarim Circle

In 5381 (1621) he founded the circle of hidden tzaddikim (nistarim) who studied Kabbalah in holiness and purity. Before his death he transmitted his entire Torah and secrets to his foremost student Rabbi Yoel of Zamoshtsh (Rabbi Yoel Baal Shem), who led the circle after him. Rabbi Yoel's student Rabbi Adam Baal Shem later introduced the Baal Shem Tov into the circle of hidden tzaddikim.

This chain — Eliyahu Baal Shem → Yoel Baal Shem → Adam Baal Shem → Baal Shem Tov — constitutes the spiritual genealogy that prepared the Chassidic movement.

## Connection to Chabad

Through his student-chain leading to the Baal Shem Tov, Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem is a foundational figure in the spiritual ancestry of Chabad. Additionally, Rabbi Baruch Batlan — great-great-grandfather of the Alter Rebbe — was his student, making Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem a teacher in the direct family tradition of the Alter Rebbe. The Frierdiker Rebbe documented the nistarim circle's history in his Memoirs (Sefer HaZikronot), which is the primary source for Rabbi Eliyahu Baal Shem's biography.
