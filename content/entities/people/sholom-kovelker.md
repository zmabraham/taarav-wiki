# Sholom Kovelker (Levertov)

Son of R. Dov Kovlyaker (Levertov), who had been arrested in Moscow and died in prison. Sholom came to Samarkand during WWII. He was one of the first three students of the underground Tomchei Temimim established there by Mikhael Teitelboim.
