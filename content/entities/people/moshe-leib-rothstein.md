# Rabbi Moshe Leib Rothstein (Zusia Shifrin)

Secretary to [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz). Born approximately 5660 (1900). His real/original name was Zusia Shifrin. Married Keila, daughter of R' Yisrael Chanowitz.

## Escape via Trans-Siberia

Together with [[entities/people/shmuel-zalmanow]] and [[entities/people/yitzchak-dov-aushpal]], R' Rothstein escaped from Europe via:
- **Vilna** → **Riga** → **Russia** (Trans-Siberian Railroad) → **Japan** (Kobe) → **San Francisco** → **New York**

He arrived in New York on **Parshas Noach, Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan 5701** (autumn 1940).

## Post-War Service

After the war, R' Rothstein resumed service as Rayatz's secretary from **5706** (1945–46). Following Rayatz's passing in 5710 (1950), he served as secretary to the **7th Lubavitcher Rebbe**, R' [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-schneerson]].

He passed away on **9 Marcheshvan 5728** (1967).
