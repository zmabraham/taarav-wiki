# Rabbi Nissan Nemenov

**Born**: 1907, Russia  
**Died**: 1984, France  
**Role**: Senior Chabad mashpia (spiritual guide) in France; one of the most influential Chassidic teachers of the twentieth century

---

## Overview

Rabbi Nissan Nemenov was one of the most revered Chabad mashpi'im (spiritual mentors) of the twentieth century. Born in Russia, he studied at the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva during the dangerous Soviet underground period, absorbing the deep Chassidic tradition directly from the generation that had learned under the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rebbe Rashab]] and the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]].

After escaping the Soviet Union, he settled in France, where he became the central mashpia for European Chabad — a role he held for decades.

---

## Tomchei Temimim in the Soviet Underground

Rabbi Nemenov was a product of the heroic underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva system that the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] maintained in the Soviet Union despite severe persecution. During this period, studying Torah — let alone Chassidus — was a criminal act, and the students and teachers risked imprisonment and worse. This crucible shaped Rabbi Nemenov into a figure of extraordinary depth and commitment.

---

## Mashpia in France

After arriving in France, Rabbi Nemenov became the primary mashpia for the Chabad yeshiva and community there. The [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|seventh Rebbe]] sent many students to learn from him, recognizing his unique ability to transmit the inner dimension of Chassidic teaching.

Rabbi Nemenov was known for:

- **Depth of Chassidic knowledge** — He possessed a comprehensive mastery of the entire corpus of Chabad Chassidic literature, from the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe's]] Tanya through the [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab|Rashab's]] deep hemshechim.
- **Personal spiritual guidance** — He guided students not just in intellectual study but in the personal, experiential dimension of avodas Hashem (divine service) that is the hallmark of Chassidic life.
- **Living connection** — He represented a living chain of transmission from the Chassidic world of pre-war Russia to the postwar generation.

---

## Legacy

Rabbi Nemenov is remembered as a towering figure in the transmission of authentic Chassidic experience. Many of the prominent Chabad mashpi'im and scholars of the late twentieth century were his students. He bridged the world of pre-war Russian Chassidism and the modern Chabad movement, ensuring that the inner, experiential dimension of Chassidus was not lost in the upheavals of the twentieth century.
