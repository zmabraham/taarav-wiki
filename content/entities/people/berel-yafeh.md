# Berel Yafeh

A Chabad chassid in Vitebsk who hosted newly arriving yeshiva students. When R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin arrived in Vitebsk on Rosh Chodesh Iyar 5688 (1928), he was directed to R. Berel Yafeh's home, where he stayed for the day and the night, noting that it was Rosh Chodesh and there was space available as others had gone to a farbrengen.
