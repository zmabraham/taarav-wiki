# Dr. Nissan Mindel

**Born**: c. 1912  
**Died**: 1999  
**Role**: Secretary of the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]]; post-WWII emissary to Europe; prolific Chabad author

---

## Overview

Dr. Nissan Mindel served as personal secretary to the [[entities/people/rayatz|Rayatz]] (sixth Rebbe) and later held key positions under the [[entities/people/rebbe-7th|seventh Rebbe]]. He was also a prolific author who translated and explained Chassidic works for English-speaking audiences, making him one of the most important bridges between Chabad's Russian/Yiddish world and Western readers.

---

## Service to the Rayatz

Nissan Mindel joined the Rayatz's circle in the American period, serving as personal secretary and handling much of the Rebbe's English-language correspondence. Given the Rayatz's limited English and the need to engage American Jewish institutions, government officials, and the press, Mindel's role as English secretary was strategically critical.

He was described in [[summaries/rayatz-biography|*Undaunted*]] as a post-WWII emissary to Europe — tasked with reaching survivors, assessing the state of Jewish communities, and coordinating relief and rescue activities on behalf of the Rayatz in Brooklyn.

---

## Authorship

After the Rayatz's passing, Mindel continued his work under the seventh Rebbe and became one of Chabad's most important English-language writers:

- Translated key Chassidic texts
- Wrote accessible books explaining Chabad philosophy
- Contributed to Kehot Publication Society's English catalog

His work made Chabad's intellectual tradition accessible to the American Jewish community in ways that directly supported the seventh Rebbe's outreach mission.
