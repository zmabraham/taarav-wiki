# R' Shmuel Tzvi Lison

**Chabad figure in Lithuania during the interwar period; documented in sources on Chabad in Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia**

## Biography

R' Shmuel Tzvi Lison was a Chabad Chassid and leader active in Lithuania during the interwar period. He is documented in the Toldos Chabad BePolin, Lita, u'Latvia as part of the Chabad presence in the Lithuanian communities.

Lithuanian Jewry was largely non-Chassidic in orientation, but significant Chabad communities existed throughout the country, particularly in smaller towns and in Kaunas (Kovno). The interwar period (1918–1940) was a time of relative autonomy for Lithuanian Jews under the independent Lithuanian Republic, which gave Jewish communities more freedom to organize religious education and communal life than was possible in Soviet Russia.

R' Lison's work in Lithuania was part of the broader Chabad organizational effort during the Rayatz's leadership, when Chabad attempted to maintain and expand its presence in the communities that remained outside Soviet control.

## Significance

R' Shmuel Tzvi Lison represents the layer of Chabad leadership in Lithuania — a country where Chabad was a minority presence within an already minority Orthodox community. His activities helped maintain Chabad identity in a challenging cultural environment.
