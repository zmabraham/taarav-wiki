# R' Hershel Gotlib (Kavner)

**Rosh Yeshiva at Tomchei Temimim, Lubavitch**

## Overview

R' Hershel Gotlib (also recorded as "Kavner" — apparently referring to his place of origin, Kovna/Kaunas) served as a Rosh Yeshiva (head of a division) at Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch during the early twentieth century. He taught one of the upper-level classes of the yeshiva. His full name appears to be R' Hershel Kavner Gotlib.

## At Tomchei Temimim

R' Avraham Eliya Axelrod, in a letter preserved in R' Yisrael Jacobson's archive describing his years of study at Tomchei Temimim, records that in "summer 5667" (1907) he studied under "R' Hershel Kavner [Gotlib], Rosh Yeshiva." The summer semester at Lubavitch was one of two annual terms, and R' Hershel taught the more advanced students.

Later in the same description, Axelrod describes the Kherson branch of Tomchei Temimim (founded around 5677/1916), where R' Moshe Yosef Gotlib was among the students — quite possibly a relative of R' Hershel.

## Connection to Tzvi Gotlib

The book mentions R' Tzvi Gotlib from Kovna as the Rosh Mesivta (Rosh Yeshiva, designated by R"M in the text) at the Kherson branch of Tomchei Temimim — R' Yisrael Jacobson notes that this was his second appointment as Rosh Mesivta at Tomchei Temimim. It is probable that R' Hershel Gotlib (Kavner) and R' Tzvi Gotlib are from the same Gotlib family from Kovna, though the precise relationship is not specified in the sources.

## Significance

R' Hershel Gotlib's role at Tomchei Temimim represents the senior faculty who shaped the Torah scholarship of the yeshiva in its middle period, after the yeshiva had expanded beyond its initial founding stage but before the disruptions of WWI and the Russian Revolution. The Gotlib family's continued involvement with Tomchei Temimim branches (appearing in both Lubavitch and Kherson) suggests a family with deep institutional commitment to the yeshiva.
