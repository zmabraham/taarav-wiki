# R' Yehuda Leib of Yanowitz (Maharil)

**Elder brother of the Alter Rebbe; served as rabbi of Yanowitz; helped transcribe and publish Torah Or**

## Biography

R' Yehuda Leib was the elder brother of R' Shneur Zalman of Liadi (the Alter Rebbe), both sons of R' Baruch of Vitebsk. He is known by the acronym Maharil. As a young man he studied alongside his younger brother, and the two shared a close bond throughout their lives.

R' Yehuda Leib served as rabbi of Yanowitz (Yanowitch), a town in the Vitebsk gubernia of White Russia. He was deeply involved in the early Chabad movement and was one of the key figures responsible for transcribing and editing the Alter Rebbe's discourses. Tradition credits him with significant editorial work on the Alter Rebbe's Torah Or, the collected Torah discourses that were later published posthumously.

He outlived his younger brother, who died in Piena in 1812 on the way from Liadi during the Napoleonic retreat. The Maharil witnessed the early leadership of the Mitteler Rebbe and continued to be a respected elder of the Chabad community.

## Significance

As the Alter Rebbe's brother, the Maharil represents the immediate family circle at the origin of Chabad. His role in transcribing Torah Or underscores the collaborative nature of early Chabad literary production, where the Rebbe's oral discourses depended on dedicated disciples and family members for their preservation. His presence in Yanowitz also extended Chabad influence to an additional White Russian town.
