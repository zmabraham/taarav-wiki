## Biography

Founder and first Lubavitcher Rebbe. Founded the Chabad school of Chassidism. Author of the Tanya, the foundational text of Chabad philosophy, and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav. Twice imprisoned by the Czarist government. Established the town of Liadi as his base and later fled Napoleon's invasion. His yahrzeit is 24 Tevet.
