# R. Yitzchak HaMatmid

R. Yitzchak HaMatmid ("the Diligent One") was an elder Chabad chassid in Vitebsk who made a profound impression on the young yeshiva students there in the late 1920s. He is remembered for his extraordinary dedication to Torah study and his role in transmitting chassidic teachings orally.

## Description
R. Yitzchak HaMatmid is mentioned in the Zichronos memoir in connection with the Vitebsk Tomchei Temimim underground yeshiva period (around 5688/1928). He was an elder chassid who, despite the dangerous Soviet environment, continued to learn and transmit chassidic teachings.

His epithet "HaMatmid" refers to the ideal of uninterrupted, intensive Torah study — a hallmark of the Tomchei Temimim yeshiva ethos. He repeated chassidic maamarim (discourses) from memory, helping to preserve the oral tradition of Chabad teachings in a time when possessing printed chassidic books was dangerous.
