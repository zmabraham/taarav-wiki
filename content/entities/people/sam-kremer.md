# Attorney Sam Kremer

American attorney involved in the rescue of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) from Nazi-occupied Warsaw in 1939.

## Rescue Efforts

Together with Attorney [[entities/people/asher-rabinowitz]], Kremer worked in the USA to mobilize diplomatic and political support. He helped coordinate the involvement of US government officials, members of Congress, and other prominent figures.
