# R' Gershon Simpson

**Temim; treasurer of Agudas HaTmimim; active in early American Chabad**

## Overview

R' Gershon Simpson was a graduate of Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch (main campus) who settled in New York and became one of the founding members and treasurer of Agudas HaTmimim — the organization of Tomchei Temimim alumni founded in America in 1925.

## Background

R' Gershon and his brother R' Eliya Simpson both studied for an extended period at Tomchei Temimim in Lubavitch. R' Yisrael Jacobson described both brothers as "pillars" of Agudas HaTmimim — meaning the core founding group around whom the organization was built.

## Difficult Adjustment to America

When Jacobson arrived in America and encountered R' Gershon Henoch Eichorn (another Temim), he found him working in a laundry for $30 a week — a situation Jacobson found distressing for a talmid chacham. He tried to arrange a position as shammash (synagogue caretaker) for him, but the conditions were unsuitable. R' Gershon Simpson was part of the small circle Jacobson describes: former Temimim trying to maintain their identity and find meaningful roles in American Jewish life, where the infrastructure for traditional learning and Chabad religious life was severely underdeveloped.

The book notes a Yiddish saying that captures the atmosphere: the Temimim who came to America described themselves as having "run away to America" (ontlofn in Amerika) — reflecting that the Rebbes had not generally encouraged emigration to America, and those who went felt a degree of guilt or at least awareness that they had chosen a spiritual challenge.

## Founding and Role in Agudas HaTmimim

At the founding meeting of Agudas HaTmimim on Motzaei Shabbat Shemini 5686 (1925), R' Gershon was elected treasurer. The initial meetings were held at his brother R' Eliya's home on 116 West Street.

## Later Chabad Activities

When the Rayatz visited America in 5689–5690 (1929–1930), R' Gershon Simpson was among the approximately 30 Temimim who attended for yechidus before Rosh Hashana.

## Significance

R' Gershon Simpson, together with his brother R' Eliya, represents the family network that provided a stable base for early American Chabad. The two brothers, both Lubavitch-trained Temimim, gave the fledgling Agudas HaTmimim a family-anchored leadership structure — R' Eliya as president, R' Gershon as treasurer — that helped sustain the organization through its first critical years.
