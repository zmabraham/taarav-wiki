# R. Dovber Kavalyaker (Levertov)

A prominent Chabad Chassid in **Soviet Moscow** who is known both by the name "Kavalyaker" and "Levertov." He played a crucial role in saving the narrator of *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* from the German advance in 1941, and was subsequently arrested by the NKVD and exiled.

## The Warning That Saved Lives

In 5701 (1941), as the German Wehrmacht advanced rapidly toward Moscow, the narrator consulted R. Dovber about whether to flee. R. Dovber gave decisive advice: **"Leave immediately."** He urged the narrator not to wait, and this counsel prompted the narrator's family to flee Moscow eastward to [[samarkand|Samarkand]] in Central Asia. This decision saved them from the German occupation and the Holocaust.

> R. Dovber Kavalyaker (Levertov) advised the narrator to flee Moscow at once as the Germans approached — advice that preserved the narrator's family.

## Arrest and Exile

After providing this advice, R. Dovber Kavalyaker was himself **arrested by the NKVD** (Soviet secret police) and **exiled** to an unknown location in the Soviet interior. His fate after exile is not known from the memoir.

## Identity

The memoir refers to him as "R. Dovber Kavalyaker (Levertov)" — indicating he was known by two names or that "Kavalyaker" was a nickname. The term *kavalyaker* (from the Russian *кавалер*, "chevalier/bachelor") may have been a personal sobriquet.

## Significance

R. Dovber represents the Chabad leaders who actively helped Chassidim navigate the dangers of the Soviet-Nazi dual threat — giving practical guidance even at personal risk. His arrest came shortly after providing the warning.
