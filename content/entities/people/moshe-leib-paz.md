# R. Moshe Leib Paz

A Chabad Chassid from **[[klimovitch|Klimovitch]]** (Klimavichy), Belarus, who served as **shochet** (ritual slaughterer), **mohel** (circumciser), and Chabad Chassid in his community. He is the grandfather-in-law of the unnamed narrator of *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu*.

## Background

- **Hometown**: [[klimovitch|Klimovitch]] (Klimavichy), a town in the Mogilev region of Belarus
- **Occupations**: Shochet (ritual slaughterer) and mohel (circumciser) — twin roles that marked him as a key communal religious functionary
- **Identity**: A Chabad Chassid, part of the Belarus Chabad community

## Significance

R. Moshe Leib represents the "triple-role" figure common in small Jewish towns: shochet, mohel, and de facto religious authority — often in towns too small to support a full-time rabbi. In the Soviet era, these individuals were the backbone of underground Jewish religious life.

## Family Connection

- **Son-in-law**: [[zusman-pradkin|R. Zusman Pradkin]] of Shumyatz (also a shochet)
- **Granddaughter**: The narrator's wife
- The family's Paz surname appears in both grandfather (Moshe Leib Paz) and the Moscow shamash [[zusha-paz|R. Zusha Paz]] — suggesting a broader Paz family network in the Belarus region
