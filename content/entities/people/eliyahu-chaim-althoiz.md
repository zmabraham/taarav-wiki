# Rabbi Eliyahu Chaim Althoiz הי"ד

Chabad rabbi in Riga, Latvia.

## Connection to Rayatz

R' Althoiz received an esrog (citron used for Sukkos) from [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) — indicating he was among the trusted Chabad community in Riga.

## Martyrdom

R' Eliyahu Chaim Althoiz was burned alive in a synagogue by the Nazis on **10 Kislev 5702** (approximately December 1941), together with:
- R' [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin]]
- R' [[entities/people/yitzchak-horowitz-masmidam]]
