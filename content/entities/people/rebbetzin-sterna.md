# Rebbetzin Sterna

**Wife of the Alter Rebbe; daughter of R' Yehuda Leib of Vitebsk; mother of the Mitteler Rebbe**

## Biography

Rebbetzin Sterna was the wife of R' Shneur Zalman of Liadi, the Alter Rebbe and founder of Chabad Chassidus. She was the daughter of R' Yehuda Leib, a respected figure in the Vitebsk community. The marriage brought the young Shneur Zalman into a family with deep ties to White Russian Jewry.

Rebbetzin Sterna played an integral role in supporting the Alter Rebbe's leadership during decades of intense activity: the rapid growth of the Chabad movement, two arrests by the Czarist authorities (1798 and 1800), the flight from Liadi during Napoleon's 1812 invasion, and the hardships of the road. According to sources in Toldos Chabad BeRussia, she was known for her personal strength, her management of the Rebbe's household in Liadi, and her devotion to the Chassidic community.

She was the mother of R' DovBer Schneerson, the Mitteler Rebbe and second leader of Chabad. She predeceased the Alter Rebbe by a short time, or died within the same general period of the Napoleonic retreat. Chassidic sources preserve accounts of her wisdom and her role in supporting scholars and students who came to Liadi.

## Significance

Rebbetzin Sterna embodies the essential but often underdocumented role of the Rebbe's spouse in sustaining the Chabad court. The Rebbetzin's management of the household and hospitality to thousands of Chassidim who visited Liadi over decades was a pillar of the movement's practical functioning. Her Vitebsk origins also illustrate the geographic networks that shaped the Alter Rebbe's connections within White Russia.
