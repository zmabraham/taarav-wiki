# Zalman Schneersohn (Rav, finance manager)

A Chabad rav who was responsible for managing the financial stipends for the rabbis of anash in the Moscow/Russia region. He would send R. Berel Kornitser and others on financial missions. His wife also traveled on these financial matters. Not to be confused with other Schneersohns in the Chabad dynasty.
