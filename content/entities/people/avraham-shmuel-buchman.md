# R' Avraham Shmuel Buchman

Chabad activist in Poland who helped operate the clandestine yeshiva in the Radom ghetto during WWII.

## Radom Ghetto Yeshiva

During the German occupation of Poland, a secret Tomchei Temimim yeshiva operated in the Radom ghetto. R' Buchman was involved in maintaining this underground educational institution despite the extreme danger.
