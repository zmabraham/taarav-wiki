# Bricha

The **Bricha** (בריחה — "flight/escape") was a clandestine **post-WWII underground escape network** that organized the illegal migration of Jewish refugees and Holocaust survivors from Eastern Europe westward to the DP (displaced persons) camps in Germany and Austria, and ultimately to Palestine and other destinations.

## Origins and Character

The Bricha was organized primarily by **young Israel-oriented men** (often members of Zionist youth movements or Haganah) who had survived the war. They established a network of safe houses, guides, and bribed officials across:
- Poland
- Czechoslovakia  
- Hungary
- Austria
- Germany

## Scale of Operations

The Bricha facilitated the **flight of hundreds of thousands of Jewish refugees** from Eastern Europe in the years 1945–1948. It operated in a legal gray zone — most of the borders crossed were done without proper documentation, relying on bribes, smuggling routes, and sympathetic officials.

## Funding and Coordination

The memoir *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* describes the Bricha as coordinated with funding from:
- **[[joint-distribution-committee|JDC/Joint]]** (American Joint Distribution Committee) — financial support
- **[[vaad-hahatzala|Va'ad HaHatzala]]** (American rescue committee) — coordination

## Route Used by Chabad Refugees

The narrator of *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu* used the Bricha route to escape from the Soviet Union westward:
1. **Samarkand** → [[lemberg|Lemberg (Lvov)]] — initial escape from the USSR
2. **Lemberg** → Poland → further west
3. Eventually to [[paris|Paris]] (arriving before Shavuot 5707/1947)

The route through Lemberg was a key crossing point from Soviet-controlled territory into Poland.

## Chabad and Bricha

Many Chabad Chassidim used the Bricha to escape the Soviet Union after WWII. While the Bricha was primarily organized by Zionist groups, its services were used by religious Jews who simply wanted to escape Soviet territory, regardless of their views on Zionism.

## Historical Significance

The Bricha was one of the largest organized illegal population movements in modern history. It significantly contributed to the demographic pressure that helped lead to the establishment of the State of Israel in 1948, as European powers and the US were confronted with the reality of hundreds of thousands of homeless Jewish survivors.
