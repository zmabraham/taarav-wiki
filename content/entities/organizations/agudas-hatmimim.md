# Agudas HaTmimim (אגודת התמימים)

**Association of Tomchei Temimim alumni; founded multiple times; New York chapter founded 5686 (1925).**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founded (original)** | ~5670 (1910) in Lubavitch, with Rayatz as chairman |
| **Re-founded** | 5678 (1918) |
| **New York chapter** | Motzaei Shabbat Shemini Atzeret 5686 (1925) |
| **NY Founding location** | 137 W. 116th Street, home of R' Eliyahu Simpson |
| **NY founding members** | ~20 TT alumni |

## History

The idea of an alumni association for Tomchei Temimim graduates predates the New York chapter. In 5670 (1910), the Rebbe Rashab himself encouraged its founding, and the Rayatz served as chairman. Minutes, regulations, and a membership list from that era survived in the possession of R' Chaim Itkin of Centerville, Iowa.

The original organization was disrupted by WWI (~5674/1914). It was reconstituted in 5678 (1918) but disrupted again by the Bolshevik revolution.

The New York chapter was founded at a meeting on Motzaei Shabbat Shemini Atzeret 5686 (1925) at the home of R' Eliyahu Simpson. About 20 members attended — all TT alumni who had studied at various points, whether in the large hall or the children's classes. The organization maintained active correspondence with the Rayatz and received his enthusiastic support and encouragement.

## Role

Agudas HaTmimim served as the organizational backbone for American Chabad, predating the formal establishment of Agudas Chasidei Chabad. It kept in contact with the Rayatz, organized activities for TT alumni in the USA, and helped maintain Chabad identity in America.
