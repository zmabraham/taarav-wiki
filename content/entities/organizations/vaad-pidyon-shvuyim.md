# Vaad Pidyon Shvuyim (ועד פדיון שבויים)

Committee for the Redemption of Captives (literally: Committee for Ransoming Prisoners). Wartime relief organization established by [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) upon his arrival in the United States in 1940.

## Establishment

Rayatz established the Vaad upon arriving in New York (9 Adar Sheni 5700 / March 1940) to coordinate assistance for Jews trapped in Nazi-occupied Europe and Soviet-controlled territories.

## Activities

1. **Fundraising** in the USA and elsewhere
2. **Lists of families needing aid**:
   - ~100 families (~500 souls) in Lithuania
   - 163 persons in Nazi-occupied Poland
3. **Food packages** sent via Lisbon to those in need
4. **Visa procurement**: Obtained US government visas for 47 families (first list) and 62 more families (second list)
5. **Currency transfer**: Coordinated transfer of funds to Riga through R' [[entities/people/mordechai-chafetz]] and R' [[entities/people/yehoshua-mizrach]]'s currency exchange arrangement
