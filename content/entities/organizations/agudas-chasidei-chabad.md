# Agudas Chasidei Chabad (אגודת חסידי חב"ד)

**The central organizational body of Chabad-Lubavitch; managed by Israel Jacobson in New York from 5686.**

## Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | New York, ~5686 (1926) onward |
| **Key manager** | Israel Jacobson |
| **Relationship to Rayatz** | Direct correspondence; managed his American activities |

## Overview

Agudas Chasidei Chabad served as the main organizational vehicle for Chabad activities in America during the pre-770 era. Israel Jacobson managed it for years, handling correspondence with the Rayatz, organizing community activities, printing operations (including the Likkutei Torah), and coordinating the Rayatz's visit to America in 5689–5690 (1929–1930).

## Rayatz's First American Visit (1929–1930)

The organization was central to hosting and supporting the Rayatz during his first American visit:
- Arrival: 13 Elul 5689 (1929)
- Departure: 20 Tammuz 5690 (1930)
- High Holidays 5690 held at "Anshei Lubavitch" synagogue, Brownsville, Brooklyn (195 Watkins St.) under R' Tzvi Dakhavich
- The Rayatz prayed in Brownsville for Rosh Hashana after a delegation from "Anshei Lubavitch" (with ~1,200 members) invited him

## Printing Operations

Jacobson notes that the organization was involved in printing the Likkutei Torah. Details of the printing are described in chapter 25 of the memoirs.

## WWII Relief Role

During WWII, Agudas Chasidei Chabad under Rayatz's direction coordinated rescue and relief activities for Jews in Europe, including through the [[entities/organizations/vaad-pidyon-shvuyim]].
