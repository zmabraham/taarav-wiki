# Kolel Chabad

The **Kolel Chabad** (כולל חב"ד) was the central Chabad charitable organization for supporting the Chassidic community in the Holy Land (Eretz Yisrael). It operated from the late 18th century through the 20th century, evolving from an informal support structure to a formal organization with a constitution and elected leadership.

## Origins

### Pre-Separation Period (1777–1805)

When [[menachem-mendel-of-vitebsk|Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk]] led the first Chassidic aliya in 5537 (1777), the [[alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] organized support for the Holy Land community from Russia. This was informal and ad hoc.

### Establishment as Separate Kolel (5565 / 1805)

In 5565 (1805), **Kolel Chabad** was formally established as a separate charitable fund, distinct from the general Kolel of all Chassidic immigrants. This formalized the Chabad community's financial independence in the Holy Land.

**First Nasi**: The Alter Rebbe himself — overseeing from Russia
**First Gabai**: [[yaakov-of-smilian|R. Yaakov of Smilian]]

## Administrative Structure

Throughout the 19th century, Kolel Chabad had a **two-tier structure**:

**Diaspora tier** (Russia):
- **Nasi** (President): The reigning Chabad Rebbe
- **Gabai**: The practical administrator of fundraising
- Emissaries (*shadranim*) who traveled to communities collecting donations

**Holy Land tier**:
- **Memunei** (Administrators) in Jerusalem/Hebron: Distributed funds to community members
- Local leadership in each Chabad settlement

## The Rayatz Reorganization (5693 / 1933)

The [[rayatz|Rayatz]] (R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn) reorganized Kolel Chabad with a formal **Tochnon (constitution)** in 5693 (1933), requiring:
- Regular elections
- Formal governance structure
- **Va'ad Pekidei Kolel** (Committee of Administrators)

## Financial Activities

- Collected **ma'amad** money (voluntary support contributions)
- Managed endowments, including **Sir Moses Montefiore's endowment** for the Hebron community
- Distributed funds to Chabad families in Israel according to agreed formulas
- Organized periodic fundraising campaigns in diaspora communities

## Controversy

The long-running dispute between the [[alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] and [[avraham-of-kalisk|R. Avraham of Kalisk]] (leader of the Holy Land community) affected Kolel Chabad's finances for decades, as it raised questions about who had authority to distribute funds and how the community in the Land of Israel was to be governed.
