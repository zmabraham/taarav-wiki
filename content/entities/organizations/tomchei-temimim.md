# Tomchei Temimim (תומכי תמימים)

The central yeshiva network of the Chabad-Lubavitch movement, founded in Lubavitch in 5657 (1897) by the Rebbe Rashab (R' Shalom Dov Ber Schneersohn).

## Branches in Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia

During the tenure of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz), Tomchei Temimim had multiple branches across Poland, Lithuania, and Latvia:

- **Warsaw** — Central Poland branch
- **Otwock** — Rayatz's base, major yeshiva
- **Riga** — Latvia branch (led by R' [[entities/people/yehuda-aber]])
- Various smaller towns throughout Poland and Lithuania

## WWII

When Germany invaded Poland in 1939, Tomchei Temimim faced catastrophe. Students and faculty:

1. **Fled via Trans-Siberia**: Many students and faculty escaped via Vilna → Trans-Siberian Railroad → Kobe, Japan → Shanghai, where a functioning yeshiva continued throughout the war (see [[entities/events/trans-siberian-escape-1940-41]])
2. **Underground in the Radom ghetto**: A clandestine yeshiva operated in the Radom ghetto (see [[entities/events/radom-ghetto-yeshiva]])
3. **Martyrdom in Riga**: R' [[entities/people/yehuda-aber]] (Rosh Yeshiva) and many students perished in Riga, Kislev 5702

## Post-War

The Shanghai yeshiva relocated to New York after the war, reestablishing Tomchei Temimim in America.
