# Rabbi Menachem Mendel Horenstein

**Husband of Rebbetzin Sheina Horenstein (youngest daughter of the Rayatz); perished in the Holocaust in 1942.**

---

## Overview

Rabbi Menachem Mendel Horenstein was the husband of Rebbetzin Sheina Horenstein (nee Schneersohn), the youngest daughter of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz). He and Sheina were unable to escape Nazi-occupied Poland in 1940 when the rest of the Rayatz's family escaped to America. Both were murdered in the Holocaust in 1942.

Rabbi Horenstein's death represents one of the personal losses that marked the Rayatz's final decade — a loss that was inseparable from the broader destruction of European Jewry that the Rebbe witnessed from his position in Brooklyn, continuing to lead Chabad while knowing that his youngest daughter and son-in-law had been killed.

## Life and Role

Rabbi Horenstein and his wife Sheina lived in Poland during the late 1930s. When Germany invaded Poland in September 1939 and occupied Warsaw, the Rayatz's family — already in Poland — faced immediate mortal danger. The rescue mission of early 1940 succeeded in extracting the Rayatz, his wife, and his two elder daughters (Chana and Chaya Mushka) along with their husbands. Sheina and Rabbi Horenstein were not included in this escape; the reasons are not entirely documented, but the outcome was tragic.

They remained in Nazi-occupied Poland and were murdered in 1942, part of the systematic extermination of Polish Jewry. Rabbi Horenstein's death, alongside Sheina's, was the most personal expression of the Rayatz's own experience of the Holocaust — the loss of his youngest daughter and son-in-law directly to the Nazi genocide that he had been rescued from.
