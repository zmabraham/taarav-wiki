# Eliezer Karasik (Underground Chassid)

**Devoted Chassid in the underground network in Soviet Russia; participant in clandestine activities to sustain Jewish life under Communism.**

---

## Overview

Eliezer Karasik was a Chassid and activist in the underground Chabad network operating within Soviet Russia. During the systematic Soviet assault on Jewish religious life, Karasik participated in the clandestine activities that kept Chassidic Judaism alive — including underground Torah education, maintaining community connections, and the broader infrastructure of Jewish resistance to Sovietization.

Like many in the underground network, Karasik's activities were conducted in full knowledge of the consequences if discovered. The Soviet regime treated unauthorized religious organization as a political crime, and the penalties were severe. His participation in the network reflects the deep commitment of Chabad activists who understood their work as a continuation of the movement's tradition of self-sacrifice (mesirat nefesh) for Jewish continuity.

Karasik was one node in this network — a person who chose to remain engaged in Jewish religious life despite the Soviet prohibition, and who contributed to the collective effort to keep the tradition alive.
