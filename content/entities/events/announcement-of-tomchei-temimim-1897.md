# Announcement of Tomchei Temimim

## What Happened
At the wedding reception, the Rashab announces his revolutionary plan to open a yeshivah integrating Talmud and chasidic philosophy.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
