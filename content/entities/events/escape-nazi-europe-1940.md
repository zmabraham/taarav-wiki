# Escape from Nazi-Occupied Europe

## What Happened
Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak and his immediate family escaped from Nazi-occupied Europe in early 1940. The journey involved transit through several countries before boarding a ship for America. Not all his family could be saved; his daughter Sheina and son-in-law remained behind and perished in the Holocaust.

## Significance
Represented both a miraculous salvation and a profound personal tragedy. The Rebbe was saved but was forced to leave behind thousands of Chassidim and his own daughter who later perished.
