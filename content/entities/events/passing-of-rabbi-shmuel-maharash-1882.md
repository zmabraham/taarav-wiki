# Passing of Rabbi Shmuel (Maharash)

## What Happened
Three days after Yom Kippur, Rabbi Shmuel passes away in Lubavitch at age forty-eight.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
