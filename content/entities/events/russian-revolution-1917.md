# Russian (Bolshevik) Revolution

## What Happened
The Bolshevik Revolution of October 1917 (November by the Western calendar) brought the Communist Party to power in Russia. This seismic event fundamentally changed the conditions for Jewish religious life. The new regime's militant atheism and its Yevsektsiya (Jewish Section) directly targeted Chabad and all forms of traditional Jewish observance.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
One of the most consequential events in the history of Chabad and world Jewry. The Soviet state's systematic assault on religion would force the Frierdiker Rebbe to build an entire underground infrastructure to sustain Jewish life.
