# Funeral of the Rayatz

## What Happened
Thousands crowd Eastern Parkway for the Rebbe's funeral. He is interred at Old Montefiore Cemetery in Queens.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
