# Raza Relocates to Vitebsk

## What Happened
Rabbi Zalman Aharon (the Raza) relocates from Lubavitch to Vitebsk, deferring leadership to his younger brother.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
