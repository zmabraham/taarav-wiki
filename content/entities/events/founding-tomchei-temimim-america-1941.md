# Founding of Tomchei Temimim in America

## What Happened
In 1941, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak established Tomchei Temimim yeshiva in Brooklyn, transplanting the flagship Chabad educational institution to American soil. This was a critical step in building a sustainable Chabad presence in the New World and ensuring the continuity of authentic Chassidic education.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
Established the educational foundation for Chabad's growth in America. The yeshiva trained the next generation of Chabad rabbis and emissaries who would carry the movement forward.
