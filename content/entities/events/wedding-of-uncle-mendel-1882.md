# Wedding of Uncle Mendel

## What Happened
Two-year-old Yosef Yitzchak watches his uncle Mendel's wedding. During mourning, his mother brings him to his father's study where he is moved by intense praying.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
