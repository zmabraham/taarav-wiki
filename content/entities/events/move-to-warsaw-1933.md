# Move of Chabad Court to Warsaw

## What Happened
In 1933 Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak moved his court from Riga to Warsaw, Poland. Warsaw had a massive Jewish population and offered a more central location for Chabad activities in Europe. He established his court and yeshiva in the Warsaw-Otwock area.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
Moved Chabad's operational center deeper into Europe's Jewish heartland, allowing the Rebbe to reach more communities. Warsaw was the largest Jewish city in the world at the time.
