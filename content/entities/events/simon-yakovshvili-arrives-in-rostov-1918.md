# Simon Yakovshvili Arrives in Rostov

## What Happened
Simon Yakovshvili stops in Rostov for Rosh HaShanah, meets the Rashab, and decides to stay to study Torah.

This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
