# German Invasion of Poland

## What Happened
Germany invaded Poland on September 1, 1939, beginning World War II. Warsaw was bombed and quickly fell under siege. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak was in Otwock, near Warsaw, when the invasion began. The catastrophe that would destroy Polish Jewry had commenced. His rescue from Nazi-occupied Warsaw became an urgent international priority.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
Set in motion the chain of events that would lead to the Rebbe's rescue from Poland and his eventual arrival in America. Also unleashed the Holocaust that murdered most of European Jewry, including his daughter Sheina.
