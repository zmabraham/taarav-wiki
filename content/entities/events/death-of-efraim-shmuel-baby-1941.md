# Death of Baby Son Efraim Shmuel in Samarkand (1941)

Shortly after arriving in Samarkand, R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin and his wife Figa Rivka lost their infant son Efraim Shmuel. The child had been named after R. Yisrael Yehuda's father. This personal tragedy occurred during the difficult conditions of wartime displacement.

## Description
The infant Efraim Shmuel died shortly after the family arrived in Samarkand. The loss was a profound personal blow occurring amidst the upheaval of wartime displacement and exile. The child had been named in honor of R. Yisrael Yehuda's father, R. Efraim Shmuel Levin of Lipovetsh.

The memoir records this loss with deep personal feeling. Despite this tragedy, the family continued to build life in Samarkand and contributed to the thriving underground community that developed there.
