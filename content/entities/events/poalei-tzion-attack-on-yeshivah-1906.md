# Poalei Tzion Attack on Yeshivah

## What Happened
Members of Poalei Tzion march into the yeshivah. Days later, 200 return with weapons, throw stones, and shoot at the yeshivah.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
