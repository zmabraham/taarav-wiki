# The Yarmarka Incident

## What Happened
A formative childhood incident in 1891 when the young Yosef Yitzchak, at age eleven, encountered a merchant at the Lubavitch fair who challenged the value of traditional Jewish life. The Rashab used this episode to teach his son about standing firm in one's identity and the importance of mesiras nefesh.

## Significance
One of the key formative stories of the Frierdiker Rebbe's childhood, illustrating the Rashab's method of education through real-life experiences and the importance of Jewish identity under social pressure.
