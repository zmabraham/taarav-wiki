# Yarmarka and Arrest

## What Happened
During the yarmarka, Yosef Yitzchak operates a microloan fund. After defending a butcher, he is wrongfully arrested but released.

This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
