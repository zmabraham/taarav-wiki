# Mordechai Ben Hillel Visits Lubavitch

## What Happened
Enlightenment activist Mordechai Ben Hillel visits and opens a secular school with 130 students despite the Rashab's opposition.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
