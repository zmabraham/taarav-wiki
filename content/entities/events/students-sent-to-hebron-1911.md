# Students Sent to Hebron

## What Happened
The Rashab sends a group of outstanding students, led by Rabbi Zalman Havlin, to establish Toras Emes in Hebron.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
