# Arrival in Riga After Soviet Exile

## What Happened
Following his expulsion from the Soviet Union in late 1927, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak settled in Riga, Latvia. He established his court there and made Riga the base for international Chabad operations, maintaining contact with underground networks in Soviet Russia while directing the movement globally.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
Riga became the new center of Chabad world operations for several years, giving the Rebbe a free platform to direct the movement and fight for the Jews he had left behind in Soviet Russia.
