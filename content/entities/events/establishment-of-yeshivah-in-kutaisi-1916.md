# Establishment of Yeshivah in Kutaisi

## What Happened
Jewish community leaders in Kutaisi ask the Rashab for help, and he dispatches rabbis to establish a yeshivah for Sephardic Jews.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
