# Rashab's Illness and Recovery

## What Happened
The Rashab falls severely ill and travels to Moscow, receiving a bleak prognosis but recovers and lives another twenty-four years.

- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
