# Rashab's Illness (Pneumonia)

## What Happened
The Rashab contracts pneumonia and is dangerously ill for six weeks, asking Yosef Yitzchak to stay by his side.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
