# Assassination of Alexander II

## What Happened
Czar Alexander II of Russia is assassinated. Alexander III rolls back reforms and increases oppression of Russian Jews.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
