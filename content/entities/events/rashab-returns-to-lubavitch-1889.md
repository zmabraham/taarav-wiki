# Rashab Returns to Lubavitch

## What Happened
The Rashab and wife return to Lubavitch. Nine-year-old Yosef Yitzchak feels renewed maturity and begins facing his father during maamarim.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
