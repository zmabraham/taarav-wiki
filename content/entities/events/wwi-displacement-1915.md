# World War I Displacement from Lubavitch

## What Happened
During World War I, the Schneersohn family and the Chabad community were forced to flee Lubavitch as the front approached. This ended 102 years of Chabad being centered in the town of Lubavitch, though the movement retained its name. The Rashab eventually settled in Rostov-on-Don.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]
- [[entities/people/rebbe-rashab]]

## Significance
Marked the end of Lubavitch as the physical center of the Chabad movement after 102 years. The movement would never return, though it kept its name. This displacement set the stage for the Rashab's death in Rostov.
