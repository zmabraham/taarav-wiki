# Rashab Accepts Leadership

## What Happened
The Rashab fully accepts the position of Rebbe, taking his father's seat near the holy ark after eleven years.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
