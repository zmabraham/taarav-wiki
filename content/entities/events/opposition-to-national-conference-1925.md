# Opposition to National Conference

## What Happened
Lev Gurewicz attempts to enlist the Rebbe to support a national conference. The Rebbe opposes it as a Yevesektzia-backed plot.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
