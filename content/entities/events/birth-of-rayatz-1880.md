# Birth of Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (1880)

Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn — the Rayatz, sixth Lubavitcher Rebbe — was born on June 30, 1880 (12 Tamuz 5640) in Lubavitch. At his circumcision, his grandfather predicted he would grow up to be a Rebbe.

## Description

The Rayatz was born in Lubavitch, the town that had served as the seat of Chabad leadership since the time of the Mitteler Rebbe. At his circumcision, his grandfather — the Maharash (Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn, 4th Rebbe) — was said to have predicted that the infant would grow to lead the Chabad movement.

His father, the Rashab, was at this time still a young man who had not yet assumed the full mantle of leadership; that would come only in 1893. The Rayatz spent his early childhood in Lubavitch under his father's close guidance, beginning formal education at age four.
