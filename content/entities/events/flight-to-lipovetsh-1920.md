# Flight from Zhivotov to Lipovetsh (1920)

The Levin family's forced departure from Zhivotov after Purim 5680 (early 1920), as escalating pogrom violence made remaining in the town impossible. They traveled by wagon through deep mud to Lipovetsh, arriving just before Passover.

## Description
After Purim 5680, word spread in Zhivotov that murderous bands were approaching and even the local Ukrainian villagers were joining in the killing. The entire community of Zhivotov abandoned the town. The Levin family loaded as many possessions as they could onto a hired wagon shared with acquaintances, and joined the exodus.

The roads were deep in mud and ruts; the passengers frequently had to get off the wagon and push it through the mire. After several days they arrived in Lipovetsh, just before Passover.

On arrival they were brought by the local rav and shochtim to the home of R. Moshe Litvak, a respected elderly member of the community with a large apartment. He provided them with matzos and they made the Passover sedarim in Lipovetsh for the first time.

The family consisted of six people, all of whom arrived safely (B"H).
