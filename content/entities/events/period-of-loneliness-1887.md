# Period of Loneliness

## What Happened
The Rashab and wife frequently seek medical treatment. Yosef Yitzchak finds solace with elderly Chasidim and begins recording chasidic lore.

- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
