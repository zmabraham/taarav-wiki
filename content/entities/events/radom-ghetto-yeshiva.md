# Radom Ghetto Underground Yeshiva

A clandestine Tomchei Temimim yeshiva that operated secretly within the Radom ghetto during the German occupation of Poland.

## Background

After Germany established the Radom ghetto (1940), Jewish education was forbidden under pain of death. Despite this, Chabad activists organized a secret yeshiva to continue Torah education.

## Operation

The yeshiva functioned underground, with students and teachers risking their lives at every session. R' [[entities/people/avraham-shmuel-buchman]] was among those who maintained this institution.

## Fate

The Radom ghetto was liquidated in 1942–1943. Most of the ghetto's Jewish population was deported to Treblinka. The ultimate fate of the yeshiva participants is not fully detailed in the source.
