# Leadership Conclave in Lubavitch

## What Happened
Chabad elders convene and implore the Rebbe's sons to assume leadership. They initially refuse but consent to recite maamarim privately.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
