# First Visit to the Rebbe in New York (1962)

In 5722 (1962), R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin traveled from Kfar Chabad to New York for the first time to spend Rosh Hashana with the Rebbe (R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson). This was a deeply moving experience after decades of longing, during which he had communicated with the previous Rebbe (Rayatz) only by correspondence.

## Description
R. Yisrael Yehuda flew from Lod on a charter flight and had a layover in Paris, where he met with R. Refael Vilshansky of the Parisian Chabad community. From Paris the group continued to New York.

Upon arrival at 770 Eastern Parkway, R. Yisrael Yehuda experienced the atmosphere of Rosh Hashana at the Rebbe's court for the first time. The Rosh Hashana farbrengen (chassidic gathering) was a profound experience. He received a private blessing from the Rebbe.

He also visited the Ohel — the gravesite of the Frierdiker Rebbe (Rayatz) and the previous Rebbes — which held special significance for him, as he had been so close to the Rayatz through correspondence during the Soviet years.

The memoir records this visit as among the most significant experiences of his life, the fulfillment of years of longing to be in the presence of the Rebbe.
