# Aliya to Eretz Yisrael and Settlement in Kfar Chabad (1949)

In 5709 (1949), R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin made aliya to Eretz Yisrael and settled in the newly established Kfar Chabad, where he would live for the rest of his life.

## Description
After escaping the Soviet Union using Polish identity papers in 1946 and spending years in DP camps and transit, R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin arrived in Eretz Yisrael in 1949 and settled in Kfar Chabad. The village had been established under the direction of the Frierdiker Rebbe (R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn) as a center for Chabad life in Eretz Yisrael, and it attracted many of the former Soviet underground yeshiva alumni.

Kfar Chabad became R. Yisrael Yehuda's permanent home. From there he wrote his memoirs (completed 18 Elul 5755/1995), published articles in Kfar Chabad journal, and remained an active member of the Chabad community in Israel.

His arrival marked the culmination of a journey that had taken him through Zhivotov, Lipovetsh, Vitebsk, Polotsk, Nevel, Kyiv, Moscow, Malachovka, Yegarovsk, Voronezh, Kharkov, Krivoi Rog, Samarkand, and many other Soviet cities, before escape through Poland to Eretz Yisrael.
