# Arrest and Death Sentence

## What Happened
Just after midnight, the Rebbe is arrested by GPU officers and taken to Shpalerka prison, sentenced to death by firing squad.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
