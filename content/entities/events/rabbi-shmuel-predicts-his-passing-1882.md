# Rabbi Shmuel Predicts His Passing

## What Happened
Rabbi Shmuel implies to his wife that his passing is imminent, telling her he will 'rise up thirty-two steps in a spiritual way'.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
