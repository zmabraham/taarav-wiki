# Release from Exile

## What Happened
The Rebbe is informed of his release from exile and returns to Leningrad two days later.

- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
