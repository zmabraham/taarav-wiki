# Marriage of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin in Kharkov (1938)

R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin married Figa Rivka, daughter of R. Meir Gorkow, in Kharkov in 5698 (1938). The marriage was arranged through the underground Chabad network and took place during a period of intensifying Soviet repression.

## Description
The marriage was arranged within the underground Chabad community, connecting two families committed to maintaining Torah observance under Soviet rule. R. Meir Gorkow was a respected member of the Chabad community and the father of the bride. The wedding took place in Kharkov, where a branch of the underground Tomchei Temimim network operated.

The couple would subsequently live through the upheavals of World War II, fleeing to Samarkand and later making aliya to Eretz Yisrael.
