# Rescue Mission from Nazi Warsaw

## What Happened
Through the efforts of American Chabad representatives and with assistance from the U.S. State Department, a rescue mission was organized to extract Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak from Nazi-occupied Warsaw in early 1940. The operation involved diplomatic negotiations, German military intermediaries, and considerable danger. The Rebbe, with his family, was brought via Berlin and then Amsterdam to board a ship for the United States.

## Significance
A dramatic wartime rescue that saved the Rebbe and enabled him to continue his work in America. The operation required navigating Nazi bureaucracy, American diplomacy, and German military officials.
