# Yosef Yitzchak's Prayer at the Ohel

## What Happened
The ten-year-old sneaks out in a snowstorm to the Ohel to pray for his father's recovery. The fever miraculously breaks.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
