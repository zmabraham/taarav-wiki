# Rescue of Rayatz from Warsaw (1939–1940)

The rescue of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz, 6th Lubavitcher Rebbe) from Nazi-occupied Warsaw — one of the most dramatic diplomatic rescue efforts of WWII.

## Background

Rayatz was in Warsaw when Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939 (1 Tishrei 5700). Despite danger, he initially refused to flee. After persuasion, he agreed to seek escape.

## Key Dates

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1 Sep 1939 (1 Tishrei 5700) | Germany invades Poland; Warsaw bombed |
| 2 Tevet 5700 (Dec 1939) | Rayatz escapes from Warsaw through Berlin to Riga |
| 24 Adar Rishon 5700 (Mar 1940) | Rayatz departs Riga for Stockholm/Gothenburg |
| 9 Adar Sheni 5700 (Mar 1940) | Rayatz arrives in New York |
| 19 Elul 5700 (Sep 1940) | Rayatz moves to 770 Eastern Parkway, Brooklyn |

## The Rescue Network

### German Side
- **Klaus Shenk** and **Ernst Bloch** — German officers of partial Jewish ancestry who facilitated the passage
- **Admiral [[entities/people/wilhelm-canaris]]** — Head of the Abwehr (German military intelligence) who enabled the transit

### US Diplomatic
- **Attorney [[entities/people/asher-rabinowitz]]** and **Attorney [[entities/people/sam-kremer]]** — coordinated US effort
- **Judge [[entities/people/louis-brandeis]]** — former Supreme Court justice, intervened
- **Congressman [[entities/people/sol-blum]]** and **Congressman [[entities/people/emanuel-celler]]** — political pressure
- **James [[entities/people/james-roosevelt]]** — son of President Roosevelt
- **Secretary of State [[entities/people/cordell-hull]]** — US diplomatic weight
- **[[entities/people/robert-pell]]** (State Dept) — operational coordination
- **Ambassador [[entities/people/alexander-kirk]]** (US Embassy Berlin) — facilitated Berlin transit

### Latvian Side
- **Alfred [[entities/people/alfred-bilmanis]]** — Latvian envoy to USA, arranged entry to Latvia

### In Riga
- **R' [[entities/people/mordechai-dubin]]** — Riga community leader, assisted during Rayatz's stay

## Accompanying Rayatz
- R' [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin]] — secretary (could not later escape; martyred in Riga 5702)
- R' [[entities/people/chaim-lieberman]] — librarian
