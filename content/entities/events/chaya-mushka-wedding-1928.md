# Wedding of Chaya Mushka and Menachem Mendel

## What Happened
On 14 Kislev 5689 (November 14, 1928), Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak's middle daughter Chaya Mushka married Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneersohn in Warsaw. The wedding was a major event in the Chabad world, celebrated with thousands of Chassidim. The date 14 Kislev is celebrated annually in Chabad as a festive day.

## Significance
This wedding united the daughter of the sixth Rebbe with the man who would become the seventh Rebbe. 14 Kislev is observed annually as a Chabad celebration.
