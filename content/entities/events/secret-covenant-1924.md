# Secret Covenant

## What Happened
In Moscow, the Rebbe formalizes a 'secret covenant' with nine trusted Chasidim, swearing an oath of self-sacrifice for Jewish education.

## Key Participants
- [[entities/people/rayatz-frierdiker-rebbe]]

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
