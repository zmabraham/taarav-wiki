# Departure from Lubavitch

## What Happened
The Rashab and Rayatz depart Lubavitch with their families due to the advancing German army, ending 102 years of Chabad in Lubavitch.

## Significance
This event is part of the broader narrative of Chabad leadership during the life of Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz).
