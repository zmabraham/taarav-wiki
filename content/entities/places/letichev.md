# Letichev (Letitshov)

A shtetl near Derazhnya in the Kamenets-Podolsk district (Podolia), Ukraine. Letichev was the birthplace of R. Yosef Levin (ca. 5612/1852), the author's paternal grandfather.

## Key Facts
| Field | Value |

| Country | Ukraine (Podolia) |

| Nearby town | Derazhnya (Dirazshne) |
| Family connection | Birthplace of R. Yosef Levin, paternal grandfather of the memoir author |

## Description
R. Yosef Levin was born in Letichev around 5612 (1852) and later served as rabbi in various towns of the region including Letichev, Zhivotov, Dashov, and Linetz.
