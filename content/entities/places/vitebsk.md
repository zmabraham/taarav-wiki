# Vitebsk

A major city in Belarus (then Soviet Russia), which served as a key hub for the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva network. Vitebsk was a railroad junction where students traveling to Polotsk, Nevel, and other yeshiva locations changed trains.

## Description
Vitebsk was a major transit point and also hosted its own branch of the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva. The author first arrived in Vitebsk in spring 5688 (1928) together with Avrahamele Kvash and Dovid Grin, having traveled from Kyiv without permission. He was astonished to see so many young men with peyes and long tzitzis — a stark contrast to the desolated Jewish world of Lipovetsh.

The author stayed at the home of R. Berel Yafeh in Vitebsk. There was also a famous chassid, "R. Yitzchak HaMatmid," who would repeat maamarim (Chassidic discourses) in the beis midrash during bein hashemashos (twilight), drawing large crowds. The wooden beis midrash in the Zarutchei neighborhood of Vitebsk (near another synagogue) was a center of activity.

A student named Gedalia from Retsitsa had earlier attended the Vitebsk yeshiva and written letters home about the vibrant learning there, which inspired the author to travel there.
