# Korenietz — קאָרעניץ

**Eastern Polish/Volhynian Chabad Community**

**Location**: Eastern Poland / Volhynia (interwar period; now Ukraine)
**Jewish Population**: Significant Jewish community through early 20th century
**Significance**: Served by R' Shlomo Eliyahu Oshpal as Chabad rabbi
**Holocaust**: Jewish community annihilated during WWII

---

## Overview

Korenietz (Yiddish: קאָרעניץ; also spelled Korenets) was a town in Volhynia, the eastern region of interwar Poland (now northwestern Ukraine). The town had a Chabad community served by [[entities/people/shlomo-eliyahu-oshpal|R' Shlomo Eliyahu Oshpal]] as its Chabad rabbi. Volhynia was a region where Chassidism of various stripes was deeply rooted, and Chabad had communities in many of its towns.

---

## Geography

Korenietz is located in the Volhynia region — the area of eastern Poland that borders the historic Ukraine. This region, historically part of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth and then the Russian Empire, had a dense Jewish population with strong Chassidic traditions, including Chabad.

---

## Chabad in Korenietz

### The Community

Korenietz's Chabad community:

- **Chassidic region**: Volhynia had deep Chassidic roots; Chabad was one of multiple Chassidic groups
- **Established community**: A functioning Chabad presence with its own rabbi
- **Community services**: Chabad prayer, learning, and communal functions

### R' Shlomo Eliyahu Oshpal

The Chabad rabbi of Korenietz:

- **Role**: Served as the dedicated Chabad rabbi of the community
- **Period**: Active in the interwar period
- **Contribution**: Maintained Chabad tradition in the Volhynian Chabad network
- **Fate**: Fate in the Holocaust documented in the Toldos Chabad source

---

## The Holocaust

Volhynia's Jews suffered particularly brutal persecution during the Holocaust:

- **German occupation**: Eastern Poland was occupied by Germany after the invasion of the Soviet Union (1941)
- **1942 destruction**: Volhynian communities were largely liquidated in 1942
- **Total loss**: Korenietz's Jewish community was annihilated
