# Nevel

A town in the Pskov oblast of Russia (near the Belarusian border), location of one of the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva branches. R. Mordechai Sternberg (son of the rabbi of Lipovetsh) studied there.

## Description
Nevel was one of the underground Tomchei Temimim locations. A footnote in the memoir notes that R. Mordechai Sternberg of Lipovetsh studied at the Tomchei Temimim in Nevel and in Kharkov, and left Russia in 5706 (1946). He later lived in Bnei Brak from 5723 (1963).
