# Treblinka

Nazi German extermination camp in occupied Poland. Approximately 900,000 Jews were murdered here between July 1942 and October 1943, primarily from Warsaw and the surrounding region.

## Chabad Martyrs

[[entities/people/sheina-horenstein]] (Rebbetzin Sheine, youngest daughter of [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]]) and her husband [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-horenstein]] were murdered at Treblinka. The date is variously recorded as 15 Elul, 3 Tishrei, or 25 Cheshvan 5703 (1942–1943).
