# Latvia

## Description

Baltic state where Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak lived in exile after leaving the Soviet Union in 1927. Riga, Latvia's capital, served as his base of operations for international Chabad activities for several years. Latvia provided relative freedom for religious Jewish life compared to Soviet Russia.
