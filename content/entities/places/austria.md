# Austria

**Chabad and Viennese Jewry**

Full documentation: [[summaries/chabad-austria|אדמו"רי חב"ד ויהדות אוסטריה]]

---

## Overview

Austria — particularly Vienna — was a significant way-station for Chabad Rebbes traveling between Eastern Europe and Germany for medical treatment and communal purposes. Vienna was one of the great centers of Central European Jewry, and Chabad Rebbes engaged with its Jewish leadership during the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

---

## Key Connections

- The Rayatz visited Vienna during his Riga years (1927–1933) as part of his Western European diplomatic activities
- Vienna served as a transit point between Lubavitch/Russia and Germany
- The Chabad-Austria book documents specific interactions between the Rebbes and Austrian Jewish leadership

---

## Holocaust

Austria's Jewish community was annihilated following the Anschluss (1938) and the Holocaust. The Viennese Jewish world — one of the most culturally rich in Europe — was destroyed.
