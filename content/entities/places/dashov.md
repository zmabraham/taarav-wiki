# Dashov

A shtetl in the Vinnytsia region of Ukraine, near Zhivotov and Titov. Dashov was the hometown of the author's maternal family — the Bilienky family.

## Description
Dashov was home to R. Yisrael Bilienky, who served as shochet (ritual slaughterer) in the town and was the author's maternal grandfather. His wife Chava, his daughter Leah Beila (the author's mother), and other family members lived there. The author's mother grew up in Dashov and was educated in a traditional cheder there (unusual for a girl, but reportedly the regional custom).

R. Yisrael Bilienky was a son-in-law of the Rav Rabinowitz (of Dashov).
