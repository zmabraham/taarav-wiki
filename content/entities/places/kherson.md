# חרסון — Kherson

**Location**: Southern Ukraine, on the Dnieper River near the Black Sea  
**Character**: Major southern Ukrainian port city  
**Chabad significance**: Branch location of Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva; underground yeshiva center in the Soviet era

---

## Overview

Kherson, the major port city at the mouth of the Dnieper River in southern Ukraine, was one of the cities in the **Tomchei Tmimim** network — both as a formal branch during the pre-revolutionary period and as an underground yeshiva location during the Soviet era.

---

## Tomchei Tmimim in Kherson

The _Zikaron LeBnei Yisrael_ memoir of [[entities/people/yisrael-jacobson|R' Yisrael Jacobson]] lists **Kherson** among the cities where he studied in the Tomchei Tmimim yeshiva network (alongside [[entities/places/lubavitch|Lubavitch]], [[entities/places/vitebsk|Vitebsk]], Shchedrin, and [[entities/places/kremenchug|Kremenchug]]).

The southern location of Kherson — far from Lubavitch in the north — reflects the geographic breadth of the Tomchei Tmimim network, which established branches throughout the Russian Empire in cities with significant Jewish populations.

The _Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu_ memoir of M. Shusterman also lists **Kherson** among the cities of underground yeshiva study during the Soviet era, alongside [[entities/places/vitebsk|Vitebsk]] and [[entities/places/mogilev|Mogilev]].

---

## Jewish Community

Kherson had a substantial Jewish community in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, as was typical of major Ukrainian cities. The presence of Chabad yeshiva branches reflected the community's size and Chabad's penetration into southern Ukraine.
