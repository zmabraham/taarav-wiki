# Poland

## Description

Country where Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak lived from 1933 until the German invasion in 1939. Poland had a massive Jewish population and was home to a vibrant Chassidic life. The German invasion and subsequent occupation led to the Holocaust that destroyed Polish Jewry.
