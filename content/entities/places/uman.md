# Uman

A city in central Ukraine, historically in the region between Vinnytsia and Kyiv gubernias. Uman is famous as the burial place of Rebbe Nachman of Breslov. The memoir mentions it as the area where the author's paternal grandfather R. Yosef Levin served as rav in several towns (including Linetz, in the area of Uman).
