# Voronezh

A major Russian city south of Moscow, where the author fled after receiving his military exemption in Yegarovsk (autumn 5696 / 1935). He was sent there by the yeshiva network to manage an underground division.

## Description
After narrowly escaping detection in Moscow and receiving his military exemption, the author fled immediately from Moscow to Voronezh. A year later, the leadership of the underground yeshiva network sent him to manage an underground division in Krivoi Rog (Kryvyi Rih). His time in Voronezh represents one of the many cities in the decade-long network of underground yeshiva locations described in the memoir's introduction.
