# Radom

City in central Poland. During WWII, the Germans established a ghetto in Radom for the local Jewish population.

## Underground Yeshiva

A secret Tomchei Temimim yeshiva operated clandestinely within the Radom ghetto during the German occupation. R' [[entities/people/avraham-shmuel-buchman]] was among those who maintained this underground institution, risking their lives to continue Torah education despite the prohibitions and extreme danger.
