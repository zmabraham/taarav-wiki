# Vinnitsa (Vinnytsia)

The capital of Vinnytsia oblast in west-central Ukraine. In Tsarist times it was the center of the Podolia gubernia (province). The memoir situates the author's home region — Zhivotov, Titov, Dashov, Lipovetsh — all within the Vinnitsa province.

## Key Facts
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Country | Ukraine |
| Role in memoir | Regional administrative center; all the author's hometown shtetlach are in "Flekh Vinnitze" |

## Description
Referenced in the memoir as the provincial center (gubernia) for the region where Zhivotov, Titov, Dashov, and Lipovetsh were located. The author's paternal grandfather R. Yosef Levin served as rav in various towns of this region, including in the area of Uman (on the border of Vinnytsia and Kyiv gubernias).
