# Kharkov (Kharkiv)

A major Ukrainian city, formerly the capital of Soviet Ukraine. Kharkov was one of the cities where the underground Tomchei Temimim operated, and it was where the author married in late winter 5698 (1938).

## Description
Kharkov was one of the major cities where the underground Tomchei Temimim network operated. The author spent time there and eventually married his wife, Ita Dvora (daughter of R. Meir Gorkow), in Kharkov in late winter 5698 (1938). The Rav Berel (Grinfeld) Kornitser served as mashgiach in the Tomchei Temimim in Kharkov at some point.

During WWII, as the German forces advanced, the author and his wife fled Kharkov for Central Asia (Samarkand). Before leaving, his wife went to the police with his identity documents and obtained a new joint identity card with a five-year military exemption. This proved critical later in Samarkand.
