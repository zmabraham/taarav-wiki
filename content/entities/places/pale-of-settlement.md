# Pale of Settlement

## Description

The region of the Russian Empire within which Jews were required to reside. Encompassed present-day Poland, Lithuania, Belarus, Ukraine, and parts of Russia. The vast majority of the Chabad communities and the setting for most of the Frierdiker Rebbe's early life were located within the Pale.
