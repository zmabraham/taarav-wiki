# Otwock (Atvotsk)

A town near Warsaw, Poland. [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) relocated here from Warsaw in early Tammuz 5695 (1935) and remained until the end of 5699 (1939), approximately four years.

## Rayatz's Residence

Rayatz's address in Otwock: **Rabbi J. Schneerson, Otwozk, Pruse 6**

During the Otwock period, special postcards were printed for the library (as in Warsaw), now listing:
> *Bibliothek U. Handschriftensammlung Des Oberrabiners I. Schneersohn, Ljubawitsch, Otwock (Poland)*

No street address appeared on the postcards because books were stored in crates within the residence (due to lack of proper space).

Rayatz wrote about the library's cramped conditions in 18 Tammuz 5698 (1938): "I and my books are in exile, squeezed and pressed, with no proper room."

## Tomchei Temimim

The Otwock yeshiva of Tomchei Temimim was a central institution during these years.

## After Rayatz's Departure

After Rayatz escaped to the USA, his son-in-law [[entities/people/menachem-mendel-horenstein]] and daughter [[entities/people/sheina-horenstein]] remained in Otwock. They were eventually deported and perished in Treblinka.
