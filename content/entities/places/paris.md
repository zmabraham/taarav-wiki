# Paris

## Description

Capital of France. A major transit and refugee city for Jews fleeing Eastern Europe. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak and his family passed through Paris during their escape from Nazi-occupied Poland in 1940, before embarking for the United States. Many Chabad Chassidim were stranded in Paris as refugees.
