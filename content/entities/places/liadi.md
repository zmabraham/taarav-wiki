# Liadi

## Description

Town in Belarus where the Alter Rebbe (first Lubavitcher Rebbe) established his court after being forced to leave Liozna. Liadi is associated with the flight of the Alter Rebbe during Napoleon's invasion in 1812, during which he passed away.
