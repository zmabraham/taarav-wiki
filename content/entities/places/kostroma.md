# Kostroma

## Description

City in north-central Russia, northeast of Moscow. The location designated as the Rebbe's place of exile after his 1927 arrest, when his death sentence was commuted to three years of exile. Kostroma was a remote, difficult posting, but the Rebbe was eventually permitted to leave the Soviet Union entirely.
