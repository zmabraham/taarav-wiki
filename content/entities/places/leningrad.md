# Leningrad (St. Petersburg)

City in Russia (formerly St. Petersburg, renamed Petrograd 1914, then Leningrad 1924, reverted to St. Petersburg 1991).

## Lubavitch Library Acquisition

In **5685 (1924)**, [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) was based in Leningrad. There he purchased the book collection of bibliographer R' [[entities/people/shmuel-weiner]] for $5,000. The contract was signed on **8 Marcheshvan 5685**. This collection formed the foundation of the Lubavitch Library.

R' [[entities/people/mordechai-dubin]] (from Riga) and Dr. A. Cohen (Berlin) helped obtain Soviet government permission to export the collection from Russia.
