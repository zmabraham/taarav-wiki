# Brooklyn (Crown Heights)

## Description

Borough of New York City; specifically the neighborhood of Crown Heights. Home to 770 Eastern Parkway, the Chabad world headquarters and the Rebbe's residence from 1940. The community that grew around 770 became the nucleus of the worldwide Chabad movement.
