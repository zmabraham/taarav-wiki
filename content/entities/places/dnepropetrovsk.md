# Dnepropetrovsk (Yekaterinoslav)

## Description

Major city in central Ukraine, known as Yekaterinoslav during the imperial period. Served as the rabbinate of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn, father of the seventh Rebbe. A significant Jewish community existed there before the Soviet persecutions and the Holocaust.
