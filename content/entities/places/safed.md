# Safed

**See: [[entities/places/tzfas|Tzfat (Safed)]]**

"Safed" is the common English spelling for the city known in Hebrew as Tzfat (צפת) and in Chabad parlance as Tzfas. For the full page covering this holy city, its Kabbalistic heritage, and its Chabad connections, please see [[entities/places/tzfas|Tzfat]].

---

## Name Variants

The city is known by several names:

- **Tzfat** (צפת) — Modern Hebrew
- **Safed** — Traditional English spelling
- **Tzfas** — Yiddish/Chabad pronunciation
- **Zefat** — Alternative transliteration

All refer to the same city in the Upper Galilee of northern Israel, one of Judaism's four holy cities and a historic center of [[concepts/kabbalah|Kabbalah]].
