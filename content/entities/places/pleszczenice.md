# פלעשצעניצע — Pleszczenice

**Location**: Minsk gubernia (province), Russian Empire; present-day Belarus  
**Character**: Small town in the Minsk region  
**Chabad significance**: Home community of [[entities/people/avraham-chaim|Rabbi Avraham Chaim Rosenbeim]] and its Chabad kehilla

---

## Overview

Pleszczenice (Yiddish: פלעשצעניצע / Pleszchenitze; Russian: Плещеницы) is a small town in the Minsk region of present-day Belarus that served as home to a vibrant Chabad kehilla in the nineteenth century. It is documented primarily through the biography _Toldot Avraham Chaim_ (Book 2 of the Mesibbv L'Chassidus series).

---

## The Chabad Community

The _Toldot Avraham Chaim_ documents Pleszczenice as home to an organized Chabad community with:

- [[entities/people/avraham-chaim|Rabbi Avraham Chaim Rosenbeim]] as a central communal figure
- [[entities/people/hendl-kugel|R' Hendl Kugel]] as the community's **mashpia** (spiritual guide/mentor)

The presence of a dedicated mashpia reflects the organized Chabad communal structure that extended from Lubavitch's center to peripheral towns throughout White Russia (Belarus). These small-town Chabad communities were the grassroots reality of the movement — often invisible in the grand historical narratives but vital to its survival and character.

---

## Avraham Chaim's Story

[[entities/people/avraham-chaim|Rabbi Avraham Chaim Rosenbeim]] was a prominent figure in Pleszczenice who maintained relationships with three successive Chabad Rebbes (Tzemach Tzedek, Maharash, and Rashab), was imprisoned in [[entities/places/smolensk|Smolensk]] for approximately two years around 1888–1890, and eventually emigrated through Frankfurt and Amsterdam to America — following the classic route of Russian-Jewish emigration.
