# Birobidzhan (Birobidjan)

A remote territory in the Soviet Far East near the Chinese border, designated by Stalin as a "Jewish Autonomous Oblast" (EAO). The Soviet government and Yevsektsia promoted it as a Jewish homeland but in practice used it as a dumping ground for undesirables.

## Description
In the memoir, Birobidzhan is described as a tool of Soviet oppression of Jews. The government compelled Jewish families from Ukrainian shtetlach to relocate there. Initially propaganda was used, then force. Families were made to stand in swamps and cut down trees, load timber onto freight cars, and build wooden houses in harsh conditions. The memoir notes massive insects that carried terrible diseases, severe hunger, and near-impossibility of escape.

Stalin reportedly wished to concentrate all Soviet Jews there — creating what the memoir calls a "ghetto." Jewish prisoners also had their Siberian exile sentences converted to Birobidzhan exile.

The Yevsektsia published a Yiddish newspaper called "Emes" (Truth) there as part of the propaganda campaign.
