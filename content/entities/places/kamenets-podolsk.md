# Kamenets-Podolsk

A historic city and district center in Podolia, Ukraine. Referenced in the memoir as the center of the district containing Letichev (Letitshov) — the birthplace of the author's paternal grandfather, R. Yosef Levin. Also home to the Bilienky family's extended relatives in Dirazne.
