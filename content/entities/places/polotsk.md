# Polotsk

A city in Belarus (Soviet Russia), location of one of the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva branches. Students traveling through Vitebsk would continue to Polotsk to study there.

## Description
Polotsk hosted one of the underground Tomchei Temimim branches in Soviet Russia. Vitebsk served as a railroad junction for students going to both Polotsk and Nevel. The Zichronos memoir mentions that students changed trains at Vitebsk to reach Polotsk.
