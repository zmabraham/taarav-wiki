# Lisbon, Portugal

Capital of Portugal. During WWII, neutral Portugal and its capital Lisbon served as a major embarkation point for Jews and other refugees escaping Europe to the Americas.

## Chabad Connection

[[entities/people/menachem-mendel-schneerson]] (the future 7th Rebbe) and [[entities/people/chaya-mushka-schneersohn]] escaped from Vichy France to Lisbon, from which they boarded the ship "Serpa Pinta" and arrived in New York on **28 Sivan 5701** (1941).

Food packages were also sent from Lisbon to Jews trapped in occupied Europe, as part of the [[entities/organizations/vaad-pidyon-shvuyim]] relief operations.
