# Kapust (Kopys / Копысь)

**Town on the Dnieper River in the Mogilev gubernia; primary printing center for Chabad literature from 1800 onward**

## Overview

Kapust (also known as Kopys or Kopust; Belarusian: Копысь; Russian: Копысь) is a small town on the western bank of the Dnieper River in the Mogilev gubernia of White Russia (present-day Belarus). The town had an established Jewish community from at least the seventeenth century and became one of the most important centers of Hebrew printing in the region.

## Chabad Printing Center

Kapust's central role in Chabad history derives from its function as the primary printing location for Chabad texts beginning in the early nineteenth century. The printer Rabbi Mordechai Halevi Horwitz operated from Kapust and produced nearly all of the Alter Rebbe's major published works from approximately 1800 onward:

- **Tanya** (Likkutei Amarim): The Kapust edition of תק"ס (1800) was one of the earliest major reprintings of the Tanya after the first Slavuta edition of 1796/7.
- **Torah Or**: The Alter Rebbe's collected Torah discourses, edited and prepared by Rabbi Yehuda Leib of Yanowitz after the Alter Rebbe's death (1812).
- **Shulchan Aruch HaRav**: The Alter Rebbe's comprehensive halachic code, published in stages.
- Various other Chabad and chassidic texts.

The Kapust press continued operations well into the nineteenth century under subsequent printers, maintaining its association with Chabad literature. The town's name became so closely associated with Chabad printing that works produced there were often identified simply as "Kapust editions."

## The Kapust Chabad Community

Beyond printing, Kapust also had a local Chabad community and eventually its own branch of Chabad leadership. The town's identification with Chabad through the press reinforced its status as a Chabad-affiliated community. Later in the nineteenth century, a branch of the Chabad rabbinic dynasty was associated with Kapust (the Kapust Chassidim, led by Rabbi Shlomo Shneur Zalman of Kapust, a descendant of the Alter Rebbe).
