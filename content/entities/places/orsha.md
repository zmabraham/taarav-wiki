# Orsha (Arsha)

**Location**: Mogilev gubernia, Belarus  
**Also Known As**: Arsha (Russian: Орша,Оршаа)

---

## Overview

Orsha (also spelled Arsha in the archival documents) is a city in present-day Belarus, located in the Mogilev gubernia during the Russian Imperial period. It appears in the 1825 investigation records as one of the communities interrogated regarding the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe's]] chassidic support network (maavad).

---

## Connection to the 1825 Investigation

When the Babinovich district court sent its instructions to local courts to take testimony about whether the [[entities/concepts/maavad|chassidic support system]] was voluntary or coerced, Orsha was named alongside [[entities/places/kapust|Kapust]] and [[entities/places/shklov|Shklov]] as a target community for witness interrogation. The instructions demanded testimony under oath about:
- Whether Orsha residents had written in 1813 to Kremenchug asking the Rebbe to come
- Whether they had committed to support him and compensate his family's losses
- Whether the annual collections were burdensome
- Whether the Rebbe received a fixed salary or only voluntary donations

The results from these community interrogations formed part of the exculpatory evidence that the donations were entirely voluntary.
