# United States of America

## Description

Country where Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak rebuilt Chabad after his rescue from Nazi Europe in 1940. He famously challenged the notion that 'America is different,' insisting that authentic Torah observance was possible and necessary in the American context. The foundation he laid became the basis for worldwide Chabad expansion.
