# Chernigov (Chernihiv)

A city in northern Ukraine, on the Dnieper River. After the near-arrest in Kyiv, the author fled to Chernigov by boat (Parakhod) down the Dnieper, traveling throughout Shabbat.

## Description
After the NKVD raid on the Kyiv synagogue where yeshiva students were studying, the author escaped without his luggage or identity papers. On Friday afternoon, he bought a boat ticket and boarded a Parakhod (river steamer) headed to Chernigov. The boat traveled along the Dnieper, stopping at various village landings, and the author spent the entire Shabbat on the upper deck, arriving in Chernigov.
