# Berlin

## Description

Capital of Germany. Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak passed through Berlin during his travels in Europe. During the Nazi era, Berlin became the center of the regime that persecuted and ultimately attempted to annihilate European Jewry. The German Jewish community's fate was emblematic of the broader Holocaust.
