# Linetz

A shtetl in the Vinnytsia/Uman region of Ukraine (in the area of Uman, Vinnytsia-Kyiv gubernias). Linetz was the birthplace of the author's mother, Leah Beila Bilienky (Levin).

## Description
The author's mother was born in Linetz, where the Bilienky family lived before moving to Dashov. According to a letter written by the author's mother to the Rayatz (dated Tammuz 5694), she noted that her parents were descendants of R. Avraham Yehuda of Linetz. R. Yisrael Bilienky (the author's grandfather) also served as shochet; his father-in-law, R. Yosef Yehuda, served as shochet in nearby Ir Chadash, and was himself a descendant of R. Nachman Kasover and his son R. Moshe.

The rav of Zhivotov, R. Yosef Levin (the author's paternal grandfather), also served in Linetz at some point.
