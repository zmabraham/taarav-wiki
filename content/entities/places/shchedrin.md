# Shchedrin

**Town near Bobruisk in White Russia; Chabad community with Tomchei Temimim branch; Chabad connections documented through the Rayatz era**

## Overview

Shchedrin is a small town (Belarusian: Шчадрын) near Bobruisk in the Mogilev gubernia of White Russia (present-day Belarus). The town had a Chabad community and is specifically mentioned in Chabad history sources as having a Tomchei Temimim yeshiva branch.

## Chabad Presence

Shchedrin is mentioned in the Shusterman memoir (Lmaan Yadau) — specifically, a Rabbi Yirchmiel Binyaminzon who served in Zlobin had previously been rabbi in Shchedrin (there styled "Streshein"/שצעדרין — the nearby town of Streshin). The area around Bobruisk was one of the most densely Chabad-populated regions in White Russia.

The town hosted a branch of the underground Tomchei Temimim network during the Soviet era, part of the broader effort to maintain Chabad education despite Soviet suppression.
