# Babinovich District

**Location**: Mogilev gubernia, Russian Empire  
**Also Known As**: Babinovitch (Russian: Бабинович)

---

## Overview

Babinovich District (uyezd) was a judicial district within the Mogilev gubernia that included the town of [[entities/places/lubavitch|Lubavitch]], where the [[entities/people/mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] resided. When [[entities/people/governor-khavanski|Governor Khavanski]] transferred the investigation case from his Vitebsk offices to the appropriate jurisdictional court, it went first to the Babinovich District Court of Peace.

---

## Role in the Investigation

The Babinovich District Court received the case file from Governor Khavanski's office on 16 December 1825 (document number 872). The court was instructed by the Mogilev gubernia prosecutor's office to:
- Review the file promptly
- Interrogate the Rebbe (who was ill, so a court investigator was sent to Lubavitch)
- Send questions to Jewish communities in the district about the support system

The Babinovich court sent a police officer on 16 January 1826 to Lubavitch with written questions for the Rebbe and for the Jewish communities of Kapust, Arsha, and Shklov. However, local court officials mishandled the case:
- They failed to bring the Rebbe to court or interrogate him locally as required by law
- The written questions were sent but responses were delayed or lost
- The case stalled for weeks

By February 1826, the Mogilev gubernia prosecutor was sufficiently alarmed by the mishandling to send an angry report to Khavanski about the Babinovich court's "lack of seriousness" and incompetence. This led to the case being transferred to the Mogilev gubernia Supreme Court for more capable handling.

The Masar book notes that all further records of the actual trial — the testimony of Kapust, Arsha, and Shklov communities; the Babinovich court's decision of July 1826; the Mogilev Supreme Court's decision of November 1826 — were not found in the Khavanski archive and remain in other repositories.

---

## Distinction from Babinowitz

This Babinovich District Court is distinct from the separate Chabad community page [[entities/places/babinowitz|Babinowitz]], which refers to a different locality and a branch of [[entities/concepts/tomchei-temimim|Tomchei Temimim]] yeshiva.
