# Ukraine

## Description

Eastern European country that was home to millions of Jews and major Chabad communities. Under Soviet rule, Ukraine was a site of intense anti-religious persecution. Rabbi Levi Yitzchak Schneersohn served as chief rabbi of Dnepropetrovsk in Ukraine before his arrest and exile.
