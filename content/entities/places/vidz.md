# Vidz (Widz) — ווידז

**Early Chabad Community Under Mitnagdic Persecution**

**Location**: Lithuania (now Vidsiai, northeast Lithuania)
**Jewish Population**: Small Jewish community in 18th–19th century
**Significance**: One of the earliest Chabad communities in Lithuania; persecuted by Vilna authorities
**Holocaust**: Jewish community annihilated 1941

---

## Overview

Vidz (Yiddish: ווידז; Polish: Widze; Lithuanian: Vidsiai) was one of the earliest centers of Chabad Chassidism in Lithuania, established in the late 18th century. The community was founded and led by devoted Chassidim who maintained their practice despite intense opposition from the Mitnagdic establishment centered in [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]].

In 1798, the Vidz Chabad community suffered alongside other Lithuanian Chassidim when approximately 30 Chassidim from Vilna and surrounding communities — including Vidz — were arrested on accusations of being dangerous sectarians. The episode was directly connected to the denunciations that led to the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe's]] arrest in the same year.

---

## Geography

Vidz is located in northeastern Lithuania, in a region historically home to a mix of Jewish, Polish, Lithuanian, and Belarusian populations. The town lay within the sphere of influence of the Vilna Jewish community, which meant its Chabad inhabitants were subject to the authority of the most anti-Chassidic center in Europe.

---

## Chabad in Vidz

### Founding Period

The Chabad community in Vidz was among the earliest established in Lithuania:

- **Late 18th century**: Small group of Chassidim settled in Vidz and established a minyan
- **Alter Rebbe's influence**: Community connected to the court of the [[entities/people/alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]] in Liadi
- **Secret worship**: Early period required discretion given Mitnagdic hostility
- **Separate minyan**: Chassidim held their own services according to Nusach Ari

### Persecution and the 1798 Arrests

The Vidz community was directly involved in the wave of persecution following the death of the [[entities/people/vilna-gaon|Vilna Gaon]] in 1797:

- **1797 Hoshana Rabbah Herem**: Shortly after the Gaon's death, a new excommunication was declared against Chassidim
- **Denunciations**: Mitnagdic leaders reported Chassidim to the Russian government as dangerous sectarians
- **1798 Arrests**: Approximately 30 Chassidim from Vilna and surroundings, including Vidz, were arrested
- **Interrogation**: Community members were brought before government authorities
- **Connection to Alter Rebbe's arrest**: The same wave of denunciations led to the Alter Rebbe's arrest in Liadi in 1798

### Aftermath and Recovery

Following the persecution:

- **Continued presence**: Despite persecution, the Chabad community in Vidz persisted
- **19th century**: Community maintained its Chassidic character through the generations
- **Reduced hostility**: As Mitnagdic opposition waned after the early 19th century, the community faced less danger

---

## The Holocaust

Like virtually all Jewish communities in Lithuania, Vidz's Jews were murdered by the Nazis and their Lithuanian collaborators in 1941, shortly after the German invasion of the Soviet Union (June 1941).

---

## Vidz in Chabad History

Vidz represents:

- **Early heroism**: Among the first Chassidim in Lithuania willing to face persecution for their beliefs
- **Mitnagdic persecution**: A concrete example of how early Chabad communities suffered under Vilna's authority
- **Persistence**: Despite arrests and excommunications, the Chassidic spark was not extinguished
