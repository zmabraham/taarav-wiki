# Riga

Capital of Latvia. An important center of Jewish life and a key location in the history of Chabad.

## Rayatz's Time in Riga

[[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) lived in Riga from approximately 5688 (1927/1928) until 5693 (1933), after being expelled from the Soviet Union. During this period:
- The Lubavitch Library was established in Riga with the collection purchased from R' [[entities/people/shmuel-weiner]] in Leningrad
- R' [[entities/people/chaim-lieberman]] began work as librarian
- Special library postcards were printed: "Bibliothek Lyubawitch, Riga"
- R' [[entities/people/david-frankel]] (Husyatin) visited to research the collection
- Researchers came from various places to use the library

## WWII Rescue Transit Point

After escaping from Warsaw through Berlin (2 Tevet 5700 / December 1939), Rayatz was received in Riga. He remained there until **24 Adar Rishon 5700** (March 1940), when he departed for Stockholm and then to the USA. R' [[entities/people/mordechai-dubin]] (Riga community leader and member of the Seim) assisted him there.

The Chabad secretaries [[entities/people/moshe-leib-rothstein]], [[entities/people/shmuel-zalmanow]], and [[entities/people/yitzchak-dov-aushpal]] also passed through Riga on their escape route via Trans-Siberia.

## Soviet and Nazi Occupation

After Soviet occupation in 1940, [[entities/people/mordechai-dubin]] was arrested by the Soviets. R' [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin]] and his family, being Russian citizens, could not obtain new Latvian passports and were trapped.

After Nazi Germany occupied Latvia in 1941, mass murder of Riga's Jews began. In **Kislev 5702** (late 1941):
- R' [[entities/people/yechezkel-feigin]], R' [[entities/people/yitzchak-horowitz-masmidam]], and R' [[entities/people/eliyahu-chaim-althoiz]] were burned alive in a synagogue (10 Kislev 5702)
- R' [[entities/people/yehuda-aber]] (Rosh Yeshiva) and his family perished
- [[entities/people/mordechai-dubin]] was released by Soviets on 23 Kislev 5702; survived in Saratov
