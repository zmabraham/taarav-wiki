# Yegarovsk (Yagarovsk)

A town approximately 100 verst (km) from Moscow, in the Moscow region. The author was required to register and appear there for military induction, as this was his designated military district.

## Description
Yegarovsk was the military district center where the author (R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin) was required to register and appear for military induction. The commander there was described as "a wicked man and enemy of Israel." The author needed to renew his passport there as well.

The miraculous military exemption episode took place at Yegarovsk on 11 Tishrei 5696 (1935). Following the Rebbe's advice (conveyed via a written response) to wear eyeglasses, the author wore reading glasses borrowed from an elderly person and successfully deceived the eye doctor, receiving a complete exemption from service. He then ran back to obtain the official stamps on his exemption booklet, brazenly walking into the commander's office and getting four stamps before being recognized.

Many anash from the Moscow area lived or gathered at Yegarovsk because it was easier to operate away from the capital.
