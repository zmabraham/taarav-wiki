# Kupishok (Kupiškis) — קופּישאָק

**Lithuanian Chabad Community; Martyred Rabbi R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich**

**Location**: Northeastern Lithuania (now Kupiškis)
**Jewish Population**: Several hundred to 1,000+ Jews in early 20th century
**Significance**: Chabad community served by the martyred R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich
**Holocaust**: Jewish community annihilated 1941

---

## Overview

Kupishok (Yiddish: קופּישאָק; Lithuanian: Kupiškis) was a town in northeastern Lithuania with an established Chabad community. The town is located in the Aukštaitija region, historically part of the Kaunas (Kovno) gubernia. The community is remembered in Chabad history primarily through its rabbi, [[entities/people/yisrael-noach-chatzkeivich|R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich]], who served here and was martyred in the Holocaust in 1941.

---

## Geography

Kupishok is located in northeastern Lithuania, in a region with a significant Jewish presence across multiple small towns. The community was part of the broader network of Lithuanian Chabad communities directed from [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] during 1927–1933.

---

## Chabad in Kupishok

### The Community

Kupishok's Chabad community maintained:

- **Synagogue**: A Chabad minyan or synagogue following Nusach Ari
- **Community services**: Kosher slaughter, mikvah, and communal functions
- **Chassidic life**: Farbrengens, Torah learning, and connection to Chabad leadership

### R' Yisrael Noach Chatzkeivich — Rabbi and Martyr

The central figure of Kupishok Chabad:

- **Role**: Served as the Chabad rabbi of Kupishok, documented from 1928
- **Period**: Active in the interwar period (1920s–1930s) through the Holocaust
- **Community leadership**: Provided rabbinical and spiritual guidance to the community
- **Part of the network**: Connected to the Chabad structure coordinated from [[entities/places/riga|Riga]] by the Rayatz
- **Martyrdom**: Murdered by the Nazis and their Lithuanian collaborators in 1941
- **Legacy**: Remembered among the martyred rabbis of Lithuanian Chabad

---

## Interwar Period

Under Lithuanian independence (1918–1940):

- **Religious freedoms**: Constitutional protections for minority religious communities
- **Network connections**: Part of Lithuanian Chabad network coordinated from Riga

---

## The Holocaust

### 1941 Destruction

Lithuania's Jews were among the first to be systematically murdered after the German invasion in June 1941:

- **Speed of murder**: Lithuanian Jews were murdered within weeks and months of the German invasion
- **R' Chatzkeivich murdered**: The Chabad rabbi was killed with the community
- **Total loss**: The Jewish community of Kupishok was annihilated

---

## Kupishok's Place in Chabad History

Kupishok represents the many small Lithuanian towns where Chabad had taken root, supported by dedicated local rabbis who gave their lives alongside their communities in the Holocaust.
