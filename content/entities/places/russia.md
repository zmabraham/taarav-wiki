# Russia

## Description

The country where Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak was born and raised and where Chabad had its historic roots. Under the Czars, Jews faced periodic persecution; under the Soviets beginning in 1917, religious life faced systematic destruction. The Rebbe fought to maintain Jewish life there until his exile in 1927.
