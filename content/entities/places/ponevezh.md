# Ponevezh (Panevėžys) — פאָנעוועז

**Lithuanian Chabad Community with Two Synagogues and a Dedicated Mashpia**

**Location**: Central Lithuania (now Panevėžys, fourth-largest city in Lithuania)
**Jewish Population**: Approximately 6,000–7,000 Jews in early 20th century
**Significance**: Chabad community with two synagogues; home of mashpia R' Shmuel Tzvi Lisson; martyred in Holocaust
**Holocaust**: Jewish community annihilated 1941

---

## Overview

Ponevezh (Yiddish: פּאָנעווּעזש; Lithuanian: Panevėžys) was one of Lithuania's important provincial cities and home to a significant Chabad community with two separate Chabad synagogues — an indication of how well-rooted Chassidism had become there. The city is famous in the broader Jewish world for the Ponevezh Yeshiva, but within Chabad history it is known especially for the martyred mashpia [[entities/people/shmuel-tzvi-lisson|R' Shmuel Tzvi Lisson]], who served the Chabad community there and was murdered in the Holocaust.

---

## Geography

Ponevezh is located in central Lithuania, approximately 130 km north of [[entities/places/vilna|Vilna]] and 100 km east of the Baltic coast. It was a regional center with significant Jewish commercial and cultural activity.

---

## Chabad in Ponevezh

### Two Chabad Synagogues

The existence of two separate Chabad synagogues in Ponevezh reflects:

- **Substantial community**: Enough Chassidim to support two distinct congregations
- **Possible differentiation**: Different groups or neighborhoods within the Chabad community
- **Organizational strength**: Community organized enough to maintain two functioning institutions

### R' Shmuel Tzvi Lisson — Mashpia

The central figure in 20th-century Ponevezh Chabad:

- **Role**: Served as mashpia (spiritual guide/mentor) for the Chabad community
- **Period**: Active in the interwar period and until the Holocaust
- **Dedication**: Known for personal spiritual refinement and dedication to his community
- **Martyrdom**: Murdered in the Holocaust (1941) along with the rest of Ponevezh Jewry
- **Legacy**: Remembered as one of the martyred mashpiim of Lithuanian Chabad

### Community Life

Ponevezh Chabad maintained:

- **Two synagogues**: Regular prayer services in Nusach Ari
- **Farbrengens**: Chassidic gatherings on Shabbos and Yom Tov
- **Connections**: Links to the broader Chabad network in Lithuania

---

## Ponevezh and the Broader Jewish World

### The Mitnagdic Capital

Ponevezh was also known as a center of Mitnagdic Torah scholarship:

- **Ponevezh Yeshiva**: One of the great Lithuanian yeshivos, representing the Mitnagdic tradition
- **Tension and coexistence**: The Chabad community existed alongside the dominant Mitnagdic culture
- **Differentiated status**: Chassidim maintained their distinct identity in this center of opposition

---

## The Holocaust

### 1941 Destruction

When the Nazis invaded Lithuania in June 1941:

- **Rapid murder**: Lithuanian Jews were among the first to be systematically murdered
- **Ponevezh massacre**: The Jewish community was rounded up and shot in mass executions
- **R' Lisson's death**: The Chabad mashpia was among those murdered
- **Total destruction**: The Jewish community of Ponevezh was annihilated

---

## Modern Panevėžys

Today Panevėžys is Lithuania's fourth-largest city with a tiny Jewish presence. The Ponevezh Yeshiva continues in [[entities/places/bnei-brak|Bnei Brak]], Israel, where it was reestablished by survivors.

---

## Ponevezh in Chabad History

### Symbolic Importance

Ponevezh represents several aspects of Lithuanian Chabad:

- **Two-synagogue community**: A mature Chabad presence in an unlikely Mitnagdic stronghold
- **Dedicated leadership**: The mashpia system working at the local community level
- **Martyrdom**: The destruction of a flourishing community in the Holocaust
