# Krivoi Rog (Kryvyi Rih)

An industrial city in south-central Ukraine. The author was sent there by the underground yeshiva network leadership to manage an underground Tomchei Temimim division, for approximately one year before his marriage in Kharkov.

## Key Facts
| Field | Value |

## Description
After his time in Voronezh, the yeshiva network leadership sent the author to Krivoi Rog to manage an underground branch. He remained there until his marriage in Kharkov in late winter 5698 (1938), after which his wandering decade of underground yeshiva life came to an end.
