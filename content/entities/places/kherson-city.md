# Kherson

**City in southern Ukraine; Chabad community documented in the 19th and early 20th centuries; site of Tomchei Temimim branch during Soviet era**

## Overview

Kherson is a major city in southern Ukraine at the mouth of the Dnipro River, founded as a fortress city in 1778. It served as the administrative center of the Kherson gubernia, which included much of southern Ukraine and the Crimea.

## Chabad Presence

### 19th Century

The Kherson gubernia contained several Chabad communities during the 19th century. The nearby city of Nikolaev (in the same gubernia) had a distinguished Chabad community including R' Avraham David Lavot and R' Asher Grotsman.

### Tomchei Temimim Branch

During the Soviet era (c. 1930), Kherson hosted one of the clandestine branches of Tomchei Temimim yeshiva. The Shusterman memoir (Lmaan Yadau) mentions studying at "ישיבת תו"ת בחערסאן" — Tomchei Temimim in Kherson — during the winter of 1930 (5690). This branch was part of the underground network that the Rayatz established to continue Chabad education despite Soviet persecution.
