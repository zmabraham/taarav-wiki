# Vilna (Vilnius)

Capital of Lithuania (known in Yiddish as Vilna, Hebrew ווילנא). A major center of Jewish learning and culture — the "Jerusalem of Lithuania."

## WWII Escape Hub

In 1940–1941, Vilna served as a critical gathering point for Jewish refugees from Nazi-occupied Poland seeking escape routes. Being under Soviet control (Lithuania was annexed by the USSR in June 1940), thousands of Jews including many Chabad students and families gathered here.

Key figures who passed through:
- R' [[entities/people/moshe-leib-rothstein]], R' [[entities/people/shmuel-zalmanow]], and R' [[entities/people/yitzchak-dov-aushpal]] (secretaries of Rayatz)
- Tomchei Temimim yeshiva students and their families

From Vilna, refugees traveled to **Kovna** (Kaunas) where Japanese Consul [[entities/people/chiune-sugihara]] issued thousands of transit visas to Japan, enabling escape via the Trans-Siberian Railroad.
