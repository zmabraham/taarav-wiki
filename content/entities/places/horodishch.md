# Horodishch (Harodzishtz)

**Town in the Minsk gubernia of White Russia; site of a Tomchei Temimim branch during the Soviet era**

## Overview

Horodishch (Belarusian: Гарадзішча; also transliterated Harodzishtz or Horodyshche) is a small town in the Minsk region of present-day Belarus. Like many White Russian towns, it had a substantial Jewish population and a Chabad community.

## Chabad Presence

Horodishch hosted a branch of the underground Tomchei Temimim yeshiva during the Soviet era (1920s–1930s). This was part of the extensive network of clandestine Chabad educational institutions that operated throughout White Russia and Ukraine after the Bolshevik Revolution made open religious education impossible.

The town was in the general region of Pleshchenitsy and Bobruisk — an area with deep Chabad roots — making it a natural location for an underground branch.
