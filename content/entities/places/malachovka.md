# Malachovka (Malakhovka)

A settlement near Moscow where a community of Chabad chassidim gathered. The Frierdiker Rebbe used it as a summer retreat during his time in Soviet Russia. In the 1930s it also served as a gathering place for anash who could not obtain Moscow residence permits.

## Description

Town near Moscow that served as a summer retreat for Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak and his family during his time in Soviet Russia. It was a location where some Chabad activity took place and where the Rebbe could operate with slightly more freedom during warmer months.

In the memoir of R. Yisrael Yehuda Levin (Zichronos), Malachovka is described as a refuge for Chabad chassidim in the mid-1930s. The author spent time there around Pesach 5694 (1934) and encountered the deeply inspiring R. Zalman Moshe, who prayed with extraordinary fervor. R. Elya Balkin was also present, known for declaring: "What is a Rebbe? A Rebbe is the Ein Sof clothed in a body."
