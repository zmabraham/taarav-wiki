# 770 Eastern Parkway, Brooklyn

Chabad-Lubavitch world headquarters, located in the Crown Heights neighborhood of Brooklyn, New York.

## History

[[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) arrived in New York on **9 Adar Sheni 5700** (March 1940) after his rescue from Europe. He moved to 770 Eastern Parkway on **19 Elul 5700** (September 1940).

770 became and remains the central headquarters of the Chabad-Lubavitch movement. The Lubavitch Library, rescued from Europe (100 boxes arriving Sivan 5701), was ultimately housed here.
