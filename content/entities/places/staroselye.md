# Staroselye

A town in **Belarus** (in the historical Vitebsk region), historically significant to Chabad as the location associated with **Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroselye** — one of the most prominent disciples of the [[alter-rebbe|Alter Rebbe]].

## Geographic Location

Staroselye (Старасельле in Belarusian, Старосёлы in Russian) is in the Vitebsk Oblast of modern Belarus. It is relatively close to Vitebsk and Shklov, all of which were important centers of early Chabad activity.

## Historical Significance

### Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroselye

[[aaron-halevi-staroselye|Rabbi Aharon Halevi of Staroselye]] was a major disciple of the Alter Rebbe who eventually founded his own branch of Chabad — the Staroselye branch — based on a somewhat different approach to Chassidic philosophy, particularly regarding the nature of the soul and the path to divine service.

### The Mitteler Rebbe Trial (1825)

During the legal proceedings against the [[mitteler-rebbe|Mitteler Rebbe]] in 5585–5588 (1825–1828), Rabbi Aharon of Staroselye was **summoned from Staroselye** to Vitebsk for interrogation. He was questioned by the Russian authorities on 19 October 1825 (interrogation conducted 21–28 October 1825) as part of the investigation into the Chassidic movement.

This demonstrates the broad scope of the Russian investigation — reaching not only the Mitteler Rebbe himself in Lubavitch, but also other leading figures of the Chassidic world in distant towns.

## Community Life

Staroselye maintained a Chassidic community associated with R. Aharon's branch of Chabad-Chassidus, which survived for some time after his death (1828) before eventually merging back into mainstream Lubavitch Chabad.
