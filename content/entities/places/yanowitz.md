# Yanowitz (Yanowitch)

**Town in the Vitebsk gubernia of White Russia; rabbinic seat of R' Yehuda Leib of Yanowitz (Maharil), brother of the Alter Rebbe**

## Overview

Yanowitz (also spelled Yanowitch or Yanovitch; Belarusian: Янавічы/Yanovichi) is a small town in the Vitebsk region of present-day Belarus. During the late 18th and early 19th centuries, it was notable within the Chabad world as the seat of R' Yehuda Leib, brother of the Alter Rebbe.

## Chabad Presence

The primary Chabad figure associated with Yanowitz is:

- **R' Yehuda Leib of Yanowitz (Maharil)** — elder brother of the Alter Rebbe (R' Shneur Zalman of Liadi); served as the town's rabbi; participated in editing and transcribing the Alter Rebbe's Torah Or

The town's proximity to Vitebsk — the family's home city — made it a natural rabbinic posting for a member of the Schneersohn family.

## Geographic Context

Yanowitz sits in the Vitebsk gubernia, in the heartland of White Russia (Belarus). The region was densely populated with Chabad Chassidim throughout the 19th and early 20th centuries. The Vitebsk area produced many of Chabad's founding families.
