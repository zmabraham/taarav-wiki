# Dirazne (Derazhnya)

A town in Podolia, Ukraine, near Kamenets-Podolsk. The author's paternal aunt Chaya Bracha (daughter of R. Yosef Levin) lived there with her husband R. Malkiman and their family until World War II, after which nothing more is known of them.
