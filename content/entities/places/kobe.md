# Kobe, Japan

Port city in Japan. Served as the primary arrival and temporary refuge point for Jewish refugees who escaped Europe via the Trans-Siberian Railroad in 1940–1941.

## WWII Refugee Transit

After obtaining Japanese transit visas from [[entities/people/chiune-sugihara]] in Kovna, Lithuania, Jewish refugees (including many Chabad students and [[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]]'s secretaries) traveled the Trans-Siberian Railroad through the Soviet Union to Vladivostok, then by ship to Kobe, Japan.

They stayed in Kobe for varying periods while arranging onward travel. Most eventually proceeded to **Shanghai**, China, where conditions were more suitable for an extended stay and where a Tomchei Temimim yeshiva was established.

R' [[entities/people/moshe-leib-rothstein]], R' [[entities/people/shmuel-zalmanow]], and R' [[entities/people/yitzchak-dov-aushpal]] passed through Kobe on their way to San Francisco and then New York, arriving Cheshvan 5701 (autumn 1940).
