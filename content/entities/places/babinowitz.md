# Babinowitz

**Small town in White Russia near Vitebsk; notable Chabad community with a Tomchei Temimim branch; origin of Chabad leaders including R' Berke Chein**

## Overview

Babinowitz (Беларуская: Бабінавічы; Russian: Бабиновичи) is a small town in the Vitebsk region of Belarus. It had a significant Chabad community and is mentioned in multiple Chabad historical sources.

## Chabad Presence

Babinowitz is notable as:

1. **Tomchei Temimim branch** — The town hosted one of the branches of the underground Tomchei Temimim network during the Soviet era
2. **R' Berke Chein** — Rabbi Dov Ber (Berke) Chein, a celebrated Chabad figure who was later martyred by the Soviets, had connections to Babinowitz

The Vitebsk-area towns, of which Babinowitz was one, formed a cluster of Chabad communities with particularly deep roots given the region's historical connection to the founding of Chabad (the Alter Rebbe's family was from nearby Vitebsk).
