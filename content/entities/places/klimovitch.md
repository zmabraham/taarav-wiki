# Klimovitch

A town in the **Mogilev region of Belarus** (Klimavichy/Климавічы in Belarusian; Климовичи in Russian), home to a Chabad community and the birthplace/base of [[moshe-leib-paz|R. Moshe Leib Paz]] — grandfather-in-law of the narrator of *Lemaan Yede'u Banim Yivaledu*.

## Geographic Context

Klimovitch (Klimavichy) is a district center in the Mogilev Oblast of modern Belarus. It lies southeast of the city of Mogilev, in the region that was historically part of the Pale of Settlement and had significant Jewish communities.

## Jewish and Chabad Community

The memoir records R. Moshe Leib Paz as:
- **Shochet** (ritual slaughterer) in Klimovitch
- **Mohel** (circumciser) — serving the community's birth ritual needs
- **Chabad Chassid**

The combination of shochet and mohel was common in small towns: both roles were essential for Jewish communal life, and both required specific training and certification.

## Family Network

The Paz family of Klimovitch connects to:
- [[moshe-leib-paz|R. Moshe Leib Paz]] — shochet and mohel of Klimovitch
- [[zusman-pradkin|R. Zusman Pradkin]] of Shumyatz — his son-in-law (also a shochet)
- [[zusha-paz|R. Zusha Paz]] of Moscow — possibly related (same surname)

This network of Paz family members across multiple towns (Klimovitch, Shumyatz, Moscow) illustrates how Chabad families maintained religious occupations and identities across generations even under Soviet pressure.
