# Spalerno Prison (Leningrad)

## Description

The Soviet prison in Leningrad where Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak was held following his arrest on June 15, 1927. Also known as Shpalernaya. He was interrogated there by GPU agents including Agent Lulav. The conditions were harsh and the interrogations intense, yet the Rebbe maintained his composure and faith throughout.
