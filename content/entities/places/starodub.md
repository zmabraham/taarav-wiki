# Starodub

**Town in the Chernigov gubernia of the Russian Empire (present-day Bryansk Oblast, Russia); site of a significant Chabad community that suffered mass arrest in 1881**

## Overview

Starodub was a sizable provincial town in the Chernigov gubernia, with a substantial Jewish population. The Chabad community there was one of the important congregations in the region, maintaining a yeshiva and a community of devoted Chassidim.

## Chabad Presence

Starodub hosted a functioning Chabad community throughout the 19th century. Key figures include:

- **R' Yehoshua Natan Gnesin (Hershel Neta)** — head of the Chabad yeshiva in Starodub, a significant scholar and educator
- **R' Meshulam Zalman Neymark** — served as rabbi of Starodub before moving to Vitebsk and Nevel

The town was also home to families connected to Hebrew literary culture — the family of Uri Nissan Gnessin (the 19th-20th century Hebrew modernist writer) may have been related to the Gnesin scholarly family.

## The 1881 Arrests

In 1881, following the assassination of Tsar Alexander II, Czarist authorities conducted mass arrests of Chassidim in Starodub. Approximately 80 Chassidim were arrested in a single action. The charges were related to unlicensed religious practice and "fanaticism." This incident was one of the larger coordinated persecutions of a specific Chabad community in the late Czarist period.
