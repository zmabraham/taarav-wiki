# Warsaw (Varshe / Warszawa)

Capital of Poland. A major center of Jewish life and one of the largest Jewish communities in the world before the Holocaust.

## Chabad History

[[entities/people/rayatz-yosef-yitzchak-schneersohn]] (Rayatz) lived in Warsaw from **Elul 5693 (1933)** to **early Tammuz 5695 (1935)**, approximately two years. During this time, he led the Chabad movement and maintained the Lubavitch Library there.

The library was transferred from Warsaw to Otwock when Rayatz moved. During the Warsaw period, regular postcards were printed for the library bearing the "Bibliothek Lyubawitch" header.

## WWII

Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939. Warsaw came under heavy bombing immediately. Rayatz remained in Warsaw initially but eventually agreed to flee. He escaped from Warsaw on 2 Tevet 5700 (December 1939) through Berlin to Riga.

The Lubavitch Library's 100 boxes of books were shipped from Warsaw via Stockholm to New York (arriving Sivan 5701 / 1941). However, 17 boxes of books were left in Warsaw and disappeared, and 3 boxes of manuscripts deposited at the US Embassy were lost when the US entered the war.

Post-war, R' [[entities/people/shlomo-zilberberg]] and R' [[entities/people/pinchas-klein]] found some surviving library books in Warsaw held by the Jewish Central Committee's historical division.
