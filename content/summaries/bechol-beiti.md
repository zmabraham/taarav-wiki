## Overview

"BeChol Beiti Neeman Hu" — a phrase from Numbers 12:7, meaning "He is trusted throughout My house," spoken by God regarding Moses — is used here as a title for a biography of a devoted Chabad servant or functionary. The title evokes both the Hebrew concept of sacred trust and the role of someone faithfully serving the Chabad movement's central institutions across generations.

The book's twenty chapters and introduction suggest a comprehensive biography covering a significant individual's relationship to Chabad leadership and institutions.

---

## The Title's Significance

The biblical phrase "faithful in all My house" (בכל ביתי נאמן הוא) carries layered meaning in Chabad context:

- The "house" of Chabad — the institutional world centered at 770 Eastern Parkway
- The concept of a trusted servant who has access throughout — someone who served the Rebbes intimately
- The Mosaic archetype of selfless, total dedication

The choice of this title suggests the subject was someone who dedicated their entire life to serving Chabad's institutions and leadership — a "man of the court" in the deepest sense.
